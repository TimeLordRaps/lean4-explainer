    L4DEL explanation

    The book first models proofs as data (Proof p), and an axiom is just a constant of such a type.

    1. Implies p q is defined as p → q, and Proof p is a structure with one field, proof : p, wrapping a proof of p.
    2. axiom and_commut (p q : Prop) : Proof (Implies (And p q) (And q p)) postulates a proof of the commutativity of And with no argument for it.
    3. With p q : Prop in scope, #check and_commut p q reports Proof (Implies (p ∧ q) (q ∧ p)).
    4. This is a sketch of how a proof system could be represented; the book then moves to the real Lean treatment of propositions as types.

    See also: modus_ponens
