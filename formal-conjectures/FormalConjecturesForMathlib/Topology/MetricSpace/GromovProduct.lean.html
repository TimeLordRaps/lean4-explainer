The Gromov product

The Gromov product of y and z with respect to x in a pseudometric space is (y, z)_x = (dist x y + dist x z - dist y z) / 2.

This file is copied from Mathlib.Topology.MetricSpace.GromovProduct (leanprover-community/mathlib4#43641). It is in Mathlib from v4.34.0.
