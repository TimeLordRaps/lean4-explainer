Finite hypergraphs and block designs

Finite hypergraphs are finite families of finite vertex sets. This file supplies uniformity, weak vertex coloring, independent sets, transversals, designs, and finite extremal counts. Finset.toHypergraph connects this presentation to Mathlib's Hypergraph.

The ground type includes isolated vertices. The support is H.biUnion id when only incident vertices should be counted. Colorings require two differently colored vertices in every edge; empty and singleton edges therefore admit no proper coloring.
