Topological groups admitting a Lie group structure

A topological group G admits a Lie group structure if it is continuously isomorphic to a finite-dimensional real-analytic Lie group. Lie groups are Hausdorff but not assumed to be second countable: every discrete group is a 0-dimensional Lie group.

Main definitions

  • LieGroupPresentation G n: a continuous group isomorphism from G to an n-dimensional real-analytic Lie group.
  • AdmitsLieGroupStructure G: G has a LieGroupPresentation in some finite dimension.

Main results

  • admitsLieGroupStructure_of_lieGroup: a Hausdorff real-analytic Lie group admits a Lie group structure.
  • admitsLieGroupStructure_of_discreteTopology: a discrete group admits a Lie group structure.
  • AdmitsLieGroupStructure.locallyCompactSpace: a group admitting a Lie group structure is locally compact.
