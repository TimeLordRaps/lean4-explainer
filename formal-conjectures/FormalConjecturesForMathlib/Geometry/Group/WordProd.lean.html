Evaluation of words

A generating family P : Group.Generators G ι indexed by ι gives rise to an evaluation map P.wordProd : List (ι × Bool) → G.

This file is copied from Mathlib.Geometry.Group.WordProd (leanprover-community/mathlib4#43118). It is in Mathlib from v4.34.0.

Main definitions

  • Group.Generators.wordProd: the canonical map from a word List (ι × Bool) over a generating family ι to the corresponding group G. It sends each (i, true) to P.val i and (i, false) to (P.val i)⁻¹.
