Hilbert functions, lex ideals and ideals of pure powers

Let k be a field and let S = k[x₁, …, xₙ], graded by total degree, with the variables ordered by x₁ > x₂ > ⋯ > xₙ. This file defines the Hilbert function d ↦ dim_k I_d of an ideal I of S, monomial ideals, lex ideals, and the ideal generated by pure powers of the first c variables. The sum of a lex ideal and an ideal of pure powers is a lex-plus-powers ideal.

Main definitions

  • MvPolynomial.hilbertFunction: the Hilbert function d ↦ dim_k I_d of an ideal I.
  • MvPolynomial.IsMonomialIdeal: the ideal is generated by monomials.
  • MvPolynomial.IsLexIdeal: a monomial ideal that, in each degree, contains every monomial that is lexicographically larger than one of its monomials.
  • MvPolynomial.purePowersIdeal: the ideal (x₁ ^ d₁, …, x_c ^ d_c).

References

  • C. A. Francisco, B. P. Richert, Lex-plus-powers ideals, in Syzygies and Hilbert functions, Lect. Notes Pure Appl. Math. 254, 113-144.

    The Hilbert function of a homogeneous ideal I of $k[x_1, \dots, x_n]$, sending d to $\dim_k I_d$. This is finite, since $I_d$ is a subspace of the finite dimensional space $S_d$. Two homogeneous ideals have the same Hilbert function in this sense if and only if the graded quotients by them do, since $\dim_k (S/I)_d = \dim_k S_d - \dim_k I_d$.

    A lex ideal is a monomial ideal L such that for monomials u, v of the same degree with u larger than v in the lexicographic order, v ∈ L implies u ∈ L. Here the variables are ordered by $x_1 > x_2 > \dots > x_n$, which for exponent vectors is toLex.
