Sequences whose partial sums are dominated by the next term

If A : ℕ → ℕ is strictly increasing from index n₀ on and A 0 + ⋯ + A (m - 1) ≤ A (m + 1) for m ≥ n₀, then removing infinitely many terms from A destroys completeness. This is the argument of Graham [Gr64d] for the sequence n ↦ fib n - (-1) ^ n: once three terms A m₁ < A m₂ < A m are removed, the remaining terms below A (m + 1) sum to at most A (m + 1) - 2, so A (m + 1) - 1 is not a sum of distinct remaining terms.
