Practical numbers

A positive integer $n$ is called a practical number if every positive integer $m ≤ n$ can be represented as a sum of distinct divisors of $n$.

References:

  • Wikipedia, Practical number
  • OEIS A005153

Main definitions

  • Nat.IsPractical: A natural number $n$ is practical if every positive integer $m ≤ n$ can be expressed as a sum of distinct divisors of $n$.
