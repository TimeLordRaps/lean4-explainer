Mahler's Z-numbers

A Z-number is a positive real number ξ such that the fractional part of ξ (3/2)^n lies in [0, 1/2) for every positive integer n. Mahler asked whether any Z-number exists; this is still open.

References:

  • [Mah68] Mahler, Kurt. "An unsolved problem on the powers of 3/2." Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society 8.2 (1968): 313-321.
  • Wikipedia, Mahler's 3/2 problem

Main definitions

  • IsZNumber: a real number is a Z-number.
