W abbrev
An abbreviation for the van der Waerden number for 2 colors, commonly written as W(k). This represents the smallest integer N such that any 2-coloring of {1, ..., N} must contain a monochromatic arithmetic progression of length k. · Erdos138.W
W def
We define the 2-colour van der Waerden numbers $W(k, r)$ to be the least quantities such that if $\{1, ... , W(k, r)\}$ is coloured red and blue then there is either a red $k$-term progression or a blue $r$-term progression. · Green14.W
