Q def
$Q(n)$ is the largest prime-power divisor of $n$: the maximum of $p^{v_p(n)}$ over prime divisors $p$ of $n$. It is 0 when n ≤ 1. · Erdos700.Q
Q abbrev
$Q(x)$ is the number of powerful integers $n$ with $1 \le n \le x$. · PowerfulNumbersDensity.Q
Q abbrev
$Q(N; q, a)$ is the number of perfect squares among the first $N$ terms of the arithmetic progression $\{q n + a : 0 \le n < N\}$. · RudinsConjecture.Q
q abbrev
Let $q(n, k)$ denote the least prime which does not divide $\prod_{1 \le i \le k}(n + i)$. · Erdos457.q
q def
The complementary prime factor $q = \frac{n!}{p - 1} + 1$. · OeisA55487.q
Q3 abbrev
$Q_3$ is the 3-dimensional hypercube graph (8 vertices, 12 edges). Vertices are 3-bit vectors. Two vertices are adjacent iff they differ in exactly one bit. · Erdos567.Q3
