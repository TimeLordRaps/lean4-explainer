V def
$V(n,k)$ is the number of primes $p$ such that $p^\alpha$ exactly divides $n+k$ and for all $0 \le i < k$, $p^\alpha$ does not divide $n+i$, where $\alpha$ is the multiplicity of $p$ in the factorization of $n+k$. · Erdos889.V
