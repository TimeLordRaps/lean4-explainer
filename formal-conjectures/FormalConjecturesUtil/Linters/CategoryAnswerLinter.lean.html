The Category Answer Linter

The CategoryAnswerLinter ensures that declarations tagged @[category research solved] do not leave answer(sorry) in their statement: once a problem is solved, the answer should be filled in explicitly, e.g. answer(True).

The check is syntactic. Under the default google.answer setting, answer(sorry) at Prop elaborates to a bare True carrying no annotation, so the placeholder is no longer visible in the elaborated term.

A statement that deliberately keeps answer(sorry) (for instance because the problem is independent of ZFC, or because the answer is a constant that is not known explicitly) can opt out with set_option linter.style.category_answer false in.
