    Suppose that $A + A$ contains the first $n$ squares. Is $|A| \geq n^{1 - o(1)}$?

    It is known that necessarily $|A| \geq n^{2/3 - o(1)}$, whilst in the other direction there do exist such $A$ with $|A| \ll_C n / \log^C n$ for any $C$.
