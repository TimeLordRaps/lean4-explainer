    Suppose that $A \subset \mathbb{F}_2^n$ is a set with $|A + A| \leq K|A|$. Is it true that $A$ is covered by $K^{O(1)}$ translates of a subspace of size $\leq |A|$?

    Solved by [GGM25], with at most $2K^{12}$ translates. The factor $2$ cannot be omitted: for $A = \mathbb{F}_2^n \setminus \{0\}$ one has $K = 2^n / (2^n - 1)$, so $K^C \to 1$ as $n \to \infty$, while every subspace of size at most $|A|$ is proper and two translates are needed.
