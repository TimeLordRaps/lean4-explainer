Conjecture 1.74 (Minimal topological groups)

by V. P. Platonov

Describe all "minimal topological groups", that is, non-discrete Hausdorff topological groups all of whose proper closed subgroups are discrete. The minimal locally compact groups can be described without much effort, but the problem is probably complicated in the general case.

A Tarski monster group equipped with a non-discrete Hausdorff group topology is a minimal topological group in this sense, since all its proper subgroups are finite (hence discrete in any Hausdorff group topology). Such topologizable Tarski monsters exist by a theorem of Klyachko, Olshanskii and Osin.

References:

  • The Kourovka Notebook
  • A. A. Klyachko, A. Yu. Olshanskii, D. V. Osin, On topologizable and non-topologizable groups, Topology Appl. 160 (2013), 2104–2120, arXiv:1210.7895, Theorem 1.4.

    There exists a Tarski monster group that admits a non-discrete Hausdorff group topology. This follows from Theorem 1.4 of Klyachko, Olshanskii and Osin, which gives a topologizable Tarski monster of every sufficiently large odd exponent $n$; taking $n$ to be a prime $p$ makes every non-trivial proper subgroup cyclic of order $p$. Any such group is a minimal topological group.
