Sparse Ruler

A sparse ruler of length $L$ is a sequence of marks $0 = a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_m = L$. A distance $k \in \mathbb{N}$ can be measured if there are $i, j \in \{1, \dots, m\}$, such that $k = a_j - a_i$.

One question concerns the structure of optimal rulers. Wichmann [Wi63] gave a parametric family of sparse rulers and speculated that every sufficiently large optimal ruler is of his type. The Wikipedia article records that no optimal ruler of length $1, 13, 17, 23$ or $58$ is a Wichmann ruler, and that every other optimal length up to $213$ is attained by one; non-Wichmann optimal rulers also occur alongside Wichmann ones at lengths $9, 29, 50$ and $68$.

The asymptotic growth of the minimal number of marks of an optimal ruler of length $L$ — i.e. the limit of $l(n)^2 / n$, conjectured to lie in $[2.434\ldots, 3]$ — is the subject of FormalConjectures.ErdosProblems.«170» (there phrased via $F(N)/\sqrt{N}$), and is not restated here.
