    If $f$ is not reciprocal, all the coefficients of $f$ are odd and $M(f) > 1$, then $M(f) \ge M(X^2 - X - 1)$.

    A polynomial $f$ of degree $d$ is reciprocal when $X^d f(1/X) = \pm f$, so nonreciprocity rules out both signs. The negative sign has to be excluded as well: the polynomial $X^7 - X^6 - X^5 - X^4 + X^3 + X^2 + X - 1$ has all coefficients odd and satisfies $X^7 f(1/X) = -f$, but $1 < M(f) = 1.5823\dots < M(X^2 - X - 1)$.
