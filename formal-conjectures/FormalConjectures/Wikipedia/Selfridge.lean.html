    A number p satisfies the Selfridge condition if

    1. p is odd,
    2. p ≡ ± 2 (mod 5),
    3. 2^(p-1) ≡ 1 (mod p)
    4. (p+1).fib ≡ 0 (mod p)

    This is the condition that is tested in the PSW conjecture. Note: this is non-standard terminology.
