    Vizing's conjecture (1968).

    For all finite simple graphs $G$ and $H$, the domination number of the Cartesian (box) product satisfies $\gamma(G \,\square\, H) \ge \gamma(G)\,\gamma(H)$.

    Vizing's conjecture when γ(H) = 1.

    If H has a dominating vertex, so that $\gamma(H) = 1$, then Vizing's inequality reduces to $\gamma(G) \le \gamma(G \,\square\, H)$, which is the projection bound dominationNumber_le_dominationNumber_boxProd. This is the simplest of the known cases of the conjecture (see [BDGHHKR12]).
