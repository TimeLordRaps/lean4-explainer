Kaplansky's Conjectures

Reference: Wikipedia

Throughout, "torsion-free" means that the identity is the only element of finite order (Monoid.IsTorsionFree). This is weaker than Mathlib's IsMulTorsionFree, which asks for uniqueness of roots and fails for the Promislow group below.
