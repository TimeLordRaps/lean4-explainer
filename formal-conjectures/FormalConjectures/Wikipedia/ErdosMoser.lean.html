The Erdős–Moser equation

For positive integers $k$ and $m$, let

$$S_k(m)=1^k+2^k+\cdots+(m-1)^k.$$

The Erdős–Moser conjecture says that $S_k(m)=m^k$ has only the solution $(k,m)=(1,3)$.

References:

  • Wikipedia: Erdős–Moser equation
  • B. C. Kellner, On stronger conjectures that imply the Erdős–Moser conjecture
