Infinitude of Pell number primes

References:

  • Wikipedia
  • A86383

The Pell numbers $P_n$ are defined by $P_0 = 0$, $P_1 = 1$, $P_{n+2} = 2*P_{n+1} + P_n$. OEIS A129

The conjecture says that there are infinitely many prime Pell numbers.
