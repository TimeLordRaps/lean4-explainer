The Goormaghtigh conjecture

A repunit is a number whose digits in some base are all $1$. Here a nontrivial representation has at least three digits. The Goormaghtigh conjecture says that $31$ and $8191$ are the only numbers having nontrivial repunit representations in two different bases.
