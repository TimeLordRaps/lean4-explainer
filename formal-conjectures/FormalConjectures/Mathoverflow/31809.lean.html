    Does there exist a category that is pretriangulated but not triangulated?

    Yes. Theorem 1.2 of [CLLZ26] twists the triangulated structure on the category of finitely generated projective modules over the preprojective algebra of type $A_5$ over $\mathbb{F}_2$. The result is pretriangulated but does not satisfy the octahedral axiom.
