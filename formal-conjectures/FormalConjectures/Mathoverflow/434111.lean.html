  3. theorem MathOverflow434111.restricted_prime_number_theorem The conjecture claims that $\pi_n\sim\frac n{2\ln(n)}$ sorry line 60 source

    The conjecture claims that $\pi_n\sim\frac n{2\ln(n)}$.

    In other words, primes are distributed among the much sparser sequence $(S_n)_n$ with essentially the same density as in the positive integers, up to a factor of $2$.
