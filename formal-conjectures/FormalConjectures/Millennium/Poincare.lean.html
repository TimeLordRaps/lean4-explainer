  6. def PoincareConjecture.SmoothConjectureFor The predicate that the smooth Poincaré conjecture holds in dimension line 81 source

    Source documentation

    The predicate that the smooth Poincaré conjecture holds in dimension $n$, i.e. that any smooth $n$-dimensional manifold that is homotopy equivalent to the sphere is in fact diffeomorphic to the sphere. As in ConjectureFor, the manifold must be Hausdorff: ChartedSpace and IsManifold do not imply this, and in every positive dimension there is a non-Hausdorff smooth manifold that is homotopy equivalent to the sphere.
