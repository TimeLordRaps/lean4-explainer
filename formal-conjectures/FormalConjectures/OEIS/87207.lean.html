Binary representation of primes that divide a number, in decimal

The value $a(n)$ is given by $$ a(n) = \sum_{p \mid n, p \text{ prime}} 2^{\pi(p) - 1} $$ where $\pi(p) = \mathrm{primeCounting}(p)$ gives the 1-based index of the prime $p$.
