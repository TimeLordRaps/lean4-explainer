Tug of war score between prime gap increases and decreases

Score at stage $n$ in "tug of war" between prime gap increases vs. prime gap decreases: start with score $= 0$ at $n = 1$ and at stage $k > 1$, increase (resp. decrease) the score by $1$ if the $k$-th prime gap is greater (resp. less) than the previous prime gap.
