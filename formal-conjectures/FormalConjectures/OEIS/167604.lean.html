Chua's Euclidean prime sequence

For a product $n$ of the preceding terms, Chua's sequence chooses the least prime dividing $d + n / d$ for some divisor $d$ of $n$. The open question is whether every prime occurs.

References:

  • OEIS A167604
  • Andrew R. Booker, A variant of the Euclid--Mullin sequence containing every prime
