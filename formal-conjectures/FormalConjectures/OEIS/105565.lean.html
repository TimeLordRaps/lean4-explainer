Indicator sequence for 5 Fibonacci numbers with n digits

$a(n) = 1$ if exactly 5 Fibonacci numbers exist with exactly $n$ digits, otherwise $0$. For the partial sums $S(n) = \sum_{k=1}^n a(k)$, it is conjectured that $\beta-2 < S(n)-\alpha n < \beta-1$, where $\alpha = \log(10)/\log(\phi) - 4$ and $\beta = \log(5)/(2\log(\phi)) - 1$.
