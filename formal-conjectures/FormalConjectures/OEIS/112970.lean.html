A generalized Stern sequence

A112970: A generalized Stern sequence, defined by the recurrence relations: $a(2n+1) = a(n)$ and $a(2n) = a(n) + a(n-2)$ with $a(0)=1$, $a(1)=1$ and $a(n)=0$ for $n \le -1$.
