Sum of two squares, a power of 3, and a power of 5

Any integer $n > 1$ can be written as $a^2 + b^2 + 3^c + 5^d$ where $a, b, c, d$ are nonnegative integers.

Zhi-Wei Sun has offered a $3,500 prize for the first proof.
