    $a(1) = 2$; $a(2n)$ = lcm of all previous terms + 1; $a(2n+1)$ = lcm of all previous terms - 1. The sequence is correctly generated by the recurrence relation $a(1)=2$, $a(2)=3$, and for $n \ge 3$: $$a(n) = a(n-1)^2 + (-1)^n a(n-1) + (-1)^n.$$ We use $0$-based indexing here, so $a(n)$ corresponds to the OEIS sequence $a(n+1)$.
