Eighth root of the generating function of an Apéry-like sequence

The auxiliary sequence used for the Hankel matrix, defined as $$\sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k}^2 \binom{n+k}{k}^2$$

Determinant of the $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ Hankel-type matrix with $(i,j)$-entry equal to A005259$(i+j)$ for all $i,j = 0,\dots,n$. The entry function A005259 is taken to be $\sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k}^2 \binom{n+k}{k}^2$.
