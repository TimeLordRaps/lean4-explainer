Asymptotics of subsets of $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$ with integer average

Number of nonempty subsets of $\{1, 2, 3, \dots, n\}$ whose elements have an integer average.

Benoit Cloitre conjectured the asymptotic expansion: $$a(n) = \frac{2^{n+1}}{n} \left(1 + \frac{1}{n} + \frac{3}{n^2} + \frac{13}{n^3} + \frac{75}{n^4} + \frac{541}{n^5} + o\left(\frac{1}{n^5}\right)\right)$$ where the coefficients $1, 1, 3, 13, 75, 541, \dots$ are the Fubini numbers (preferential arrangements, A000670).
