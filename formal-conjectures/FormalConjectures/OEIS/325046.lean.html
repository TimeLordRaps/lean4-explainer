Pronic indices for odd coefficients of $\sum_{n \ge 0} x^n \frac{(1+x^n)^n}{(1-x^{n+1})^{n+1}}$

Coefficients of G.f. $\sum_{n \ge 0} x^n \cdot \frac{(1 + x^n)^n}{(1 - x^{n+1})^{n+1}}$.

The term $a(N)$ is the coefficient of $x^N$ in the generating function. Expanding the terms, we get a formula for $a(N)$: $$a(N) = \sum_{n=0}^N \sum_{k=0}^n \mathbf{1}_{n + nk + (n+1)j = N} \binom{n}{k} \binom{n+j}{j}$$ where $j = \frac{N - n(k+1)}{n+1}$.
