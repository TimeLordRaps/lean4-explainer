Central trinomial coefficients

Central trinomial coefficients: largest coefficient of $(1 + x + x^2)^n$, which is the coefficient of $x^n$ in the expansion of $(1 + x + x^2)^n$.

  7. theorem OeisA2426.conjecture An integer $n > 3$ is prime if and only if $a(n) \equiv 1 \pmod{n^2}$ sorry line 64 source

    An integer $n > 3$ is prime if and only if $a(n) \equiv 1 \pmod{n^2}$. We have verified this for $n$ up to $8 \cdot 10^5$, and proved that $a(p) \equiv 1 \pmod{p^2}$ for any prime $p > 3$ (cf. A277640).
