A binomial coefficient sum

A binomial coefficient sum: $$a(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \left( \binom{n}{k} - \binom{n}{k-1} \right)^3$$ where $\binom{n}{-1} = 0$.
