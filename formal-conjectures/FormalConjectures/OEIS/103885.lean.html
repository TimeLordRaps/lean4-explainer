$a(n) = [x^{2n}] \left(\frac{1 + x}{1 - x}\right)^n$

The sequence is given by the combinatorial identity: $a(n) = \sum_{k = 0}^n \binom{n}{k} \binom{2n+k-1}{n-1}$ with $a(0) = 1$.
