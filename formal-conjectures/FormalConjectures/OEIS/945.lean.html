Euclid-Mullin sequence

The Euclid-Mullin sequence starts with $a(1) = 2$. Each subsequent term is the smallest prime factor of one plus the product of all preceding terms. We extend the sequence by $a(0) = 1$ and write $b(n)$ for the product of the first $n$ official terms.
