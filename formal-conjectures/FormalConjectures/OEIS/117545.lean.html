Least $k$ such that cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_k(n)$ is prime

$a(n) = \min \{k \in \mathbb{N} \mid 0 < k \wedge \text{Prime}(|\Phi_k(n)|) \}$, where $\Phi_k(n)$ is the $k$-th cyclotomic polynomial evaluated at $n$.
