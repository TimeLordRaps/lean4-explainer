Fibonacci-Collatz sequence

Fibonacci-Collatz sequence: $a(1)=1, a(2)=2$; for $n > 2$, let $\mathrm{fib} = a(n-1) + a(n-2)$; if $\mathrm{fib}$ is odd then $a(n) = 3 \cdot \mathrm{fib} + 1$ else $a(n) = \mathrm{fib}/2$.
