Recurrence involving LCM: $a(n) = x(n+1)/x(n) - 2$

$a(n) = x(n+1)/x(n) - 2$ where $x(1)=1$ and $x(n) = 2 x(n-1) + \operatorname{lcm}(x(n-1),n)$ for $n > 1$.
