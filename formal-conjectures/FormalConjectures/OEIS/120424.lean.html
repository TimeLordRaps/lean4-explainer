Half-Fibonacci sequence

$a(0) = 1, a(1) = 3$; for $n \ge 2$, $a(n) = f(a(n-1)) + f(a(n-2))$ where $f(x) = x/2$ if $x$ is even and $f(x) = x$ if $x$ is odd.
