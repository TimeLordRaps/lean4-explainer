Number of prime powers strictly between $n$-th prime and $(n+1)$-th prime

The sequence $a(n)$ is the number of prime powers $k$ strictly between the $n$-th prime $p_n$ and the $(n+1)$-th prime $p_{n+1}$: $p_n < k < p_{n+1}$.
