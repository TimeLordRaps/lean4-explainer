A108306: Expansion of $(3x+1)/(1-3x-3x^2)$

This sequence satisfies the linear recurrence relation $a(0)=1$, $a(1)=6$, and $a(n) = 3a(n-1) + 3a(n-2)$ for $n \ge 2$.
