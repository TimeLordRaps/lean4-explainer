Period of $p$-th powers modulo $n$

a: Period of cubes mod $n$. The $n$-th term $a(n)$ is the smallest positive integer $T$ such that $\forall k \in \mathbb{N}$, $(k+T)^3 \equiv k^3 \pmod n$.

The length of the minimal positive period of the sequence $k^p \pmod n$. $a_p(n) = \min \{ T \in \mathbb{N}^+ \mid \forall k \in \mathbb{N}, (k+T)^p \equiv k^p \pmod n \}$.
