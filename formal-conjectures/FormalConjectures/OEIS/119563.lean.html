Sum of Fermat number and Mersenne number minus 1: $2^{2^n} + 2^n - 1$

Define $F(n) = 2^{2^n} + 1$ (the $n$-th Fermat number) and $M(n) = 2^n - 1$ (the $n$-th Mersenne number). Then $a(n) = F(n) + M(n) - 1 = 2^{2^n} + 2^n - 1$.
