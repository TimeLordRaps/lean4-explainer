Recurrence with fourth powers of binomial coefficients

The sequence is defined by $a(1) = 2$, and for $n \ge 2$, $$(2n+1)^3 a(n) = 32n^3 a(n-1) + (21n^3 + 22n^2 + 8n + 1) \binom{2n-1}{n}^4.$$
