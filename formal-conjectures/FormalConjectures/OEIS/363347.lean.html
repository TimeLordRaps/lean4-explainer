    Denominator of the continued fraction $$\frac{1}{2 - \frac{3}{3 - \frac{4}{4 - \frac{5}{\dots - \frac{n-1}{(n-1) - \frac{n}{-4}}}}}} $$ The value of the continued fraction is $C_n = 1/R_2(n)$. If $R_2(n) = N/D$ in reduced form, $C_n = D/N$. The sequence $a(n)$ is the denominator of the final fraction, which is $\vert N \vert$.
