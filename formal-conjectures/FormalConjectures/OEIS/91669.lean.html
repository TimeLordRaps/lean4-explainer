Primality and primitive root property from divisibility $n \mid (a(n-1) + 2^{n-2})$

$a(n) = \frac{2^{n-1}}{n!} \prod_{k=1}^{n-1} (2^k-1)$. The sequence $a(n)$ is composed of natural numbers, thus we define it as a function $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$.
