Recurrence with bitwise XOR

The sequence is defined by $a(0) = 0$, $a(1) = 1$, and for $n \ge 0$, $$a(n+2) = (a(n+1) \mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}} (n+2)) - a(n),$$ where $\mathrm{XOR}$ is the bitwise exclusive-or operator on integers.
