Collatz step differences

Differences in adjacent elements of the sequence quantifying the steps needed for $n$ to converge to 1 in the Collatz Conjecture. $$a(n) = \mathrm{A006577}(n+1) - \mathrm{A006577}(n)$$ for $n > 0$.
