Numerator of a sum involving binomial coefficients

$a(n)$ is the numerator of $\sum_{k = 1}^n \frac{1}{k^3} \binom{n}{k}^2 \binom{n+k}{k}^2$ for $n \ge 1$ with $a(0) = 0$.
