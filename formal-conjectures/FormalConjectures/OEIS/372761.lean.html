Unique realization of odd primes $p \notin \{3, 5\}$ by continued fraction values

$a(n)$ is the denominator of the finite continued fraction $$\frac{1}{2 - \frac{3}{3 - \frac{4}{4 - \frac{5}{\dots - \frac{n-1}{(n-1) - \frac{n}{n+4}}}}}}$$
