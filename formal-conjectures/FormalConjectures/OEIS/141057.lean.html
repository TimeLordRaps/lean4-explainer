Number of Abelian cubes of length $3n$ over an alphabet of size 3

An Abelian cube is a string of the form $x x' x''$ with $|x| = |x'| = |x''|$ and $x$ is a permutation of $x'$ and $x''$. The number of Abelian cubes of length $3n$ over an alphabet of size 3 is given by $$a(n) = \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k}^3 \sum_{j=0}^k \binom{k}{j}^3.$$
