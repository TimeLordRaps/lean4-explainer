The 1680-Conjecture

Any nonnegative integer can be written as $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + w^2$ with $x, y, z, w$ nonnegative integers such that $x^4 + 1680 y^3 z$ is a square.

Zhi-Wei Sun has offered a prize of 1,680 RMB for the first proof.
