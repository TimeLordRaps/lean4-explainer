Product of composite numbers in triangular intervals

Product of all composite numbers between $n(n-1)/2+1$ and $n(n+1)/2$ (including boundaries), where $n(n-1)/2 = \binom{n}{2}$ and $n(n+1)/2 = \binom{n+1}{2}$.
