Recurrence $a(n+2) = |a(n+1)a(n) - a(n)a(n+1)|$ via concatenation

The sequence starts with $a(1) = 0, a(2) = 1$. For $n \ge 1$, $$a(n+2) = |\text{concat}(a(n+1), a(n)) - \text{concat}(a(n), a(n+1))|$$ where concatenation is in decimal representation.
