Coefficients of $\prod_{k>0} (1 - x^k/k!)$

The sequence $a(n)$ has exponential generating function $$E(x) = \prod_{k=1}^\infty \left(1 - \frac{x^k}{k!}\right),$$ so that $a(n) = n! [x^n] \prod_{k=1}^n \left(1 - \frac{x^k}{k!}\right)$.
