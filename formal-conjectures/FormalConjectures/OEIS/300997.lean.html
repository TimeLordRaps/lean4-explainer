Step difference bound $a(n+1) - a(n) \in \{1, 2\}$ in mass-redistribution cellular automata

$a(n)$ is the number of steps needed to reach a stable configuration in the 1D cellular automaton initialized with one cell with mass $n$ and based on the rule "each cell gives half of its mass, rounded down, to its right neighbor". The stable configuration is $n$ cells with mass 1.
