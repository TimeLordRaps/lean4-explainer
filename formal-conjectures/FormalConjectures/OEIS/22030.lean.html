A nonlinear recurrence sequence

For even $n$, $a(n+2)$ is the greatest integer such that $a(n+2)/a(n+1) < a(n+1)/a(n)$; for odd $n$, the least integer such that $a(n+2)/a(n+1) > a(n+1)/a(n)$; $a(0) = 4, a(1) = 16$.
