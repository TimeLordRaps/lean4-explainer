Product of two consecutive primes modulo the next prime

The sequence is defined by $$a(n) = \mathrm{prime}(n) \cdot \mathrm{prime}(n+1) \bmod \mathrm{prime}(n+2),$$ where $\mathrm{prime}(k)$ is the $k$-th prime number ($\mathrm{prime}(1)=2$).
