Number of odd primes between $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$ with $(n/p) = 1$

$a(n)$ is the number of odd primes $p$ between $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$ such that the Legendre symbol $\left(\frac{n}{p}\right) = 1$.

References:

  • Z.-W. Sun, "Conjectures involving primes and quadratic forms", arXiv preprint arXiv:1211.1588 [math.NT], 2012.
