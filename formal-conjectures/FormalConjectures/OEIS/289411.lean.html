Symmetry of digit sum differences $\operatorname{sign}(S_5(k) - S_1(k))$

a: $\mathrm{a}(n) = \sum_{k=0}^n \mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{A007953}(5k) - \mathrm{A007953}(k))$. $\mathrm{A007953}(n)$ is the digital sum of $n$ in base 10. The sequence is non-negative, so the sum over $\mathbb{Z}$ is converted to $\mathbb{N}$.
