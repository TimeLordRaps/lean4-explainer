Decimal encoding of the prime factorization of $n$

If $n$ has prime factorization $p_1^{e_1} \dots p_r^{e_r}$ with $p_1 < \dots < p_r$, then its decimal encoding is $p_1 e_1 \dots p_r e_r$.
