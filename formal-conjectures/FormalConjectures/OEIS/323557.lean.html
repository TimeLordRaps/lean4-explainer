Pronic indices for odd coefficients of $\sum_{n \ge 0} x^n \frac{(1+x^n)^n}{(1+x^{n+1})^{n+1}}$

Coefficients of G.f. $\sum_{n\ge 0} x^n \cdot \frac{(1 + x^n)^n}{(1 + x^{n+1})^{n+1}}$. The $m$-th term $a(m)$ is the coefficient of $x^m$, which is explicitly given by the sum: $$ a(m) = \sum_{n=0}^m \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} (-1)^j \binom{n+j}{j},$$ where $j = \frac{m - n(k+1)}{n+1}$, and the term is zero unless $j$ is a natural number.
