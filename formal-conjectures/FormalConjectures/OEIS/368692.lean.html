Integrality of the factorial ratio $\frac{(12n+6)! (6n+9)!}{108 (4n+2)! (2n+3)! ((6n+5)!)^2}$

The factorial ratio $$a(n) = \frac{(12n + 6)! \cdot (6n + 9)!}{108 \cdot (4n + 2)! \cdot (2n + 3)! \cdot ((6n + 5)!)^2}$$ It is conjectured that $a(n)$ are integers.
