Convergence of fraction numerators $a(n)/b(n)$ to $e$

a: Numerators of a sequence of fractions converging to $e$. $$a(1) = 3, a(2) = 5$$ For $n > 2$: $$a(n) = \begin{cases} \left(\frac{n+2}{2}\right) a(n-1) - a(n-2) - \left(\frac{n-2}{2}\right) a(n-3) & \text{if } n \text{ is even} \cr 2 a(n-1) + n a(n-2) & \text{if } n \text{ is odd} \end{cases}$$
