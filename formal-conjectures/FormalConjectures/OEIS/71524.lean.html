Determinant of matrix with entries indicating primality of $i^2 + j^2$

The sequence $a(n)$ is the determinant of the $n \times n$ matrix $M$ defined by $M(i,j) = 1$ if $i^2 + j^2$ is prime, and $0$ otherwise, where $1 \le i, j \le n$.
