Least $k > 0$ such that $(k+1)(k+2)\cdots(k+n) + 1$ is prime

The sequence $a(n)$ is the least positive integer $k$ such that $(k+1)(k+2)\cdots(k+n) + 1$ is prime, if such $k$ exists; otherwise $a(n) = 0$.
