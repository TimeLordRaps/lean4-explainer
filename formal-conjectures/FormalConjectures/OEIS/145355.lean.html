Factorial distance to nearest square

The sequence is defined as $$a(n) = \mathrm{round}\left(\frac{\mathrm{round}(\sqrt{n!})}{\left|(\mathrm{round}(\sqrt{n!}))^2 - n!\right|}\right)$$ for $n \ge 2$.
