Number of pairs of twin primes between $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$

$a(n)$ is the number of pairs of twin primes between $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$. This counts the number of primes $p$ such that $p$ and $p+2$ are both prime, and the entire twin prime pair $(p, p+2)$ lies strictly between $n^2$ and $(n+1)^2$. That is, $n^2 < p$ and $p + 2 < (n+1)^2$.
