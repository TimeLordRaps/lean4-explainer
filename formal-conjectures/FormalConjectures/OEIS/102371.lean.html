Conjectures associated with A102371

The sequence $a(n)$ is defined by the recurrence relation $a(1)=1$, and for $n>1$, $a(n) = a(n-1) \operatorname{XOR} (a(n-1) + n)$. The conjecture asks if $a(n) = 2^n - 1 - \operatorname{A105033}(n-1)$ for $n \ge 1$.
