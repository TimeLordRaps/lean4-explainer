A binomial coefficient summation

A binomial coefficient summation: $a(n) = S(3, n) / S(1, n)$, where for a positive integer $r$ we define $$S(r,n) = \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \left( \binom{n}{k} - \binom{n}{k-1} \right)^r$$ with $\binom{n}{-1} = 0$.

References:

  • A003162
  • H. W. Gould, Problem E2384 (proposal), Amer. Math. Monthly, 81 (1974), 170-171
