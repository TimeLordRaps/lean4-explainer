Trajectory of 103 under the Reverse and Add! operation in base 3

The sequence $a(n)$ is the trajectory of $103$ under the Reverse and Add! operation carried out in base $3$, written in base $10$. $a(0) = 103$, and $a(n+1) = a(n) + \text{rev}_3(a(n))$.
