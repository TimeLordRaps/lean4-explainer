Numerator of $\sum_{k=1}^n 2^k/k$.

Conjecture: for $n > 3$, $\textrm{numerator}(-2/n + \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{2^k}{k}) == 0 (\textrm{mod} n^2)$ if and only if n is prime.
