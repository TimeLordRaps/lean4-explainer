Conjectures associated with A113252

Corresponds to m = 6 in a family of 4th order linear recurrence sequences

This sequence is defined by the linear recurrence relation a(n) = -4 a(n-1) + 144 a(n-3) + 1296 a(n-4) for n > 3. Initial values are a(0) = -1, a(1) = 4, a(2) = 92, a(3) = 784.
