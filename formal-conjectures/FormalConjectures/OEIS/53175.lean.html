Catalan-Larcombe-French sequence

The Catalan-Larcombe-French sequence defined by $a(0)=1$, $a(1)=8$, and $$n^2 a(n) = 8(3n^2 - 3n + 1) a(n-1) - 128(n-1)^2 a(n-2)$$ for $n \ge 2$.

References:

  • A053175
  • [ZS18] Zhu, B.-X., Sun, Z.-W., Hankel-type determinants for some combinatorial sequences, Int. J. Number Theory 14 (2018), 1265-1277
