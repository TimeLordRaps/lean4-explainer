Number of primes in $n$-th row of triangle $k^2 - k + p_n$

$a(n)$ is the number of primes in the $n$-th row of the triangle $T(n, k) = k^2 - k + p_n$ for $1 \le k \le n$, where $p_n$ is the $n$-th prime ($p_1=2, p_2=3, \dots$).
