$\gcd(\mathrm{numerator}(H_n), n!)$

$a(n) = \gcd(\mathrm{A001008}(n), n!)$, where $\mathrm{A001008}(n)$ is the numerator of the $n$-th harmonic number $H_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{i}$.
