Number of primes $p$ such that $n^n \le p \le n^n + n^2$

The sequence $a(n)$ counts the number of prime numbers in the interval $[n^n, n^n + n^2]$: $$a(n) = |\{p \text{ prime} \mid n^n \le p \le n^n + n^2\}|$$

  7. theorem OeisA69922.conjecture Question: for any $n > 0$, is there at least one prime $p$ such that $n^n \le p \le n^n + n^2$? sorry line 69 source

    Question: for any $n > 0$, is there at least one prime $p$ such that $n^n \le p \le n^n + n^2$? In this case, that would be stronger than the Schinzel conjecture: "for $m > 1$ there's at least one prime $p$ such that $m \le p \le m + \log(m)^2$" since $n^2 < \log(n^n)^2 = n^2 \log(n)^2$.
