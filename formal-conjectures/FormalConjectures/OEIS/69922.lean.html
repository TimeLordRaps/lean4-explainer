Number of primes $p$ such that $n^n \le p \le n^n + n^2$

The sequence $a(n)$ counts the number of prime numbers in the interval $[n^n, n^n + n^2]$: $$a(n) = |\{p \text{ prime} \mid n^n \le p \le n^n + n^2\}|$$
