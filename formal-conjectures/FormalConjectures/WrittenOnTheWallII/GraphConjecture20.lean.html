    WOWII Conjecture 20

    For a simple connected graph G, b(G) ≥ n(G) / ⌊deg_avg(G)⌋, where b(G) is the size of a largest induced bipartite subgraph, n(G) is the number of vertices, and deg_avg(G) = (∑ v, deg(v)) / n(G) is the average degree.
