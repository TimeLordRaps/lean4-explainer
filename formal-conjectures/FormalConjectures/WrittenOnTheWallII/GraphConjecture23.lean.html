    WOWII Conjecture 23

    For a simple connected graph G, b(G) ≥ ⌊α(G) + dist_avg(M) / 2⌋, where b(G) is the size of a largest induced bipartite subgraph, α(G) is the independence number, M is the set of maximum-degree vertices, and dist_avg(M) is the average distance between distinct vertices of M.

    This conjecture is false; there is a counterexample with b(G) = 19, α(G) = 15, and dist_avg(M) = 10.
