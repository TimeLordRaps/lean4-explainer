Written on the Wall II - Conjecture 430a

Reference: E. DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, Conjectures of Graffiti.pc

The conjecture is false for the nonuniform clique blow-up of P₇ with blob orders (1,4,12,19,12,4,1). Its independent domination number is three, its center-neighborhood independence number is two, and its Caro--Wei sum is less than two. The claimed inequality therefore becomes 3 ≤ 2.
