    WOWII Conjecture 40

    For a nontrivial connected graph G the size f(G) of a largest induced forest satisfies f(G) ≥ ceil((p(G) + b(G) + 1)/2) where p(G) is the path cover number and b(G) is the largest induced bipartite subgraph size.
