    WOWII Conjecture 18

    For a simple connected graph $G$, the size $b(G)$ of a largest induced bipartite subgraph satisfies $b(G) \ge \alpha(G) + \lceil \sqrt{\mathrm{dist}_{\max}(M)} \rceil$, where $\alpha(G)$ is the independence number, $M$ is the set of maximum-degree vertices, and $\mathrm{dist}_{\max}(M) = \max\{\mathrm{dist}_G(u,v) \mid u, v \in M\}$ is the maximum distance between two maximum-degree vertices (DeLaVina's dist_max(M)). Proven by Benny John (Feb. 2006), generalizing Schindl's proof of conjecture 17.
