Written on the Wall II - Conjecture 160

Reference: E. DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, Conjectures of Graffiti.pc

Definitions

For a vertex $v$ in $G$, $T(v)$ is the number of triangles incident to $v$: $$T(v) = |\{\{u, w\} \subseteq N(v) \mid u \sim w\}|$$ i.e., the number of pairs of neighbors of $v$ that are themselves adjacent.

The invariant maxTrianglesAtVertex G is the maximum of $T(v)$ over all vertices.

Conjecture 160 uses both $\max_v T(v)$ and $\chi_{C_4}(G)$, the $C_4$-free characteristic function: it is 1 when $G$ contains no cycle of length four and 0 otherwise. The cycle need not be induced. These invariants lower-bound the WOWII invariant $L_s(G)$, the maximum number of leaves over all spanning trees of $G$ (exposed as SimpleGraph.Ls G : ℝ).

The earlier formalization used the number of induced four-cycles. The historical conjecture instead uses this binary $C_4$-free indicator.

Provenance. Statement corrected by Dominic Dabish.
