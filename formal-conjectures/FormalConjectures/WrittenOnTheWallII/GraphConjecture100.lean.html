Written on the Wall II - Conjecture 100

Verbatim statement (WOWII #100, status O):

If G is a simple connected graph, then α(G) ≤ CEIL[(maximum of λ(v) + 0.5*length(Ḡ))/2]

Source: http://cms.uhd.edu/faculty/delavinae/research/wowII/all.html#conj100

The WOWII HTML uses length(Ḡ) (the bar denotes graph complement); the extracted JSON in our private repo previously dropped the overline. The formal statement below uses the Euclidean norm of the degree sequence of Gᶜ.

Reference: E. DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, Conjectures of Graffiti.pc

Definition of graph length

The WOWII definitions popup defines length(H) as the square root of the sum of the squares of the vertex degrees. This is degreeL2Norm H in Lean. Combined with the overline above, the inequality reads: α(G) ≤ ⌈(max_v l(v) + 0.5 · degreeL2Norm(Gᶜ)) / 2⌉ where l(v) = indepNeighbors G v.
