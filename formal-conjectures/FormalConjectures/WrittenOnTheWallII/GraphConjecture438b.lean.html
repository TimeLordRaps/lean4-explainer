Written on the Wall II - Conjecture 438b

Reference:E. DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, Conjectures of Graffiti.pc

WOWII 438b asks whether

alpha₂(G) ≤ alpha(G) + alpha(G[V \ H₂]) + |E(G[H₂])|,

where H₂ is the set of vertices of degree at most two. The conjecture is true. In fact, the same inequality holds with an arbitrary vertex subset in place of H₂.
