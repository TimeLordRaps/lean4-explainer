Written on the Wall II - Conjecture 181

Reference: E. DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, Conjectures of Graffiti.pc

The expression deg_avg(B(G²)) is formalized by measuring degrees in G², the graph whose maximum-eccentricity vertices induce B(G²).

The conjecture is false for the triangular graph T(7) = L(K₇): it has at most 16 leaves in a spanning tree, largest induced bipartite subgraph order 6, and independence number 3. Its square is K₂₁, whose vertices all have degree 20, so the claimed inequality would give 22 ≥ 23.
