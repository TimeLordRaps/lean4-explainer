Written on the Wall II - Conjecture 176

Reference: E. DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, Conjectures of Graffiti.pc

The conjecture is false for the graph D₇, formed from two triangles by joining distinguished vertices with a path of seven edges. It has at most four leaves in a spanning tree and largest induced bipartite subgraph order ten. The two maximum-degree vertices of its square are at distance five in the original graph, so the claimed inequality would give 14 ≥ 17.
