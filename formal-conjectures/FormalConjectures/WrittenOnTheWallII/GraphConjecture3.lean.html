    WOWII Conjecture 3

    For a connected simple graph G, the number of leaves in a maximum spanning tree satisfies Ls(G) ≥ G.indepDominationNumber * MaxTemp(G), where G.indepDominationNumber is the independent domination number and MaxTemp(G) is max_v deg(v)/(n(G) - deg(v)).
