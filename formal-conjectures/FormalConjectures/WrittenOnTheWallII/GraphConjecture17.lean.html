    WOWII Conjecture 17

    For a simple connected graph G, the size b(G) of a largest induced bipartite subgraph satisfies b(G) ≥ α(G) + ⌈diam(G) / 3⌉, where α(G) is the independence number of G and diam(G) is the diameter of G.
