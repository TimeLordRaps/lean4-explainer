    The Gourevitch series identity: The following identity holds: $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1 + 14 n + 76 n^2 + 168 n^3}{2^{20 n}} \binom{2n}{n}^7 = \frac{32}{\pi^3}.$ This was originally conjectured in [G2003] by Guillera and proven in [A2025] by Au.
