Claude's Cycles

Reference: Claude's Cycles by Donald E. Knuth (2026)

Fix m ≥ 2. Consider the directed graph with vertex set (ZMod m)³, where from each vertex (i, j, k) there are directed arcs to (i+1, j, k), (i, j+1, k), and (i, j, k+1) (arithmetic mod m). The goal is to partition all 3m³ directed arcs into three edge-disjoint directed Hamiltonian cycles (each of length m³).

Knuth describes an explicit construction, found by Claude (Anthropic), that achieves this decomposition for all odd m ≥ 3. The case m = 2 is known to be impossible [Aub82]. The even case m > 2 is also settled. Knuth's paper says so in its final section, added in the 14 April 2026 revision: "Breaking news: The problem for even values of m is no longer in doubt!" Ho Boon Suan's algorithm [Ho26] is proved correct for even m ≥ 8 in [GPT26], and Aquino-Michaels [AM26] gives a decomposition for the even case that is simpler. The statement below starts at m = 4, which is below the range [GPT26] covers, so it also rests on the explicit solutions in [Kn26], whose header gives even m ≥ 4.
