  1. theorem Erdos713.erdos_713.parts.i Documented declaration: i sorry line 49 source

    Is it true that, for every bipartite graph $G$, there exists some $\alpha\in [1,2)$ and $c>0$ such that $$\mathrm{ex}(n;G)\sim cn^\alpha?$$

    The condition that $G$ have at least two edges excludes degenerate forbidden graphs whose extremal number is eventually zero, for which the displayed asymptotic with $c>0$ is impossible.
