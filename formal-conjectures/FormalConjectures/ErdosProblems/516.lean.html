    Let f = ∑ aₖzⁿₖ be an entire function with strictly increasing exponents such that nₖ > k (log k) ^ (2 + c). Then limsup (fun r => ratio r f) atTop = 1. This is proved in [Ko65]. Without StrictMono n, repeated exponents let nonzero coefficients cancel, and f = 0 satisfies the other hypotheses.
