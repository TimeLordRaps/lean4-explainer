    Let $p$ be an odd prime. Is it true that the equation $(p-1)!+a^{p-1}=p^k$ has only finitely many solutions?

    Originally proposed by Erdős and Graham [ErGr80]. Brindza and Erdős [BrEr91] proved that there are finitely many such solutions.
