    Erdős, Herzog, and Piranian [EHP58] conjecture that if $\{ z: \lvert f(z)\rvert\leq 1\}$ is connected then its diameter is at least $2$.

    The answer is yes, and connectedness is not needed. For monic $f$ of degree $n \geq 1$ the set $\{ z: \lvert f(z)\rvert\leq 1\}$ has logarithmic capacity $1$ [Ra95, Theorem 5.2.5], and Pólya [Po28] proved $\mathrm{cap}(E) \leq \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{diam}(E)$ for every compact $E \subseteq \mathbb{C}$, see [BPS02, (1.2)]. Hence the diameter is at least $2$.
