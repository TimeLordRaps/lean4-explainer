    Let $r\geq 2$ and let $G$ be an intersecting $r$-uniform hypergraph with chromatic number $3$. Must $G$ contain $O(r^2)$ many vertices? Vertices are counted in the union of the edges, so isolated vertices are excluded.

    Alon has provided a counterexample to the first question: this hypergraph is intersecting, its chromatic number is $3$, and it has $\asymp 4^r/\sqrt{r}$ many vertices.
