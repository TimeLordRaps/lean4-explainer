  1. def Erdos264.IsIrrationalitySequence Documented declaration: IsIrrationalitySequence line 45 source

    A sequence $a_n$ of integers is called an irrationality sequence if for every bounded sequence of integers $b_n$ with $a_n + b_n \neq 0$ and $b_n \neq 0$ for all $n$, the sum $$ \sum \frac{1}{a_n + b_n} $$ is irrational.

    Note: there are other possible definitions of this concept. See FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/263.lean for another possible definition.

  5. theorem Erdos264.erdos_264.variants.ko_tao_neg Documented declaration: ko_tao_neg sorry line 81 source

    Kovač and Tao [KoTa24] generally proved that any strictly increasing sequence of positive integers $a_n$ such that $\sum \frac{1}{a_n}$ converges and $$ \liminf_{n \to \infty} (a_n^2 \sum_{k > n} \frac{1}{a_k^2}) > 0 $$ is not an irrationality sequence.

    [KoTa24] Kovač, V. and Tao T., On several irrationality problems for Ahmes series. arXiv:2406.17593 (2024).
