    Source documentation

    A sequence $a_n$ of integers is called an irrationality sequence if for every bounded sequence of integers $b_n$ with $a_n + b_n \neq 0$ and $b_n \neq 0$ for all $n$, the sum $$ \sum \frac{1}{a_n + b_n} $$ is irrational.

    Note: there are other possible definitions of this concept. See FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/263.lean for another possible definition.
