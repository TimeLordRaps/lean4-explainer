    Let $0 < \epsilon < 1$. Is it true that, if $k$ is sufficiently large, then $$R(G) > (1-\epsilon)^k R(k)$$ for every graph $G$ with chromatic number $\chi(G)=k$?

    The restriction $\epsilon < 1$ excludes negative bases in $(1-\epsilon)^k$.
