    Neither G nor its complement contains a balanced complete bipartite graph $K_{k,k}$ on more than m vertices as a (not necessarily induced) subgraph. That is, there are no two disjoint sets of k vertices with $2k > m$ such that every pair of vertices from different sets is adjacent in G, or every such pair is non-adjacent in G.
