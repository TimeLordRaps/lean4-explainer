  1. def Erdos839.SumOfConsecutiveFree A sequence $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ is "sum-of-consecutive-free" if no term equals the sum of a contiguous block of earlier terms line 43 source

    A sequence $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ is "sum-of-consecutive-free" if no term equals the sum of a contiguous block of earlier terms. That is, for all $i$, $a_i \neq a_j + a_{j+1} + \cdots + a_k$ for any $j \leq k < i$.
