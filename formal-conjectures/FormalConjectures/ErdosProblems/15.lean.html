    Is it true that $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n\frac{n}{p_n}$ converges, where $p_n$ is the sequence of primes?

    Note: In the problem statement, $p_n$ is the $n$-th prime, indexed such that $p_1=2, p_2=3, \ldots$. We 0-index here to reflect how Nat.nth works.

    Note: convergence here is convergence of the sequence of partial sums, which is what the problem asks about. Summable would be the wrong notion: it is unconditional summability, equivalent over $\mathbb{R}$ to absolute convergence, and $\sum_n n/p_n$ diverges.
