  4. theorem Erdos162.erdos_162 Let $\alpha > 0$ and $n \geq 1$ sorry line 65 source

    Let $\alpha > 0$ and $n \geq 1$. Let $F(n,\alpha)$ be the largest $k$ such that there exists some 2-colouring of the edges of $K_n$ in which any induced subgraph $H$ on at least $k$ vertices contains more than $\alpha\binom{|H|}{2}$ many edges of each colour.

    Prove that for every fixed $0 \leq \alpha \leq 1/2$, as $n \to \infty$, $F(n,\alpha) \sim c_\alpha \log n$ for some constant $c_\alpha$.

    We require $\alpha < 1/2$: at $\alpha = 1/2$ no colouring works for any k ≤ n (see erdos_162.variants.half).
