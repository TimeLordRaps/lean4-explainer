Erdős Problem 19

The Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture: if $G$ is an edge-disjoint union of $n$ copies of $K_n$, is $\chi(G) = n$?

We model $G$ by a family of $n$ vertex sets of size $n$ (the copies of $K_n$) which pairwise share at most one vertex and which cover the vertex type. Two copies of $K_n$ are edge-disjoint exactly when their vertex sets share at most one vertex.
