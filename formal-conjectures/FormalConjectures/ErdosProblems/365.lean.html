  2. theorem Erdos365.erdos_365.parts.i Do all pairs of consecutive powerful numbers $n$ and $n+1$ come from solutions to Pell equations? line 80 source

    Do all pairs of consecutive powerful numbers $n$ and $n+1$ come from solutions to Pell equations? In other words, must either $n$ or $n+1$ be a square?

    The answer to the first question is no: Golomb [Go70] observed that both $12167=23^3$ and $12168=2^3 3^2 13^2$ are powerful.

    We require $n>0$ since Nat.Powerful also includes $0$.
