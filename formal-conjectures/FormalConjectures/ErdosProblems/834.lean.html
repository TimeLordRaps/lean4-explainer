    Does there exist a $3$-critical $3$-uniform hypergraph in which every vertex has degree $\geq 7$, where criticality means that the transversal number is $3$ and deleting any edge reduces it to $2$?

    In the first formulation, the transversal notion of criticality, Li [Li25] proves that a $3$-critical $3$-uniform hypergraph must have a vertex of degree $\leq 6$.
