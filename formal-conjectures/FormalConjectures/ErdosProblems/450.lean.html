    How large must $y=y(\epsilon,n)$ be such that the number of integers in $(x,x+y)$ with a divisor in $(n,2n)$ is at most $\epsilon y$?

    The bound is required for every $x$ and every window length at least $y$, and $y(\epsilon,n)$ is the least such threshold (or $\infty$ if there is none). A linear scale $y \le C(\epsilon) n$ is known to suffice for fixed $\epsilon$ and all large $n$ (see erdos_450.linear_scale_suffices).
