  6. lemma Erdos124.erdos124.melfi_construction Documented declaration: melfi_construction sorry line 107 source

    For any $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists an infinite sequence $2 \le d_0 < d_1 < \dots$ such that the series $\sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{d_i - 1}$ converges to a value at most $\varepsilon$, and yet for every $k \ne 0$ all sufficiently large integers can be written as $\sum_{i \in I} a_i$ for some finite set $I$, where each $a_i$ is divisible by $d_i^k$ and has only the digits $0, 1$ when written in base $d_i$.

    The restriction $k \ne 0$ excludes the degenerate representation of $n$ as a sum of $n$ copies of $1 = d_i^0$ taken from $n$ distinct bases.

    Proved by Melfi [Me04, Proposition 1]
