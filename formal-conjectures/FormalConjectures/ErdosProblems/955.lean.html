  9. theorem Erdos955.erdos_955.variants.empty_preimage Erdős [Er73b] proved that there are sets $A$ of positive density such that $s^{-1}(A)$ is empty sorry line 99 source

    Source documentation

    Erdős [Er73b] proved that there are sets $A$ of positive density such that $s^{-1}(A)$ is empty.

    Here "positive density" means positive lower density: Erdős proved that the set of untouchable numbers (those not of the form $s(n)$) has positive lower density [Er73b, Satz I]. Pollack and Pomerance [PoPo16] conjecture that this set has a natural density and give a heuristic for its value. The same result is stated as erdos_418.variants.sigma in Erdős Problem 418.
