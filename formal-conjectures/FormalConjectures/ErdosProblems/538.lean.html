    Let $r\geq 2$ and suppose that $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ is such that, for any $m$, there are at most $r$ solutions to $m=pa$ where $p$ is prime and $a\in A$. Give the best possible upper bound for $\sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{n}$.

    Erdős observed that $\sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{n}\ll r\frac{\log N}{\log\log N}$, and the order Θ_r(log N / loglog N) is known (see erdos_538.matching_order). The best possible upper bound is the asymptotic size of the largest reciprocal sum maxMass r N over admissible A.
