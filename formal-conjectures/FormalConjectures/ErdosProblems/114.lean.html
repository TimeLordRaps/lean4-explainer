    If $p(z) \in \mathbb{C}[z]$ is a monic polynomial of degree $n$, is the length of the curve $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \lvert p(z)\rvert = 1\}$ maximised when $p(z) = z^n - 1$?

    We require $n \geq 1$. For $n = 0$ the only monic polynomial is $1$, whose level set is all of $\mathbb{C}$, while $z^0 - 1 = 0$ has an empty level set.
