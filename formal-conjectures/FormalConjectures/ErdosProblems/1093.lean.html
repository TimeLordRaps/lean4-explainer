    If defined, the deficiency is the count of $0 \le i < k$ such that $n - i$ is $k$-smooth, that is, divisible only by primes $\le k$.

    Nat.smoothNumbers m is the set of positive naturals all of whose prime factors are $< m$, so "$k$-smooth" in the sense above is Nat.smoothNumbers (k + 1).
