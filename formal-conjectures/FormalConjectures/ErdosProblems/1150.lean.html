    The trivial lower bound from Parseval's identity: for any polynomial $P$ of degree $n$ with coefficients in $\{-1, 1\}$, we have $\max_{|z|=1} |P(z)| \geq \sqrt{n+1}$.

    This follows from Parseval's identity: $$\frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{2\pi} |P(e^{i\theta})|^2 d\theta = \sum_{k=0}^{n} |a_k|^2 = n+1$$ since each $|a_k|^2 = 1$. The circle average is bounded by the pointwise supremum squared, so $\max_{|z|=1} |P(z)|^2 \ge n+1$, whence $\max_{|z|=1} |P(z)| \ge \sqrt{n+1}$.
