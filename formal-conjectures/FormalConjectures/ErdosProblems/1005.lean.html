  4. theorem Erdos1005.erdos_1005 Let $\frac{a_1}{b_1}, \frac{a_2}{b_2}, \ldots$ be the Farey fractions of order $n \geq 4$ sorry line 70 source

    Let $\frac{a_1}{b_1}, \frac{a_2}{b_2}, \ldots$ be the Farey fractions of order $n \geq 4$. Let $f(n)$ be the largest integer such that if $1 \leq k < l \leq k + f(n)$ then $\frac{a_k}{b_k}$ and $\frac{a_l}{b_l}$ are similarly ordered, in other words $(a_k - a_l)(b_k - b_l) \geq 0$. Estimate $f(n)$: in particular, is there a constant $c > 0$ such that $f(n) = (c + o(1)) n$ for all large $n$?

    Mayer [Ma42] proved that $f(n) \to \infty$ and Erdős [Er43] that $f(n) \gg n$. Van Doorn [vD25b] proved $(1/12 - o(1)) n \le f(n) \le n / 4 + O(1)$ and conjectured that $f(n) = (1/4 + o(1)) n$, which was proved by Cipollini and GPT-5.5; see erdos_1005.variants.constant.
