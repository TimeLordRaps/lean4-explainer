The lower bound is due to Konyagin [Ko14] and the upper bound to Elsholtz and Planitzer [ElPl21]. (erdosproblems.com gives $c_0=1.26408\cdots$; [ElPl21, Remark 3] gives $c_0=1.5979102\ldots$.)
