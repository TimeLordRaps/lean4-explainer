    A natural number $n$ is said to have property Erdos261Prop if there exist $t \ge 2$ pairwise distinct positive integers $a_1, \ldots, a_t$ such that $n / 2^n = \sum_{1 \le k \le t} a_k / 2^{a_k}$.
