    Erdős [Er93] conjectured that $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4) = \frac{n^{3/2}}{2} + \frac n4 + O(n^{1/2})$ for all $n$, having proved the upper bound $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4) \le \frac{n^{3/2}}{2} + \frac n4 + O(n^{1/2})$ in [Er75].
