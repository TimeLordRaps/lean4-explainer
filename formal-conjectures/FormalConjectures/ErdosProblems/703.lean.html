  4. theorem Erdos703.erdos_703.variants.frankl_furedi Documented declaration: frankl_furedi sorry line 89 source

    Frankl and Füredi [FrFu84b] proved that, for fixed $r$ and $n$ sufficiently large in terms of $r$, the maximal $T(n,r)$ is achieved by taking $$\mathcal{F} = \left\{ A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,n\} : \lvert A\rvert> \frac{n+r}{2}\textrm{ or } \lvert A\rvert < r\right\}$$ when $n+r$ is odd, and $$\mathcal{F} = \left\{ A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,n\} : \lvert A\backslash \{1\}\rvert\geq \frac{n+r}{2}\textrm{ or }\lvert A\rvert < r\right\}$$ when $n+r$ is even.
