    Let $f:\mathbb{N}\to \{-1,1\}$ be a multiplicative function. Is it true that $$ \lim_{N\to \infty}\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n\leq N}f(n)$$ always exists?

    The answer is yes, as proved by Wirsing [Wi67], and generalised by Halász [Ha68].
