    Let $h(n)$ be minimal such that every graph on $n$ vertices where every set of $7$ vertices contains a triangle must contain a clique on at least $h(n)$ vertices. Estimate $h(n)$. In particular, do there exist constants $c_1, c_2 > 0$ such that $$n^{1/3+c_1} \ll h(n) \ll n^{1/2-c_2}?$$

    The lower half has a positive answer (Erdos813.erdos_813.variants.lower); the upper half is Erdos813.erdos_813.variants.upper.
