    Is it true that for every lacunary, strongly complete sequence A that is not complete whenever infinitely many terms are removed from it, lim A (n + 1) / A n = (1 + √5) / 2?

    The answer is no. A counterexample recorded at [erdosproblems.com/346] has all successive ratios at least 6 / 5, but has subsequences of successive ratios tending to two different limits, (1 + √5) / 2 and (1 + √5) / 2 + 1 / 4.
