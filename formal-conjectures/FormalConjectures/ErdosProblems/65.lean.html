    Let $G$ be a graph with $n$ vertices and $kn$ edges, and $a_1<a_2<\cdots$ be the lengths of cycles in $G$. Assume $n>0$ and $k>0$. Is it true that $$\sum\frac{1}{a_i}\gg \log k?$$

    Gyárfás, Komlós, and Szemerédi [GKS84] have proved that this sum is $\gg \log k$, so that only the second question remains.
