    Conjecture 1.1 (Dean, 1988). For every integer $k \geq 3$, every finite simple graph with minimum degree at least $k$ contains a cycle whose length is divisible by $k$.

    A cycle has length at least 3, so the divisor is never 0 and the statement is not satisfied for a trivial reason. SimpleGraph.minDegree is 0 on a graph with no vertices and on a graph with no edges, so the hypothesis excludes both.
