Fernandes' conjecture on the 2-generation of even direct product permutation groups

Reference: arxiv/2605.12342 Groups of permutations that are even on maximal proper subsets, and related monoids Reference:

  • arxiv/2605.12342 Groups of permutations that are even on maximal proper subsets, and related monoids by Vítor H. Fernandes
  • K26

For positive integers $m, n \ge 2$, let $\mathrm{S}_m \times \mathrm{S}_n$ be the direct product of symmetric groups on $[m] = \{1, \dots, m\}$ and $[n'] = \{1', \dots, n'\}$. Define $$ \Gamma_{m \oplus n} = \{(\sigma_1, \sigma_2) \in \mathrm{S}_m \times \mathrm{S}_n : \mathrm{sgn}(\sigma_1) = \mathrm{sgn}(\sigma_2)\} $$ to be the index-$2$ subgroup of pairs of permutations with equal parity, i.e., those whose product action on $[m] \cup [n']$ is an even permutation.

It is known that $\mathrm{rank}(\Gamma_{2 \oplus 2}) = 1$, $\mathrm{rank}(\Gamma_{3 \oplus 3}) = 3$, $\mathrm{rank}(\Gamma_{4 \oplus 3}) = 3$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\Gamma_{4 \oplus 4}) = 3$.

Conjecture 1 (Fernandes, 2026): For all integers $m \ge n \ge 2$ such that $(m, n) \notin \{(2,2), (3,3), (4,3), (4,4)\}$, the group $\Gamma_{m \oplus n}$ has rank $2$ (i.e., is $2$-generated).
