TxGraffiti Conjecture 3: independent domination versus saturation (regular)

Reference: arxiv/2507.17780 In Reverie Together: Ten Years of Mathematical Discovery with a Machine Collaborator by Randy Davila, Boris Brimkov, Ryan Pepper

Conjecture 3 (open since 2020) asserts that for every $r$-regular graph $G$ (every vertex has degree $r$), $$i(G) \le \mu^*(G),$$ where $i(G)$ is the independent domination number and $\mu^*(G)$ the saturation number (minimum size of a maximal matching).
