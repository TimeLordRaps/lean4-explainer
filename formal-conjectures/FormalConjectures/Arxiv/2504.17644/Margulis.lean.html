    Huang–Shi, Theorem 1.2

    Let F be a finite field of characteristic p ∈ {3, 5, 7, 11}, and set K = F((t⁻¹)), A = F[t]. Here K is modelled by the Laurent series field F⸨X⸩ with X = t⁻¹, so that A is embedded via t ↦ X⁻¹. Let

    • D be the diagonal subgroup of SL₄(K),
    • Γ = SL₄(A) the lattice subgroup embedded into SL₄(K) via the natural inclusion A →+* K.

    Then there exists z : SL₄(K)/Γ such that the D-orbit of z has compact closure but is not closed.
