    Convergence a.e. on one period, together with periodicity, gives convergence a.e. on the whole real line.

    1. Use periodicity of f and of its partial Fourier sums to reduce a.e. convergence on the line to a.e. convergence on [0, 2*pi].
    2. The a.e. statement on the interval is carleson_interval', which in turn comes from an exceptional-set estimate (exceptional_set_carleson') through a distribution-function criterion for a.e. convergence.
