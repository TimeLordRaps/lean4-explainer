    Proves that the old, weak version of classical Carleson is implied by the L^p version.
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    A continuous periodic function is bounded and hence lies in every L^p, so the Carleson-Hunt a.e. convergence result for L^p functions applies.

    1. Unfold ClassicalCarleson and take f continuous and periodic.
    2. Apply carleson_hunt_real with p equal to infinity.
    3. Boundedness of f on [0, 2*pi] from continuity gives membership in L-infinity on the period.

    Prerequisites: carleson_hunt_real

    See also: ClassicalCarleson, classical_carleson
