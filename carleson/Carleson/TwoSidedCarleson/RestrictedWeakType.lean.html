    Theorem 10.0.1, reformulation

    L4DEL explanation

    Lorentz-type bounds at two exponents bracketing q are combined by real interpolation to give a strong (q,q) bound.

    1. Choose two exponents q0 = (q+2)/2 and q1 = (q+1)/2 that bracket q.
    2. Obtain Lorentz-type bounds for the Carleson operator at q0 and q1 from two_sided_metric_carleson_hasLorentzType.
    3. Apply a real-interpolation theorem for Lorentz spaces (exists_hasLorentzType_real_interpolation) to get HasStrongType with constant C_carleson_hasStrongType.
    4. Caveat: the file Classical/ClassicalCarleson.lean says this theorem currently depends on sorryAx, so it is not fully proved in this version.

    Prerequisites: C_carleson_hasStrongType, two_sided_metric_carleson_hasLorentzType

    See also: classical_carleson
