  37. def P_poly S_real n k is the number of $k$-element subsets of $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$ whose sum is divisible by $k$ line 302 source

    S_real n k is the number of $k$-element subsets of $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$ whose sum is divisible by $k$. By the roots of unity filter, this exact count can be written as: $S_{n,k} = \frac{1}{k} \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} \sum_{A \in \binom{n}{k}} \omega^{j \sum A}$. The main term $j=0$ yields exactly $\frac{1}{k} \binom{n}{k}$. For $j > 0$, the roots of unity evaluated over the subsets yield bounded periodic sums. The maximal magnitude of these periodic products is achieved when the order of the root is 2, which bounds the error term magnitude strictly by $2 \cdot 2^{n/2}$.
