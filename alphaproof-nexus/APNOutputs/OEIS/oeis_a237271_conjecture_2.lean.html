    A237271: Number of parts in the symmetric representation of $\sigma(n)$. a(n) is $1$ plus the number of pairs $(d_k, d_{k+1})$ of consecutive divisors of $n$ such that $d_{k+1}$ is odd and $d_{k+1} \ge 2 d_k$.

    The formula used is $1 + |\{(d_k, d_{k+1}) \in \text{consecutive pairs of divisors of } n \mid d_{k+1} \text{ is odd and } d_{k+1} \ge 2 d_k\}|$, which is a known characterization of the sequence.
