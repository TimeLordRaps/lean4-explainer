    L4DEL explanation

    both calculations land exactly on the positive integer M + 1 − dR dB

    — Human-written proof companion for Erdős 138

    The only way this algorithm could result in an invalid coloring is if both choices are blocked

    — George Tsoukalas et al., AlphaProof Nexus paper, Erdős 138 appendix

    any two arithmetic progressions of length k and step size <k with the same start/end point must have at least one other point in common

    — Erdős Problems 138 discussion (Adam Zsolt Wagner and Thomas Bloom)

    While fewer than k new points have been appended, a two-colouring with no old k-progression and no current (k+1)-progression can always colour the next point without creating a (k+1)-progression.

    1. If both colours were blocked, ap_must_end would produce a red and a blue k-term prefix ending at the same new point.

    3. Stepping back d₁ places along the colour-0 progression and d₀ places along the colour-1 progression reaches the same integer N+i+1-d₀d₁, forcing it to have both colours.
