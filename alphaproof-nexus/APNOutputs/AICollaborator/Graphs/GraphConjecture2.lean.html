    Source documentation

    WOWII Conjecture 2

    For a simple connected graph G, Ls(G) ≥ 2 · (l(G) - 1) where l(G) is the average independence number of the neighbourhoods of the vertices of G.

    L4DEL explanation

    The key idea is to look at the degrees of the vertices on the right side

    — George Tsoukalas et al., AlphaProof Nexus paper (Appendix I)

    We prove Graffiti.pc Conjecture 2, a 1996 conjecture listed as open

    — Independent proof by Yanmohan Wang, Tianyue Dai, and Rui Tong (2026)

    In every finite connected simple graph, the maximum number Ls(G) of leaves in a spanning tree is at least 2(l(G)-1), where l(G) is the average, over vertices, of the largest independent set inside each open neighbourhood.

    1. Choose a maximum independent set in every vertex neighbourhood and let c(x) count how many of those chosen sets contain x; double counting identifies the average of c with l(G).
    2. For every edge xy, independence and a spanning-tree construction bound c(x)+c(y) by Ls(G)+2.
    3. Vertices with c(x) > Ls(G)/2+1 form an independent heavy set, and each light vertex has only boundedly many heavy neighbours.
    4. A discharging inequality moves each heavy vertex's excess to light neighbours without exceeding their slack, proving l(G) ≤ Ls(G)/2+1 and hence the claimed result.
