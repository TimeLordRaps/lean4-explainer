    Source documentation

    Type of a pure order ideal: the number of maximal monomials.

    L4DEL explanation

    The type is the number of maximal monomials.

    — Human-written Hilbert-functions proof companion

    typePO(P) is the cardinality of maximalMonomials(P.ideal). In Lean these are written typePO P and maximalMonomials P.ideal. Thus “type 2” in the theorem means that the pure order ideal has exactly two maximal monomial generators.
