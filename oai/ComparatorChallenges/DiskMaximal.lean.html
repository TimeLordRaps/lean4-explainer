  10. theorem OAI.CenteredDiskEndpoint.main_endpoint Challenge statement: Endpoint Sobolev regularity of centered disk averages sorry line 56
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The centered disk maximal operator takes the supremum of averages of $|f|$ over disks centered at each point. The formalization proves that every real $f\in W^{1,1}(\mathbb R^2)$ has a maximal function that is finite almost everywhere, belongs to $W^{1,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}$, and has a globally integrable weak gradient satisfying $\|\nabla Mf\|_1\le C\|\nabla f\|_1$ for one absolute constant $C$. The earlier signed finite-band estimate for smooth compactly supported functions is also retained. The general endpoint statement covers the Sobolev setting of the paper; a BV extension is outside these statements.
