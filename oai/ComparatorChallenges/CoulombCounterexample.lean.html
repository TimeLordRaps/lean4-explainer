    The Monge ansatz asks whether an optimal multi-marginal transport plan can be induced by maps from one marginal. For the three-marginal Coulomb cost in $\mathbb R^3$, the formalization constructs a smooth compactly supported probability density, with smooth compactly supported square root, for which no pair of measure-preserving Borel maps attains the Kantorovich minimum. Nevertheless, the Monge and Kantorovich infima are equal: preserving maps with finite costs approach the minimum. This is the three-dimensional Coulomb result; the paper's inverse-power extensions in every dimension are outside this statement.
