  13. theorem OAI.Yau.Target.yau_nodal_set_upper_bound_counterexample Challenge statement: A fixed smooth metric with unbounded normalized nodal four-volume sorry line 65

    L4DEL explanation

    This disproves the smooth upper-bound assertion in Yau’s nodal-set conjecture

    — OpenAI Yau power-law counterexample manuscript (abstract)

    On S⁴×S¹, one fixed smooth Riemannian metric supports nonzero real Laplace eigenfunctions with eigenvalues tending to infinity while four-dimensional nodal measure divided by √λ also tends to infinity. This refutes a uniform O(√λ) upper bound for that metric.

    1. MainTarget existentially fixes one smooth metric g on Base × Circle, a positive eigenvalue sequence λ(k), and a sequence of nonzero smooth real functions u(k).
    2. The eigenfunction equation is written in every extended chart using the coordinate Laplacian built from g, and nodalMeasure is the intrinsic four-dimensional Hausdorff measure of the zero set.
    3. Lean states λ(k) → ∞ and nodalMeasure(g,u(k))/√λ(k) → ∞. The manuscript proves the stronger fixed-power excess λ^(1/2+ε₀); that exponent ε₀ is not present in this target. The challenge body is sorry, with paired solution OAI.Geometry.NodalSets.Main.
