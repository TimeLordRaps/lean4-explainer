    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The formalization proves that the irrationality exponent of $\pi$ is exactly two. For every $\nu>2$, all sufficiently large positive denominators $q$ satisfy $|\pi-p/q|\ge q^{-\nu}$ for every integer numerator $p$. It also states the exact supremum characterization using infinitely many rational approximations. The paper's convergence consequence for the Flint–Hills series is outside this selected statement.
