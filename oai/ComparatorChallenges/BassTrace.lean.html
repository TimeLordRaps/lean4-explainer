    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The formalization proves the complex Bass trace conjecture for every group: the Hattori–Stallings trace of each virtual class of finitely generated projective right $\mathbb C[G]$-modules vanishes on conjugacy classes of infinite-order elements. No finiteness, countability, or geometric hypothesis on $G$ is imposed. For torsion-free $G$, it identifies the trace on $K_0(\mathbb C[G])$ with the integral augmentation rank and proves the characteristic-zero Kaplansky idempotent conjecture: for every commutative unital domain $R$ of characteristic zero, an idempotent in $R[G]$ is $0$ or $1$.
