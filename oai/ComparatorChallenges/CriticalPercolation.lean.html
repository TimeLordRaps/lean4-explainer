  11. theorem OAI.CriticalPercolation.BondGraph.no_percolation_at_criticality Challenge statement: Critical percolation on every quasi-transitive graph sorry line 52

    Critical Bernoulli bond percolation has no infinite cluster when the critical probability is below one.

    Annotation provenance. This overlay contains 9 curated AI-written annotations and 1307 mechanical source-derived annotations. They have not been human-reviewed as explanations; treat them as unverified and not mathematical authority. Retained generation history: The 71 original curated annotations were produced in a Claude Sonnet 5.5 session at medium effort; Claude Opus 5.5 is recorded in that session as the advisor model, not the direct author. The bulk source-derived expansion was generated mechanically from pinned source comments by tooling developed in a GPT-5.6 Sol session at ultra reasoning; GPT-5.6 Sol did not originate the quoted source prose.

    The critical-percolation question asks whether an infinite cluster can remain at the threshold. The formalized result proves that, at their respective critical probabilities, nearest-neighbor bond and site percolation on $\mathbb Z^3$ almost surely have no infinite cluster. Equivalently, almost surely every vertex belongs to a finite cluster in each model. The formalization proves absence of an infinite cluster at criticality for Bernoulli bond percolation on every infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with critical probability $p_c<1$. At $p=p_c$, the probability that any infinite cluster exists is zero. The graph model permits bond multiplicities.
