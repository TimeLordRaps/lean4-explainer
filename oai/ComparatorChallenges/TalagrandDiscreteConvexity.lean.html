    Talagrand's expectation-threshold conjecture asks whether the fractional and integral expectation thresholds are comparable by an absolute constant. The formalized result proves $q_f(\mathcal F)\le25\cdot512^4 q(\mathcal F)$ for every nonempty proper increasing family on a finite nonempty ground set. Both thresholds use cover budget $1/2$, and fractional covers may assign weights to every subset, including the empty set. The formalized result proves Talagrand's discrete-convexity assertion with $k=2^{75}$. If a family of subsets of a finite nonempty ground set has Bernoulli-$p$ measure at least $1-1/k$, then the sets not contained in a union of $k$ members form a $p$-small family: they have a containment cover of total $p$-cost at most $1/2$. This holds for every $0<p<1$, with repeated members allowed in the union and no monotonicity assumption.
