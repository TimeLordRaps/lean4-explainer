  12. theorem OAI.Problem337.egyptian_length_is_minimum Challenge statement: Short Egyptian fractions sorry line 48
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    An Egyptian-fraction expansion writes a rational number as a sum of distinct unit fractions. The formalization proves that every $a/b$ with $1\le a0$; padding can preserve that denominator. If $v(k)$ is the least integer at least $2$ absent from all exact $k$-term expansions of $1$, then eventually $e^{e^{k/600}}\le v(k)\le1+k^{2^{k-1}}$, and $\liminf_{k\to\infty}\log\log v(k)/k\ge\log2/257$.
