  1. theorem OAI.InternalCatalan.catalan_irrational Challenge statement: Catalan's constant is irrational sorry line 7

    L4DEL explanation

    Catalan’s constant is irrational.

    — OpenAI Catalan manuscript (Theorem 1.1)

    the equivalent of Apéry's theorem “ζ(3) ∉ Q” for Catalan's constant has not yet been proved

    — Tanguy Rivoal and Wadim Zudilin (2003), p. 1

    Catalan's constant is the alternating reciprocal-square sum G = Σ(j ≥ 0) (-1)^j/(2j+1)^2, equivalently the Dirichlet beta value β(2). This declaration says that no rational number equals that real series.

    1. Lean's Irrational predicate means there is no rational r whose coercion to the reals equals the infinite sum.
    2. The displayed summand alternates sign with j and divides by the square of the odd integer 2j+1, so the target is the standard Catalan constant rather than the unrelated Catalan numbers.
    3. The challenge file itself ends in sorry; its comparator configuration selects OAI.NumberTheory.Catalan.Main as the separate released solution module checked against this exact statement.
