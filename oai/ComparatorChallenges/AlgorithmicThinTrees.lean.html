  1. structure OAI.StrongThinTree.MultiGraph A finite loopless multigraph with individually indexed edge copies line 9

    L4DEL explanation

    every parallel copy counts separately

    — OpenAI algorithmic strong-thin-tree manuscript

    MultiGraph n m has vertices Fin n and edge copies Fin m. Each edge copy has a left and right endpoint, and loopless rules out an edge whose two endpoints coincide.

    1. Parallel edges are allowed because two different members of Fin m may have the same endpoint pair.
    2. The edge index is therefore an identity for a copy, not merely a pair of vertices. That distinction matters when cuts, trees, and binary multiplicities count parallel copies.
