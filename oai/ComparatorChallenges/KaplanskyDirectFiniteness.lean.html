  1. def OAI.KaplanskyCounterexample.MainClaim Challenge statement: Failure of direct finiteness in characteristic 2 line 7

    L4DEL explanation

    a finite field K of characteristic two, a finitely presented group G

    — OpenAI Kaplansky counterexample manuscript (p. 1)

    A well-known conjecture of Kaplansky states that for any group G and any field K the group ring KG is directly finite

    — Jan Trlifaj (1990), p. 427

    A group algebra over a finite field of characteristic 2, of a finitely generated group, can contain elements a, b with a*b = 1 but b*a different from 1.

    1. The claim has the form: there are a finite field K with CharP K 2, a finitely generated group G, and a, b in MonoidAlgebra K G.
    2. Those satisfy a * b = 1 and b * a != 1.
    3. This Lean abstraction asks only for finite generation; the manuscript's stronger finitely presented, odd-torsion construction supplies such a witness but is not repeated in MainClaim's type.
