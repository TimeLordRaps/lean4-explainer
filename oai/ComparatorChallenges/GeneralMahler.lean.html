    The symmetric Mahler conjecture asks for the different, stronger constant 4n /n! on the restricted class of centrally symmetric bodies

    — OpenAI general-Mahler manuscript (abstract)

    Among all compact convex bodies with interior in $\mathbb R^n$, the smallest possible product of the body's volume and the volume of an optimally centered polar is $(n+1)^{n+1}/(n!)^2$. Exactly the simplices attain it.

    1. polarAt K z contains the vectors $y$ whose pairing with every displacement $x-z$ from the chosen interior center is at most one. volumeProduct K is the infimum of $|K|\,|(K-z)^\circ|$ over all interior centers $z$.
    2. The hypotheses require positive dimension, compactness, convexity, and nonempty interior. No central symmetry, smooth boundary, or strict convexity is assumed.
    3. The conclusion is a conjunction: the sharp lower bound holds, and equality is equivalent to the existence of $n+1$ affinely independent vertices whose convex hull is exactly $K$.
    4. This is the nonsymmetric theorem. The stronger constant $4^n/n!$ for centrally symmetric bodies is a different statement, already exposed separately on the symmetric-Mahler page. The manuscript's functional inequality is outside this comparator target. The body is sorry, paired with OAI.Geometry.Convex.GeneralMahler.Theorem.
