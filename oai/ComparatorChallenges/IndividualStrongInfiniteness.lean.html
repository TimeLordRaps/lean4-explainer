  53. theorem OAI.MainGap.Manuscript.individual_to_strong_main Challenge statement: Weak pure infiniteness and Cuntz-algebra absorption sorry line 319
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The formalization proves that a complex $C^*$-algebra in which every positive element is properly infinite is strongly purely infinite, with the full positive-element diagonalization property. No exactness, nuclearity, unitality, or simplicity assumption is needed for this implication. For exact algebras, proper infiniteness of one fixed finite amplification of every positive element already implies individual proper infiniteness and strong pure infiniteness.
