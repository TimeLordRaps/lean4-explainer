  39. theorem OAI.DeligneDrinfeld.main Challenge statement: The Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture sorry line 189
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture predicts that the rational Grothendieck–Teichmüller Lie algebra is freely generated by one element in each odd weight $3,5,7,\ldots$. The formalization establishes this for the rational solution space of antisymmetry, the three-term equation, and the four-strand pentagon with the Ihara bracket. It also gives the corresponding continuous isomorphism after completion by weight. The regularized-transport proposition and graph-complex consequences are not included.
