  19. theorem OAI.PureTypeSystem.weak_implies_strong Challenge statement: Weak normalization implies strong normalization in pure type systems sorry line 116

    System-wide weak normalization implies strong normalization for every PTS, including arbitrary sort types, nonfunctional rules and annotated terms.

    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The formalization proves the $\beta$-Barendregt–Geuvers–Klop conjecture for pure type systems: if every legal expression in every valid context has some terminating $\beta$-reduction sequence, then every $\beta$-reduction sequence from every such expression terminates. Reduction is allowed inside type annotations, and the specification may have arbitrary sorts and nonfunctional axioms or rules.
