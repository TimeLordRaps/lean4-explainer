  9. theorem OAI.HigherDimensionalBallPacking.main_theorem Challenge statement: Symplectic ball packing in higher dimensions sorry line 36
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The formalization proves Siegel and Yao's ball-packing criterion in every symplectic dimension $2n$ with $n\ge3$. For $k\ge1$ closed standard balls of positive capacities $R_1,\ldots,R_k$, disjoint symplectic embeddings into the interior of a ball of capacity $R$ exist exactly when $\sum_i R_i^n<R^n$ and $R_i+R_j<R$ for all $i\ne j$. Capacity is $\pi$ times squared Euclidean radius, and each embedding is defined on a neighborhood of its closed source ball. The linked statements include both the full equivalence and the necessity direction.
