  23. theorem OAI.CoveringOrder.optimal_order Challenge statement: The optimal order of convex-body covering density sorry line 113

    Translative and lattice covering densities over all convex bodies and over centrally symmetric convex bodies have the same asymptotic order.

    Annotation provenance. This overlay contains 9 curated AI-written annotations and 1307 mechanical source-derived annotations. They have not been human-reviewed as explanations; treat them as unverified and not mathematical authority.     The formalized result gives an absolute constant $C>0$ such that every convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge2$, admits a covering by translates along one full-rank lattice $L$ with density $|K|/\mathrm{covol}(L)\le Cn\log n$. The covering is exact, and no symmetry, boundary regularity, or volume normalization is assumed. The formalization determines the order of the largest covering density in dimension $n$. For all sufficiently large $n$, the suprema of translative covering density and lattice covering density are each bounded above and below by absolute positive multiples of $n\log n$, both over all convex bodies and over centrally symmetric convex bodies.
