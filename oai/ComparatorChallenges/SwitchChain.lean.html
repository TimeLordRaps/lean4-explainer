    The Kannan–Tetali–Vempala conjecture asks for polynomial mixing of the switch chain for every graphical degree sequence. The formalization proves the simple undirected case: for $n\ge4$, the lazy chain that proposes switches on four vertices has total-variation mixing time at distance $1/4$ at most $2n^8$. It also proves switch connectivity, the stated exponential total-variation bound, and a spectral gap of at least $1/(24n^2\binom n4)$ when more than one graph realizes the degree sequence. The paper's exactly uniform sampling algorithm is outside these selected chain estimates.
