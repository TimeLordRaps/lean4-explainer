    The formalization proves ordinary two-point cancellation for multiplicative functions. For the Liouville function on every fixed pair of nonproportional affine forms, the correlation sum up to $X$ is $O(X/(\log X)^c)$ for an absolute $c>0$, with the implied constant depending on the forms. For two one-bounded multiplicative functions, if at least one is uniformly nonpretentious against all Dirichlet-character twists with frequency $|t|\le N$, then their shifted and nonproportional affine correlation sums divided by $N$ tend to zero. These are ordinary averages, with no logarithmic averaging.
