  13. theorem OAI.SquareRootDegree.main Challenge statement: Boolean functions violate the square-root degree bound by arbitrary factors sorry line 57

    The two clauses of the literal main theorem. Unboundedness above is the real-valued formulation of the displayed supremum being positive infinity.

    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The formalization disproves the Gopalan–Servedio square-root degree conjecture by an unbounded factor. For every $C>0$, it gives a nonconstant Boolean function on a finite sign cube for which the sum of its linear Fourier coefficients exceeds $C\sqrt{\deg f}$, where $\deg f$ is its real multilinear degree. It also proves that the ratio of the sum of the absolute values of those coefficients to $\sqrt{\deg f}$ is unbounded among positive-degree Boolean functions.
