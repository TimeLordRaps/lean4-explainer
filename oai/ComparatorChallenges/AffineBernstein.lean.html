    A complete, smooth, uniformly convex solution of the affine maximal surface equation on a convex domain must be a paraboloid.

    1. The hypotheses fix n between 3 and 9, an open convex nonempty domain, smooth u with positive definite Hessian, the affine maximal equation holding, and completeness for the graph's Euclidean path metric.
    2. The conclusion has three parts: the domain is the whole space, u is a quadratic polynomial with symmetric positive definite matrix, and an affine equivalence carries the standard paraboloid onto the graph of u.
