  23. theorem OAI.GAD.main Challenge statement: Exact unassisted classical capacity of generalized amplitude damping sorry line 113

    L4DEL explanation

    It equals the one-shot Holevo capacity and is given by a one-variable maximum.

    — OpenAI generalized-amplitude-damping manuscript (abstract)

    We then establish several upper bounds on its classical, quantum, and private capacities.

    — Sumeet Khatri, Kunal Sharma, and Mark M. Wilde, Information-theoretic aspects of GAD (2020)

    For every damping strength and thermal occupation in $[0,1]$, the unrestricted $n$-use Holevo information is exactly $n$ times its one-use value, an explicit one-variable maximum attains that value, and the operational unassisted classical capacity equals it.

    1. holevo γ ν n optimizes over all finite ensembles of $n$-qubit density matrices; inputs need not be pure, product, or equiprobable. The first conjunct proves additivity across every positive block length.
    2. The second conjunct supplies a maximizer $p\in[0,1]$ for the scalar objective γ ν p and identifies every block optimum with $n/\log 2$ times that objective.
    3. The third conjunct identifies the operational capacity, defined through codes with collective output measurements and vanishing average error, with the one-use Holevo value. The fourth verifies that every maximizing equiprobable phase-pair product ensemble attains every block optimum.
    4. Scope boundary: unlike the stronger pinned manuscript abstract, this comparator theorem does not state additivity with an arbitrary different partner channel or minimum-output-entropy additivity. It covers repeated uses of the same generalized amplitude-damping channel.
    5. Khatri, Sharma, and Wilde previously established parameter ranges and upper bounds for generalized amplitude-damping capacities. The cited OpenAI result claims an exact unrestricted classical-capacity formula beyond those bounds.
    6. Trust boundary: this comparator declaration ends in by sorry. The pinned comparator configuration pairs it with OAI.InformationTheory.AmplitudeDamping.Main; the site's Lean trust result, not this annotation, determines whether that separate solution module checks.
