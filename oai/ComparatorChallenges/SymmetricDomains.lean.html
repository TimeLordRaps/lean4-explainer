    The formalized result answers the paper's symmetry question affirmatively. A nonempty connected bounded semialgebraic subset, relatively open in a complex affine algebraic set, is smooth and biholomorphic to a bounded symmetric domain whenever a discrete group acts properly and holomorphically with compact quotient. The action need not be free, and the ambient algebraic set may be singular.
