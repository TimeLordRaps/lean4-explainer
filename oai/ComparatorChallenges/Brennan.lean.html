    Brennan's conjecture asserts that $|\phi'|^s$ is area-integrable for $4/30$ and the spectrum value $B_{\mathcal S}(-2)=1$. The Koebe map and its inverse give divergence at all four boundary exponents. Kraetzer's proposed integral-means spectrum predicts $B_b(-1)=1/4$ for bounded univalent maps. The formalized result proves $B_b(-1)<1/4$, contradicting that prediction. The underlying estimate gives constants $0<\varepsilon<1/4$ and $C<\infty$ such that the normalized circular mean of $|f'|^{-1}$ is at most $C(1-r)^{-1/4+\varepsilon}$ for every normalized univalent disk map and $1/2\le r<1$. The bound requires neither bounded image nor boundary regularity, and no numerical value of $\varepsilon$ is specified.
