  5. theorem OAI.HadwigerCounterexample.exists_counterexamples Challenge statement: Counterexamples of arbitrarily large order sorry line 38

    L4DEL explanation

    constructing arbitrarily large graphs whose chromatic number exceeds their Hadwiger number

    — OpenAI Hadwiger-counterexample manuscript (abstract)

    For every bound M there is a graph on m >= max(M, 5) vertices with independence number at most 2 whose chromatic number is above its Hadwiger number.

    1. The graph G on Fin m has independence number at most 2.
    2. Its connected matching number is below m/100 and its Hadwiger number is below 26m/75 + 2/3, which is itself below m/2.
    3. The chromatic number is finite, is at least m/2, and exceeds the Hadwiger number; the challenge file leaves the proof as sorry.

  6. theorem OAI.HadwigerCounterexample.not_hadwiger_conjecture Challenge statement: Hadwiger's conjecture fails sorry line 49

    L4DEL explanation

    We disprove Hadwiger’s conjecture

    — OpenAI Hadwiger-counterexample manuscript (abstract)

    The statement denies that every finite graph's chromatic number is at most its largest clique-minor size.

    1. Graphs are simple graphs on Fin m, and hadwigerNumber is the supremum of t such that the graph has a clique minor of order t (connected disjoint branch sets, pairwise touching).
    2. The statement is the negation of "for all m and all graphs G on Fin m, chromaticNumber G is at most hadwigerNumber G".
    3. This challenge file leaves the proof as sorry.

    Sources and attribution: Pinned Lean source (fd4aeeb2ee4f), OpenAI Hadwiger-counterexample manuscript (abstract), Pinned OpenAI formalization note 157, Hugo Hadwiger (1943), original-paper bibliographic record
