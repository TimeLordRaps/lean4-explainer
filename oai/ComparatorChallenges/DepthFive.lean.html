    Let $\mathrm{IMM}_{n,n}$ be the $(1,1)$ entry of the product of $n$ independent $n\times n$ variable matrices. The formalization proves that, over every characteristic-zero field, syntactically homogeneous depth-five $\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\Pi\Sigma$ circuits computing this polynomial require at least $n^{\sqrt n/400}$ gates for all sufficiently large $n$, with a threshold independent of the field. Arbitrary bottom support, finite fan-in and fan-out, and sharing are allowed. It also gives, over every field and for $n\ge2$, circuits with at most $n^{\sqrt n+4}$ gates. These are the lower and upper bounds selected from the paper.
