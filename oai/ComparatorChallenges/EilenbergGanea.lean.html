  26. theorem OAI.EilenbergGanea.source_main Challenge statement: A finitely generated Eilenberg-Ganea counterexample sorry line 113

    L4DEL explanation

    a finitely generated residually finite group of integral cohomological dimension two and geometric dimension three

    — OpenAI Eilenberg-Ganea counterexample manuscript (abstract)

    The least dimension of such a complex KΠ is called the geometric dimension of Π

    — Samuel Eilenberg and Tudor Ganea (1957), original paper

    One explicit height-kernel group is finitely generated and residually finite, has integral cohomological dimension two, admits a three-dimensional classifying space, and admits no two-dimensional classifying space.

    1. SourceGroup is the kernel of the homomorphism sending every generator of a right-angled Artin group to 1; the Artin group is built from the finite flag-complex data encoded by seedGraph.
    2. The first two conjuncts assert finite generation and residual finiteness. The third identifies the projective dimension of the trivial integral group-ring module as exactly two.
    3. The final conjuncts give a three-dimensional connected CW classifying space with contractible cover and rule out every two-dimensional one. Together, these say geometric dimension three while cohomological dimension is two.
    4. This is an existence result for the specifically defined SourceGroup, not a statement about all groups of cohomological dimension two. The comparator body is sorry, paired with OAI.Topology.EilenbergGanea.Main.

    Sources and attribution: Pinned Lean source (fd4aeeb2ee4f), OpenAI Eilenberg-Ganea counterexample manuscript (abstract), Pinned OpenAI formalization note 249, Samuel Eilenberg and Tudor Ganea (1957), original paper
