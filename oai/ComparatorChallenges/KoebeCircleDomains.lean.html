  14. theorem OAI.Problem047.koebe_circle_domain Challenge statement: Koebe's unrestricted circle-domain uniformization sorry line 62

    L4DEL explanation

    every domain in the Riemann sphere is conformally equivalent to a circle domain

    — OpenAI Koebe circle-domain manuscript (abstract)

    Any domain Q in C, whose boundary has at most countably many components, is conformally homeomorphic to a circle domain

    — Zheng-Xu He and Oded Schramm (1993), Theorem 0.1

    Every nonempty connected open subset of the Riemann sphere is conformally equivalent to a circle domain, with no countability restriction on its complementary components.

    1. The input U is any open connected set in the sphere. The output is a set V and a sphere map f such that V is a circle domain and f is a conformal equivalence from U to V.
    2. IsCircleDomain V says that V is open and connected and that each connected component of its complement is either a singleton or the Möbius image of a closed round unit disk.
    3. He and Schramm proved the countably connected case in 1993. The pinned manuscript states the unrestricted result; this distinction matters because the Lean theorem contains no countability assumption. Its comparator body is sorry, paired with OAI.Analysis.CircleDomains.Main.

  15. theorem OAI.Problem047.removability_implies_rigidity Challenge statement: Conformal removability implies circle-domain rigidity sorry line 68

    L4DEL explanation

    a circle domain with conformally removable boundary is conformally rigid, with no restriction on the number of complementary components

    — OpenAI circle-domain rigidity manuscript (abstract)

    two rigidity conjectures of He and Schramm are in fact equivalent, at least for a large family of circle domains

    — Malik Younsi (2015), arXiv v1 abstract

    If the boundary of a circle domain is conformally removable, then every conformal equivalence from it to another circle domain is the restriction of a Möbius transformation, regardless of how many complementary components there are.

    1. The theorem separately assumes that U and V are circle domains, that frontier U is conformally removable, and that f is a conformal equivalence from U to V.
    2. IsConformallyRemovable quantifies over orientation-preserving sphere homeomorphisms conformal off the compact set. The conclusion supplies one Möbius transformation M agreeing with f at every point of U.
    3. This is only the removability-to-rigidity direction associated with the He–Schramm program; it is not an equivalence and it does not claim the converse. The body is sorry, paired with OAI.Analysis.CircleDomains.Main.

    Sources and attribution: Pinned Lean source (fd4aeeb2ee4f), OpenAI circle-domain rigidity manuscript (abstract), Pinned OpenAI formalization note 071, Malik Younsi (2015), arXiv v1 abstract
