The selected theorem checks disjointness and the move counts throughout play; it does not impose the paper's smaller finite-board restriction. The linked supplements reconstruct the older $35$-move appendix's finite recursive certificate and prove four conditional winning templates from partial positions, assuming the required Maker cells are present and Breaker avoids the corresponding finite envelope.
