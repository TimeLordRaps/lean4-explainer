  20. theorem OAI.DiscreteFree.source_endpoints Challenge statement: A uniformly discrete counterexample to bounded approximation in Lipschitz-free spaces sorry line 156
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    Kalton's question asks whether uniform discreteness of a metric space forces its Lipschitz-free space to have the bounded approximation property. The formalization constructs a countable metric space with all distinct points at distance at least one whose real Lipschitz-free space has the approximation property but fails every bounded approximation bound. The metric space is unbounded and not proper. The earlier quantitative renorming result for real $\ell_1$ is retained: for each integer $p\ge1$, an equivalent complete norm has the $2B_p$-bounded approximation property but fails the $p$-bounded approximation property, where $B_p=300p^2+150p+5$.
