  2. theorem OAI.SymmetricMahler.symmetric_mahler Challenge statement: Symmetric Mahler volume bound sorry line 15
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    For a centrally symmetric convex body K in n dimensions, volume(K) times volume(its polar) is at least 4^n / n!.

    1. K is compact, convex, symmetric under x to -x, with nonempty interior, in Fin n to R with n at least 1.
    2. coordinatePolar K is the set of p with the sum of p_i v_i at most 1 for all v in K.
    3. The conclusion compares the product of the two Lebesgue volumes (taken as real numbers) with 4^n / n!; the challenge file leaves the proof as sorry.
