  77. theorem OAI.Grothendieck.elementary_expansion Challenge statement: The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis sorry line 465
    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis asks whether algebraic $\infty$-groupoids recover the homotopy theory of spaces. The formalized result is the elementary-expansion theorem used in this approach: for every Grothendieck coherator in the Ara–Henry convention and every boundary-cellular model, attaching an $(n+1)$-disk along its source $n$-face induces a weak equivalence. The later semi-model structure and full comparison with the homotopy theory of spaces are not included.
