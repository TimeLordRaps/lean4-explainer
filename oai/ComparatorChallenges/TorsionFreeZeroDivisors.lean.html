    Kaplansky's zero-divisor conjecture asserts that the group algebra of a torsion-free group over a field has no zero divisors. The formalized result constructs a finitely presented torsion-free group $G$ and nonzero elements $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F_2[G]$ with $\alpha\beta=0$, giving a counterexample. The same group admits a finite two-dimensional classifying space $K(G,1)$.
