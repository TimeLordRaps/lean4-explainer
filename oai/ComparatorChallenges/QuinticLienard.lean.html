  6. theorem OAI.QuinticLienard.main Challenge statement: Exactly two is the sharp limit-cycle bound for quintic Liénard systems sorry line 39

    L4DEL explanation

    has at most two geometrically distinct isolated periodic orbits, and the bound is attained

    — OpenAI quintic Liénard manuscript (abstract)

    the maximum number and position of Poincaré's boundary cycles (cycles limites) for a differential equation of the first order

    — David Hilbert (1900), Mary Frances Winston Newson translation

    For the planar system x' = y-F(x), y' = -x, every real polynomial F of degree at most five has at most two geometrically distinct isolated periodic-orbit images, and some such F has exactly two.

    1. IsPeriodicOrbit requires a nonconstant solution z, a positive period T, periodicity with period T, and identifies the orbit C with the range of z.
    2. IsLimitCycle additionally requires an open neighborhood U containing C in which every periodic orbit contained in U has the same image C; the theorem bounds the extended cardinality of this set by two for every degree-≤5 polynomial.
    3. The second conjunct supplies a degree-≤5 polynomial attaining cardinality two, so the upper bound is sharp. The challenge body is sorry; its paired solution module is OAI.Analysis.LienardCycles.Main.
