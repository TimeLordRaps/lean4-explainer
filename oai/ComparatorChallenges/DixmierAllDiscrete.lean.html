    Dixmier's unitarizability problem asks whether a discrete group is amenable exactly when every uniformly bounded Hilbert-space representation is similar to a unitary one. The formalization establishes this equivalence for every discrete group. For every nonamenable group and every $\varepsilon>0$, it also gives a nonunitarizable representation with uniform operator bound at most $1+\varepsilon$. The Hilbert space can be chosen separable for countable groups; a separate linked statement records a separable witness with uniform bound $101$ in that case.
