Averaging diagonal Hilbert-valued phases

The weighted prediction bound of §9 for finite-dimensional Hilbert vectors and coordinate-dependent frequencies. The retained-set estimate is the proved constant-one diagonal Fourier gain; the marginal cancellation is exact.
