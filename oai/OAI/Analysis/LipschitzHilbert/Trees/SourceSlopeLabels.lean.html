The slope-label multiplicity assumption in §2 of the manuscript gives a uniform number of labels active at a fixed source direction. This includes vector-valued selectors: taking the union over all bin components does not increase the count. The last lemmas split the active labels at every scale into a fixed finite set of lattice slots.
