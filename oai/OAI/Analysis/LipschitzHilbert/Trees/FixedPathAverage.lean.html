Uniform averages of the actual graph prediction

The band-limited probability is an explicit mixture. Its graph-prediction average is at most one by the proved phase gain. Removing the path carrier then bounds the lower deviation by the off-path coefficient mass, preserving the relative path drop when passing to the principal probability.
