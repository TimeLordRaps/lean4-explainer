Simple tests for Fourier interpolation on the actual averaging measure

A finite measurable profile partitions the real line into its fibers. Its matrix entries are actual Fourier integrals over those fibers. The two finite interpolation endpoints follow from positivity and the proved improved fourth-moment theorem.
