Vertical differentiation of a general planar Schwartz function has the Fourier multiplier 2π i η. Consequently every scaled covariant vertical jet used in the packet construction preserves spectral zeros. The proof uses one-dimensional differentiation and product Fubini, so it does not impose a tensor decomposition on the planar packet.
