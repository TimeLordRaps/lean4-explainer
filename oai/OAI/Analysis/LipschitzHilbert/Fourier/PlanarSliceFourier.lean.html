Fourier support of affine-line restrictions

Fubini and the area-preserving shear identify the transverse Fourier transform of a slice coefficient with the planar transform on the fiber ξ+sη=ω. One-dimensional Fourier inversion then proves exact vanishing when this fiber misses the planar spectrum. No tensor representation is used.
