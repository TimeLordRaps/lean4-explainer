Published inputs for the Lipschitz-direction Hilbert transform

The two classical estimates used by the manuscript are stated as propositions. Their deterministic consequences make the quantifiers needed for the finite rectangle and martingale applications explicit.

  • Lacey--Li, On a conjecture of E. M. Stein on the Hilbert transform on vector fields, Memoirs AMS 205 (2010), no. 965, result 1.10 and equation (1.11); DOI 10.1090/S0065-9266-10-00572-7. In the author version arXiv:0704.0808 these occur on printed p. 3, with the finite-family proof in Chapter 3. This is the published form of arXiv:math/0601213v2, Theorem 1.4. We use the finite admissible-rectangle weak (2,2) estimate.
  • Zorin-Kranich, Weighted Lépingle inequality, Bernoulli 26 (2020), 2311--2318, Theorem 1.1, with p = 2, r = 3, and weight one. We use only real-valued finite martingales on probability spaces.

The deterministic consequences below make the quantifiers relevant to the application explicit: a union of high-average rectangles, and a variation sum whose increasing indices may be chosen separately at each point.
