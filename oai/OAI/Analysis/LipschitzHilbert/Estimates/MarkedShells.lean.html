Marked mass shells

The shell estimate and weighted sum of §10, res:marked-shell-count and res:marked-weight-sum. A positive drop at every marked index makes the corresponding masses separated. We use shells of ratio four, grouping pairs of the manuscript's dyadic shells, so that the geometric ratio is at most one half throughout the required exponent range.
