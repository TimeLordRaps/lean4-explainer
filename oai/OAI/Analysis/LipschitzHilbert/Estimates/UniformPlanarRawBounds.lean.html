A common finite collection of Schwartz seminorm bounds gives a common raw-kernel constant for an entire packet family. The constant is explicit and independent of the particular planar profile.

    The concrete general planar raw kernels and their horizontal derivatives obey uniform product-envelope bounds whenever the profile constant is uniformly bounded. The exact amplitude is (2^n*Cprof)*(M+2)*R^2, and differentiation costs exactly 1/l.
