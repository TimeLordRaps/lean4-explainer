The weak closure of the trace representation

Finite trace vectors define positive functionals on the GNS weak closure. Their supremum is additive, homogeneous, lower semicontinuous, and agrees with the original trace on its finite ideal.

This is the trace representation construction in The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations.
