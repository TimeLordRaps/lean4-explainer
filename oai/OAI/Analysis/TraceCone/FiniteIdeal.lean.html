Finite ideals of extended traces

For an extended trace, the norm closure of the square-integrable ideal is the closed ideal generated by the finite-trace positive elements. Positive elements belong to this ideal exactly when each positive cut has finite trace. This gives the scalar limit at zero in the canonical trace convergence of The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations, Section 2.
