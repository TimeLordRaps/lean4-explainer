The GNS representation of an extended trace

The square-integrable ideal carries the seminorm obtained by polarization of x ↦ τ (star x * x). Its Hilbert space completion supports bounded left multiplication by the algebra.

This is the trace representation used in the paper The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations.
