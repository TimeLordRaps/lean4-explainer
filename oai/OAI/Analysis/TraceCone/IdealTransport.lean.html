Ideal transport through the extended trace cone

Additive idempotents recover closed ideals. The scalar limits at zero and infinity recover the finite and zero ideals of an extended lower semicontinuous trace. These constructions give the ideal transport in Section 2 of The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations (OpenAI, 2026).
