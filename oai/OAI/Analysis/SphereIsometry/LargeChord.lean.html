Excluding large aligned-chord ratios

The secant estimate holds for every support. Reusing a support in the equality case makes the sign strict, and the literal inverse configuration gives the opposite image sign. A small radial perturbation then violates the actual defect bound. No compactness assumption on either ambient space is used here.
