Actual geometric points of iterated subdivision

Initial vertices are coordinate unit vectors. Every later vertex is the finite barycenter of the nonempty old face that it represents. Simplex membership, exact carriers, and the geometric mesh bound follow from this recursive formula.

    (k : ℕ) → (iterData I k).Vertex → I → ℝ
