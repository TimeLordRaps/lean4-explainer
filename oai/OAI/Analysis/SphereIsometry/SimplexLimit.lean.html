Extracting a fixed point from shrinking labelled simplex cells

The analytic limit step of the simplex fixed-point argument starts with a concrete family of simplex points, one for each coordinate at every subdivision level. The finite Sperner argument supplies this family, and the subdivision estimates supply its shrinking diameter. No fixed-point principle is assumed here.
