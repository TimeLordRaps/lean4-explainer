Regrouping a convex hull over a finite cover

For a finite family of nonempty sets, every point in the convex hull of their union is a convex combination with one point from each individual convex hull. The proof establishes convexity of the represented set. Zero coefficients are handled by choosing points inside the corresponding sets, without assuming that those sets contain the origin.
