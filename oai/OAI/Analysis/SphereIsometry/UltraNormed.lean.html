Bounded sequences with the ultralimit seminorm

The carrier below is a fresh wrapper, not the bounded submodule with its inherited product topology. Only algebra is transported to the wrapper. Its topology is then constructed from the ultralimit seminorm. The separation quotient therefore identifies zero ultradistance, not eventual equality.
