The actual radial return map

For fixed f, y and 0 < t < 1, the two interior centers are t • y and t • f y. The map follows the two radial contacts. The opposite direction is literal vector negation, with no antipodality assumption on f. The ambient extensions are continuous on the unit sphere; no continuity away from that sphere is asserted.
