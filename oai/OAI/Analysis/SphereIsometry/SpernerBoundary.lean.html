Boundary labels and restriction of the finite Sperner count

Admissible labels lie in the union of the vertex carriers. A good boundary facet therefore has exactly the carrier omitting the last coordinate. Mapping lower-dimensional faces through a vertex embedding preserves the label count after the coordinate embedding Fin.castSucc.
