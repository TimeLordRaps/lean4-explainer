Positive combinations in a common support face

A norm-bounded functional that is one on a strictly positive barycenter is one on every vertex. The resulting simultaneous support keeps the enlarged convex hull in the unit sphere. For an infinite sequence, finite-prefix hull containment passes to a closed ambient set without choosing a common support for the entire sequence.
