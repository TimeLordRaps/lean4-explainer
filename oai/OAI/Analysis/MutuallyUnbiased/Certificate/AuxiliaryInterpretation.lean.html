Interpreting the certificate's expanded auxiliary functions

The integer tagged terms are ordinary finite linear combinations of the manuscript's two auxiliary functions. Their real Gram products expand into the exact mixed-moment expressions used by the certificate. Positive semidefiniteness of a coefficient matrix proves nonnegativity independently of how its entries or its finite lists of terms were obtained.
