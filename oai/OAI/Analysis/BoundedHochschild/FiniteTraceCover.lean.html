Normal positive functionals for the finite trace cover

Every nonzero projection in an abstract von Neumann algebra is detected by a normal positive state supported on that projection. This constructs the normal positive functionals needed for a later trace cover; it does not assume or assert that finiteness alone supplies a trace.
