The classical input for algebras represented as full operator algebras

Kadison--Ringrose, Cohomology of operator algebras I (1971), Theorem 4.4, includes the type I vanishing result. Here its full-operator-algebra case is proved using the explicit rank-one homotopy in OperatorPrimitives, and is transported across an actual star algebra equivalence. No cohomological vanishing hypothesis is assumed.
