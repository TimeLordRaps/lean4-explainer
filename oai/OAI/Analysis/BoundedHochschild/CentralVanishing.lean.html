Vanishing for central partitions into proved cases

This is an unconditional application of the tracial argument and the full operator algebra homotopy. Its structural hypothesis gives actual central corners, each either faithfully normally tracial of type II₁ or explicitly isomorphic to a full operator algebra. It makes no cohomological assumptions. It does not assert that every von Neumann algebra has such a partition.
