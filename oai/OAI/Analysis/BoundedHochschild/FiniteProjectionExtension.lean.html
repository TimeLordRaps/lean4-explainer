Extension of embeddings in finite corners

A partial embedding into a finite projection extends whenever an embedding of the larger initial projection exists. This is the cancellation step used in Ringrose, Lectures on the trace in a finite von Neumann algebra, Lemma 5.1. The construction uses only the predual and finiteness, not a trace.
