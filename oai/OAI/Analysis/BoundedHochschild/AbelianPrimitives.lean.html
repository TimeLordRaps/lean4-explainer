Norm-one primitives on abelian von Neumann algebras

The abelian case of the classical type-I input is proved directly. Finite families of projections admit common finite partitions. Their separability homotopies have norm at most one; a bounded weak-* limit is exact on every projection, hence everywhere by density of the span of projections.
