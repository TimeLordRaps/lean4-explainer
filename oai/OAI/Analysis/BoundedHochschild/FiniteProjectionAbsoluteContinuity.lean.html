Absolute continuity of finite projection comparison

The projection argument behind Ringrose, Lectures on the trace in a finite von Neumann algebra, Lemma 5.2. Projections separately subequivalent to an orthogonal sequence admit decreasing dominating projections with zero weak-star limit. No tracial functional is used.
