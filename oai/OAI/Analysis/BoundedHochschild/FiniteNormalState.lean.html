Normal states and the remaining tracial averaging step

A normal state faithful on central projections is faithful on positive central elements. The property passes to its actual central corner. Composing such a state with a faithful normal positive central averaging map gives a faithful normal tracial state. Constructing that map from finiteness is a separate remaining theorem; no such map is postulated here.
