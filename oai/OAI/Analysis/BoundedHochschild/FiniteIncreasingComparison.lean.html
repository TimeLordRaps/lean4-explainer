Increasing limits of subequivalent projections

Ringrose, Lectures on the trace in a finite von Neumann algebra, Lemma 5.1: an increasing supremum of projections subequivalent to a fixed finite projection is still subequivalent to it. The proof aligns successive partial isometries inside the finite corner and then takes their chain supremum.
