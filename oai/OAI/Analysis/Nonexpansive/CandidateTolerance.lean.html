Conservative candidate scales imply the exact retained-budget tolerance

The construction may request a smaller return tolerance, using its complete candidate counter. Every retained earlier ordinal is at most that counter, so its geometric budget bounds this requested scale from below. The exposed tolerance remains the exact dyadic multiple of the retained-budget minimum.
