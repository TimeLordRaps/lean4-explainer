The finitely generated family of nonexpansive selfmaps

The family consists of actual maps of the norm subtype. Its constructors are identity, resolvents, composition, and binary convex combinations. Its image in the ambient module of maps to X is convex, and the finite convex-combination theorem below proves closure under every finite family. Thus the binary presentation also gives closure under finite convex combinations. All displacement constants are proved from the derivation.
