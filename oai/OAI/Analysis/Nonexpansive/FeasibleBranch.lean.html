A feasible branch with finite prefix witnesses

Feasibility is an existential proposition on a finite node, so the feasible nodes at each level still form a finite type. Restriction to an ancestor preserves the same witness. The elementary graded form of König's lemma then yields one coherent branch, with a witness for every finite prefix.

The witness set is arbitrary. In the fixed-point argument it is instantiated with the whole unit ball of the closed difference span. No compactness or surjectivity of projections between feasible levels is used here.
