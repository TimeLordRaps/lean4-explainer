Scalar estimates for finite leaf switching

The first estimates telescope the actual scaled ancestor recurrence. The second estimates bound a finite dyadic tolerance tail, including an empty tail. These lemmas involve only real scalars and finite sums; the switching construction supplies their hypotheses from its actual maps and weights.
