A genuine minimal invariant set

The order is reverse inclusion among the nonempty norm-closed convex invariant subsets of a separable invariant set. Intersections of chains are nonempty in the actual weak topology. No weak continuity of the nonexpansive map is used: invariance passes to intersections pointwise.
