Tolerances from the actual budgets on the tree

At every positive depth, take the minimum of all budgets at that level and all earlier positive levels, capped by one. The cap makes the construction work for arbitrary positive budgets. When those budgets are at most one, the cumulative minimum at every positive depth is attained by an actual node, so the cap does not change the prescribed finite minimum.

The tolerance is the cumulative minimum multiplied by the dyadic coefficient 2^(-(k+1)). Its value at depth zero is exactly one half. Every finite tail is bounded by every actual budget at its initial or an earlier depth.
