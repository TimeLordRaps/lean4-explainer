The actual recursively constructed infinite frontier tower

A stage is an already constructed finite frontier with its chronological candidate counter and the actual ancestor maps. The next stage is obtained by the concrete finite parent selections. No infinite tree, future detector, or future coefficient is supplied to this recursion.
