Actual adaptive selection of one parent's candidate group

Every state carries a valid complete candidate history. Its threshold is computed from that history before the next map is chosen. The actual compact detector then produces a legal new record. The adaptive anchor chooses a finite return, and only afterward does positive-support extraction select its children. Neither candidate existence nor a return law is assumed.
