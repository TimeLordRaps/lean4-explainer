Actual evaluated prediction outputs and their compact detector sets

At a candidate one level below a completed cutoff, the parent residual inputs are formed from the earlier candidate labels. The parent maps and the prior tree evaluation are then applied in the stated order. Compactness chooses weak limits of these already evaluated outputs. Uniform coefficient estimates give norm continuity of their image without weak continuity of a nonlinear map.
