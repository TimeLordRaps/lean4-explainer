Simultaneous approximation in the pointwise weak topology

A convex family of weak-valued maps can approximate each point in its closure in norm at any prescribed finite family of arguments. The finite family is handled as one vector in the finite product normed space. Consequently the chosen approximating map is the same at every argument.

No completeness, reflexivity, or nontriviality assumption is required here.
