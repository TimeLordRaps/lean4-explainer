The candidate ledger of the actual recursively constructed tree

The full ordered candidate groups supply the ledger's records. The actual positive-support embeddings select its retained entries. Thus its parent map and its retained maps agree with the tree extracted from the frontier tower, including all discarded records in every earlier prefix.
