Weak compactness from the actual canonical bidual map

The inverse of the surjective canonical inclusion is weak-star to weak continuous because its scalar evaluations are precisely the evaluations of the bidual. Banach–Alaoglu then gives weak compactness of norm balls. Closed convex subsets are weakly closed by Hahn–Banach separation.
