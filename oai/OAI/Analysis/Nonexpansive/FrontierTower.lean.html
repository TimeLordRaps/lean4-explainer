From actual finite extensions to one infinite evaluated tree

A tower records the concrete successor extension at every finite stage. Its coherence equalities identify each new frontier with that extension. The infinite tree, its positive weights, and its nonlinear evaluation are extracted from these records. Finite and infinite evaluation agree by induction on the number of extensions.
