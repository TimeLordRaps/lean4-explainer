The actual anchor of the resolvent tail families

The tail-limit set is an intersection of closures in the weak carrier. Its points are then viewed in the original normed space. The idempotent constructed from the actual resolvents fixes the selected anchor, and compactness plus separation gives the required norm-convex-hull property.
