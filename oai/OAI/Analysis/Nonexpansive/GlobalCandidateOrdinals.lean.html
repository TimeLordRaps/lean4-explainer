Global candidate ordinals

Every level reserves its full candidate interval. Retained candidates are indexed by an order embedding into that interval. Hence rejected candidates still consume their original ordinal, and labels at distinct levels cannot collide. None of the arithmetic requires candidate counts to be positive.
