Finite adaptive return to an anchor

The tail closures and the anchor's closed-convex-hull membership are the actual analytic input to this helper. The anchor construction proves those inputs for the actual resolvent tails.   2. theorem OAI.ReflexiveFixedPoints.adaptive_finite_selection line 57

    Source documentation

    Fully state-dependent finite selection. Every threshold is read from the preceding state, and the legal next state is chosen after the map. Only after a finite prefix has been obtained are its coefficients chosen.

    The returned selected list includes every candidate, including those of zero weight. positive_support_reindex separately extracts the retained children in their original order without changing this full history.
