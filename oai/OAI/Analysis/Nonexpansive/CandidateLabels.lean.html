Actual global labels of the retained candidates

Within a parent, the original positive-support embedding keeps the candidate indices. Concatenation preserves the parent order. The stage's base counter then places this entire finite sequence after all preceding sequences. Consequently the labels are globally injective even though zero-weight candidates leave gaps among the labels of retained children.
