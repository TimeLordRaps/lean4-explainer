Reindexing a finite prediction history

Equivalent finite candidate labels give the same residual inputs, actual nonlinear predictions, and chosen weak limits. The coefficient domain is transported in both directions, so the full detector sets also agree. These identities connect a recorded finite prefix to the final ledger without changing any map or passing a weak limit through a nonlinear operation.
