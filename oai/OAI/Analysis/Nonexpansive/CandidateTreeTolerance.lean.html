Exact tolerance for the actual recursively constructed tree

Every earlier retained candidate ordinal precedes the counter at the current finite stage. Thus the conservative scale used by actual adaptive selection is bounded by the precise retained-budget tolerance, which is the scale exported by the tree construction.
