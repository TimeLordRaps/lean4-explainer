Metrizability of norm-separable weakly compact sets

For a dense sequence in the norm carrier, choose a norming functional for each pairwise difference. These countably many functionals separate the points of the set. Their evaluations embed the compact weak carrier into a countable product of real lines. No separability of the ambient space, or of its entire dual, is assumed.
