Compact semigroups of nonexpansive selfmaps

Convexity converts pointwise weak closure into finite simultaneous norm approximation. This justifies composition of limits without asserting weak continuity of an arbitrary nonlinear map. Fixed right translations are continuous and the compact semigroup theorem then gives an idempotent.
