The compact semigroup supplied by the actual resolvent tails

The ambient convex hull of each resolvent tail consists of genuine generated selfmaps. Changing their values to the weak carrier therefore gives a convex, composition-closed family of nonexpansive selfmaps. The decreasing sequence of their pointwise weak closures has an actual idempotent.
