Optimality and uniqueness from a supporting potential

This packages the limiting-potential step of the arbitrary-target argument. The potential need only be Lipschitz on the source body and support the candidate map almost everywhere. Its restricted conjugate supplies the dual certificate, and Rademacher regularity makes its equality locus a graph.
