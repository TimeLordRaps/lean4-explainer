Normalized finite-volume Lipschitz laws on the triangular lattice

This file proves well-definedness of the normalized Lebesgue law in the free coordinates and the almost-sure nonzero-vertex statement. It contains no SLE or GFF convergence theorem and assumes no limiting law, effective stiffness, or tangent-flux coefficient.
