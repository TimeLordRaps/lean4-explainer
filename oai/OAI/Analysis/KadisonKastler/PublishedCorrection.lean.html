The published multiplication-correction input

PublishedMultiplicationCorrection is the application of Ricard--Roydor, A non-commutative Amir--Cambern theorem for von Neumann algebras and nuclear C-algebras*, J. Funct. Anal. 267 (2014), Proposition 3.2 (arXiv:1108.1970v2), to associative, involution-compatible products on a concrete von Neumann algebra. It is an explicit hypothesis, not an axiom.

The downstream theorem constructs the corrected star-algebra equivalence and proves the 32e complete bound. Surjectivity of the original map and spatial implementation are treated separately.
