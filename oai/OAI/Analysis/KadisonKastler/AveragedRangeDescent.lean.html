Descent when only the averaged conjugates lie in the embedded range

The averaging map acts on the upper ambient operator algebra. Its range on other inputs need not lie in the embedded lower algebra. The map below uses the inverse of a faithful embedding only on the averaged conjugates of the source algebra, which is exactly the range condition in §8.
