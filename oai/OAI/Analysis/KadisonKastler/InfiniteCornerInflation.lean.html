Inflation through countably many matrix corners

The hypotheses are the algebraic matrix-unit relations and the strong sum of their diagonal projections. They give an actual Hilbert direct-sum decomposition. A corner operator is then applied on each summand; in particular a corner unitary inflates with no loss in displacement.
