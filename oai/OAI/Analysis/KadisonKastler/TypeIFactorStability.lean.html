The type I branch and the projection dichotomy

The diffuse predicate is exactly the projection clause of Generator.IsII1Factor and TypeIIInfinityFactor. Its alternative, for a scalar-center algebra, is the existence of a minimal projection. This dichotomy is proved directly. The separable type I branch uses the constructed finite-group embedding.
