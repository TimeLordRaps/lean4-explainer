Phase coefficients in a tracial sequence quotient

The multiset coefficients are finite noncommutative polynomials. Their bounded-sequence versions therefore descend exactly to the coefficients of the constant partition and the quotient unitary. The free-word estimate therefore applies to those quotient coefficients.
