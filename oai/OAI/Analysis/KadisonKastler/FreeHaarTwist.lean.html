Finite word reductions for unitary twists of a free Haar unitary

The reduction splits an uncentered separator into its centered and scalar parts. A scalar separator is removed only between powers of the same sign, so their merged exponent is nonzero. These are algebraic trace arguments, with no additional free-probability input.
