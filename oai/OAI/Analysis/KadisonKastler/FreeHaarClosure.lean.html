The two free-projection moments under tracial approximation

Finite Laurent-polynomial moments follow from the alternating Haar moments. Cauchy--Schwarz then extends the required degree-two and degree-four identities under trace-two-norm approximation. In these degrees no uniform operator bound on the approximants is needed.
