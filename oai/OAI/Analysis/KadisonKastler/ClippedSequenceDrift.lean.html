Restoring the coordinate coefficient drift

The quotient norm estimate makes the discarded spectral tails strongly zero. This applies to both fixed vectors and vectors moved by a bounded unitary sequence. The latter restoration retains exactly the commutator of that unitary, independently of the number of coefficients.
