Actual unitary polar decompositions in finite matrix models

The scalar algebra is represented on the one-dimensional Hilbert space. The proved matrix-von-Neumann bridge supplies concrete representations of its finite matrix amplifications. Finite faithful trace and scalar center then give the unitary polar decomposition.
