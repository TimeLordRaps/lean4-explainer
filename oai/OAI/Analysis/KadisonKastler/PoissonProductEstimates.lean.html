Quantitative estimates for the two analytic smoothing fields

Boundary products of two approximate unitary fields have quadratic error. Once the Hardy product identity identifies their positive-height product with its Poisson integral, positivity of that integral retains the error. The product identity is a hypothesis of these estimates.
