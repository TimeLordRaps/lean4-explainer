Transport and correction of multiplication

These are the algebraic and norm estimates in the UCP criterion of §2 of Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability (2026). No perturbation theorem is assumed here. If a linear equivalence correcting the transported product is supplied, its composition with the original map gives an actual algebra equivalence.
