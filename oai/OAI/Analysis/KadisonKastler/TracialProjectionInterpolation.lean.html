Interpolating a projection inside one diagonal slot

  1. theorem OAI.KadisonKastler.exists_tracial_projection_interpolation line 20

    If the trace of p lies between that of q and q+d, cut just the orthogonal slot d. The resulting projection has exactly the trace of p and hence is unitarily conjugate to p in the original finite factor.
