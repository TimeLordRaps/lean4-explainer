The real regular representation and the core embedding

The translation and gauge operators below act on L²(ℝ,H) itself. A strongly continuous unitary group gives the faithful embedding π(x)(t) = U(t)* x U(t). No crossed-product or fixed-point identification is assumed in these constructions.
