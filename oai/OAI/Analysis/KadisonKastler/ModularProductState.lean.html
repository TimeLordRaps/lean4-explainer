A strongly invariant state with the two modular product marginals

For the actual cyclic separating representation of a type III factor, the left and right algebras are simple and conjugate. The constructed product state therefore has a strongly invariant extension to all ambient operators. This is the product-state step of Marrakchi, arXiv:1811.10253v2, Section 6.
