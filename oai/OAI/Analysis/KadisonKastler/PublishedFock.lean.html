The published faithful free-product representation input

The only input here is the standard faithful reduced-free-product representation of a scalar-free family with a faithful tracial state. The creation, annihilation and preservation formulas are those of Ricard–Xu, Khintchine type inequalities for reduced free products and applications, J. reine angew. Math. 599 (2006), §1 and Fact 2.6 (arXiv:math/0505302v2, printed pp. 3 and 7).

We use only finite diagonal algebras with equal trace projections. The input supplies their representation and structural identities, without assuming any projection-word norm estimate. The latter has been proved in FreeProjectionWords from those identities.
