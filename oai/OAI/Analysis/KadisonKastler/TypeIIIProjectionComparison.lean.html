Nonzero projections in separably acting type III factors

A nonfinite projection contains a proper isometry and hence countably many orthogonal copies of a nonzero defect. Factoriality supplies a countable source partition under this defect. It embeds the identity into the original projection; concrete Schroeder--Bernstein gives the required equivalence.
