Strong stability when the source algebra is injective

The one-sided amenable embedding gives a nearby conjugate subalgebra of the neighbor. Riesz's lemma forces this inclusion to be equality once the unit-ball gap is below one. No second perturbation or injectivity of the neighbor is required.
