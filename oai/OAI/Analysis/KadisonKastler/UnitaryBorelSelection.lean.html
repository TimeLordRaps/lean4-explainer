Finite-measure unitary selection

The Borel relation is an analytic image of Baire space. Measurable prefix hulls construct a section of its first coordinate for the given finite measure. This is the finite-measure consequence of Kechris, Classical Descriptive Set Theory, Theorems 18.1 and 21.10.
