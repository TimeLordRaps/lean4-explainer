The published factorial-core criterion

Takesaki, Duality for crossed products and the structure of von Neumann algebras of type III, Acta Math. 131 (1973), Lemma 8.2 and Corollary 9.7, identifies type III₁ by factoriality of the modular core. Blackadar, Operator Algebras, III.4.8.1--4 (p.329), gives the semifinite core and its independence of the faithful normal state. A semifinite factor has core M ⊗ L∞(ℝ), so the factorial-core criterion also excludes that case.

Only this consequence is retained. The modular action has a KMS certificate; factoriality of the concrete core is a premise to be proved by the application, never a field of a product-construction input.
