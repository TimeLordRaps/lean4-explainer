Actual matrix stages in the conjugate right algebra

Conjugation changes the scalar coefficients by complex conjugation, while the conjugated matrix-unit entries retain their usual product laws. Thus the right algebra is generated by actual increasing full matrix stages. This lets the same finite averaging argument transfer its irreducibility to a nearby commutant.
