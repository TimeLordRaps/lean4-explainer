Modular preparation from an expected finite subalgebra

The trace used here is only on the finite subalgebra. Its composition with an actual bimodular expectation is represented by the supplied vector. The modular conjugation data then give precisely the common right-vector identity used by matrix averaging and graph comparison. The corrected neighbor is proved cyclic and separating when the two ordinary gaps are small; these properties are not supplied as preparation assumptions.
