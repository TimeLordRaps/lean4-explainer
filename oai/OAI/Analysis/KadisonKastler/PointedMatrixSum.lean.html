Tensor products of pointed matrix-site systems

The two site families act in the two tensor legs. Finite words split into their two subwords, and their vector moments multiply. Cyclicity follows from the dense tensor-product orbit; separation follows by the same argument for the commutants. No modular or infinite-product input is used.
