This is the algebraic argument recalled in §5 of Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability (2026), following I. N. Herstein, Jordan homomorphisms, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 81 (1956), 331–341. The target is assumed prime explicitly; no von Neumann algebra representation is needed for this algebraic step.
