The four-dimensional pointed matrix state

Left and right matrix multiplication act on the Hilbert space of two by two matrices. The square root of the diagonal density is cyclic for both actions. An orthonormal basis beginning at that vector makes the reference vector the zero-coordinate vacuum used by the infinite product.
