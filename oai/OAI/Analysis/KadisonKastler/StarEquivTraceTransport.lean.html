Tracial and bounded strong transport by a factor isomorphism

Uniqueness of the normalized finite-factor trace makes an algebraic star-isomorphism preserve the tracial two-norm. The concrete finite-vector trace formulas then give strong-operator continuity on every bounded ball, in both directions. No continuity of the isomorphism is assumed.
