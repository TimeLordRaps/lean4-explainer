The scalar boundary mean on actual L-infinity classes

The source is the bounded family of L-infinity sections indexed by the countable discrete group. Multiplication and nonsingular pullback are defined on the actual almost-everywhere classes. The scalar conditional-expectation property includes the module and action identities.
