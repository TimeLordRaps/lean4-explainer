Countable copies of a full projection

A maximal orthogonal family with sources under a full projection gives a countable partition of the identity. Two orthogonal isometries provide room for countably many copies of the full projection. The partition embeds into their sum, and concrete projection Schroeder--Bernstein moves that sum onto the identity.
