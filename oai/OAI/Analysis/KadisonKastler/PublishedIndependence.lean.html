Popa's finite-base independence input

Popa, Independence properties in subalgebras of ultraproduct II1 factors, J. Funct. Anal. 266 (2014), Theorem 0.1(b), Theorem 4.3 and its proof, printed pp. 20 and 24 (arXiv:1308.3982v3).

Theorem 4.3 explicitly permits both endpoint letters to be one. Its proof constructs the diffuse abelian algebra from a Haar unitary. We retain exactly that unitary and the mixed-moment identity, only for a finite base algebra. Testing the conditional expectation against base elements is its faithful-trace characterization. No controlled coordinate lift or scalar freeness of a conjugated matrix model is assumed here.
