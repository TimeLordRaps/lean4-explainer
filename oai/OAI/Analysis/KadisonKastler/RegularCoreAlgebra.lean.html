The concrete regular core and its first fixed-point inclusion

The tensor algebra is the commutant of the constant copy of P' on L²(ℝ,H). The regular core is the von Neumann algebra generated by the concrete regular embedding and translations. Its generators lie in the tensor algebra and are fixed by the common regular action. This proves the first fixed-point inclusion without a crossed-product assumption.
