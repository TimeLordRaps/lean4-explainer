The common unitary action on rescaled graphs

Left and right multiplication by common unitaries acts on graph vectors through one unitary of the fixed two-coordinate Hilbert space. Thus each closed graph reduces this unitary whenever the right action belongs to the relevant commutant and has the prescribed value on the common vector.
