Row-column bounds for bilinear multiplication

The Haagerup bound is expressed directly by all finite rectangular matrix products, as in the UCP criterion of §2 of Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability (2026). For multiplication transported through a linear equivalence, this is exactly the matrix defect already bounded in CompletelyBounded.
