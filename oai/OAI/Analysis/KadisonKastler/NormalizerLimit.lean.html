Recovering a normalizer from weak limits of conjugations

The smoothing argument supplies exact normalizers close to moving linear maps. Only the latter maps need to converge. A weak limit of the actual conjugations is UCP and has range in the original algebra; the complete error bound then gives a nearby exact normalizer.
