Self-adjoint strong approximation

This is the first step in discharging bounded density. The existing bicommutant theorem gives unbounded strong approximation. Taking real parts preserves weak convergence; orthogonal complements of the finite tuple orbit then recover strong approximation by self-adjoint elements.
