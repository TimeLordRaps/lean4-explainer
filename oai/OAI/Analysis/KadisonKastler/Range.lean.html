The surjectivity step in the completely positive criterion

The first paragraph of Proposition 2.4 in the manuscript proves surjectivity from a reverse unit-ball gap strictly below one. The normed-space argument uses Riesz's lemma and requires no operator-algebra perturbation theorem.
