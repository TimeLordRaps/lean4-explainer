Quantitative exclusion of the reversed Jordan orientation

The calculation in §5 of Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability excludes an antihomomorphism that is sufficiently close to the inclusion. Closeness alone gives a 3e + e² estimate; contractivity improves it to 3e. The applications to Jordan maps keep the embeddings in the common ambient algebra explicit.
