Phase coefficients as sums of words in conjugated copies

Removing the first label gives an exact decomposition of a prescribed multiset fibre. The phase-coefficient recursion is therefore precisely the sum of interleaved projection words. A unitary moves every intervening factor to the left and conjugates each projection by its remaining power.

    The word e₀ v e₁ v ... v eᵣ₋₁, with an empty-word convention.

    def interleavedWord (e : α → A) (v : A) : (r : ℕ) → (Fin r → α) → A
  | 0, _ => 1
  | r + 1, t => e (t 0) * (if r = 0 then 1 else v) *
      interleavedWord e v r (Fin.tail t)
