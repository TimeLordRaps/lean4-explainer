The published common tracial position input

This is the application-specific part of Cameron--Christensen--Sinclair-- Smith--White--Wiggins, Kadison--Kastler stable factors, Duke Math. J. 163 (2014), Lemma 4.10(i)--(iv),(vii), printed pp. 20--21, arXiv:1209.4116v3. The constant is 50948 * sqrt g and the hypothesis is g < 1.74 * 10^(-13).

We specialize their amenable subalgebra hypothesis to a scalar relative commutant, which is the only case used here. The output records algebraic equivalences onto the represented images and their actual vector traces. No isomorphism between the two nearby factors is assumed.
