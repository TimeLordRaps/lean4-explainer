Abelian averaging of a spatial derivation implementer

A spatial derivation preserving a von Neumann algebra also preserves its commutant. Averaging over any commuting unitary subgroup of the commutant keeps the same derivation, preserves the norm bound, and makes the implementer commute with that subgroup. This is the abelian averaging step in the spatial splitting argument.
