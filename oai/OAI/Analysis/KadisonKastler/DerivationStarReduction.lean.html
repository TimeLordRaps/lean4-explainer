Reduction to anti-star derivations

Sakai's spatial construction treats derivations satisfying D (star x) = -star (D x). Every bounded complex derivation is a complex linear combination of two such derivations. That decomposition transfers innerness from anti-star derivations to arbitrary bounded complex derivations.
