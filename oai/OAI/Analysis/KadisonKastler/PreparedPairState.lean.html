The common vector functional centralizes the shared algebra

Matching left and right vector actions, including their adjoints, gives the centralizer identity directly by the Hilbert-space adjoint formula. The source's common conjugation supplies these matches on unitaries; the four-unitary span then gives every element of the common algebra.
