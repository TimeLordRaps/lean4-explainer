Adjoint compatibility of the continuous spectral cutoff

A two-by-two block argument transports continuous functional calculus through an intertwiner between selfadjoint elements. Applied twice to xx* and x*x, it shows that the cutoff commutes with adjoints. This provides the left-sided fourth-moment estimate from the right-sided one.
