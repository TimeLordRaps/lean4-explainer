Anticommuting unitaries in finite factors

The two Pauli matrices in a unital system of two-by-two matrix units give selfadjoint anticommuting unitaries. Every II₁ factor contains such a system, so the quantitative Jordan orientation test applies without an additional assumption on the source factor.
