Simultaneous graph signal and clipping-cost selection

The normalized sign sum is passed through an actual real-linear signal map. A constant orbit signal gives its exact second moment and the Hilbert fourth-moment bound. Finite selection then retains the signal and bounds the actual vector-state clipping cost in the same sample.
