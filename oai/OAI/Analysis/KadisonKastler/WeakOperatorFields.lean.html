Essentially bounded weak operator fields

The representatives are bounded operator-valued functions with measurable evaluations of the function and its adjoint. Their closed C-star algebra is quotiented by the closed star ideal of almost-everywhere zero fields. The resulting norm is bounded by every essential operator-norm bound.
