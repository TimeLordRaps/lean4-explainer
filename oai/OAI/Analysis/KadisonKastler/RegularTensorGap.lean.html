Complete matrix bounds control the regular tensor gap

The scalar Hilbert coordinates identify the actual regular tensor algebra with the canonical countable amplification. Uniform finite-matrix approximation, or actual common countable matrix units, therefore bounds its gap. No ordinary-gap amplification principle is assumed.
