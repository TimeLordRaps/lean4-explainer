Pinching a vector state across an almost central projection

This is the first state approximation in Haagerup's Lemma 3.6 (Acta Math.158(1987), p.138). The pinched state is constructed from two vector functionals and retains faithfulness. Its norm distance from the original state is controlled by the left/right projection gap.
