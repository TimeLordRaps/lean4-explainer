Strict decrease after removing a bounded-below off-diagonal corner

The spatial innerness argument uses a self-adjoint two-by-two operator whose off-diagonal corner is bounded below on both summands. Removing that corner strictly decreases the norm. The estimate is purely a C-star algebra calculation.
