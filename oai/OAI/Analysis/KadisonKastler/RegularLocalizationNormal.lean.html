Bounded weak continuity of localization averages

The constant representation is a countable diagonal amplification in scalar Hilbert coordinates. Its bounded weak continuity therefore gives bounded weak continuity of the translated-cutoff sandwich. This permits finite packet compressions to be averaged before taking their limit.
