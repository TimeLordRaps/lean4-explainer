Conditional strong stability for injective represented algebras

The hypothesis ChristensenMutual is the mutual near-inclusion consequence of Cameron–Christensen–Sinclair–Smith–White–Wiggins, Kadison–Kastler stable factors, Duke Math. J. 163 (2014), Theorem 3.3(i), building on Christensen, Near inclusions of C-algebras* (1980). The stability theorem is conditional on this near-inclusion property.
