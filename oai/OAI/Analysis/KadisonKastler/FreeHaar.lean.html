Free Haar moments imply freeness of the conjugated diagonals

The hypothesis records ordinary alternating centered moments with nonzero interior Haar powers. The last power may be zero. A telescoping product identity and traciality convert products from conjugated copies into exactly these moments. No free-diagonal conclusion is a field of the hypothesis.
