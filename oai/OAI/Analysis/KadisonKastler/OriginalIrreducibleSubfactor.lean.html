Pulling the increasing matrix construction to the original representation

The original representation need not possess a tracial vector. We construct the matrix stages on the canonical separable trace Hilbert space, pull each finite stage back algebraically, and take its actual original weak closure. Commutation with that closure implies commutation with every pulled-back stage and hence with the irreducible algebra in the trace representation. Injectivity of the closure of the increasing matrix algebras is a hypothesis.
