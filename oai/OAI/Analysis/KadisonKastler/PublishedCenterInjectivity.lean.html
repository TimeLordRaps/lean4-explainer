Published injectivity of the concrete center

Blackadar, Operator Algebras, Corollary IV.2.2.10, p. 377 (PDF p. 385), states that commutative von Neumann algebras are injective. Proposition IV.2.1.4(iii), p. 373, gives the norm-one projection in every faithful representation. Only centers in their given representation are retained.
