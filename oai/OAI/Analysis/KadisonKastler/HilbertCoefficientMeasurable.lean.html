Measurability from countably many Hilbert coefficients

The coefficients against a countable dense family give a continuous injection into a countable product of copies of ℂ. Lusin–Souslin makes this map a measurable embedding. Thus measurability of the coefficients implies strong measurability, without assuming a Pettis theorem.
