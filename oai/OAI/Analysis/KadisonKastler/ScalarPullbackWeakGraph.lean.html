Weak closure of the nonsingular pullback graph

Pullback need not be a bounded operator on L2. Its graph is nonetheless closed: norm-convergent pairs have subsequences converging pointwise, and nonsingularity transports the first almost-everywhere limit. Linearity then makes this graph weakly closed. This suffices for the boundary averages.
