Finite matrix corners in semifinite diffuse factors

The type-II-infinity predicate uses scalar center, two orthogonal isometries, projection semifiniteness, and diffuseness. Choosing one finite nonzero projection and applying the countable-copy theorem constructs the matrix corner and its II₁ factor structure. There is no trace or stability conclusion in the type predicate. Projection semifiniteness is exactly the criterion in Blackadar III.1.4.2; III.1.4.6 identifies the properly infinite continuous semifinite part as type II∞.
