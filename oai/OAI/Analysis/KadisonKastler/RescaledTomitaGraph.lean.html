Rescaled Tomita graphs in a fixed two-coordinate Hilbert space

A fixed conjugation identifies the conjugate copy of the Hilbert space with the original one. In these coordinates the graph vector is (x Ω, c J (x* Ω)). The same identification is used for both algebras. No closability or modular spectral theorem is needed for the closed linear graph subspace or the elementary projection comparison below.
