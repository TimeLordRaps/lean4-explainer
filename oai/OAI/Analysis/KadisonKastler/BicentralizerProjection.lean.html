A contractive state-preserving projection onto the bicentralizer

The compact asymptotic orbit hull supplies an element of the bicentralizer. Its pairings with every bicentralizer vector are prescribed, which makes the choice unique and proves linearity. The orbit norm bound gives the contractive projection on the original algebra.
