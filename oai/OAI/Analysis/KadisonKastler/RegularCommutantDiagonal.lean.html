Generation of the bounded diagonal fields

The algebra generated by constant operators from P and scalar characters contains every bounded measurable P-valued field. Its commutant is recovered by the proved diagonal decomposition and countable commutant tests. This is a concrete generation statement, without any crossed-product input.
