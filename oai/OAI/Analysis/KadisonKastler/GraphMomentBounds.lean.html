Expected costs before graph clipping

The cost uses the actual vector state and the two fourth powers. The finite-sample estimate retains the diagonal correction, so increasing the number of samples removes the diagonal cost. The final scalar lemma chooses a stage and a sample size from convergence of the mixed moments.
