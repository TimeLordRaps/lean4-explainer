Countable copies of a full projection

Blackadar, Operator Algebras, III.1.3.6 (author PDF, printed p.243), asserts that a full projection in a properly infinite, locally countably decomposable von Neumann algebra admits a countable orthogonal family of equivalent projections with strong sum one. Separably acting algebras are countably decomposable. The input below is only this projection assertion.

The projection's central support is the already constructed orbit-closure projection. Proper infiniteness is expressed by two isometries with orthogonal ranges. No trace, corner type, or perturbation conclusion is part of this published input.

Primary source: https://bruceblackadar.com/Mathematics/Cycr.pdf
