The type-II part of the published type stability lemma

Christensen--Sinclair--Smith--White, Perturbations of C-algebraic invariants*, GAFA 20 (2010), 368--397, Lemma 3.5 (author manuscript pp. 9--10), proves that two von Neumann algebras with the same centre and distance less than 1/10 have the same finite type I, type II₁ and infinite central parts. Here only diffuseness of the neighbour of a II₁ factor is retained. The centre and finiteness conclusions are already proved locally.

Primary author manuscript: https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/25426/1/25426.pdf
