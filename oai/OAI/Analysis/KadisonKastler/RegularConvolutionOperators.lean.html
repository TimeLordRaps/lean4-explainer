Integrable convolution operators in the regular commutant

The common regular action is strongly continuous by its gauge equivalence to translations. Its integrable scalar averages belong to the generated commutant algebra. Products of two such averages with a constant commutant coefficient give the finite packet averages used in the reconstruction argument.
