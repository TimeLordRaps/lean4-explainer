Fixed decomposable operators in the regular representation

The gauge transforms a decomposable operator fixed by U_s ⊗ ρ_s into a translation-fixed multiplier. The preceding scalar Fubini argument then makes it a constant operator, so the original operator belongs to the concrete regular image π(B(H)).
