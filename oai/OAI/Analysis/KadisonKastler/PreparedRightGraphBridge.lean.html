Prepared graph comparison from common right actions

The vector and conjugation identities are imposed only on the shared increasing matrix algebra. In particular, no tracial state on either ambient algebra is needed. The relative right-action data imply the required centralizer identities, and matrix averaging transfers relative irreducibility to the nearby algebra.
