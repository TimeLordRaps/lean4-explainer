A scalar invariant mean for every discrete abelian group

Finite iterated Cesaro averages give a positive, unital, contractive family of scalar functionals. An ultrafilter extending the directed finite-set/length filter gives a simultaneous scalar limit. The explicit telescoping bound proves invariance. This constructs the mean used for the real modular and character groups without an amenability premise.
