Commutant estimates from individual matrix approximants

A matrix approximant from N commutes entrywise with a contraction in N′. The complete commutator is therefore bounded by twice the matrix error. The commutant distance formula gives distance at most that error to M′; normalization costs a further factor two for unit balls. No linear map selecting the matrix approximants is assumed.
