The finite-corner argument for the actual regular cores

This specializes the proved injective-matrix argument and character descent to the concrete regular representation. The semifinite matrix subalgebra and its finite initial corner are explicit data. In particular, none of the action, fixed-point, or range-identification conclusions is assumed.
