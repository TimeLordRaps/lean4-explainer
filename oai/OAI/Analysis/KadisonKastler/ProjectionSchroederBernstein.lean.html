Schroeder--Bernstein for concrete projection equivalence

The proof constructs the unilateral shift on the iterated defect of a partial isometry, then adds the identity on its orthogonal complement. The construction uses the existing strong sums of partial isometries; no finiteness or trace assumption occurs.
