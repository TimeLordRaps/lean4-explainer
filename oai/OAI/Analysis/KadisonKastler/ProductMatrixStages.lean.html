Deriving the product-state argument from finite matrix data

A remote copy of a finite tensor block has identical matrix moments. Every other finite block embeds in the product of these two blocks and a finite complement. These are finite tensor-product statements; no centralizer irreducibility or crossed-product conclusion is assumed.
