Factor and finite-isometry structure of represented corners

Countable full matrix units identify the original algebra with the actual amplification of its first corner. A central corner operator therefore inflates to a central ambient operator. Finiteness of the first projection also passes directly to the represented corner by extension by zero. No trace or semifinite decomposition is assumed in these arguments.
