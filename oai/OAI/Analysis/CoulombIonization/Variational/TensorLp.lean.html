    Right s-finiteness makes the product kernel measurable, so L² factors have an L² tensor. Without an a.e. measurable kernel, Mathlib's product uses an arbitrary chosen measure, whose integrability is characterized by memLp_tensor_iff_choice, not guaranteed by the factors. The finite-dimensional Haar hypotheses in the applications imply right s-finiteness.
