source

The concrete von Neumann algebra generated by the modular coefficient representation and the regular translations. This defines the continuous core representation and its universal containment property. Semifiniteness and a trace on this algebra are not included as assumptions or assertions.

The representation is the one used in Anantharaman--Delaroche (1995), Proposition 3.6, p.329.
