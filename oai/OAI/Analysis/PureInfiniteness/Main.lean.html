Weak pure infiniteness and Cuntz-algebra absorption

Individual proper infiniteness implies strong pure infiniteness for arbitrary, possibly nonunital complex C*-algebras. Exactness removes a fixed finite amplification. The rectangular epsilon formulation of Cuntz comparison agrees with its sequential formulation.
