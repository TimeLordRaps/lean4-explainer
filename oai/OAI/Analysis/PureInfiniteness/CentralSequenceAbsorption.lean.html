Absorption from the published central-sequence embedding

The embedding below is into (Aω ∩ A') / Ann(A, Aω). This is the central-sequence embedding immediately preceding Proposition 4.4 in Kirchberg, Central sequences in C-algebras and strongly purely infinite algebras*, Abel Symposia 1 (2006), 175--231. The structural hypotheses specialize Proposition 4.4(1),(3),(5) and Remark 4.6 to the absorbing algebra: nuclearity, simplicity, and a connected approximate half-flip in a faithful C*-completion of its algebraic tensor square. Tensor absorption follows from quotient intertwining.
