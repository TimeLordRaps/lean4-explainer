Finite merging of positive abelian-range maps. The orthogonality is relative to finitely many positive values, so this construction works in the nonunital ambient ultrapower without assuming a multiplier Cuntz family.

  1. theorem OAI.WeakPureInfiniteness.finite_abelian_merge line 27

    On any finite set, all matrix coefficients of a finite family can be recovered from one positive abelian-range map. This is the local corner-sum step in KR02 Lemma 7.16, with no unitality assumption on the ambient algebra.
