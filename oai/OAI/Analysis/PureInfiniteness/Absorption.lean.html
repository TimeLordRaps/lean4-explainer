Cuntz-algebra absorption

Absorption is expressed by a star-algebra isomorphism with the minimal tensor product. The implication from nuclear strong pure infiniteness is the specialization to O∞ of E. Kirchberg, Central sequences in C-algebras and strongly purely infinite algebras*, in Operator Algebras: The Abel Symposium 2004 (2006), pp. 175--231, Proposition 4.4(4),(5) and Remark 4.6, together with the central-sequence embedding preceding Proposition 4.4.

For a possibly nonunital separable algebra, the unital central embedding is into (Aω ∩ A') / Ann(A, Aω). The zero algebra is included.

Source: https://abelsymposium.no/symp2004/preprints/kirchberg.pdf.
