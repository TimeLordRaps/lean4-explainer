Null traces on the nondegenerate components of a level union

Generic level unions have only countably many nondegenerate components. Consequently a trace that is null on each such component is null on their union. At free points the new coordinate has one trace value, so the levels meeting this union form a Haar-null set. This is the fixed-prior- tuple step in the manuscript's compatibility argument.
