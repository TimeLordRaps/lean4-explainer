Passing finite-hole flux estimates to the limit

The rigidity manuscript's finite-hole estimate bounds the flux with an error of sixteen times the remaining mixed-radius sum. Square-summability of the source and target radii makes this error tend to zero along arbitrary finite subsets of the index type, giving eq:limiting-flux.
