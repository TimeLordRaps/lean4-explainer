Integer-translated fillings on the pulled-back circle cover

Section 2, Step 4, of the Koebe manuscript fills one sheet over each filling disk and uses its integer translates on all other sheets. Here the cover is the actual pullback of ℝ → ℝ/ℤ by the original circle coordinate. The filled real function is the original height plus the base correction g - u on each disk. Thus it has exactly the original deck increments and projects to the circle map already constructed by CirclePatching.

The differentials on every sheet are those of the selected real filling. No additional lifting, period, or winding input is assumed.
