Cluster oscillation of transported terminal branches

The finite model's actual inverse transports a real branch near a physical terminal. All its cluster values at the assigned complementary component come from the physical boundary. Thus the physical square-sum oscillation estimate survives uniformization without a boundary-extension hypothesis.
