Passing a connected exterior through the finite sphere chart

Deleting an interior point from a connected set preserves connectedness when that point has a connected punctured neighborhood. Applied at infinity, this turns the connected sphere inverse image of a quotient exterior into the connected unbounded planar exterior needed for smooth contour filling.
