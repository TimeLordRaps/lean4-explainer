Uniform crossing from a compact physical core

This supplies the geometric input to the length-payment argument in the generic-level-continua lemma. A compact core inside the physical domain has a uniform ball neighborhood there. Uniform continuity of the quotient map makes these balls too small to contain a large quotient continuum.
