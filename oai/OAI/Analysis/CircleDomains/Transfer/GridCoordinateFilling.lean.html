Smooth coordinate fillings from the actual boundary grids

The contour theorem gives one possible active label at each boundary. The reciprocal-grid calculation turns its avoidance conclusions into inactive-lattice avoidance for the actual local real branches. Thus the smooth filling theorem applies at all sufficiently small widths.
