Selecting a compatible grid with small costs

This is the numerical selection step in 047-02, Section 2. A small expected wedge cost and a terminal count whose scaled expectation tends to zero give a positive grid spacing and a compatible sample for which both realized costs are small. The random staircase and its compatibility conditions are explicit hypotheses of this selection theorem.
