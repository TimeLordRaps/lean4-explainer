Simultaneous compatibility of every lattice tuple

The finite-transfer construction needs one choice of shifts that avoids every bad tuple formed from distinct labels and lattice levels. The integer offsets constitute a countable family, so the individual absolute-continuity conclusions hold simultaneously. In particular they hold for the finite period-one grids at reciprocal-integer spacings.
