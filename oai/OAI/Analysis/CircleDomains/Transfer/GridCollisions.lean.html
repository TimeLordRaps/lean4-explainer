Finite terminal flags from actual independent grid shifts

For two different coordinate labels, their lattice-hit events have product probability. Countably many flagged points and finitely many label pairs can therefore be counted by Tonelli. Square-summable band widths imply a finite expected collision count, hence only finitely many flagged collisions for almost every choice of the grids.
