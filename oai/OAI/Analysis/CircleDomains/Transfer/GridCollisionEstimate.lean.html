Vanishing scaled expectation for the actual collision count

The probability estimates are multiplied by the square of the grid spacing. This turns the normalized band probabilities into the clipped products min h a * min h b, dominated by summable oscillation products. Thus the actual random count, with its spacing-dependent law, satisfies the small-expectation premise of the reciprocal-grid selection theorem.
