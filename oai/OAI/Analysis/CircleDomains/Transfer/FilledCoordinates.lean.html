Simultaneous smooth coordinate fillings

Compact connected collars are chosen inside the supplied real-branch domains and away from all inactive lattices. A single sufficiently small width makes every inactive staircase constant there. Rank-one collar extensions then patch into the retained coordinates across all the pairwise disjoint filling disks. No lifting theorem is assumed.
