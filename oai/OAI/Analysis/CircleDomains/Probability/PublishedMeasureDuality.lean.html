source

Measure-family duality and probability lifting.

  • Ambrosio–Di Marino–Savaré, JEMS 17 (2015), 1817–1853, DOI 10.4171/JEMS/546, Theorem 5.1 (p. 1830), Corollary 5.2(b) (p. 1833): positive modulus of a Suslin image yields a probability plan on finite measures with an L² barycenter. MeasureImageDuality is the p = 2 consequence for a Borel image of a standard Borel space.
  • Bogachev, Measure Theory, vol. II (2007), Theorem 6.9.1 gives a measure-dependent measurable selector. MeasurePlanLifting states its probability-lifting consequence, used in the proof of ADMS, Theorem 7.2, p. 1839.

The two propositions separate measure-space duality from probability lifting; neither assumes a compact-support conclusion.
