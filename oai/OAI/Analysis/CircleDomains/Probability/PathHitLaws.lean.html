Closed path-hit relations and the joint upper semicontinuity of their probabilities. The time interval is compact, so projection of the closed incidence relation is closed. Weak convergence and the existing Portmanteau theorem then apply to the product with the moving point mass. This supplies the actual hit function used in law fusion.
