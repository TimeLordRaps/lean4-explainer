Collapse forced by barriers of vanishing energy

The line-payment inequality prevents barriers of vanishing energy from crossing every horizontal line over a fixed interval of positive length. Applied to both coordinate projections, this forces a compact connected planar set to be a singleton. The hypotheses specify barrier existence, crossings, and absolute continuity of the restrictions.
