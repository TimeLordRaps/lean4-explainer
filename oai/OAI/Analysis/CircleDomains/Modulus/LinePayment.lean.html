Energy paid by a line crossing a barrier

A barrier that is zero at an end of [-L, L] and equals one somewhere on the segment has squared derivative integral at least 1 / (2 * L). This follows from the fundamental theorem of calculus and the integral Cauchy–Schwarz inequality.

Both absolutely continuous and C¹ restrictions satisfy this estimate. Fubini's theorem gives the rectangular energy lower bound when horizontal slices are almost everywhere absolutely continuous.
