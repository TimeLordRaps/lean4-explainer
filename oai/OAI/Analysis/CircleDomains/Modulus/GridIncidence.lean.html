Actual probabilities that a shifted lattice meets a band

A grid of spacing h meets [a,b] exactly when the shift modulo h lies in the projection of this interval. Haar measure of that projection is at most min h (b-a). Under the actual normalized interval law for the shift, the probability is at most min 1 ((b-a)/h).
