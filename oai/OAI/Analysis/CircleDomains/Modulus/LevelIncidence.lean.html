Finite level unions and analytic compatibility failures

Repeated labels are allowed in a finite tuple of levels. Closed level graphs give a closed union graph; its nondegenerate-component relation is Borel by the compact hyperspace argument. After restriction to a Borel set of free points, projection over the point is analytic.
