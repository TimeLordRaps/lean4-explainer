Weighted dust incidence

This is the dust part of eq:weighted-level-bound in Section 1 of 047-02. A point contributes its weight on a measurable set of levels. Tonelli converts the expected total incidence to the sum of weights times level probabilities; the band-incidence estimate bounds these probabilities, and square summability makes the result finite.
