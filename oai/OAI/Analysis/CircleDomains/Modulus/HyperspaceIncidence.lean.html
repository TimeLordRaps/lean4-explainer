Closed continuum incidences in compact level graphs

The compatibility condition quantifies over subcontinua of a finite level union. The hyperspace of nonempty compact sets is compact, and connectedness, point membership and containment in a closed level graph are closed conditions. Projection therefore preserves closedness for continuum incidences with a fixed positive size witness.
