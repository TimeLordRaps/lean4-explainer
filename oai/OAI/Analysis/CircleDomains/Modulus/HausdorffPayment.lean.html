Length payment by crossing a ball

The generic-level-continua proof in Section 1 of 047-02 uses radial distance on a subcontinuum crossing a ball contained in the physical domain. Its image contains the radius interval, so one-dimensional Hausdorff measure pays at least the ball radius.
