The published finite circle-uniformization input

Conway, Functions of One Complex Variable II, GTM 159 (1995), Theorem 15.7.9, p. 106, gives circle uniformization of nondegenerate finitely connected regions. FiniteCircleUniformization records only its existence conclusion on the sphere, omitting normalization and uniqueness. It is an explicit proposition parameter, not an axiom.

The complement components of a finite family of disjoint closed connected sets are identified below. This checks the finiteness and nondegeneracy hypotheses for completed surfaces, also after an arbitrary sphere homeomorphism such as compact metric uniformization.
