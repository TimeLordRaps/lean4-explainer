The published coefficient area theorem and exterior bounds

Ahlfors, Conformal Invariants (1973), Theorem 5-1, p. 83, states that the Laurent coefficients of a normalized exterior univalent map satisfy the weighted square-sum bound. The proposition below includes its Laurent expansion, in the zero-constant-term normalization needed here. It is an explicit published input.

Only a coarse uniform exterior bound is needed for normal-family compactness. It follows from the coefficient inequality and a geometric series, with no additional analytic input.
