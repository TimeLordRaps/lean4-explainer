Metric conformality in the concrete sphere charts

Metric conformality records the pullback Gram matrix and positive orientation of the actual real differential. When the source metric is a positive scalar multiple of the Euclidean metric, these equations imply the complex Cauchy--Riemann equation and a nonzero derivative.
