Limits of omitted points and disks

Hurwitz applied to Fₙ - wₙ keeps every convergent sequence of omitted points out of a nonconstant locally uniform limit's image. The argument works on each retained core. Convergence of disk parameters then keeps the whole limiting closed disk in the complement.
