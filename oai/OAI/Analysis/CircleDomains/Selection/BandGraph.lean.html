+# Compact interval-band graphs for bounded real coordinates

Take the closure of the physical graph and fill the interval between cluster values in each fiber. This constructs a compact band graph; on the open physical domain it is exactly the original graph. Its projection modulo one consequently gives compact level sets with the correct physical fibers.
