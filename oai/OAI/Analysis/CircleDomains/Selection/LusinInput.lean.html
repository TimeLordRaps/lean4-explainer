Completed-measure compatibility for finite level tuples

Lusin's universal-measurability theorem applies to the finite-dimensional tori used for level choices: A. S. Kechris, Classical Descriptive Set Theory, GTM 156 (1995), Theorem 21.10, p. 155. Every analytic subset of a standard Borel space is measurable for the completion of each sigma-finite Borel measure. Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-4190-4

The compatibility statement uses analyticity of compact incidence sets and nullity of the free trace image on each nondegenerate component.
