Stability of bands under boundary-vanishing approximation

The smoothing step in Section 6 changes a coordinate by an error tending to zero at every dust point. Its cluster values, interval bands and mod-one level incidences at those points are therefore exactly preserved. No regularity of either coordinate on the dust is required.
