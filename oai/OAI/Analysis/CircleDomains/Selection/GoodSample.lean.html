Simultaneous choice of small random quantities

The finite-transfer grid selection needs one sample for which both the wedge integral and terminal count are small, while retaining the conull compatibility conditions. The proof below uses the integral of the sum of the two normalized quantities, so its conclusion preserves any specified conull event.
