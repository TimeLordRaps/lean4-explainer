Physical terminal disks and their staircase oscillations

A finite set of quotient dust points is surrounded by disjoint smooth physical Jordan disks, with whole fibers inside the disks and boundaries in the physical domain. The disks can be confined to prescribed neighborhoods. Liminf and limsup bounds on those neighborhoods give the staircase oscillation bound a + 4h, uniformly in width and shift.
