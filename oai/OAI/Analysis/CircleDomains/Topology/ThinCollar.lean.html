Interpolation in a thin exterior neighborhood

For a compact retained core, integrals over shrinking exterior neighborhoods tend to zero. A continuous family of energy densities can consequently be interpolated smoothly to its original value near the core with arbitrarily small added energy. No measure-zero assumption on the core boundary is used.
