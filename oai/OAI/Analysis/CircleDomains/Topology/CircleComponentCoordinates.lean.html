Center and radius coordinates of circle-domain components

If a circle domain contains infinity, every complementary component is an ordinary closed disk, allowing radius zero. A bound on the whole complement bounds both parameters. Thus no choice of disk coordinates is an extra hypothesis in the marked-disk compactness argument.
