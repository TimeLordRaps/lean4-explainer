From physical grid avoidance to inactive staircase branches

At reciprocal spacing every integer lattice level represents one of the finite circle levels. Consequently the actual physical boundary avoidance constructed by the contour theorem gives exactly the shifted-lattice avoidance needed by the smooth filling theorem, including for local real lifts of circle coordinates.
