Saturation of physical Jordan interiors

A connected quotient fiber cannot cross a boundary consisting of singleton fibers. Thus physical Jordan interiors whose boundaries lie in the original domain are saturated, and project to open quotient neighborhoods. This is the saturation assertion preceding the small-disks lemma in Section 1.
