Connected compact neighborhoods of filling boundaries

Small closed metric thickenings of a compact connected planar curve are connected and compact. They give connected collars inside any prescribed open boundary neighborhood, as required for simultaneous staircase inactivity. No smooth tubular-neighborhood theorem is needed.
