Countable selection of compatible open barriers

A basis enumeration selects one admissible candidate at each finite stage. If removing previous candidates allows a candidate with the same or larger open set, every candidate admissible for the final family has its open set covered by that family. This is the selection step only; countability of unavailable ends is separate.
