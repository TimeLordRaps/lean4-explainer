Topology of projected physical disks

Physical Jordan disks with boundary in the original domain have saturated interiors and saturated closures. Their images are open with connected complements, and disjoint physical closed disks give disjoint quotient closed disks. These properties allow finite dust covers to be used in the descending grid construction without postulating a disk-selection lemma.
