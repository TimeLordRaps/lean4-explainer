Trapping the bounded side of a circle-model image

A compact exterior piece has closed image. Its open interior has open image, and their difference is the image of the boundary circle. A connected set avoiding that boundary and meeting the open image must lie entirely in the image. This supplies the topological part of the uniform bound for finite circle models.
