Filling the actual descended circle cutoff

The collar functions are genuine local lifts of the original circle coordinates. On the one-plateau of the cutoff their staircases project to the actual modified maps. Smooth rank-one filling therefore applies to those maps, rather than to a separately specified surrogate.
