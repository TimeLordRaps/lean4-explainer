Crossing segments from nested bounded regions

On any vertical line meeting the inner region, the bottommost points of the inner and outer closures lie on their frontiers. Strict containment puts the outer point strictly below. Thus prescribed zero/one frontier values provide the crossing segments needed by line payment.
