Localizing nondegenerate components

Boundary bumping turns a nondegenerate continuum through a point into one in any prescribed neighborhood. This is the localization used in the lower-grid obstacle argument. Totally disconnected dust cannot destroy a singleton component of a compact closed obstacle.
