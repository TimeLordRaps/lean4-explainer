Real lifts of circle coordinates near filling disks

The quotient ℝ → ℝ/ℤ is a covering map in Mathlib. Its lifting theorem therefore gives actual continuous real branches on simply connected open sets. Smoothness is expressed in the ordinary real quotient charts AddCircle.equivIco; a continuous lift is locally one of these charts plus an integer, so the lift is smooth. No external lifting axiom is used.
