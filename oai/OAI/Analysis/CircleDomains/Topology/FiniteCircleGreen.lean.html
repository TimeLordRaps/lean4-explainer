source

The precise classical Green input used below.

M. Spivak, Calculus on Manifolds (1965), Theorem 5-5, p.124, Stokes' theorem on a compact oriented manifold with boundary. The specialization is a bounded smooth Jordan region with finitely many pairwise disjoint round holes and a smooth real one-form. Circle boundaries have positive disk orientation, so their integrals are subtracted. The compact region lies inside the open set of smoothness. https://www.cimat.mx/~gil/docencia/2013/topologia_variedades/spivak-calculus-on-manifolds.pdf
