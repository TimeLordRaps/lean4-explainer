Merging finite physical disk families away from an obstacle

Section 2, Step 3 of the Koebe manuscript merges overlapping preliminary disks without introducing intersections of new boundaries with the lower grid obstacle. A regular cutoff gives the same merge directly: if Q is their compact union and its boundary misses F, then Q is relatively clopen in F ∪ Q. The established contour construction applied to that larger obstacle covers all of Q by disjoint smooth Jordan disks. No transversality or general-position input is required.
