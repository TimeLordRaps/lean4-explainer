Filling disks for the actual free/flagged grid setup

The levels below are the actual equally spaced circle levels of the compact band graph. At a free point a singleton real band gives one level per label. At a flagged point outside the terminal collision set, two distinct label grids cannot occur. The pair family may represent each unordered pair in either orientation. These facts discharge the lower-obstacle premise of the descending contour construction.
