Topological parts of the component argument

The quotient theorem below proves the totally disconnected dust conclusion from the component-collapse properties of the quotient. Moore's 1925 sphere-decomposition theorem supplies the sphere realization; that published input is not assumed to provide the dust conclusion for free.
