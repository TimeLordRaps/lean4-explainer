Circle contours of compact regular levels

Two classical inputs are separated from the contour construction.

  • CompactOneManifoldClassification is the compact boundaryless case of Milnor, Topology from the Differentiable Viewpoint (1965), Appendix "Classifying 1-Manifolds", theorem p. 55 (proof pp. 55--57). https://www.ux1.eiu.edu/~cidelman/Classes/4855%20and%205220/Supplementary%20Texts/MilnorTopDiffVpt.pdf
  • PlanarSchoenflies is Moise, Geometric Topology in Dimensions 2 and 3, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 47 (1977), Theorem 10.4: a continuous injection of the unit circle into the plane extends to a homeomorphism of the plane. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-9906-6

Both are explicit proposition parameters, not axioms. Neither input asserts the manuscript's obstacle-isolating disk selection. Smooth local charts, finite components, their planar embeddings and the contour cover are proved.
