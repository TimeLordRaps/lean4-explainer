Common-width selection with actual transferred energy bounds

After the geometric disk construction, one sufficiently late width gives smooth filled coordinates, a metric retaining the spherical background, and both the total and localized energy estimates. The cost is measured only on the original domain. Periodic and masked coordinates are treated separately from this real-coordinate analytic assembly.
