Finite smooth Jordan neighborhoods

The contour construction in Section 1 of the Koebe manuscript is derived from the smooth cutoff and regular level, scalar Sard, compact one-manifold classification, and planar Schoenflies. Outer selection and containment give the finite family of Jordan neighborhoods.
