From planar coarea charts to compact quotient-level topology

A finite collection of Lipschitz parameterizations covers the physical part of each level. Scalar coarea in each parameter domain gives finite Hausdorff length almost everywhere. The quotient-level argument then gives the finite-continuum bound, countably many nondegenerate components, and local connectedness of every subcontinuum.

The chart-cover inclusion and the compactness of the level are geometric hypotheses; PlanarScalarCoarea supplies the analytic identity.
