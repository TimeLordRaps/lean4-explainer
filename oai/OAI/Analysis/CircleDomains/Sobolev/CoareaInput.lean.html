Scalar coarea and its finite-energy consequences

The planar scalar coarea identities specialize Federer, Geometric Measure Theory, Springer (1969), §3.2.11 (coarea) and §3.2.12 (weighted coarea), DOI: 10.1007/978-3-642-62010-2. For a C¹ real function on an open subset of the Euclidean plane, the integral of the one-dimensional Hausdorff measures of its fibers equals the integral of the norm of its differential. The locally Lipschitz version follows from the stated Lipschitz coarea theorem by a countable relatively compact covering.

PlanarScalarCoarea and WeightedPlanarScalarCoarea express these specializations as propositions. They require neither finite energy nor topological conclusions. The length function need only be Lebesgue measurable; Borel measurability is unnecessary.

Finite area and finite squared differential imply finite integrated length, and integer periodization gives finite physical length for almost every circle-valued level.
