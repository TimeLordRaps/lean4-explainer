Intrinsic differentials and cutoff modifications of circle coordinates

The differential is computed in a genuine real quotient chart. Local integer transition functions make it independent of the chosen lift. The cutoff staircase descends to the circle and is smooth in the same charts; no global real lift or extra topological input is required.
