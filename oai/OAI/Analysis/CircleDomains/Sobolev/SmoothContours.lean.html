Compact smooth cutoffs and regular superlevel neighborhoods

This is the first half of the common contour construction in §1 of the Koebe manuscript's small-disks proof. The cutoff is constructed using Mathlib's smooth Urysohn theorem and a relatively compact open neighborhood; its existence is unconditional.

Only the regular-value choice is conditional on a published input: Arthur Sard, The measure of the critical values of differentiable maps, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 48 (1942), 883--890, Theorem 7.2, pp. 889--890, DOI: 10.1090/S0002-9904-1942-07811-6. We use only its specialization to compactly supported smooth maps from the plane to the real line. The assumption says that their actual critical-value image has Lebesgue outer measure zero. Original article: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1942-07811-6 Readable scan: https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/sard.pdf

No smooth Jordan disk or finite-component decomposition is assumed. The constructed neighborhood has compact closure in the prescribed open set and its boundary lies in a compact regular level set.
