Weighted coarea with the actual boundary-band incidences

The abstract dust events in the weighted-level estimate are here the actual circle-band fibers. Their measurability and measure bounds are proved from the compact band graph and its liminf–limsup budget. The final theorem applies this to the finite-plane part of a bounded real coordinate on the concrete Riemann sphere.
