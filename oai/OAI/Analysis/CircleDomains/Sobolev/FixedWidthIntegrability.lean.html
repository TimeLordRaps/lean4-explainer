Spatial integrability at every fixed staircase width

The derivative of a smooth staircase is periodic and bounded. Thus each fixed choice of shifts has integrable spatial wedges whenever the original three wedge terms are integrable. In particular, the cost identity needs no separate integrability assumption for the modified coordinates.
