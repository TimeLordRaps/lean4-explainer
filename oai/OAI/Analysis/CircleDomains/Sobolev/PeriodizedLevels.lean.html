Periodizing scalar coarea lengths

A scalar coarea estimate controls the integral of the physical length of real level sets. Integer translates of [0,1) partition the real line, so the sum of these lengths over all lifts of a mod-one level has the same integral on [0,1). Countable subadditivity then gives finite physical length for almost every mod-one level.
