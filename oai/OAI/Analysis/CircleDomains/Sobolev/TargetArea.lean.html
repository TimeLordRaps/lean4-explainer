Target area from the exterior area theorem

Ahlfors, Conformal Invariants (1973), Exterior Area Theorem, supplies the area bound for the set omitted by a normalized univalent exterior map. The numerical bound yields the target-area estimate by change of variables and disjointness of the image and omitted disks.
