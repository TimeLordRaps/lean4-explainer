Tensor-norm nuclearity of O∞

Finite unused-generator corners control the Fock matrix in every C*-completion of the algebraic tensor product. The two Gram summands of the return map give a uniform factor-two norm comparison, and the C*-identity removes that factor. This proves the tensor-norm nuclearity input used by the absorption criterion.
