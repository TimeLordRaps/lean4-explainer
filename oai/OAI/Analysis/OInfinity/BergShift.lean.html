Finite Berg smoothing along a Cuntz corner shift

A finite family of orthogonal corner rotations can have a large angle in the middle and zero angles at both ends. The commutator with a row generator is controlled by adjacent angle differences. The unitary and connectedness assertions are proved for the finite construction. Matching a terminal corner between the two different tensor-factor rows is still a separate obligation.
