Actual endomorphism iterates on cyclic tower bands

Transport through a finite iterate costs the two endpoint tower errors. The adjacent-depth row identity is exact. In particular, mixed-depth orthogonality involves the inner letter of a two-letter word; it cannot be replaced by the same-depth outer-letter condition.
