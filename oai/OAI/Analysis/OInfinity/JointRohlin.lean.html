Joint source-target cyclic partitions

Convolving two commuting cyclic projection partitions produces one partition approximately shifted by both endomorphisms. This is the joint phase needed to control the row cocycle. The coefficient-path commutators are a separate requirement; no degree-zero property of the fragmentation path is asserted.
