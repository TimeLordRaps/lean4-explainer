Controlled fragmentation on a joint cyclic partition

The clock-corrected quarter paths have a uniform sampled modulus, with an arbitrarily small additive error. The finite fragmentation operations below retain that modulus and the actual cell commutators. No continuity estimate for an arbitrary choice of a polar decomposition is used.
