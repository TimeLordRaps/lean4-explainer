Genuine rotations on the retained cyclic cells

The corrected rows have a common initial projection. Cutting them by its orthogonal cells gives actual square-zero partial isometries and hence a connected unitary for any finite collection of angles. The removed cell is absent from both initial rows; the complement of their final ranges is retained. No claim that the ambient zero cell is fixed is made.
