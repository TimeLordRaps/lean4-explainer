A concrete almost central split of a canonical finite O∞ row

The corner and its coisometric row are constructed from unused canonical isometries and the finite word sum. Its complement is the range of an actual isometry. Thus the vacuum compression and the complement are both controlled without assuming a projection-classification theorem.
