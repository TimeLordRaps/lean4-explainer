Finite cocycle coefficients with an exact terminal seam

A path from V_N to 1 gives coefficients V_k α^[k](p(k/N)). The zeroth and terminal coefficients agree exactly, while consecutive coefficients satisfy the cocycle equation up to L/N. These are the coefficients for finite Rohlin gluing; commutation with the gluing projections is a separate requirement.
