Connected choices of finite binary completions

Three orthogonal buffer isometries let two finite rows with actual unused isometries be matched on the left by a connected unitary. Applying this to the finite shift equations gives a full binary completion with a connected left cocycle relative to a prescribed reference row. Left matching alone is not an assertion of conjugacy.
