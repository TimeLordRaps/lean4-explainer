Finite geometric modes for the binary corner shift

A normalized geometric block has a shift error at its two endpoints only. The squared coefficient error is exactly 2 / L; the CAR norm estimate transfers it to actual elements of the C*-algebra. Blocks whose index intervals are disjoint have zero mixed anticommutator. The shift here is x ↦ a*x*star a + b*x*star b for a relative Cuntz pair.
