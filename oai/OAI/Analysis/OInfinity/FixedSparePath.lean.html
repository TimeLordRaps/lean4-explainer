Finite stabilization inside a genuinely fixed spare corner

The first corner is the support of the original unitary. Additional equivalent corners lie in its fixed complement. A cyclic permutation of these corners turns a diagonal of path samples into adjacent increments. All finite corners and the uncovered complement are retained explicitly.
