Clock correction of the actual cyclic bands

A clock which cycles the projection partition turns a band of degree d into a block diagonal element after multiplication by its inverse dth power. The clock and the partition can be constructed inside a prescribed closed row algebra. This does not assert the endpoint cocycle equation.
