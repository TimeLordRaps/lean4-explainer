Uniform paths for actual buffered iterates

The compression of every connected unitary fixes the same full isometry inside the complementary corner. The fixed-spare construction gives these compressions a uniform 9π path bound. The resulting iterates have a bound independent of depth, with the fixed base path cost retained. Their terminal representation is still the entire isometric copy.
