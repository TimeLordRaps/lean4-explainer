Mixed stabilization in the explicit zero-unit corner

The row t₁ sᵢ t₁* + tᵢ₊₂ p is the direct sum of the source row and the negative target corner, with unit 1 - t₀t₀*. Its Gram and support formulas do not require the two rows to commute. The comparison below uses the actual third buffer and retains the exact corner transport; it does not cancel the negative summand or claim ambient half-flip.
