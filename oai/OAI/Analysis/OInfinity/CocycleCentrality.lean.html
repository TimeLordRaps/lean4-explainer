Actual cocycle commutators with periodic source towers

The same source shift occurs in the cocycle and in the tower estimate. For elements fixed by the target row, the cocycle commutator equals the source-periodicity defect. In particular an N-cycle with step error η has cocycle commutator at most Nη. No centrality on source isometries is assumed.
