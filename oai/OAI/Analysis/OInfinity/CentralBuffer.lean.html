A third canonical row in the norm ultrapower

The representatives are the explicit approximately central canonical rows, with tolerance 1/(n+1) and the first n+1 source generators tested. Their norms are uniformly one; both Gram and commutation relations hold exactly in the quotient. No commuting-buffer existence is assumed.
