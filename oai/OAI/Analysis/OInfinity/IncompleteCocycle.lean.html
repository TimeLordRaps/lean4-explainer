The actual unitary cocycle of an incomplete Cuntz row

The unitary-group endomorphism is u ↦ 1 - φ(1) + φ(u). This retains the defect projection of the canonical finite row. Its connected cocycles satisfy the exact generator recursion and an exact terminal-support formula; the terminal projection is not assigned a small norm.
