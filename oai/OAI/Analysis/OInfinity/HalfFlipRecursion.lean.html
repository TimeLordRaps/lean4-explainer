Finite recursive intertwiners for the O∞ half-flip

For two finite Cuntz families, the transfer operator sends x to ∑ i, t i * x * star (s i). Its Cesàro means are contractions and satisfy a uniform recursive intertwining estimate. These means are not asserted to be unitary; constructing nearby connected unitaries remains a separate step.
