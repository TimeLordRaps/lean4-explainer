Finite orthogonal towers and their terminal corner

The layers of a nonunital corner endomorphism form an exact finite orthogonal partition together with the last tail projection. For a Cuntz row, multiplication by each generator shifts these layers. The tail remains explicit throughout; there is no assertion that its norm becomes small as the height grows.
