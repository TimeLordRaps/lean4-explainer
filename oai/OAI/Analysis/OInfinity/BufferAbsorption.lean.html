An ambient unitary for finite-row absorption

A coisometric binary row in a corner and an isometry onto its complement give an explicit ambient unitary. Applied to the finite Fock isometry, this unitary absorbs the coisometric target row with a vanishing error. Its path component is recorded by an explicit, fixed anchor; connectedness of that anchor is not inferred from the corner relations.
