Quantitative stability of the finite Jordan–Wigner construction

The occupation parity is Lipschitz on normalized modes. Telescoping the finite parity prefixes gives the explicit (4k + 1)δ estimate, while scalar phases pass exactly through the construction. These estimates do not assert a unitary cocycle or a bound on its exponential length.
