Simplicity of O∞ from finite vacuum corners

A nonzero Fock coefficient becomes a nonzero vacuum coefficient after a word sandwich. Compressing a polynomial by an unused generator gives exactly that vacuum coefficient times the unit. Polynomial approximation therefore puts an invertible element in every nonzero two-sided ideal.
