The complementary action in mixed cancellation

An exact corner comparison determines a unitary on the complementary full isometric copy. The full error is the sum of the mixed-corner error and this concrete complementary conjugation error. A fixed-complement extension of a partial isometry is also given; connectedness is not inferred from its unitary identities.
