Finite clock corrections and their multiplication defects

Clock powers remove the finite homogeneous degrees. They are not treated as central: every multiplication defect is an explicit commutator. Terms of degree divisible by the clock period are preserved exactly. The final formula applies this correction to the actual three-term cancellation path and preserves both endpoint values.
