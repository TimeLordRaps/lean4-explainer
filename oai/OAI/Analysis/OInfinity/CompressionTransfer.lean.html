Moving a compression into a spare corner

Orthogonal isometries give a uniform path between the two compressions of a unitary. If the second range is in q, its endpoint is exactly a relative unitary extended by 1-q. Centrality of that relative unitary with respect to a coisometric pair in q remains a separate obligation.
