Finite stabilization in a coisometric two-generator corner

Splitting one range gives three branches. Folding the first two branches back into one gives another coisometric pair. This is the algebraic step behind a bounded rotation contraction of a compressed unitary. The corner unit stays q; no assertion removes its ambient complement.
