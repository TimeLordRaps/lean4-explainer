Two commuting Cuntz rows and their finite rectangular towers

The transfer partial isometry moves one source layer into one target layer. The source and target rectangles, and their terminal strips, remain explicit. These identities do not yet identify the two complete terminal tails by a unitary that intertwines both rows.
