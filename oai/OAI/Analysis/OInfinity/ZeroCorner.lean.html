An explicit coisometric pair in the complementary corner

Three orthogonal isometries suffice to construct two relative isometries whose ranges add up to 1 - r₀ * star r₀. The construction uses the checked two-swap completion, with its connected unitary retained explicitly. The unit of this pair is the stated corner projection, not the ambient unit.
