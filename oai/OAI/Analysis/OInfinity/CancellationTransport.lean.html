Actual tower transport for the cancellation path

Two row words move a cyclic tower by their respective degrees. If the unitary coefficient almost commutes with that tower, the explicit cancellation path has three transported components, with a uniform error independent of the tower height. No gauge action on the ambient algebra is assumed.
