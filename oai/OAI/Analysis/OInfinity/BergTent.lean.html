Explicit finite Berg towers from unused Cuntz generators

The angular profile is a triangular tent. Two unused orthogonal isometries supply its bottom partial isometry. This discharges the support and angular hypotheses of the finite smoothing construction. It does not identify the terminal corners belonging to two different Cuntz rows.
