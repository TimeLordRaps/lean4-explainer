Two controlled stabilization paths

The first path adds a cancelling pair on the two spare branches. The second contracts the folded pair. Their endpoints differ by at most four times the commutator error. The common corner and the ambient complement remain explicit; no central corner for an arbitrary growing cocycle is asserted.
