Controlled cancellation paths in a fixed corner

A relative unitary compressed into one range cancels its adjoint compressed into an orthogonal equivalent range by an explicit quarter-turn. The path has an angular Lipschitz bound independent of that unitary. Its complement is retained throughout; this does not construct a fixed approximately central corner for a growing family of cocycles.
