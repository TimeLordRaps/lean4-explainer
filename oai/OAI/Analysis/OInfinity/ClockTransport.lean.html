A finite clock for the actual CAR partition

The clock is the finite product of the occupation phase unitaries of the commuting Jordan–Wigner coordinates. It has exact dyadic order and cycles the corresponding partition. Its row commutators are controlled by the proved coordinate shift errors, independently of any cocycle equation.
