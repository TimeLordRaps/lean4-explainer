Continuous polar correction of the actual clock path

The correction is the fixed formula x (x* x)^(-1/2). Both Gram bounds come from the proved three-term cancellation estimates. Continuity and exact endpoint preservation are proved for this formula; no cocycle or half-flip equation is assumed or concluded.
