Finite row permutations

Permutations of a coisometric finite row give genuine unitaries after adding the ambient complement. Coordinate cycles factor into two reflections, so these cycle unitaries have uniform controlled paths. This applies to finite block permutations, not to the original nonzero-gauge row cocycle itself.
