Joint word rows and their finite coordinate cycle

The row consists of n source letters followed by one target letter. Its coordinate cycle is a finite matrix permutation, with a path bound independent of n. Identifying a different cocycle with this permutation requires an equality; no nonzero-gauge cocycle is silently substituted.
