Exact relocation of the whole buffered tail

An actual unit-isometry in the canonical vacuum corner exchanges its range with the full terminal range of w^(m+1). The swap commutes with the target family and has a uniform path. Its formula retains the residual source copy; relocation itself does not estimate that copy by zero.
