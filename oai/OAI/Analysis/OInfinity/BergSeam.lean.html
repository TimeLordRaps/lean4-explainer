Finite Berg towers: connectedness and seam formulas

The finite assembled tower is an actual connected unitary when its initial row extension and terminal coefficient are connected. Its layer sum includes the terminal coefficient explicitly. The exact boundary formula gives the seam estimate while accounting for the terminal corner.
