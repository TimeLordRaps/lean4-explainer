Exact band products and the actual adjacent-depth corner

Products sample their first coefficient at the shifted index. The final lemma uses the concrete Cuntz relation at neighboring depths, with its inner-letter orthogonality condition. It is local in the tower cell; no cyclic boundary cell is silently identified with the next depth.
