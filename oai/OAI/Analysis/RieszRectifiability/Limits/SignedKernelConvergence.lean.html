Convergence of signed weighted kernel integrals

Finite step approximations in the second variable reduce weighted product integrals to sums of weighted moments and cell masses. Integrable absolute weights and Lipschitz kernel bounds control the approximation error, allowing convergence of these finite sums to pass to the original kernel integrals.
