Bilateral approximation by active-cell surfaces

Exact tangential chart coordinates and the reference-plane height bound locate surface points near support points. Conversely, surface points in the inner ball remain close to the support by projecting onto the bilaterally approximating plane. Both estimates retain the explicit error constant at the cell radius.
