Surface-area loss in selected restart charts

At a good restart root, the mass left outside a selected surface piece is bounded by its discarded Hausdorff area. Applying this estimate at each selected root turns an area-loss hypothesis into the uniform deficit required by the recursive chart assembly; bad roots contribute an empty remainder.
