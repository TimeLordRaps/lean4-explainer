Cleaning the support lattice partition

Removing the union of all cell overlaps produces nested, disjoint measurable cells that cover the support outside one exceptional set. Net centers avoid that set, so cleaning preserves the closed cells as closures. Under the growth bounds, it also preserves cell mass and gives a unique cell at every scale almost everywhere.
