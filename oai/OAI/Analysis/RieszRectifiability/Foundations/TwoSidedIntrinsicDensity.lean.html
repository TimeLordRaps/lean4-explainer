Two-sided intrinsic densities

Lower ball-growth bounds give a positive multiple of Euclidean volume dominated by the measure. Together with the upper-growth domination, this produces a measurable density with pointwise lower and upper bounds by clipping its Radon–Nikodym derivative without changing the represented measure.
