Integrability of hard Riesz truncations

Global upper growth makes the Riesz kernel square-integrable outside each positive-radius ball. Pairing this exterior estimate with an L² input proves integrability of the hard truncation. For finite measures, integrability instead follows from the bounded exterior kernel and the inclusion of L² in L¹.
