Uniform budgets for bad cells and restarts

The class-uniform bad-beta packing constant controls multiplicity tails and the total mass of restart roots. A relative loss tolerance and a finite bad-depth budget are chosen before the measure, using only the fixed geometric and Riesz bounds. Splitting the desired deficit into two halves pays separately for the discarded high-multiplicity set and the remainders of selected restart regions. These budgets feed the recursive chart assembly without changing its image or Lipschitz conclusions.
