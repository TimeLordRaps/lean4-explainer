The Arveson commutant-distance formula reduces hyperreflexivity to uniform matrix commutator bounds and the scalar spectral estimate. The formula is recorded in Christensen, Sinclair, Smith and White, Perturbations of C-algebraic invariants*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 20 (2010), 368–397, Equation (2.1).
