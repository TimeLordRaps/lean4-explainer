Hilbert multiplicities in the invariant-intertwiner theorem

Scalar matrix coefficients in the multiplicity Hilbert space are genuine bounded endpoint intertwiners. Tensor totality reconstructs the full operator from these slices. Repeated-letter compressions provide the bounded operator on the multiplicity space used in propagation.
