Matrix-unit isolation of invariant-tensor coefficients

The permutation contractions are given by their coordinate sums. Their normalization is the number of cycles, including fixed points. We prove the matrix-unit isolation and uniqueness steps in the invariant-intertwiner argument. Schur--Weyl spanning is expressed as a multilinear-form property.
