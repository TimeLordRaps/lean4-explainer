Stable coefficients on growing matrix stages

Finite Schur--Weyl spanning, compatibility of normalized contractions, and the actual matrix-unit endpoint estimate produce one stable coefficient family. The small-cycle coefficients are proved zero; neither stability nor those zeros are added as assumptions.
