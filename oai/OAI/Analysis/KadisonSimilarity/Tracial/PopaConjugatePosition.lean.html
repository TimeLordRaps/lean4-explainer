source

The conjugacy form of Popa's free-position construction: a unitary in an irreducibly included subfactor's tracial ultrapower conjugates a prescribed constant subfactor to a copy free from the ambient constant algebra. The conjugating unitary remains in the prescribed subfactor ultrapower, and trace preservation transports the matrix presentation to its conjugated copy.
