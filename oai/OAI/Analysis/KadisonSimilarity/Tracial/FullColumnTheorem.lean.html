The full-column implementation argument

The corner commutator is constructed here, corrected by the proved cyclic implementation theorem, and extended through finite matrix coordinates with exactly the same norm. Extension by zero and the tracial commutation theorem then identify the resulting intertwiner as a right multiplier.

The finite-factor realization of the corner and its matrix coordinates remains explicit geometric data; the full-column conclusion is not an input.
