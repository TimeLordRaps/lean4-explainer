Von Neumann closure of actual operator fields

A common subsequence on countably many constant L² columns turns strong operator convergence into pointwise strong convergence almost everywhere. The uniform norm bound extends convergence from the dense columns to every fiber vector. Bicommutant closure then proves fiber membership.
