Central-decomposition data from actual projection fields

The construction uses the original measure space. On the null complement of the genuine unitized representations, the original algebra acts by zero and its unit range is the zero Hilbert space. Algebraic laws, norm formulas, von Neumann membership and countable contraction tests are all derived from the actual L² action identities.
