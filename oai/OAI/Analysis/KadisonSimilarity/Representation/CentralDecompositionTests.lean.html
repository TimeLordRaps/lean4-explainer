The countable tests in a central decomposition

Starting with representations of one norm-separable C-star subalgebra, their bicommutant-generation property, and almost-everywhere agreement with the ambient fiber representatives, the contraction tests are constructed. Only countably many agreements are used; pointwise fiber evaluation is not assumed to be a normal map on the von Neumann algebra.
