Separable reducing subspaces for finitely many tests

Take the closed complex span of the orbit of finitely many vectors under words in finitely many operators and their adjoints. The orbit is countable, so the resulting Hilbert subspace is separable. No representation estimate or separability assumption on the ambient space is used.
