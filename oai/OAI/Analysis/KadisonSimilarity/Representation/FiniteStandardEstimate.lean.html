Finite standard multiplicities from the centered free-corner test

This is a reduction theorem. Its centered-symmetry premise is an explicit local conclusion to be supplied by the free-corner argument, and is not a published-input predicate. Haar averaging, multiplicity padding, matrix GNS transport, symmetry dilation, and restoring the original operator are all performed by proved lemmas.
