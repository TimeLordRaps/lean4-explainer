Central decomposition and the uniform separable estimate

The direct-integral input records only representatives of decomposable operators, their almost-everywhere algebra operations, the scalar and matrix norm formulas, and countably many dense contraction sections. These are the specializations of Blackadar, III.1.6.1–4 and III.5.1.10–15 used here (with Kaplansky density, I.9.1.3, for the contraction tests). Representatives need satisfy algebraic identities only almost everywhere for each fixed pair.

The scalar fiber bounds, their common conull set, the factor estimates, and the final perturbation estimate are proved below.
