The spectral-step part of the hyperreflexivity deduction

SpectralStepApproximation isolates exactly the norm approximation by spectral projections that the manuscript uses. It is the step-function specialization of Blackadar, Operator Algebras (2006; revised manuscript February 8, 2017), I.6.1.3--4, with membership of spectral projections from I.9.2.1(iv). The scalar offset is essential: summation by parts gives a total variation of the remaining coefficients at most twice the operator norm.

The spectral approximation and the uniform commutator estimate together imply the hyperreflexivity bound.
