A separable C-star algebra generating a separably represented algebra

The complex linear span of the words in a countable family and its adjoints is separable. Its norm closure is therefore a separable C-star algebra. Applying this to the countable weakly dense contraction tests gives an actual norm-separable C-star subalgebra whose bicommutant is the original von Neumann algebra.
