Removing separability from the uniform commutator estimate

Each operator-norm test involves finitely many matrix entries and finitely many vectors. Their common reducing subspace is separable. We restrict the actual C-star algebra generated by those entries, use the two-vector Kaplansky specialization to reach its bicommutant, and recover the original vector test by the isometric inclusion.
