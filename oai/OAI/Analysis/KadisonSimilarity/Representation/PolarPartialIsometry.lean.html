Concrete right supports in a finite tracial von Neumann algebra

The partial isometry is constructed by extending the isometry sqrt(x* x) ξ ↦ x ξ from its dense range and setting it to zero on the orthogonal complement. Bicommutant closure keeps that operator in the algebra.
