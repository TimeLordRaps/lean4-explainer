A bounded rational transform for Kaplansky approximation

The function 2t/(1+t²) maps the real line into the unit interval. Its operator difference is controlled on two fixed vectors without a bound on the approximating selfadjoint operators. This is the bounded-transform step in Kaplansky approximation.
