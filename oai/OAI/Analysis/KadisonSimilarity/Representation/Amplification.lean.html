Matrix amplification and the main statements

Matrices act on finite Hilbert sums with their ℓ² norm. Consequently the norm in CommutatorEstimate is the concrete operator matrix norm, rather than the coordinatewise supremum norm of a matrix. No main estimate is assumed here.
