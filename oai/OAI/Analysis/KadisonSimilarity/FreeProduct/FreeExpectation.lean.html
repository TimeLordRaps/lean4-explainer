The expectation of a free compression

Scalar tracial freeness is expressed by vanishing alternating centered words. The formula E(e b e) = τ(e)^2 b is derived by testing against the second algebra, rather than included among the free-product input data.

      (htail : AlternatingCenteredWord τ P Q (!side) xs) :
