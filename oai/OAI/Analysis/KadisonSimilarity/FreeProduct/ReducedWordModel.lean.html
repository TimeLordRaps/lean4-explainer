The reduced-word part of the published free-product construction.

Ricard–Xu, J. reine angew. Math. 599 (2006), Section 1, pp. 2–3 of arXiv:math/0505302, constructs the reduced free-product Hilbert space as the orthogonal sum of alternating centered tensor words. Regrouping the first and last factors gives the coordinates below. The input is only this word construction, without any operator invariance or commutator estimate.
