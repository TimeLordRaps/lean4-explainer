Orthogonality of the actual dihedral word inclusions

Alternating-centered-word freeness computes the tracial pairing of the words e (s d)^k a. The algebraic calculation only needs e in the first free algebra; its projection and support properties enter when these pairings are used to construct the normalized Hilbert-space inclusions.
