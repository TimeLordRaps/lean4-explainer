source

The bounded Borel spectral calculus input, specialized to the centered arc of a Haar rotation in a finite dihedral von Neumann algebra. Blackadar, Operator Algebras, I.6.2.4 and I.6.2.8, supply the calculus and commutation; integration against its Haar spectral measure gives the stated moments.

The interface is local to the specified trace and algebra. Its intended application has the normal finite trace and the von Neumann algebra generated by the two symmetries. No existence assertion is made for an arbitrary C-star subalgebra or an arbitrary linear functional. The corner estimates below are proved from the spectral data, rather than included in the published input.
