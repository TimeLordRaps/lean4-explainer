Finite covering numbers and the cube packing bound

Centers are arbitrary ambient vectors throughout. Attainment and lower bounds explicitly assume a finite cover exists, because the natural-number infimum of the empty set is zero. Compactness and a neighborhood of zero supply that hypothesis in the geometric application.
