Contraction of symmetric forms

The coefficients of a contraction are obtained by summing the original coefficients against the fixed vector. Evaluation is literally evaluation of that original form with the vector in the first slot. The proof reindexes all ordered basis tuples, including repeated indices and the empty remaining tuple.
