Actual polynomials of symmetric forms

The definitions sum over ordered basis tuples, exactly as the multilinear form evaluation does. The diagonal coefficient of a basis multiset is its ordered-tuple fiber cardinality times the value of the form. This coefficient is nonzero in characteristic greater than the degree. Renaming every vector block to the same coordinate proves nonvanishing for every index tuple, including tuples with repeated indices.
