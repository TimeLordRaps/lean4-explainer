Counting and avoiding all disjoint zero-tuple witnesses

All ordered tuples are retained, including tuples with repeated indices. The probability product estimate is applied only to lists whose distinct tuple supports are pairwise disjoint. Their number is bounded by the exact number of all ordered lists, u ^ (h * w).
