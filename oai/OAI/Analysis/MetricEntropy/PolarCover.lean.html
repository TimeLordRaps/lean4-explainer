Polar covers from arbitrary uniform approximation lists

The functions on the approximation list need not be column combinations. For every nonempty coefficient fiber we choose an actual vector in the coefficient ball; empty fibers receive zero. The resulting list has exactly the original number of slots, with no factor depending on the dimension.
