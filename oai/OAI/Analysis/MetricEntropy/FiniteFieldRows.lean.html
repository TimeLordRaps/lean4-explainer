The finite-field graph matrix

The rows are literal symmetric forms, the labels are their actual contractions, and every admissible set is included as a column index. The only input to the separation theorem is the repeated-tuple nonvanishing conclusion constructed by the robust-directions argument; graph separation is proved from it.
