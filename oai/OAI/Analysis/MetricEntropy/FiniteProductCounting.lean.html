Counting events on disjoint sets of coordinates

The sample space is the actual finite function space U → E, with uniform probability given by cardinality divided by the cardinality of that space. A coordinate-swap bijection proves independence of two events from their literal disjoint supports. Finite intersections then follow by induction. No independence hypothesis or distinctness within an individual tuple is used.
