Combining graph-ball levels using the actual logarithmic profile

The profile expansion and dilation estimate are applied to each actual extended graph distance. The finite sums below retain disconnected distances, with no extra finite-distance hypothesis. The final theorem combines the two losses from column assignment and the one loss from rounding.
