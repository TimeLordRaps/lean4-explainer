Finite codes for downward rounding of grouped weights

For a positive mesh δ, a weight in [0, 1] is rounded down to δ * floor (w / δ). Its integer coefficient is an actual element of Fin (floor (1 / δ) + 1), including the possible endpoint w = 1. The error estimates apply to every indicator with values in [0, 1].
