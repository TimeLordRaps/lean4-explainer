The coordinate obstruction and rectifiability of every fiber

The ambient algebra automorphisms in this file are arbitrary complex algebra automorphisms. In particular, there is no restriction to a chosen subgroup or triangular form. The scalar-image characterization keeps the nonzero scalar which occurs when generators of two equal principal ideals are compared.
