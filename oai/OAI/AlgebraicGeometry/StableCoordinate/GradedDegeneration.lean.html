The actual integer grading of the degeneration ring

The quotient G = k[p,s,u,F,J]/(Hstar) carries the grading with weights (-1,0,0,1,1). This file constructs that grading, rather than assuming it, and records the degree-zero quadric coordinates used in the Laurent picture. It does not identify G with the associated graded ring of A.
