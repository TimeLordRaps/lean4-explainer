Enlarging the coefficient ring of a derivation

For the two polynomial charts in Section 5, a derivation annihilating the coefficient ring can be regarded as linear over that ring. The second lemma shows that annihilation of the original coefficient algebra implies annihilation of its localization, so this upgrade introduces no new condition.
