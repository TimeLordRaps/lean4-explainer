Nonzero fibers of the polynomial hypersurface presentation

Source: sources/build/source/sections/02-construction.tex, especially con:quadric-identity, con:localized-inverse, and prop:fibers.

The variables of P5 K are ordered p,s,u,F,J. This file proves the explicit algebra equivalence

K[p,s,u,F,J] / (H, p-l) ≃ₐ[K] K[x,y,z] for every nonzero l : K.

It uses actual quotient algebras and mutually inverse algebra homomorphisms, not a pointwise bijection. The identities work over any commutative ring with an inverse to p; the quotient construction is stated over any field. The transport from the presentation to the exact four-variable polynomial is supplied separately by the polynomial-presentation equivalence.
