The two actual integer gradings on the determinant hypersurface

The degrees are exactly the two rows in source equation rig:gradings. Both gradings are constructed on C = k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1) by quotienting weighted polynomial gradings. In particular, negative auxiliary and fiber degrees are retained.
