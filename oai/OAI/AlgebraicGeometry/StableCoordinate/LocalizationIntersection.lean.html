The two-localization intersection used in the rigidity argument

This module supplies the denominator-clearing step in Section 5 of the source: for relatively prime elements a and d in a unique factorization domain, the localizations away from a and d intersect in the original ring inside its fraction field. Relative primeness here means that every common divisor is a unit; it does not incorrectly assume that the ideal (a,d) is the unit ideal.
