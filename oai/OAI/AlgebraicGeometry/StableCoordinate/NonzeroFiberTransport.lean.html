This module connects the simultaneous-equation calculation in NonzeroFiber to the manuscript's successive quotient A/(p-l), then transports it through any proved polynomial-presentation equivalence. The eventual exact-polynomial endpoint supplies the presentation equivalence; these generic transport lemmas are not substituted for that endpoint.
