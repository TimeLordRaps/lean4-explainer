Locally nilpotent derivations in one polynomial variable

This file supplies the one-variable derivation step in the proof of Proposition prop:rigidity, §5 of the source manuscript. Over an integral domain of characteristic zero, a locally nilpotent derivation of K[X] maps X to a constant. Local nilpotence is stated explicitly as the vanishing of a sufficiently high iterate on every polynomial.
