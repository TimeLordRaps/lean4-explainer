Actual determinant-quotient maps from frames

A determinant frame in a commutative k-algebra, together with an image of u, gives an actual algebra homomorphism from the exact source ring k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1). This is the quotient-map construction needed when a normalized polynomial frame is turned into a lifted additive action.
