Algebra of the two charts of the determinant bundle

This module formalizes the quadric-to-determinant-ring map in Section 4 of the source. All variables are independent before taking the displayed principal-ideal quotient. The chosen sections over D(z+1) and D(z) prove the base map injective without assuming that the quadric ring is a domain.
