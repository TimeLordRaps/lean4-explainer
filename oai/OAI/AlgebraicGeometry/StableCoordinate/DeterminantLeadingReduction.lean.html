Leading locally nilpotent reduction on the actual determinant ring

This is the auxiliary-grading step in the source's rigidity argument. The input is a nonzero locally nilpotent derivation on the concrete determinant quotient preserving fiber weights and killing the exact obstruction v. The output is a nonzero locally nilpotent derivation with the same fiber weight property fixing a, d, and u. All finite generation, grading, domain, characteristic, and nonvanishing premises are discharged for this quotient; none is added to the conclusion as an unproved interface.
