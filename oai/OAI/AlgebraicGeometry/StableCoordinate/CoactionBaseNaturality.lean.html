Coassociativity on the image of an equivariant base algebra

An arbitrary polynomial family extending a genuine additive action on a base algebra already satisfies the action law on that base image. This lemma is used before the determinant-frame uniqueness argument: it supplies equality of the three base products of the two candidate composite frames.
