Complex zero loci and ideal transport

This module supplies the geometric interface needed when an ambient polynomial isomorphism is described on complex points. Polynomial substitutions induce mutually inverse polynomial maps, not arbitrary set-theoretic bijections. Hilbert's Nullstellensatz then recovers radical defining ideals from their complex zero loci. No conclusion about the manuscript's polynomial is assumed.
