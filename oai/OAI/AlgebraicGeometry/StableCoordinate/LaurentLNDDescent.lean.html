Homogeneous locally nilpotent derivations on a Laurent polynomial ring

The degree convention in the manuscript is deg τ = -1. Thus a derivation of degree m maps constants to Laurent monomials with exponent -m. We use the exponent q directly, so the source application takes q = -m.
