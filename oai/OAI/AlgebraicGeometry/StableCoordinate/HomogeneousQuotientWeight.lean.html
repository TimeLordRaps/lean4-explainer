An integral weight coaction on a homogeneous principal quotient

This construction supplies the fiber-weight action for the actual presentation k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1). It works for an arbitrary weighted-homogeneous principal relation and uses genuine quotient algebra homomorphisms. Its hypotheses are construction data, not assumptions added to the principal package.
