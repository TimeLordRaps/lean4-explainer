Constructing the normalized polynomial lift on the determinant ring

For every actual polynomial additive action on the exact quadric ring B, this file constructs a polynomial family of algebra maps on the exact C = k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1). Its restriction is the given action on B, and its parameter-zero specialization is the identity on all of C.

Existence uses the proved UFD structure on C[T], the concrete gcd frame factorization, and normalization by a constant comparison unit. No line bundle, arbitrary frame, or lift-existence premise is assumed. The additive group law for this normalized family is proved separately by frame uniqueness.
