Polynomial presentation, with arbitrary additional independent variables

The source variables in P n are p,s,u,F,J, in that order, followed by n independent variables. The matrix transformation leaves those extra variables fixed. In particular n=0 gives the unstabilized presentation and n=1 supplies the presentation needed in the one-variable stabilization.
