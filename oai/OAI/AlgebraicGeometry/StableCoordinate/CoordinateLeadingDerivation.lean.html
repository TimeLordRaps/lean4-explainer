A coordinate system produces a leading locally nilpotent derivation

This is the first half of the source's coordinate obstruction, through the construction of a nonzero homogeneous locally nilpotent derivation on the actual associated graded ring. Its distinguished invariant has negative degree; its second invariant has strictly positive degree and is nonzero. The derivation and its shift are constructed, not postulated.
