Picard triviality for the factorial affine charts

Algebraic support for bun:homotopy in §4 of the source. Over a GCD domain, all invertible modules are trivial; in particular this applies to the UFD localizations in the two charts and their polynomial extensions.

The statements below concern invertible modules, not just bijections of underlying sets. Invertible modules correspond to line bundles on an affine scheme.
