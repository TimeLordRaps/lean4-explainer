Differentiating polynomial additive actions

This file supplies the algebraic additive-action to locally nilpotent derivation interface used when the line-bundle lift is restricted to its punctured total space. The action law is an equality of bivariate polynomials, not a merely pointwise identity. No reducedness or characteristic-zero hypothesis is needed for the forward implication.
