The four-variable complex polynomial

Variables are zero-indexed internally: X 0, X 1, X 2, X 3 mean the paper's x₁, x₂, x₃, x₄. The stabilization uses Fin.castSucc, so its fifth independent variable is X 4; no subring-preservation condition is imposed on its automorphism.

Affine fibers are encoded by their complex coordinate algebras, not by sets. An algebra equivalence to the three-variable polynomial algebra is precisely the algebraic affine-space conclusion. Rectifiability is encoded by the ambient algebra automorphism carrying the defining ideal to (X 0).
