Weighted polynomial evaluation in a Laurent polynomial algebra

The source convention has degree T = -1. Consequently a variable of integer weight w i is evaluated as a constant times T (-w i). This module supplies the graded compatibility needed when extending the leading derivation to the source's Laurent localization.
