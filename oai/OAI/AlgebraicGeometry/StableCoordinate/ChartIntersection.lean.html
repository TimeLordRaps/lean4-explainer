The coefficient-ring step in the two-chart rigidity argument

A polynomial chart over a principal localization detects coefficients which belong to the fraction field of the coefficient ring. This module proves the step by denominator clearing. In particular, it does not require choosing a common overfield or treating a set-theoretic chart as an algebraic chart.

The intended application is R = k[a,d,u] and A = k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1). The two actual injective algebra maps to R[a⁻¹][b] and R[d⁻¹][c] are supplied by the determinant-ring charts.
