The differentiated lifted action preserves the actual fiber grading

This is the last interface in the manuscript's Proposition prop:lift. Once a normalized frame lift supplies the five coordinate coefficient laws, this file proves equivariance of its entire algebra homomorphism and hence preservation of every actual fiber-weight submodule after differentiation. The five identities are consumed as construction outputs, not as additional assumptions of the principal target; existence of the lifted action is separate.
