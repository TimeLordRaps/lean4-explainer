Homogeneous transport from the actual Rees algebra to the degeneration

The concrete presentation equivalence preserves every integer degree in both directions. This is proved from homogeneous polynomial representatives and the exact lower-step criterion for initial forms. Consequently actual leading derivations, including their nonzero positive-degree invariants, transport to the canonical degeneration ring without any additional grading assumptions.
