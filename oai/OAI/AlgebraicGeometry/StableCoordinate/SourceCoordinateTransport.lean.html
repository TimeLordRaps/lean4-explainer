Literal-polynomial coordinate systems in the canonical source algebra

The source presentation carries exactly aX 0 to the prescribed polynomial f. This reverses the presentation equivalence so a hypothetical coordinate automorphism of the literal four-variable polynomial gives precisely the coordinate-system direction used by the leading-derivation construction. No ambient automorphism is restricted or excluded.
