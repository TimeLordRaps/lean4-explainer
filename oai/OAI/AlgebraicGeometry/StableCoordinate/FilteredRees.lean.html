The associated graded algebra via the extended Rees algebra

For an increasing integer filtration F, the Rees algebra consists of Laurent polynomials whose coefficient at d belongs to F d. Its quotient by T is the associated graded algebra. This construction supplies an actual ring, including negative filtration indices, without treating the associated-graded algebra or its multiplicative structure as an assumed interface.
