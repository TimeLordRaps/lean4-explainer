Rigidity helpers on the actual determinant quotient

These results instantiate the general derivation arguments on the exact ring k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1). Generator nonvanishing is proved by an algebraic point, not assumed or inferred from an unproved localization identification.
