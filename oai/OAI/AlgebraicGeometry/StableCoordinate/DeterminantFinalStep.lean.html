The final elementary step of determinant rigidity

A derivation which fixes the three polynomial base generators is determined by a single coefficient. Preservation of the actual fiber grading makes that coefficient have weight -2. If it lies in the polynomial base, whose weights are nonnegative, it vanishes; so does the derivation. The coefficient's polynomiality is supplied separately by the locally nilpotent chart argument.
