Fiber-weight compatibility of normalized lifts on the determinant quotient

This specializes normalized-frame uniqueness to the actual source rings and the actual Laurent fiber coaction. A normalized polynomial lift extending a base polynomial family has the exact fiber weights coefficient by coefficient; no fiber-linearity or equivariance is assumed of the chosen lift.
