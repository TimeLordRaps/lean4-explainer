Vertical locally nilpotent derivations of the determinant ring

The vertical-coefficient step is the assertion in Section 5 that a locally nilpotent derivation fixing a,d,u has coefficient in k[a,d,u]. The coefficient is the actual element c * D b - b * D c of the determinant-one quotient, not an assumed polynomial representative.
