Homogeneous pieces of an affine modification

For an ideal I of R, this file identifies the Laurent subalgebra R[T, I/T] with the Laurent polynomials whose coefficient at exponent n belongs to I ^ max (-n) 0. The grading in the source is opposite to the Laurent exponent, so its positive degree-ℓ piece is T^(-ℓ) I^ℓ.
