Fiber weights on the actual determinant quotient

The Laurent coaction is induced by the exact weights (1,1,-1,-1,0) on a,d,b,c,u. We establish the final weight contradiction in the actual quotient ring, not just in an abstract polynomial algebra: an element in the image of k[a,d,u] with negative fiber weight is zero.
