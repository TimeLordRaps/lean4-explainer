Derivations obtained from polynomial coordinate systems

The first step of §3 of the manuscript chooses a partial derivative in a coordinate distinct from the distinguished coordinate and a positive-degree invariant. This file supplies the algebraic part of that choice: partial derivatives of multivariate polynomials are locally nilpotent, and this property, nonvanishing, and the values on coordinates transport through an actual algebra equivalence.
