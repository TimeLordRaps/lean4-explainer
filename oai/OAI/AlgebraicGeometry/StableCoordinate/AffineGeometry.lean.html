Scheme-theoretic meaning of the coordinate-ring formulation

An algebra equivalence with a polynomial ring yields an isomorphism to affine space over the coefficient field. The quotient presentation also supplies a closed immersion, so neither assertion is encoded by a bare bijection of points.
