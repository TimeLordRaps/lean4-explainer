Constants in polynomial charts

Section 5 identifies two localizations of the determinant ring with polynomial rings over principal localizations of k[a,d,u]. The needed intersection with k(a,d,u) is not a set-theoretic assertion: a polynomial over the chart's coefficient ring which becomes constant over the fraction field has that constant in its coefficient ring. The lemmas here prove that exact algebraic interface without assuming the manuscript's chart identifications.
