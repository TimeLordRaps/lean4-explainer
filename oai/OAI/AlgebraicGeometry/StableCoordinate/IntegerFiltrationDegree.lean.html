Integer filtration degree and initial forms

Section 3 uses an exhaustive separated increasing filtration indexed by all integers, including negative integers. These generic lemmas prove that every nonzero element has a least layer and that its image modulo the preceding layer is nonzero. They do not assume any coordinate obstruction or associated-graded presentation.
