Normalizing a polynomial determinant frame

A choice of trivialization gives a frame above the desired quadric base, but its specialization need not be the prescribed initial frame. A constant unit rescaling corrects it. This construction needs no domain or reducedness hypothesis; those hypotheses enter only the separate uniqueness theorem.
