A maximal degree shift detected on finitely many algebra generators

For an integer-graded algebra, the values of a derivation on finitely many generators have only finitely many homogeneous components. Thus a nonzero derivation has a maximal degree shift which is attained on a generator. These lemmas supply the finite-support step in the leading-derivation construction of Sections 3 and 5 of the supplied manuscript.
