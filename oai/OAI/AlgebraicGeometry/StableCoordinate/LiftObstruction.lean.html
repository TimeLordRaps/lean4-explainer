The actual weight-preserving locally nilpotent lift

This file completes source Proposition prop:lift for the exact rings B and C. There is no assumed action lift or line-bundle trivialization: the genuine UFD structure of C[T] gives a determinant frame, its constant-unit normalization gives a polynomial family, and normalized-frame uniqueness proves the additive group law. A second use of uniqueness proves fiber-scaling equivariance. Differentiation therefore gives a locally nilpotent derivation that extends the given one and preserves the actual constructed fiber grading.

Finally, injectivity of B → C, the identity IC = (v), and factorial closure of an LND kernel transport a nonzero invariant in I to the condition D(v)=0. All intermediate existence and equivariance interfaces are discharged here.
