Rigidity of the weighted determinant hypersurface

This is Proposition prop:rigidity of Section 5 of the paper, on the actual quotient k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1), with the two actual quotient gradings and the exact element v = a^3*b + a^2*u^2 - d^2.

All intermediate existence and regularity claims are proved: the leading locally nilpotent component, factorial closure, the two localized polynomial charts, the two-denominator intersection, and the negative-weight obstruction. There is no domain, chart, polynomiality, or auxiliary-grading hypothesis in the final statement.
