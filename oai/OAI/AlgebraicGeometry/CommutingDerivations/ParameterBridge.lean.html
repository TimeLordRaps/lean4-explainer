Explicit relative polynomial coordinates with an invertible parameter.

The coefficient ring is arbitrary, including the zero ring. In the localization application it is the Laurent coefficient ring ℂ[c,c⁻¹]. The relative presentation is explicitly isomorphic to a polynomial algebra in three variables.
