Initial exponents of algebraic power series

The algebraic relation bounds initial exponents uniformly in the weights. Generic positive perturbations extend this bound to least faces of nonnegative weights, as used in the logarithmic jet argument of Uniform Cartier sections for Fano-type contractions.
