Polynomial identities for the cusp and the explicit hypersurface

The polynomials S and P give the identity (x + s³)² + (y - s²)³ = s * (1 + F). The Bézout certificate is valid over every commutative ring. In the ambient complex polynomial ring, the variables are ordered as h, u, v, w.
