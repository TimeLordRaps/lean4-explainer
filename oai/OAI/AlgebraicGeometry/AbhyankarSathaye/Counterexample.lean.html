A polynomial hypersurface with a non-coordinate equation

The explicit polynomial F has zero fiber isomorphic to affine three-space, but has a critical point on the fiber of value -1, obstructing its being a polynomial coordinate. Both conclusions persist after adjoining any number of unused variables.
