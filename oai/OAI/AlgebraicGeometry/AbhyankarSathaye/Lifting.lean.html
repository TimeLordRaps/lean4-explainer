Lifting a cusp relation

If x² + y³ = h * s and α * h + β * y = 1, the quotient defined by U³ + h * V = x, -U² + h * W = y, and S h U V W = s is a polynomial algebra in one variable. This includes the zero ring and rings with zero divisors.
