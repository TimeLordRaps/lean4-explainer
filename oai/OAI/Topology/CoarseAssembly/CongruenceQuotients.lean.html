The actual congruence quotients and diagonal holonomies

The congruence quotient is SL₃(ℤ/2^kℤ), including the singleton quotient for k = 0. Its diagonal matrices have entries t,t⁻¹,1, and the finite-level matrices are obtained by the genuine 2-adic reduction maps.
