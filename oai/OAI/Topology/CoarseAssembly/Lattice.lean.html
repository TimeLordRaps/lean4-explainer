The triangular-lattice discs

The vertex set, adjacency, radius and depth used in §3 of the coarse Novikov manuscript. The three integer linear forms are precisely those in its hexagonal radius max {|a|, |b|, |a+b|}. These definitions do not assert the unproved identification of the filled geometric realization with a topological disc.
