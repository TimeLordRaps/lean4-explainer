A combinatorial ray charge and the half-plane argument

We use a half-open cut along the positive horizontal ray: an edge entering height zero from below has charge one exactly when its endpoint has positive first coordinate. This is a combinatorial convention at ray vertices; edges incident to the origin have charge zero. Its restriction to any of the six open lattice half-planes is an exact difference of vertex potentials.
