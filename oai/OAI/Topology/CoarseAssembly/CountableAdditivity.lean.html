Countable additivity in the first KK variable

The coordinate maps below are the proved extension-by-zero restrictions on actual Fredholm-module groups. The sole published input is their bijectivity: Blackadar, K-Theory for Operator Algebras, 2nd ed. (1998), Theorem 19.7.1, for a countable disjoint union of compact metrizable spaces. Construction of a family class and vanishing from vanishing coordinates are consequences, not assumed inputs.
