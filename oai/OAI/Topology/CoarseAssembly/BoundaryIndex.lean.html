The boundary index diagram

The algebraic final calculation in §6 of the coarse Novikov manuscript. This is a diagram lemma, not a definition or construction of coarse assembly. The two naturality equations are the precise consequences of Willett--Yu, Higher Index Theory, §§5.1, 6.7, 7.1--7.2 used in that calculation. The geometric filling and operator factorization remain separate obligations.
