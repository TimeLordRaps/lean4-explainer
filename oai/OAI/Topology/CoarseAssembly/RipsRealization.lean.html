Barycentric Rips realization and proper coarsening

Points are actual finitely supported probability weights with support of bounded diameter. The topology is induced by the barycentric coordinates. For a locally finite Rips complex this is its usual simplicial topology. Increasing-scale inclusions are proved to be closed embeddings and therefore proper, providing the continuous coarsening maps for analytic K-homology.
