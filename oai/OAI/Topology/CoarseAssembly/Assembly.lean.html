Compatible analytic indices and their coarsening limit

The scale-index homomorphisms and their compatibility are the precise published input from Willett--Yu, Higher Index Theory, §§5.1, 6.7, 7.1--7.2 (with errata 30--31). Their source is the Fredholm-module group of the actual C₀ algebra; their target is the topological K₁ of the actual nonunital algebra. The induced coarse map is constructed here by the universal property of the direct limit. No geometric detection, filling or transfer conclusion is part of this input.
