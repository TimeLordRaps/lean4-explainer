A uniform degree bound for the finite fibered graphs

A neighbor is encoded by its base vertex, one integral left move, and nine binary digits. The digits distinguish children in a successive congruence fiber; at equal or smaller depth the reduced equation already determines the target. The resulting bound is independent of the disc radius.
