The circle map of an integral Rips cocycle

On each finite component, barycentric interpolation of an integral edge cocycle defines a genuine continuous map to ℝ / ℤ. The local real lifts differ by the integral edge charge. This constructs the winding test in the manuscript, rather than assuming its topological extension.
