The group calculation for a charged closed walk

In §3, left Cayley moves commute with the right holonomy. Recording a step as (s,m) means z ↦ s * z * g^m; a pure Cayley step has m=0, and a pure base step has s=1. The accumulated endpoint formula and conjugacy obstruction are expressed using an actual finite list of steps.
