Short loops and the Rips edge cocycle

The combinatorial step in §4 of the coarse Novikov manuscript. The only hypothesis on the charge is vanishing on short closed graph walks, exactly the output required from the manuscript's small-loop lemma. This is a conditional combinatorial result, not an assumption about analytic K-homology.
