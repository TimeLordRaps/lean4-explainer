The precise published circle-index input

Only the scalar-unitary index pairing, its naturality for a parametrized loop, and the classical winding formula are assumed. The domain groups are the Fredholm-module groups.

References: Blackadar, K-Theory for Operator Algebras, 2nd ed. (1998), Proposition 18.7.1 and Corollary 18.10.3; Khalkhali, Basic Noncommutative Geometry (2009), Example 4.2.4, with the sign convention of manuscript §2.3.
