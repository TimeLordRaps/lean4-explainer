Boundary classes in the closed Roe multiplier algebra

A controlled Rips operator is a Roe multiplier even when it is not pseudolocal for the discrete vertex representation. The ideal-boundary construction is therefore performed in this actual closed multiplier algebra before constructing the coarse assembly map.
