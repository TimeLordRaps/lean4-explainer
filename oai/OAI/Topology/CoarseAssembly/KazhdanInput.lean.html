The fixed published spectral-gap input

The elementary matrices below and their inverses give a fixed finite symmetric generating set of SL3(Z), with the identity included. The input used in the conditional development is exactly the uniform lazy-averaging estimate on the finite congruence quotients, with the standard ample coefficient space. It is the specialization of Bekka–de la Harpe–Valette (2008), Proposition 1.3.2 and Example 1.7.4, explained in manuscript §5.2.

No proof of that published input is asserted during the conditional phase. The spatial Roe transfer itself is proved from it below.
