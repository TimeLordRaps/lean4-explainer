Cutting down arbitrary cycles over the unital coefficient algebras

The compact-source algebras used by the circle pairing are unital, although the Fredholm representations themselves may be nonunital. Restriction to scalar coefficients gives precisely the local estimates needed for the represented-subspace cut-down.
