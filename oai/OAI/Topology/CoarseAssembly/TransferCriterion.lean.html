The finite-fiber Roe transfer criterion

This proves the operator statement of Proposition 5.1. The required finite-propagation scalar contractions between fibers are amplified here; the bounded lift, compactness and compression are constructed, not assumed. The concrete graph family still has to supply these fiber contractions and the averaging approximants.
