Uniform block operators on Hilbert sums

The averaging operators used in the transfer act separately on finite fibers. A uniform norm bound, rather than finiteness of the set of fibers, makes their direct sum a bounded operator. The resulting operator admits the uniform approximation needed for the fiber projection.
