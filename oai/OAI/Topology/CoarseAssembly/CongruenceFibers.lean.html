The uniform bound on successive congruence fibers

A residue modulo 2^(k+1) is determined by its residue modulo 2^k and one binary digit. Applying this to the nine matrix entries bounds every fiber of the special-linear reduction by 2^9, including k=0. No surjectivity or formula for the order of the group is needed.
