The odd Fredholm-module group

The group is formed from actual analytic cycles, modulo degeneracy, unitary equivalence, orthogonal direct sum and norm homotopy. Pullback of star representations descends to this quotient. This gives the analytic domain needed for the subsequent geometric construction; no assembly or nonzero class is postulated here.
