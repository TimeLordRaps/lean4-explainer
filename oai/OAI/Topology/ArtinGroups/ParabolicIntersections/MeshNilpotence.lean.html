The published finite-Dynkin path input

MeshNilpotent is the positive-degree nilpotence assertion for the preprojective (mesh) algebra of an induced finite Dynkin graph. Its relations are displayed explicitly, rather than asserted for a complex. The standard finite-dimensionality input is Amiot, On the structure of triangulated categories with finitely many indecomposables, Bull. Soc. Math. France 135 (2007), Proposition 9.1; the essential-path formulation is Coquereaux (2002), §§3.1–3.2, and Cooper (2007), Theorem 5.4.3 and Proposition 5.6.10. On a bipartite Dynkin graph the usual signed mesh relations have the same zero sets as the displayed all-plus relations.

This module proves evaluation of the abstract relations. It does not assume their vanishing for coefficient maps of the target paper's complexes, nor assume any stabilizer or bounded-height conclusion.
