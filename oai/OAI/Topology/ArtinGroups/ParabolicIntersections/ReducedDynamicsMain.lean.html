The remaining published geometric inputs

The Artin-specific edge-intersection argument of CMMW Theorem 3.2 is now proved locally. The following statements isolate the still-undischarged inputs: the general amalgam criterion (Minasyan--Osin, Acylindrical hyperbolicity of groups acting on trees, 2015, Corollary 2.2; stated as CMMW 2026, Theorem 3.1), and CMMW 2026, Proposition 4.2.

The first is stated for an arbitrary group explicitly isomorphic to a nontrivial amalgam. The second transfers weak malnormality from the edge group to all proper parabolics. Neither assumes the parabolic intersection theorem or its new argument. The local proofs supply the actual amalgam, injectivity, proper inclusions, exclusion of virtually cyclic groups, and weak malnormality of the edge group.
