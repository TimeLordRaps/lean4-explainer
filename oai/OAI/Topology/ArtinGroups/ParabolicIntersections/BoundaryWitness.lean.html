A boundary witness below a capped isolated layer

The relative frame need not be adjacent to the isolated set. Connected support finds an occupied exterior boundary color. A zero boundary cap then puts that occupation strictly below the isolated layer. This is the additional boundary argument of §5 of paper 082-03.
