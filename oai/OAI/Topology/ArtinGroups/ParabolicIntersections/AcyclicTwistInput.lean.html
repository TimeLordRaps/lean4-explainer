The published acyclic-evaluation input

Seidel--Thomas, Braid group actions on derived categories of coherent sheaves, Duke Math. J. 108 (2001), Definition 2.5 (equation (2.2)), and the first sentence of the proof of Proposition 2.12: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0001043, pp. 13 and 17. A twist is the cone of evaluation. If the entire evaluated Hom complex is acyclic, this cone is isomorphic to the original object. For bounded projective complexes the resulting isomorphism is a homotopy equivalence. Below is precisely this standard consequence in the existing vertex-test notation. It is independent of the Artin stabilizer argument.
