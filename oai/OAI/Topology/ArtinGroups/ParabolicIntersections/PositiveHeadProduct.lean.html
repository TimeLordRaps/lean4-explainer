The greedy head of a product with a simple left factor.

  1. theorem OAI.HarmonicArtin.ParabolicIntersections.positive_head_product line 15

    If h is the greatest simple prefix of b, the greatest simple prefix of a*b is a*g, where g is the gcd of the complement of a and h. This is the local normal-form step used in folding.
