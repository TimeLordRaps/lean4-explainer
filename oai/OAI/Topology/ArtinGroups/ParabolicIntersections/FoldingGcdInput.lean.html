source

Published gcd input for an admissible folding

Castella, Admissible submonoids of Artin--Tits monoids (2008), Proposition 41(2), states that an admissible folding preserves left and right gcds. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0804.0704, p. 15. The field below is exactly its common-left-atom consequence: if two expanded positive words have a common left atom, their original words have a common left atom. The folding here is the color partition. Its required Coxeter admissibility is already established by unfoldedHom_right_ascent (constant ascent sign on every color).

The field involves positive words and braid relations only. It contains no tests, family action, height, stabilizer, or intersection assertion.
