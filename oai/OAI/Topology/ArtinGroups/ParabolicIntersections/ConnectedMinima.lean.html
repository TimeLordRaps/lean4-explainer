Lowering paths across tied heights

This is the finite-path argument of §6.5 of paper 082-03. The local hypotheses are precisely the connected descending neighborhoods and the compatibility across tied adjacent vertices. They are not installed as published assumptions; their verification for the relative harmonic height is a separate part of that paper's proof.
