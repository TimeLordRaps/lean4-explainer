A CH obstruction to a prescribed categoricity threshold

Under CH, a countable relational language admits an abstract elementary class with least Löwenheim–Skolem number aleph0, two nonisomorphic models of size beth (omega 2) = hanf aleph0, and categoricity, including existence, at every cardinal at least tailThreshold. Distinct base cardinalities separate the endpoint models, while finite-support row and column translations establish the included categoricity tail.
