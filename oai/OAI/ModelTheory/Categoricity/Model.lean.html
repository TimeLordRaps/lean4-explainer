Set-sized relational structures and categoricity thresholds

Carriers are arbitrary ZFSets, rather than subsets of one fixed set. Strong substructures are induced relational substructures. The abstract elementary class axioms include isomorphism invariance, coherence, nonzero ordinal unions and smoothness over a common upper bound. The Löwenheim–Skolem bound includes the cardinality of the language, and categoricity includes existence.

The Hanf index is the initial ordinal of a cardinal successor. The lower endpoint of the categoricity tail is included.
