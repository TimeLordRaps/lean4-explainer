Augmented directed complexes and their globular cells

An augmented directed complex consists of chain groups, differentials, an augmentation and positive submonoids. A globular cell is a finite table of positive chains, with the boundary and augmentation equations in Steiner's construction nu. Its faces are truncations of this table.
