The Kan–Quillen boundary-lifting implication

Goerss–Jardine (1999), Theorems I.11.3–I.11.4 and Corollary II.8.6 imply that a Kan fibration which is a weak equivalence of actual realizations lifts against each boundary. The implication identifies acyclic Kan fibrations with the boundary right-lifting class.
