The main theorem with concrete chain, fibre and finite-reflection inputs

Normalized chains construct the orientals, their ordinal maps and the interval diagonal. Contractions and two-sieve induction show that the finite-poset adjunction units are weak equivalences. Finite reflection commutes with zero fibres; simplexwise clamping proves poset-sieve preservation. Strong deformations and algebraic boundary factorization give simplicial gluing. A semantic quotient of finite formal cells constructs small strict colimits.
