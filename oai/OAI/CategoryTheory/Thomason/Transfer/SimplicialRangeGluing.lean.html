Couniversality for finite posets in omega dimension

The induction is over the ambient finite poset. The greatest-new case uses the constructed stationary Gray prisms; otherwise two maximal deletions and their intersection give three smaller arbitrary attachments. Their actual full-fibre cover and the simplicial gluing bridge complete the step.
