Fibers of actual labelled pushouts

The published input is Ara--Maltsiniotis (2014), Lemma 2.8, arXiv:1305.5086v3, pp.14--15: either endpoint-fiber functor on the slice over the walking arrow has a right adjoint. The consequence used is preservation of small colimits by the concrete fiber functors. The same statement applies in every positive finite dimension and in omega dimension.
