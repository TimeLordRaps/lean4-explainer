Exact Kan lifting with all extended faces prescribed

Inside the extension of a subdivided simplex, take the union of its original image and the extensions of any finite collection of faces. Its inclusion is anodyne. This statement concerns the actual union of extended faces, and does not identify it with extension of the boundary.
