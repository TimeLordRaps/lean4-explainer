Finite induction for the actual adjunction-unit comparison

Empty and greatest-element posets are the base cases. Every other finite poset is covered by deleting two distinct maximal elements. The published simplicial gluing lemma applies to the natural transformation on the two actual nerve squares. All hypotheses below are proved for the constructed Street adjunctions in the following specialization.
