From a strict Gray cylinder to a homotopy of Street nerves

The simplicial map is constructed on actual oriental-valued simplices using the explicit interval Alexander--Whitney map. Naturality includes all simplex operators, hence also degeneracies. No nerve-homotopy assertion from the new manuscript is assumed.
