Exact intersection of the realized cylinder pieces

The two globe pieces have retractions which collapse the opposite piece to the common face. These identities make their intersection square a pullback after every functor, in particular after the concrete right-chain cylinder and realization. Generation of the full realized cylinder is a separate question.
