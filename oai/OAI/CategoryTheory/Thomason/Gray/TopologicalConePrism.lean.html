Gray deformation retractions induce actual Thomason weak equivalences

This connects the constructed strict cylinders, their Street-nerve homotopies, and the actual realization weak equivalences. Its classical inputs are only the specified normalized chains, right Gray comparison, Alexander--Whitney identities and Kan--Quillen WFS. No strict-category model structure is assumed.
