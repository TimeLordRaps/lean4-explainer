Finite-dimensional cells in the globe mapping family

For a functor on strict categories, maps from the images of the globes form a reflexive globular family. Identity extensions are injective, and their filtered union has actual finite-dimensional cells and globular faces. This is the underlying cell construction for the right interval internal hom; composition is constructed separately from cylinder gluing.
