The finite coefficient interface needed for ordered atom generation

This interface is shared by the normalized simplex complexes and the finite pasted-globe cylinder complexes. It records concrete basis coordinates, actual positive cells, and their disjoint ordered boundaries; generation is a theorem proved from these data, rather than a field of the interface.
