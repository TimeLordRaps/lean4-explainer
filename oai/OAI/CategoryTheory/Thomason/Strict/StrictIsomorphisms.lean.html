The cosimplicial orientals

These are the strict realizations of the explicit normalized simplex complexes. The only hypotheses are the two classical normalized-chain identities specified below (Steiner, Orientals, 2007, Section 3 and Section 4 before Theorem 4.1). Functoriality, including maps with degenerate images, is proved here.
