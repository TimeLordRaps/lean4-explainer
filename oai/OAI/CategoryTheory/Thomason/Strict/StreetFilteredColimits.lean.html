Filtered colimits for the actual Street nerve and Thomason weak class

Every degree of the Street nerve is represented by the corresponding finite oriental. Thus the actual nerve and twice-extension nerve preserve filtered colimits, in omega dimension and all finite dimensions. The realization weak-equivalence class is consequently closed under these colimits.
