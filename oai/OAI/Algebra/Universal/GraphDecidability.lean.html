Decidability of fixed-size graph witnesses

Connectivity is characterized by containment in every Boolean equivalence relation containing the marked edges. On a finite carrier this gives an executable decision procedure, and hence a decision procedure for the existence of a graph witness at each fixed size. The size of a witness in HasGraphWitness is not bounded by these results.
