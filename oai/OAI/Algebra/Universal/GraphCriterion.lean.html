Graph characterization of finite congruence lattices

A finite lattice is the full congruence lattice of a nonempty finite algebra if and only if it admits a finite nonempty lattice-colored graph satisfying IsGraphWitness. Principal congruences supply the colors in one direction; all label-decreasing self-maps supply the unary operations in the other.
