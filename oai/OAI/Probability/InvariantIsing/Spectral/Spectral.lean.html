Finite spectral resolvents

The finite-alphabet arguments in Sections 3 and 5 of the manuscript use the unique solution of ∑ a, ρ a / (b - eig a) = x above all eigenvalues. The solution is unique and satisfies deterministic bounds. The weights may vanish; their sum being one is the only normalization.
