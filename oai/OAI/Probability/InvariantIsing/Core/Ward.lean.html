Finite-volume Gibbs rotation identity

Differentiation of a normalized Gibbs average produces the observable variation and its covariance with the Hamiltonian variation. Haar invariance then gives their exact integral identity. The spin prior may have zero weights. No limiting Ward equations or pressure bounds are assumed here.
