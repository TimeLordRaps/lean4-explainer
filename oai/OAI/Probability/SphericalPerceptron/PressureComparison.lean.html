The pressure convergence theorem assumes convergence in probability. The Gaussian cavity/contact and global penalty limits, angular limit identification, finite radial Laplace formula, spectral mesh convergence, and unconditional pressure convergence under the spectral hypotheses remain unproved. The variational functionals and limit predicates below define quantities and statements, not proofs of these limits.
