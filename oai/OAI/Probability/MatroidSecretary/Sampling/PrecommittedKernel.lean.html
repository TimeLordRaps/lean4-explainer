Precommitted finite conditional-kernel tables

The secretary reconstruction may be implemented with no randomness drawn after arrivals: draw one independent kernel output for every possible finite observation before arrivals, and later read only the entry indexed by the observation. The entire table, including unused entries, is part of the initial seed.

These lemmas establish the law of that concrete construction. They do not assert independence after conditioning on the entire table, and do not assume a secretary transport theorem or a reward guarantee.
