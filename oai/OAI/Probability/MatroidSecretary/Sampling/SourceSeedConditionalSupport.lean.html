Strict support for the actual finite conditional reconstruction. Every advertised branch/mask fiber has positive source mass, so the zero-fiber fallback is never used for these concrete kernels. The conditional law is supported exactly on its fiber, not on the whole source space; this does not remove the secretary replay's every-seed mismatch guard.
