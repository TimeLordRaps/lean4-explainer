Exact conditional fibers of a finite Bernoulli product law

A fixed outside mask is a genuine conditioning event. Restricting the full product-law expectation to that event factors into its atom probability times the expectation over the unrevealed coordinates. The unnormalized identity also covers zero-probability fibers and endpoint Bernoulli rates.
