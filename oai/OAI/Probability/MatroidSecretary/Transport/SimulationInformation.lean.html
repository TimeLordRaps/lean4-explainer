Information restrictions of the one-sample simulation

These pointwise statements make explicit the information restrictions used in sections/setup.tex, Lemma lem:simulation: unused samples and online values of sacrificed labels cannot affect the execution. The final two replay identities also support the prefix/suffix construction in sections/secretary.tex. No independence, positivity, or feasibility assumptions are needed for these deterministic identities.
