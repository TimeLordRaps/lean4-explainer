The complete disjoint-query certificate contract

This is the independent-auxiliary-seed form of lem:transactions, with explicit success declarations rather than counting every certifying transaction. The inner finite product expectation has the original Bernoulli law for every seed. Soundness is required only on bit vectors with positive product mass; this keeps deterministic Bernoulli coordinates within scope without restricting impossible executions. Integrability follows from the proved logarithmic budget.
