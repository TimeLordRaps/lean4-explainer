Removing impossible invalid query branches

A no-repeat procedure need only satisfy that property along its possible transcripts. Truncating at an attempted repeated/unavailable query produces a globally fresh finite tree and does not change any lawful execution. Together with support-sensitive certificate soundness, this includes deterministic bit coordinates without imposing conditions on impossible transcripts.
