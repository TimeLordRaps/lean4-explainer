Every witness residual belongs to one family fixed before the focal masks

This strengthens the existential residual used in the inherited payoff proof to cover every system of witnesses, as required by lem:pattern-family in the pinned manuscript. The family is defined from the reference incoming path and lower-group competition, not from the focal density, guard, or test bits.
