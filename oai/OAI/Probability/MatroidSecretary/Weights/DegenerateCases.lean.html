Degenerate inputs and loop coordinates

These lemmas concern the actual optimum and online execution, not a surrogate benchmark. They make explicit the empty-ground, all-loop, zero-optimum and fully-sacrificed cases admitted by setup.tex and secretary.tex. In particular, no moment or boundedness hypothesis on a loop coordinate is needed for OPT.
