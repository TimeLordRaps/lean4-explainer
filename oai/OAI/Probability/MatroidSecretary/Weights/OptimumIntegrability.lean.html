The exact finite-moment requirement

For a finite matroid and nonnegative measurable weights, integrability of the actual offline optimum is equivalent to integrability of the nonloop coordinates. No loop-coordinate moments are imposed. This is a supporting scope check for introduction.tex, Theorem thm:main, and setup.tex's one-sample simulation argument; it does not replace its competitive claim.
