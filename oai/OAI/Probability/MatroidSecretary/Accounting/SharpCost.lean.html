Retaining the density-mask rate in the accounting budget

In accounting.tex immediately after eq:expected-cost, the manuscript notes that the exact density-mask contribution is 1 / (4 * κ), although the proof uses the more generous 1 / κ. This supplement proves that sharper inequality for the actual zero-extended cost, without conditioning on random listing.
