The one-sample endpoint on arbitrary finite labels

This is supporting transport for thm:main, not a new manuscript target. The enumeration is fixed from the label type before the input distributions. All labels, including loops, and all probability/online-information hypotheses are retained. The probability-space universe is independent of the label universe. No moment assumption on individual loop values is introduced.
