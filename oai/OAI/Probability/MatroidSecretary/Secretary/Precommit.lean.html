Predrawing every conditional seed

A finite table of independent conditional seeds can be sampled before the observation prefix. Looking up the entry indexed by the observed prefix uses no later randomness. The whole table, including unused entries, may be disclosed. These lemmas are probability identities only; an actual prefix law and an actual history-only online replay remain separate obligations.
