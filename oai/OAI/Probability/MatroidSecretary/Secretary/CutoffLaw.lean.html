Actual initial branch/cutoff law

For the complete precommitted coupling seed, the initial branch and cutoff have exactly the fair-branch/Binomial joint law in secretary.tex lines 54--66. This averages the entire conditional table; it is not conditioning on the complete Q. The cutoff is chosen before weights or arrival randomness. Empty/full cutoffs and the empty ground set are included without exclusions.
