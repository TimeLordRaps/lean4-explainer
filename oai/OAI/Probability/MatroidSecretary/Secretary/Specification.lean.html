Independent specification of the full-seed secretary endpoint

The rule and its finite initial seed law are chosen before the fixed vector of weights and before the probability space or adversary. The initial uniform order is independent of the complete seed. Only its ordered prefix is preserved in the actual execution; the suffix may depend on all available information, and even on additional randomization carried by the ambient space. No independence assumption is made on the executed order.

Finite seed types suffice for the manuscript construction: all conditional reconstruction tables can be sampled in advance. Fin q is just an enumeration of this finite seed space, with its arbitrary probability law.
