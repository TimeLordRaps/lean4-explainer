Exceptional inputs for the actual secretary execution

These lemmas specialize the canonical history-only secretary model. They do not assume a conditional-mask coupling and do not replace the competitive theorem. All pointwise statements hold for every complete initial seed, including seeds outside the support of a chosen probability law.
