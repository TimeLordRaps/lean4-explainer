Arctangent centers of probability laws

Every real probability law has a unique arctangent center without moment assumptions. The center depends only on the law, is translation-equivariant, and is continuous for weak convergence. Convergence of translated log-diameter laws yields scalar normalization limits; no FK/spanning-tree GHP convergence theorem is asserted.
