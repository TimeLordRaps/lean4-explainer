Finite Fortuin–Kasteleyn maps and the Brownian continuum random tree.

All combinatorial maps below have a distinguished dart 0 and are quotiented by orientation-preserving dart relabellings fixing that dart. A rotation system with Euler characteristic 2 encodes precisely a connected oriented-sphere map. No simplicity restriction is imposed. Vertices and edges are rotation and edge-reversal orbits, respectively. Every edge subset is included in the law. The index n here represents source size n+1, so the limit is through ALL positive edge sizes, not a subsequence.

MetricMeasureData packages the actual distance, measurable space and measure, without using arbitrary representatives of measure-preserving isometry classes as a global typeclass. Valid objects are compact metric probability spaces. The GHP extended distance is the infimum over common isometric embeddings, represented by pseudometrics on the disjoint union; zero cross distances simply identify points in the common ambient space. Tests are bounded functions continuous for that distance on valid objects. Thus GHP convergence is written in its bounded-continuous-test (weak distributional convergence) definition.

A standard normalized Brownian excursion is represented in its standard three-dimensional Bessel-bridge realization: the norm of three independent standard Brownian bridges of duration 1. No rescaling of the excursion is used.
