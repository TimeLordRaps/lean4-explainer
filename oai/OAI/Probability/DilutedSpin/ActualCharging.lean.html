    The absolute history sum with a terminal test. The old-leaf multiplier is exactly the number of eligible leaves (each contributes coefficient one). Fresh histories keep the genuine grow operation and coefficient at every step.

    noncomputable def historyMass {L : ℕ} (F : PrescribedTree L → ℝ) :
    ℕ → (S : PrescribedTree L) → ℕ → ℝ
  | 0, S, _ => F S
  | k+1, S, a => (a:ℝ) * historyMass F k S (a-1) +
      ∑ v : Internal S, |gamma S (grid L 0 L) v| * historyMass F k (grow S v) a
