A common access radius from the converging inverse guides. A fixed positive guide time has a nonzero limiting parameter value; all sufficiently late guides therefore cross one common radius while their physical images stay in the prescribed small ball. This supplies the actual uniform localization hypotheses used before the first-height restart.
