source

The finite-valued form of Rohde--Schramm, Basic properties of SLE, Annals of Mathematics 161 (2005), Theorem 4.1, pp. 898--899. The theorem extends the inverse Loewner map continuously to the closed half-plane. Keeping its values in ℂ allows arbitrary deterministic stopped charts to act on an entire fresh continuous trace, before any passage event is selected. This is an explicit published input, not a continuation or passage estimate.
