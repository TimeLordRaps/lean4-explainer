Opposite local sides of a straightened crosscut. This is a deterministic consequence of disk-image openness and the intermediate value theorem. It does not assume any classification of ribbon exits or harmonic measures.

    An open image of a disk cannot send both sides of its horizontal diameter into the same side of the vertical axis. The zero-set assumption is exactly the assertion that this diameter is the local crosscut.
