source

The stopped restart identity at every future time. Positive gauge measure on each nondegenerate trace segment and nullity of the real axis force interior trace times to be dense. This is a measurable path property, hence transfers through the actual stopped trace law. Continuity of the published inverse-map boundary extension then gives the identity also at boundary visits.
