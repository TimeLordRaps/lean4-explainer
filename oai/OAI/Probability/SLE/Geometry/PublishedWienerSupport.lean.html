The finite-window planar Brownian support input.

Millet--Sanz-Sole (1994), A simple proof of the support theorem for diffusion processes, Seminaire de Probabilites XXVIII, Theorem 3.5, p. 47: specialize to dimension two, identity diffusion and zero drift. Only positivity of uniform tubes about based continuous paths on [0,1] is retained. The product of two real Wiener laws is the planar law. The Hölder support statement implies this weaker uniform-topology consequence, using density of polygonal Cameron--Martin paths. https://www.numdam.org/article/SPS_1994__28__36_0.pdf
