The deterministic comparison behind Corollary cor:larger-gauge in An explicit exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE (26 September 2026).

The Hausdorff measures here are the existing Measure.mkMetric constructions, so the comparison applies to arbitrary countable spatial covers. No SLE estimate is assumed in the comparison theorem: positive measure for the smaller gauge is the sole input to the non-sigma-finiteness conclusion.
