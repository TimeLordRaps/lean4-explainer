One-sample matroid selection

The rule and its finite seed law depend only on the known labeled matroid. A decision sees the initial sample vector, the entire seed, and the arriving label/value prefix. Every prefix is feasible, and decisions are irrevocable. The arrival permutation may depend on all samples, values, and seed bits. Only the actual offline optimum is required to be integrable.
