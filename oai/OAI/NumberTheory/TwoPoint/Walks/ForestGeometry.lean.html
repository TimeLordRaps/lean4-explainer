The independent-direction obstruction to an incidence cycle

A hypothetical simple cycle of line and point vertices uses each line once. Its successive point differences lie along distinct independent directions. Telescoping around the cycle forces every difference to vanish, contradicting distinct adjacent points. This is the algebraic acyclicity step in q:forest-code.
