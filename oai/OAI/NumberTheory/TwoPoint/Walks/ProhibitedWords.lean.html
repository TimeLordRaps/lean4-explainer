Positive words, reversal, and descent to a minimal prohibited word

A step keeps its whole tuple and padding divisor. Positivity is the exact integer divisibility q*d ∣ x at its departure. Since the displacement is ±h*q*d, this divisibility also holds at its arrival. Restriction and reversal therefore preserve positivity, as required in the witness descent.
