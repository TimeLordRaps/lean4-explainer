The polynomial-expectation step in Braverman's proof (Proposition 6, author version https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~mbraverm/FoolAC0v7.pdf). On the Boolean cube, multilinear degree is represented by Walsh support. Preservation of low-degree expectations gives a sandwich criterion for bounded-independence comparison.
