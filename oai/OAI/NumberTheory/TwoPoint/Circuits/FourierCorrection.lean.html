Correcting an almost independent Boolean density

This is the finite Fourier correction used in the proof of the manuscript's finite-residue comparison (q:finite-law, q:fourier-correction-tv). The construction removes the chosen nonconstant Walsh coefficients and adds the constant a before normalization, retaining pointwise nonnegativity.
