Truth-table encoding and substitution for residue circuits

Any decoded residue equality is a Boolean function of its block of bits. Its explicit disjunctive normal form has depth at most two and size at most 1 + 2^B (1+B). Substituting such forms into the actual circuit syntax increases depth by at most two and multiplies size by this bound.

  | .andGate children => .andGate (fun i => substitute σ (children i))
  | .orGate children => .orGate (fun i => substitute σ (children i))
