Weighted rows and the localized square estimate

Finite weighted Cauchy--Schwarz proves the localized Schur estimate in q:weighted-rows. Constancy of the site factor on the support replaces a level-set decomposition. Summing the localized estimates only uses a bound at the support of the vector; no commutation with a projection is needed.
