The endpoint error in the rough-shift averaging argument

These are finite identities and bounds from the proof of (q:rough-shifts) in quantitative/03-analytic.tex. No short-sum or sieve estimate is assumed. The averages below use the positive integers 1,...,Y and translations 1,...,D; the resulting error is at most 2D/Y times the total weight.
