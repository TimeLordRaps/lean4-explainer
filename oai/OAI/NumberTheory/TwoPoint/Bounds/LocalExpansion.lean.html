Finite local expansions

The local sequences in manuscript Lemma lem:affine-dilation have value zero at exponent zero. A difference of two normalized local sequences represents such a sequence. Expansion over subsets then produces ordinary multiplicative components that retain the original prime values outside the fixed finite set.
