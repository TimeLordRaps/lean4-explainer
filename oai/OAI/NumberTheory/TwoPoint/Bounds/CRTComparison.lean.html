Finite CRT marginals and contraction under independent randomization

A selected finite set of pairwise coprime residue coordinates has the law of one residue modulo their product. The exact interval count from ResidueCounts gives total variation at most D / (2N). Applying any finite stochastic kernel preserves this bound; in particular one may add independent finite randomness and project to any selected encoded bits. These are the finite arithmetic and data-processing steps in q:crt-bits.
