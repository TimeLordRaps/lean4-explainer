Multiplication outside the selected prime bands preserves the typical mask. The resulting identity uses the unmasked factor on the left: the typical coefficient itself need not be multiplicative. In particular, the complete part can be extracted at every power of an outside prime, without a coprimality condition on the remaining argument.
