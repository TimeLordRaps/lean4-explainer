The actual finite fair-split channel

A site is a vector of independent Bernoulli bits. Conditional on that site, each present prime is retained independently with probability one half and each absent prime stays absent. Integrating two conditionally independent retentions gives exactly the same-site two-split law, including for arbitrary output cells. This connects that law to the actual finite channel kernel.
