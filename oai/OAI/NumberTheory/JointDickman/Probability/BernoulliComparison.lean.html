Mutually exclusive site hits versus independent Bernoulli hits

At one prime the actual site hits are mutually exclusive. Their categorical law differs from independent Bernoulli hits by at most twice the square of the sum of the site probabilities in L¹. Everything here is a finite-sum proof; no coupling estimate is assumed.
