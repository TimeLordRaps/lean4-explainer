Centering finite channels in the residue coordinate

All residues above the same base cell carry the same atom weight. Thus subtracting the residue average of the transition subtracts exactly the residue average of the channel. The corresponding two-split kernel is centered in both coordinates. A residue-independent approximation cancels, at a loss of at most four in its uniform error.
