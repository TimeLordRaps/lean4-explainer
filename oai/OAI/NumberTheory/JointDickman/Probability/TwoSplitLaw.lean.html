Two fair splits of the same prime site

The local law is constructed by first selecting a Bernoulli site and then, conditional on that hit, taking two independent fair splits. Its common and exclusive categories have mass q/4 each. Finite product comparison gives the independent common/exclusive model without assuming a coupling.
