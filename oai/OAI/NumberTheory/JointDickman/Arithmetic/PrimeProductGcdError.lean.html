Removing GCD restrictions on two independent selected prime products

The probability of a common selected prime is bounded by the sum of the products of the two marginal hit probabilities. For the independent 1/(2p) selection laws this is at most 1/(4N) when all allowed primes exceed N. The same bound controls any bounded test of the selected pair.
