Coarse approximation of the actual fair-prime channel

Normalized high-exclusive cell probabilities are compared directly with bounded Lipschitz profiles. Their product yields the smooth mixture kernel; the actual two-split comparison and the low-union estimate then transfer the coarse approximation to the original fair-prime channel.
