Published short-interval inputs for the joint Dickman problem

These definitions are propositions to be supplied as hypotheses. They are not axioms or proofs of the cited analytic theorems. In particular they do not assume joint independence at the shift one, the manuscript's new mixed decorrelation proposition, or any of its graph estimates.

The natural-number floor is harmless here: all intervals to which the inputs apply have positive left endpoint. Finset.Ioc implements the convention z < n ≤ z + H; changing the inclusion of the endpoints has the elementary O(1 / H) cost recorded in the manuscript.
