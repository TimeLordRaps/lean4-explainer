The published Dirichlet-character distance estimate

Only the fixed-modulus consequence of Klurman--Mangerel--Teräväinen, Multiplicative functions in short arithmetic progressions, Proc. London Math. Soc. 127 (2023), 366--446, Lemma 7.8, is assumed here. That lemma bounds the infimum over |t| ≤ 10X by (1/4) log (log X / log (2 conductor χ)) + O(1). For a fixed character the conductor term is absorbed in the constant. The primes dividing the modulus only add nonnegative terms to our distance. No modified character, bin label, or short average occurs in this input. Source: https://doi.org/10.1112/plms.12546, arXiv:1909.12280, Lemma 7.8.
