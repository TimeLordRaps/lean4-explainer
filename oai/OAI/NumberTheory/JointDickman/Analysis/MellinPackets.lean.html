Smoothed logarithmic windows and their Dirichlet energy

A finite sum of translates of a Schwartz window is itself Schwartz. Its Fourier transform factors into the window transform and the actual finite Dirichlet polynomial. Plancherel therefore identifies the two energies without a truncation or an arithmetic hypothesis.
