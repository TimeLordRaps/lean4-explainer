The moving-giant arithmetic in Section 8.1

The integer constants record actual products of regular small entries. At a reversal, the sampled compensation product u is inserted into both children: their constants are u * CL and u * CR. The pivot is reconstructed over the integers first, and only its giant factor is passed to either child.
