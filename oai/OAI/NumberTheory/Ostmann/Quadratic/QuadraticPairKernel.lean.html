The primitive kernel of a pair of odd squarefree denominators

Writing d = gcd n₁ n₂, the product character has squarefree kernel (n₁/d)(n₂/d) and the remaining factor is precisely the coprimality mask modulo d. This is the arithmetic decomposition in Heath-Brown (1995), §3.
