The principal term of the smooth character estimate

Published input: the conductor-one case of Montgomery–Vaughan (2007), Corollary 11.20, equations (11.32)/(11.34), p.381, with the usual smooth partial summation. This is the classical prime number theorem with its zero-free-region error, specialized to our single fixed compactly supported smooth function. It makes no assertion about a growing character family.
