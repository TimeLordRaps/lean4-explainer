Coefficient rows of the giant reversals

At a reversal one current giant is retained and the other becomes (v * Hminus - w * Hplus) / u, with all three coefficients units modulo the internal prime. The two current giants remain independent linear forms in the top giants, so every subsequent numerator is a nonzero row.
