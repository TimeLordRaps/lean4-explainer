No new invariant roots on the component base

Diagonal covariance makes each full-root invariance event invariant on the parameter space. Diagonal ergodicity makes that event null or conull. In the conull case, integration of the actual conditional measures gives root invariance of the original measure. This conclusion does not assume that the individual conditional measures are diagonal invariant.
