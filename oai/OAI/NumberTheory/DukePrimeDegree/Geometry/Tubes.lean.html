From ball covers to entropy-producing tube estimates

A cover by balls with a dimension gap between their mass exponent and covering number gives exponential tube decay. The entropy implication uses the constant-sequence specialization of Einsiedler--Lindenstrauss--Michel--Venkatesh (2009), Corollary 3.3, with its two-sided flow tubes.
