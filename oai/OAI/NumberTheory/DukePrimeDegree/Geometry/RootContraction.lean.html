Contraction of root pairs under neutral diagonal flows

The acting root group is the additive real line. If a diagonal direction expands the corresponding root character, forward right translation contracts the relative root parameter. This supplies the geometric hypothesis of the Hopf lemma even when the orbit leaves compact sets.
