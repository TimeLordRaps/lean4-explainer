Horospherical support and leaf separation

The horospherical support property of EKL (2006), Lemma 4.4(1), together with separation along a root orbit gives Lemma 4.4(2). The support property follows from the root product theorem, Proposition 8.3 of Einsiedler--Katok (2003).
