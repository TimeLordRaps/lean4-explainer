Finite measures in the matrix conjugation representation

This is the elementary recurrence part of the needed Borel-density argument. A finite measure invariant under a strict real contraction is concentrated at zero. Applied to matrix coefficients, split diagonal conjugation forces invariant finite matrix measures onto diagonal matrices.
