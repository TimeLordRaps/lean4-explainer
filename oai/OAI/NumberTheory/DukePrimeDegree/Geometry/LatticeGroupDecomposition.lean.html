The lattice group action and its ergodic components

Taking inverses writes the right lattice action as a left action. Measurable ergodic decomposition gives probability components for each closed matrix subgroup preserving the measure. Adjoining an invariant subgroup to the diagonal action preserves invariance and ergodicity of a diagonal-ergodic probability, as required by Margulis--Tomanov.
