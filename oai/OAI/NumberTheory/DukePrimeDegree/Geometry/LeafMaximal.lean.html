A covering estimate for leafwise bad sets

The relative maximal estimate used in EKL (2006), Section 4.3, rests on the one-dimensional Besicovitch covering theorem. We first prove the estimate for an arbitrary bounded family of witnessing radii. The measure on the line is arbitrary; no doubling property is used.
