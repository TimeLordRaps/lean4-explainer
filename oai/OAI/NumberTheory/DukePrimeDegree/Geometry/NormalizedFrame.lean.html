Diagonal invariance of an actual frame

A nonzero pivot minor gives a concrete normalized frame, by multiplying on the right by that minor's inverse. The one-weight conclusion for the original minors then makes its column space diagonal-invariant. The pivot may be chosen separately at each point; no invariant chart or measurable choice of the pivot is assumed.
