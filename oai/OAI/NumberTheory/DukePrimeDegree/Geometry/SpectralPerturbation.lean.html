Separated real spectra under small perturbations

The spectral step in EKL (2006), Lemma 5.3, can be obtained from diagonal row dominance and the intermediate value theorem. The determinant keeps its sign along a perturbation whose row sums are smaller than the corresponding diagonal entries. Opposite endpoint signs then locate a real characteristic root in each separated interval.
