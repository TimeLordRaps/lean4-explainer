The homogeneous space and diagonal flow

We use the column-lattice convention SL(n,ℝ) / SL(n,ℤ). Inversion identifies this with the manuscript's row-lattice convention SL(n,ℤ) \ SL(n,ℝ) and turns right diagonal translations into left translations by the inverse. No packet or Haar probability is assumed to have been constructed by these definitions.
