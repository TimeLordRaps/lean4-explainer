Conditional dynamical reduction for the prime-degree packet theorem

A ball cover with a dimension gap gives exponential tube decay. Entropy in almost every ergodic component, followed by prime-degree rigidity, then identifies the mixture with Haar probability.

The dynamical criteria are specialized as follows:

  • ELMV (2009), Corollary 3.3: a flow-invariant probability with compact-uniform two-sided tube bound exp(-2ηt) has entropy at least η.
  • Rokhlin (1967), Theorem 9.8: entropy of a normalized restriction of an invariant component decomposition is its component entropy integral.
  • EKL (2006), Theorem 1.3 and Corollary 1.4: a diagonal-invariant ergodic probability with positive entropy on the prime-degree lattice space is Haar probability.
