The actual neutral coordinate in the upper-root chart

On the positive-diagonal chart the transverse slice is the product of its unit-diagonal part and the trace-zero logarithmic diagonal. Right diagonal translation fixes the first coordinate and translates the second. This is the local center coordinate needed for comparison with the root kernel.
