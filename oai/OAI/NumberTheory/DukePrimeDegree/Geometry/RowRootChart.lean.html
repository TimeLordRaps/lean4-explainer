Simultaneous coordinates for a full row of roots

The expanding group for a canonical diagonal flow is a full row (or, after transposition, a column). A nonzero pivot provides a global chart separating all its root coordinates simultaneously. This constructs the ambient chart needed for the abelian case of the product theorem.
