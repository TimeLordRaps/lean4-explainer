Correcting a pair inside a large generic set

The two initial points may be corrected by a changing element of the measure stabilizer. Averaging the bad set along a fixed group patch gives a larger good set. For two points in that larger set, a common group translation restores membership in the original generic set. No commutation with the averaging flow is needed for this selection.
