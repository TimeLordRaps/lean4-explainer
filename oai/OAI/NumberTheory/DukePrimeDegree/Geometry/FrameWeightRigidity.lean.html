From minor weights to an invariant subspace

In a genuine Grassmannian coordinate chart, the pivot rows of a frame form the identity. A one-row replacement minor is then exactly the corresponding graph coordinate. The single-weight minor conclusion therefore forces the frame to intertwine the two diagonal operators.
