Compactness for pairs with equal leaf data

The last limiting step of the low-entropy argument: equal continuous leaf data on a compact set survive a normalized shearing limit. This isolates the compactness step from the return-time selection and the horospherical separation argument in EKL (2006), Section 4.2.
