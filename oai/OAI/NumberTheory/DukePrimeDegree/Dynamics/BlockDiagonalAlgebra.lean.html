The algebra of scalars on root blocks

For a partition of the coordinate indices, the block-scalar algebra is the actual diagonal matrix algebra whose diagonal is constant on each block. Its dimension is the number of blocks. It is also the common centralizer of the full diagonal algebra and the elementary root matrices within the blocks. These are algebraic statements, without any homogeneous-measure classification assumption.
