An explicit neutral direction excluding centralizer orbits

The two root coordinates receive the same negative weight. The remaining coordinates receive distinct positive weights. Their sum is balanced by the repeated negative weight. This discharges the elementary weight conditions in the centralizer-orbit exclusion.
