Haar rigidity from root-leaf symmetries

Projective normalization can be removed from root-leaf symmetries, and nontrivial root periods yield invariance under the corresponding unipotent group. Haar measure follows from all root periods, or from a single root and a transitive prime-degree symmetry group.
