Local orbit isolation gives a positive-mass orbit

The topological conclusion needed from the shearing argument is local isolation of the invariant-group orbits inside one positive-mass good set. Second countability then produces a countable orbit cover, so one actual orbit has positive mass. No finite-orbit existence is assumed.
