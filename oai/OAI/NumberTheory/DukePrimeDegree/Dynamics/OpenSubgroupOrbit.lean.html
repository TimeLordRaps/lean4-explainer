Selecting a positive-mass orbit of an open subgroup

An open subgroup has countable index in the second-countable matrix groups used here. A finite invariant measure on a larger orbit therefore has a nonzero restriction to one smaller orbit. The generic full set is preserved by restriction, allowing the basepoint to be chosen there. No connectedness of the larger homogeneous group is needed.
