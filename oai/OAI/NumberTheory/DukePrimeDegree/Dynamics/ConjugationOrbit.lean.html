Conjugation maps on actual lattice orbit quotients

A matrix commuting with the lattice stabilizer defines a continuous conjugation map on the corresponding homogeneous orbit quotient. Right invariance of a measure on that quotient gives conjugation invariance of its pushforward. This is the geometric bridge used by the elementary matrix-density argument.
