Diagonal recurrence for actual component velocity spaces

The kernel covariance is the equality supplied by the normalizer ergodic decomposition. The velocity space and its measurable frame are constructed from the kernel, rather than supplied as extra geometric data. Recurrence is applied separately on its measurable rank events, and gives invariance of the actual velocity space.
