Simultaneous generic representatives of a transverse pair

A correction in a block-preserving subgroup may depend on the pair. The Haar-fiber selection puts both corrected points back in the same generic set, and simultaneous block conjugation preserves the scalar within-block normalization. The subgroup is contained in the actual measure stabilizer in the application; that inclusion is explicit here.
