Scalar diagonal representatives transverse to the root blocks

After clearing the within-block off-diagonal entries, a positive split diagonal makes all diagonal entries equal. Both corrections belong to the actual invariant subgroup when it contains A and the specified roots. Thus this normalization preserves being outside that subgroup.
