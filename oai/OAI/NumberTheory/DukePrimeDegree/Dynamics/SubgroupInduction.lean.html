Cyclic induction inside an arbitrary subgroup

The permutation character of G/H is an integer combination, after multiplication by card H, of the permutation characters for cyclic subgroups contained in H. This is the form needed for the Dedekind zeta function of a possibly nonnormal intermediate field.
