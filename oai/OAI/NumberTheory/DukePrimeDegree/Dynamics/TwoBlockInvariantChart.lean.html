The two-block chart for the actual invariant lattice measure

The neutral subgroup here is the preimage of the actual measure stabilizer. Its openness is deduced from within-block root invariance and diagonal invariance, and covering-lift invariance supplies the translation invariance in the parabolic coordinates.
