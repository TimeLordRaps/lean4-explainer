Rational diagonal stabilizers of actual lattices

The rational coordinate space below is constructed from an actual lattice matrix. Its diagonal stabilizer is a concrete subalgebra of real coordinate functions, not a separately assumed field action. The absence of lattice vectors on coordinate hyperplanes makes this algebra a field of degree dividing the ambient dimension.
