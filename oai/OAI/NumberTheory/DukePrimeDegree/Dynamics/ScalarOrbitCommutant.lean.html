Scalar real commutants of prime-degree finite root-pair orbits

Rationality of the actual lattice stabilizer's commutant turns the preceding rational scalar theorem into a real scalar theorem. In particular, no nontrivial real projection can commute with the orbit group. No orbit existence is assumed beyond the finite invariant orbit explicitly supplied.
