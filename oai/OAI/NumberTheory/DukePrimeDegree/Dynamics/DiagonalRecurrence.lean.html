Vanishing of expanding scalar coordinates in a finite invariant law

This is the scalar step in diagonal recurrence on projective or Grassmannian coordinates. Bounded monotone truncation avoids assuming that the original coordinate is integrable. It will be applied to ratios of unequal diagonal weights; no stabilizer or orbit conclusion is assumed.
