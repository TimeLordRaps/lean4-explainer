A limiting shear preserves the limiting measure

This is the measure step in the unipotent shearing argument. If two families of probability measures have the same weak limit, and one is the translate of the other by converging group elements, the limiting element preserves that measure. Compact observables give uniform control even when the sampled points leave every compact set.
