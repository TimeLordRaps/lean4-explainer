The two cross-block groups and the neutral block group

These are concrete subgroups of the same determinant-one matrix group. The rectangular cross-block coordinates are additive, and the neutral group is the centralizer of the coordinate projection. This supplies the group side of the singular two-block chart, without an entropy or measure-classification assumption.
