One root pair excludes every proper block partition

The roots required here are just one opposite pair inside the actual finite orbit group. The partition may combine all singleton coordinates into one block; no root invariance is required among those coordinates. Thus this endpoint matches the open block subgroup provided by local projection fixation, without adding a diagonal-normalization hypothesis.
