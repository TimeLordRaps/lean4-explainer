The horospherical shape of a normalized shearing limit

The limiting matrix differs from the identity only in the chosen row and column, with unchanged diagonal. Its displacement is nilpotent of order at most three. This is the precise horospherical direction used in EKL (2006), Section 4.2, equations (4.6)--(4.8).
