Centralizers of actual lattice stabilizers

The matrix centralizer of an arbitrary family of rational matrices is spanned over ℝ by its rational matrices. Finite dimensionality reduces the family to a basis of its rational span. This applies, in particular, to the integral stabilizer of a homogeneous orbit in the lattice space. The identification of that centralizer with the real block algebra is kept separate from this rational linear algebra statement.
