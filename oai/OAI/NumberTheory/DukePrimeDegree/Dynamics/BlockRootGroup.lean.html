The group generated by the roots within each block

The finite-orbit fixed-vector obstruction excludes singleton blocks. Without singleton blocks, the full matrix commutant of the root-generated group is exactly the block-scalar algebra. Its full normalizer therefore lies in the scalar-algebra normalizer used in orbit reduction.
