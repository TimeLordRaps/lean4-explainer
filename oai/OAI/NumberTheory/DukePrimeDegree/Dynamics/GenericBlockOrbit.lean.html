Prime obstruction for the larger homogeneous orbit group

The actual finite-volume orbit group may strictly contain the group generated by the roots. Genericity and one root already force its stabilizer centralizer to be commutative. Thus block preservation is enough: no absence-of-singletons or split-diagonal hypothesis is needed.
