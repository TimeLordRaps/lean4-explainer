Removing the generic-basepoint condition

A finite invariant measure on a block orbit pushes to an invariant measure on the lattice space. Diagonal recurrence gives a generic basepoint almost everywhere. Changing to that basepoint removes the genericity hypothesis from the prime-degree block-orbit obstruction.
