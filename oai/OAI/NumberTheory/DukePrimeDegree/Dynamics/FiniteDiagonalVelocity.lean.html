Root recovery from finitely many diagonal recurrence statements

For each root, a single diagonal has that root as its unique largest weight. Stability under this one diagonal gives stability under all its positive integer powers, which is enough to project onto that root. Thus the actual component application requires only finitely many recurrence statements, rather than a simultaneous assertion for an uncountable family of diagonal elements.
