Centralizer limits of normalized shearing trajectories

Uniform control before the first shearing time bounds the quadratic coefficient. Since the shearing times diverge, the diagonal disturbance vanishes. Every convergent terminal matrix therefore lies in the root centralizer. These conclusions use actual special-linear matrices.
