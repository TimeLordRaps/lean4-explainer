A neutral flow for the shortest remaining positive root

For type A roots in a fixed positive chamber, choose a root of shortest index distance. Moving its two diagonal weights to their midpoint makes that root neutral and keeps every other remaining root strictly contracting. This is the root-removal step in EK2003 Proposition 8.3.
