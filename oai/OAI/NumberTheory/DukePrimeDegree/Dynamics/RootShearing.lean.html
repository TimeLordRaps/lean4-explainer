Polynomial shearing along a root subgroup

The exact matrix formula is the algebraic part of the low-entropy argument: conjugation by a root unipotent is quadratic in its parameter. The exceptional case is precisely the centralizer, and commuting with a single nonzero root element already characterizes that case.
