The two-coordinate shearing diagonal

In the row-lattice convention the weights are -1 at the root's row, +1 at its column, and zero elsewhere. Backward right translation contracts root time by exp (-2*t). The corresponding relative return matrix has a fixed diagonal difference, quadratic coefficient growing by exp (2*t), and other shearing coefficients growing by exp t. These are the rates in EKL (2006), Lemma 4.6.
