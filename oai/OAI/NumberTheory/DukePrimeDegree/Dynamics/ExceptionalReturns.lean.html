The exceptional-return strengthening

Outside one null set, every sufficiently small lift of a return along the root-pair centralizer, if it centralizes the chosen root, centralizes both roots. In particular this applies to every neutral diagonal return. This is the implication from exceptional to strong exceptional returns in EKL (2006), Lemma 4.2, on the actual arithmetic quotient.
