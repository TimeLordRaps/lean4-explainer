Local invariance under the full neutral block group

Within-block root invariance and full diagonal invariance suffice for invariance under a neighborhood of the identity in the block group. The proof uses the already constructed finite Gaussian elimination: a small block matrix outside the subgroup would normalize to a positive scalar determinant-one matrix still outside it, which is impossible. Disconnected block components are not asserted to preserve the measure.
