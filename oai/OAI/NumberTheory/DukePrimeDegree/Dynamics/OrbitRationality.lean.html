Rational block centralizers from finite-volume lattice orbits

The rational matrices in the integral orbit-stabilizer centralizer span its real centralizer. Conjugation by the actual lattice matrix transports this statement to the embedding coordinates. Finite-orbit density then identifies this transported centralizer with the block-scalar algebra.
