A dense countable subgroup preserved by one normalizer

Starting with a countable dense set, add all its integer conjugates by the chosen normalizer and generate a subgroup. This keeps countability and density while ensuring that the same invariant sigma algebra is available for both ergodic decomposition and normalizer covariance.
