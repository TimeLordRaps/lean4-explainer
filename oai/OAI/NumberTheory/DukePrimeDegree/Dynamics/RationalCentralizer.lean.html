Rationality of matrix centralizers

The commutant of a finite collection of rational matrices is spanned over the reals by its rational matrices. This is the linear algebra part of the homogeneous-orbit rationality step: it uses exactness of scalar extension, and does not assert that a lattice stabilizer is dense in a block group.
