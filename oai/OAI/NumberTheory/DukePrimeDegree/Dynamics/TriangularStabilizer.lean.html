A trace gap for positive triangular lattice stabilizers

The trace of an actual lattice stabilizer is an integer. A positive triangular stabilizer of determinant one whose trace is less than n+1 therefore has all diagonal entries equal to one. At the generic lattices used in the entropy argument it is the identity.
