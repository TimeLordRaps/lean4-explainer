From one homogeneous component to prime-degree Haar rigidity

The orbit measure may be one component of the ambient measure, and its orbit group may be disconnected. The finite-dimensional and arithmetic arguments identify Haar probability from a finite-volume component with an A-normalized group containing the invariant roots.
