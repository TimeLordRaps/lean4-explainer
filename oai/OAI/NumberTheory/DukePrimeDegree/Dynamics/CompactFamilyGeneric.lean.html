A fixed generic set for a compact family of observables

The limiting shear is selected after the generic set. Compactness of the possible shears gives a compact family of translated observables in the uniform norm. A finite approximation therefore suffices for one common generic set and time threshold. No commutation of the translating group with the averaging flow is required.
