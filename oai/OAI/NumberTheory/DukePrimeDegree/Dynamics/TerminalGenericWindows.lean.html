Generic control on a fixed terminal fraction of a real orbit

Polynomial shearing changes uniformly little on a sufficiently short fixed terminal fraction. Prefix genericity controls that fraction with the explicit loss 2 / delta. The fraction is chosen after the finite test accuracy, and is independent of the pair and stopping time.
