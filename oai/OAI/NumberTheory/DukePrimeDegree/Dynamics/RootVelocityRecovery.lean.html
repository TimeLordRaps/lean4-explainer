Recovering an actual root element from a pure root velocity

A curve in a closed subgroup whose velocity is one elementary matrix produces the corresponding root element by taking growing powers at reciprocal times. The proof compares those powers with the exact powers of a transvection. No diagonal normalization of the subgroup is needed.
