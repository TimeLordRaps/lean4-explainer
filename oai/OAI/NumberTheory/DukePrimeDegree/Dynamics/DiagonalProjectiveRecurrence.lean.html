Finite invariant projective laws of a positive diagonal map

This is a coordinate form of diagonal recurrence. A measurable nonzero representative may change by an arbitrary nonzero scalar under the dynamics. Ratios cancel that scalar. Unequal positive diagonal weights cannot both occur in the support of a representative almost everywhere. The conclusion is an actual eigenline statement, not an orbit hypothesis.
