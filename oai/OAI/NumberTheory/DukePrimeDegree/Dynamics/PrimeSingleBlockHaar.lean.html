Prime-degree Haar rigidity from a nontrivial invariant-root partition

The canonical component is genuinely ergodic for its chosen root block. The shearing argument constructs a positive-mass block orbit, and the rational stabilizer argument forces that block to be the full coordinate set. The root partition is supplied explicitly here: producing its symmetry and nontriviality from positive entropy is the separate entropy step, not a hidden homogeneous-orbit hypothesis.
