Invariant observables on asymptotic pairs

This is the measurable part of the stable-leaf argument. The two coordinates of a family of pairs have uniformly dominated marginals. If the coordinates become asymptotic, every integrable observable which is unchanged along both coordinate sequences has equal values on the pair.

The proof uses compactly supported continuous approximation in L¹ and dominated convergence. It requires no ergodicity or pointwise ergodic theorem.
