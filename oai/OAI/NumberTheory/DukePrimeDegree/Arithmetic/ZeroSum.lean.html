Summing the separated-zero comparison

The rescaled zero coordinates in the manuscript are Xρ = L (1 - Re ρ) and Yρ = L Im ρ. A uniform zero-free disk gives c² ≤ Xρ² + Yρ². Summing the elementary pointwise comparison controls the real Hadamard sum throughout the discriminant-scaled interval.
