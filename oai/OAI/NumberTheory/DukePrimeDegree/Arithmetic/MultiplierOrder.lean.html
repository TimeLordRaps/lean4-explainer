Multiplier orders and the saturation index

The initial arithmetic reduction in Section 2 of paper 031-01. A lattice is represented by its actual integer submodule of a number field, not by an abstract label. The two inclusions proved here are the algebraic input to the discriminant bound D(M) ≤ D(K) q^(2*n).
