Real characters at a simple real zero

The actual Frobenius ideal character commutes with complex conjugation. Uniqueness of the entire nonprincipal Hecke continuation therefore makes the cyclic zero orders conjugation invariant. At a simple real zero the unique contributing character is real-valued.
