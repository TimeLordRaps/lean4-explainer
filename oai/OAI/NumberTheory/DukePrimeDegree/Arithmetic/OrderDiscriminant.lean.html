The multiplier-order index

The conductor inclusion bounds the index of the multiplier order by q^n. This is the integer-index part of the discriminant-scale bound in Section 2 of paper 031-01. The discriminant is expressed by the usual index formula |disc K| [O_K : O(M)]².
