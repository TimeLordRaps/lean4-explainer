Cyclic subgroup coefficients for Stark descent

Möbius inversion on the finite poset of cyclic subgroups expresses the constant function as an integer combination of their indicators. Averaging conjugates gives the denominator-cleared cyclic permutation identity used to compare Dedekind zeta orders.
