Simple zeros in cyclic Artin factorizations

The cyclic step in Stark's exceptional-zero argument uses finite, nonnegative orders of the Hecke factors. If their product has a simple zero, exactly one factor has order one. Its zero-order character is therefore a one-dimensional character, expressed using the actual meromorphic orders in the cyclic factorization.
