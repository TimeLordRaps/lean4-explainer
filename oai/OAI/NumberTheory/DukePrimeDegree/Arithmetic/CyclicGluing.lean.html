Gluing the real characters selected by a simple zeta zero

A zero-order function on subgroups determines each cyclic restriction by its values on the subgroups generated by single elements. Conjugacy of these orders then gives precisely the class function used in odd-degree descent. No global irreducible-character decomposition is needed.
