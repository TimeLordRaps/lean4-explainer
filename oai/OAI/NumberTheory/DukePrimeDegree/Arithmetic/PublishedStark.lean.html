The published analytic input for prime-degree packets

The conditional development uses Stark, Some effective cases of the Brauer–Siegel theorem, Invent. Math. 23 (1974), 135–152, Lemmas 3 and 8 and equation (9). StarkInput records only the consequence needed here: for fields with no quadratic subfield, the nontrivial zeros are separated from 1 on the scale 1 / log D, and their real Hadamard sum converges. The zeros are indexed with multiplicity. The archimedean term and Euler-product derivative estimate then give the uniform residue comparison.
