Odd-index descent of a simple zero

Cyclic Hecke factorizations supply the real characters used by Stark's odd-degree argument. The zero order on any fixed field is an average of signs and is therefore at most one. A positive order at an odd-index subgroup is incompatible with a nonvanishing rational zeta function.
