Conditional prime-degree packet equidistribution

Complete volume-weighted local-homothety packets converge to Haar probability and form a tight family under a residue-relative uniform zeta scale bound and prime-degree positive-entropy rigidity. The arithmetic and dynamical hypotheses concern the actual homogeneous lattice space and its diagonal action. A subsequential packet limit supplies Haar probability.
