Theorem 1.1 conditional on published inputs

This is the manuscript's main result for arbitrary full-lattice local types, including varying number fields and nonmaximal multiplier orders. The only explicit inputs are StarkInput (Stark 1974, Lemmas 3 and 8 and equation (9)) and EKLPrimeInput (EKL 2006, Theorem 1.3/Corollary 1.4). The intermediate uniform zeta estimate is derived, not assumed.
