The entropy functional in the published dynamical inputs

A finite measurable partition is represented by its finite-valued label map. Its length-N names encode the join of its first N inverse images under the transformation. The liminf definition agrees with the usual limit for invariant probabilities by subadditivity; proving that agreement and the published entropy theorems is still part of the outstanding work.
