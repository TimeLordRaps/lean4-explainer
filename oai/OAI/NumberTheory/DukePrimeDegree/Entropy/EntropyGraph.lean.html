The finite coefficient argument in the high-entropy case

EKL (2006), Section 3, uses entropy symmetry to balance the row and column sums of the root coefficients. The two-step root lemma makes a composed positive edge full. The following finite argument then gives symmetric coefficients, and full coefficients whenever a third vertex is involved.
