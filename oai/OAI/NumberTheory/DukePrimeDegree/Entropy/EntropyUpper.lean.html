Entropy bounds from a small collection of orbit names

This is the finite-partition estimate used in an entropy upper bound: if all but a small mass lies on a short list of names, the entropy is bounded by the logarithm of that list, plus the cost of the exceptional mass. No dynamical covering or leafwise estimate is assumed here.
