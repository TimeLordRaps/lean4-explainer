A probability kernel on the entire subordinate future

The intrinsic product has an s-finite measurable version. Restricting it on the jointly measurable future relation and normalizing gives an actual probability kernel. On the exceptional zero/infinite-mass set we use a point mass; the intrinsic formula holds almost everywhere whenever the proved positive finite future-mass condition applies.
