Entropy symmetry for the actual diagonal action

The time-reversal theorem applies to right translations by diagonal matrices. The final lemma combines it with the precise positive-root entropy formula of EKL (2006), Proposition 3.1(3). That formula remains an explicit hypothesis here; time reversal and the coefficient balance are proved.
