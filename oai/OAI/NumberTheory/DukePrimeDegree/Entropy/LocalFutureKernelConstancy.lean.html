Pointwise constancy of the actual future kernel on a conull chart

The normalized leaf measure depends only on the transverse coordinate. After removing one null set, equal transverse coordinates and equal complete future names therefore give exactly the same ambient kernel. This is stronger than constancy for almost every pair sampled from that kernel, and is the local input for the refined-future entropy comparison.
