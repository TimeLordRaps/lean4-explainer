Entropy upper bounds from small auxiliary root-orbit observations

The intrinsic stationary future kernel is built for the given bounded branching observation. An auxiliary observation only removes transverse coordinates from joint future fibers; it need not generate the ambient measurable space. Its sole entropy cost is its countable Shannon sum.

    Each finite clip of a bounded branching observation is controlled by actual root information and the Shannon sum of the auxiliary observation. The stationary kernel and finite predecessor symbols are constructed in this proof, rather than supplied as additional hypotheses.
