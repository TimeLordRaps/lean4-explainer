Almost-everywhere periodicity gives zero finite-observation entropy

A common positive period makes every long orbit name a deterministic extension of one fixed finite name. Thus block entropy stays bounded and its entropy rate is zero. Combining this with full-A ergodicity isolates the aperiodic branch without asserting ergodicity of the chosen map.
