Entropy symmetry and the root coefficient balance

The entropy formula in EKL (2006), Proposition 3.1, is a sum of positive root weights. Equality of the entropy of an invertible map and its inverse then gives the row/column balance. The finite algebraic implication uses trace-zero test weights independently of the dynamical entropy formula.
