Sampling the published short-interval estimate

For integral short lengths, a moving short sum is constant on each unit cell of its origin. Thus its published integral controls the actual finite sum of integer origins, with outer cutoff Y+1. The progression restriction is an average of fixed frequencies and has total coefficient mass one.
