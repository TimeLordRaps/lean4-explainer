The divisor-square estimate in the mixed prime/cofactor moment. The map (d,e) ↦ (gcd(d,e), d/gcd(d,e), e/gcd(d,e)) bounds a pair of supported divisors by three supported factors whose product divides the integer. Summing the divisibility tests gives a finite Euler majorant.
