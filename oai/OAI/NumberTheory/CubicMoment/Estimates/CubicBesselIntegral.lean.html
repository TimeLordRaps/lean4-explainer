The fixed order-one-third Bessel kernel in the cubic theta expansion. The Schläfli heat integral is used as the definition; its Mellin mass is proved by absolute Fubini and the two ordinary Gamma integrals. Normalization: NIST DLMF 10.32.10, specialized to nu=1/3 and positive argument; the cubic theta expansion is DR v3 section 5.1, Eq.(5.6).
