Norm fibers for a fixed number of primary prime factors

Equal norms give b * conjugate b = b₀ * conjugate b₀. Consequently every prime factor of b is one of the prime factors of b₀ or its conjugate. For products of k primary primes this bounds every norm fiber by (2 * k)^k, uniformly in all prime supports and the norm. No general divisor estimate is needed for the structured coefficients.
