The short Möbius identity on Eisenstein ideals

Nonzero Eisenstein ideals are represented by their finite prime-ideal exponent vectors. Their arithmetic functions form the multivariate power series ring: its multiplication is exactly finite Dirichlet convolution. The norm below is the norm of the product of prime representatives, so the support statements retain the actual multiplicative ideal norm.
