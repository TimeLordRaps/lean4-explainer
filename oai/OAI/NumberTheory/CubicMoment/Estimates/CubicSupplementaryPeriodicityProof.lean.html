Modulo 9v periodicity of the actual cubic numerator symbol.

The ramified factor is treated by reciprocity against an auxiliary primary argument. The unit factor follows from Euler's finite-field definition. This proves the elementary specialization recorded in DR v3, §1.2 (1.4)--(1.5), including the zero values at common prime factors.
