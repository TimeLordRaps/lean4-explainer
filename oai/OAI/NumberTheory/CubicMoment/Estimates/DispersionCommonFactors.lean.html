Common factors in the dispersion Poisson identity

The shared Gauss factors cancel before the remaining coprimality condition is removed by the finite ideal Möbius expansion. The formulas hold even if a shared factor also divides a residual factor: the character then vanishes. There is no tacit coprimality assumption on the original pair.
