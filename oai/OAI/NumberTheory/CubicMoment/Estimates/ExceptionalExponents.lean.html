Exponent contradictions in the exceptional character moments

These are the real-variable steps in the proof of the exceptional-character-moment proposition (prop:dual) in An unconditional first moment for cubic Gauss sums. The analytic large-sieve and Gram estimates are separate inputs to these numerical steps.
