Mean values of the squarefree prime-convolution discrepancy

The ordinary Montgomery--Vaughan input is specialized to the actual coefficients obtained from independent prime factors. The full polynomial factors exactly; removing repeated primes costs the energy already proved in RepeatedPrimeSupport. The maximum multiplicity of an integer norm is kept explicit, so no divisor estimate is hidden in this deduction.
