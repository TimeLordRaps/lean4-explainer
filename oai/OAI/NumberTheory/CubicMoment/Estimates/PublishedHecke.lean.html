The finite-order Hecke prime input

The finite-order Hecke prime-sum input specializes to independent prime factors using the elementary character laws.

The input is the cubic-character consequence of Thorner--Zaman, A unified and improved Chebotarev density theorem, Algebra & Number Theory 13 (2019), Theorem 1.4, in the Siegel–Walfisz form for cubic Hecke characters. Only finite-order characters occur in this input; smooth weights and norm twists follow by partial summation.
