    The precise ordinary Hecke inputs needed for contour shifting: right-half-plane series, entireness, the regular-point functional equation, and polynomial growth in each of the required strips. See Watkins (2011) §3.6 and Thorner--Zaman (2019) §2C. The strip-growth bound follows from the completed finite-order property and weak reciprocal-Gamma growth by the Phragmén–Lindelöf theorem.
