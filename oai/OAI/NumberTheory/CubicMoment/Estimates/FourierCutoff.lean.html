A concrete Fourier cutoff kernel

The fixed cutoff is a smooth bump, one near zero and supported in [-1,1]. Its inverse Fourier transform is a normalized Schwartz kernel. Mathlib uses exp(2π i x ξ); this frequency variable differs by 2π from the height variable in the manuscript.
