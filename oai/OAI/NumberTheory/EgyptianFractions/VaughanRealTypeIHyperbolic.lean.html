Actual near-rational estimates for the two Vaughan Type I terms

The prefix adapter uses the sharper canonical harmonic hyperbola bound for its scalar aggregation. The variable hyperbolic lengths are retained throughout. In particular the right hand side has size N / q + A + q, up to logarithmic factors, rather than the much larger fixed-cap estimate obtained by replacing every N / d with N.
