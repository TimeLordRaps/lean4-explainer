    Combine the deterministic and sampled lists according to the level cutoff. The index set is kept fixed, so repeated sampled products retain their full multiplicity. The same choice also changes the auxiliary factor from C to 1. The shifted moment input is uniform over all positive divisors of the common denominator; no moment assumption about the chosen list is added.
