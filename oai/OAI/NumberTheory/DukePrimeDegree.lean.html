Prime-degree torus packets

Equidistribution of complete volume-weighted packets of arbitrary full lattices in totally real fields of fixed prime degree at least five. The packet discriminant is that of its multiplier order. The arithmetic and positive-entropy rigidity inputs are discharged, giving tightness and weak convergence to Haar probability.
