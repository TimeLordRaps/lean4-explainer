source

The injective choice of nontrivial preimages used in Ford, Section 5, p. 25, between (5.20) and (5.21). Candidate sets are partitioned by their totient value. A singleton fiber uses its given nontrivial preimage; on a larger fiber, a cyclic permutation followed by multiplication by a fixed seed preimage supplies distinct nontrivial preimages. The full fiber multiplicity is retained throughout this construction.
