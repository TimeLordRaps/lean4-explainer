source

The finite singleton-fiber reduction in Ford's exclusion (vi). If the candidate primes and the fixed seed primes are normal, a chosen preimage with a nonnormal prime can only come from a singleton candidate-totient fiber. The exceptional candidates therefore give distinct target totients, retaining the multiplicities elsewhere in the construction.
