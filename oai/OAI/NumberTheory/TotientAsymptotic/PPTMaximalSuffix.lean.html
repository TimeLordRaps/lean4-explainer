source

The maximal common-prefix step in Ford, Section 5, between (5.19) and (5.20). The ordering of the candidate primes, rather than a bound for the whole residual product, rules out a repeated leading prime. Choosing the last possible common prefix then forces every nontrivial residual preimage to have a different largest prime.
