The fixed seed introduces no nonnormal prime factors in the rotated-fiber branch of Ford's construction. For a fixed prime, the interval part of normality is eventually vacuous and the small-factor bound follows from B(S) → ∞. This is the elementary seed step in exclusion (vi), p. 27.
