Minimal structural inputs from Ford, The distribution of totients, arXiv:1104.3264v2 (14 July 2013), Theorems 10 and 16.

The exceptional sets count values with some failing preimage, as in the convention preceding Theorem 10, p. 5. Consequently every preimage of a value outside the exceptional set satisfies the property. Ford's coordinates in (4.2), p. 17, are clamped double logarithms. Theorem 16, (6.1)--(6.2), p. 29, also supplies the terminal lower bound that allows removing this clamp.

The band center below is Ford's B*rho^i*(1-i/m), not the phase-dependent bandScale in Basic.lean. Passing between these two centers is an additional analytic step, governed by the manuscript's equation alpha-comparison.
