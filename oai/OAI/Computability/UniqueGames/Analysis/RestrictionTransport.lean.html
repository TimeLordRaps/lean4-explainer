Exact transport of nested affine restrictions

The nested quotient-domain/range inclusion has exactly the effective annihilator and target obtained by comap and map. Its parameterization is linearly equivalent to the direct effective restriction, independently of any Fourier frequency. Thus normalized second moments and the outer average of their squares agree while retaining the full affine translate.
