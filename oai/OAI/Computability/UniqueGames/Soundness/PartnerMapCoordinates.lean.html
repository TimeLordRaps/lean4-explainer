Every partner linear map has independent raw coefficient coordinates

The coefficient index is exactly ConditionalIncidences.RawSlot: one global coefficient, two coefficients at every full position, and one at every single position. The coordinate bijection below is for every actual partner linear map, and its finite-uniform-law theorem has no distributional hypothesis.
