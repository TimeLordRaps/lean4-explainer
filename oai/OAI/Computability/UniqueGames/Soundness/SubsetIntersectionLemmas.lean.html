Exact overlap bound for independently sampled uniform fixed-size subsets

All expectations below are explicit rational sums over Finset.powersetCard. The marginal inclusion probability is derived from the exact binomial count of subsets containing a prescribed singleton. No probabilistic estimate is an assumption. This is the active-set overlap bound used in Lemma 6.3.
