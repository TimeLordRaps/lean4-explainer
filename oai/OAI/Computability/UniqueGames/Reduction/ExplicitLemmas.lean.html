Actual finite linear-map enumeration for the free homogeneous coordinates. The lists contain linear maps themselves, constructed by finite coordinate sums. Their exhaustiveness and lack of repetitions identify the list with the uniform map experiment. No machine running-time claim follows just from their lengths.
