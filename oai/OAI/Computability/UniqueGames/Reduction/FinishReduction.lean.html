The concrete final composition, once the matrix reduction has been proved. This constructor copies the actual occurrence list, subdivides every occurrence into four edges, and runs the full tuple-product machine. Its hypotheses are the matrix reduction's actual value bounds and finite-machine computation. It is an intermediate composition theorem, not the unconditional endpoint.
