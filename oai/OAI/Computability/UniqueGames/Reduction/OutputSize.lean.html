Polynomial encoded length of the actual output

The encoder is the foundations team's unary, delimited forward-permutation format. The instance is the actual list-based reduction, including canonical body deduplication and every repeated edge occurrence. The polynomial's coefficients depend only on the fixed gadget table and tuple dimensions.

These are output-length theorems. They do not assert a machine running-time certificate for canonicalization, enumeration, or serialization.
