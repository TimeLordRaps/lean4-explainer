The exact frame after a tuple body and its field cleanup

Loading replaces the initially empty equation-field tapes. Appending writes only the shared reversed-output header, which is outside those fields. The actual field drains therefore restore the original arena with precisely the same appended bytes. Saved occurrence indices and the odometer survive.
