The binary trace annihilator of a quadratic-block hyperplane

The trace annihilator of the field hyperplane perpendicular to a nonzero vector is exactly its field line. Scaling test vectors first upgrades binary trace orthogonality to field-valued orthogonality; explicit coordinate test vectors then identify the annihilator. No earlier gadget result is used.
