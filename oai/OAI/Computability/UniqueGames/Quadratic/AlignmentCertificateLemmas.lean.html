One polynomial controls all binary linear lifts

For fixed binary dimension, multiply the nonzero obstructions for the finitely many sets of 3r + 1 nonzero inputs. A nonvanishing specialization then obeys the alignment bound simultaneously for every binary linear map. The resulting polynomial and its degree are chosen before the extension field.
