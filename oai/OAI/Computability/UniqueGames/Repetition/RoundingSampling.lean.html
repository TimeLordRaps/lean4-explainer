Correlated rounding of finite partial assignments of equal density. A common finite list of seeds is scanned locally at every vertex. An edge succeeds when the first seed accepted by either endpoint is a jointly accepted good seed. The finite truncation error is at most (1 - density)^n. Maximizing over the finite set of deterministic labelings removes that error. No infinite random seed or assumed rounding principle is used.
