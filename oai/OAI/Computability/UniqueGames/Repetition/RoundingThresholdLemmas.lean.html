source

Exact finite threshold expansion for unit vectors. A shared seed chooses one coordinate and one atom of the common threshold partition. Squared amplitudes are acceptance probabilities, and joint acceptance has the coordinatewise minimum law. The construction permits arbitrary vertex-dependent labels and arbitrary relations between their labels.
