source

Uniform raw advice coefficients obtained by discarding unused flat coordinates. The intercept, both full columns at every position, and one singleton column at every position are independent. For a fixed singleton set, selecting the used columns gives exactly the uniform law on the dynamic raw coefficient type. The identity holds for every joint test function, not merely for marginals.
