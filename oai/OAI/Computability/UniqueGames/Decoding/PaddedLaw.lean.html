Actual linear-map realization of the sparse padded-table experiment. The finite sample has one independent full pair or singleton coefficient at each occurrence position and one independent homogeneous intercept. Its map is formed by composing a genuine map on the raw projected target with the actual parity projection. The third position contributes its parity-dependent intercept; it is not treated as a homogeneous two-coordinate projection.
