The actual visible selector used by the stochastic decoder

At fixed labeling, the matrix table depends only on the occurrence question and complement map. Its normalized row/column witness is selected from the observed row map and row value. Transporting this witness through the actual homogeneous coordinates supplies VisiblePolicies.ResponseWitness; the selected target's affine intercept is retained separately, including exact equality on the slice.
