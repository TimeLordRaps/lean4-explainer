The finite product algebra of dependency breaking.

Revealing one endpoint of each independently sampled question pair turns the remaining question weight into a product of two separate functions, one for each player. Further separate restrictions on the players' answers preserve this product. The final theorem normalizes the displayed weights, rather than assuming conditional independence as an abstract hypothesis.
