The finite base gadget with arbitrarily small nonlinear change

The harmonic rank and genericity error are chosen first. A single finite field then supplies all required genericity statements, and the actual recursion is run to its harmonic depth. The kernel quotient injects the logical alphabet. Every linear map injective there detects at least one quarter of the noise.
