Exact serialization of whole-vertex padding

The original dart rows remain in their original order, with every old tail, reverse index, and relation bit unchanged. The suffix is exactly the bit stream emitted by the checked dummy-vertex machine, starting at vertex n and dart n*d. This is an equality for the actual table codec, with no runtime premise.
