Complete serialization of the actual regularized table

Each global vertex emits its internal ports in increasing order, then its inherited port. Original darts are the first global vertices. Dummy vertices follow in increasing owner and local-dummy order. These are identities for the actual stored rows and existing codec, with arbitrary actual cloud tables.
