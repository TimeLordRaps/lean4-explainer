Compatible block frequencies

A compatible surjection on B × F2 has a unique old surjection and a new vector in the kernel of the partial projection. The new vector does not change its kernel. Consequently actual basis-invariant Fourier coefficients are constant along this fiber, yielding its exact energy multiplicity.
