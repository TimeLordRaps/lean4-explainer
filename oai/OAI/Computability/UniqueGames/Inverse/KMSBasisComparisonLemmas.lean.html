The ordered-basis lift of a Grassmann set

The ambient sample space contains every linear map, including singular maps. The lift accepts exactly maps whose range is one of the specified ell-dimensional subspaces. Thus dependence is rejected, rather than silently conditioning the ambient uniform distribution on independence.
