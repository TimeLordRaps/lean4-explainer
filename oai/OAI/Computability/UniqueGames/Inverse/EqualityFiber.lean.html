Finite equality-fiber extraction for the Grassmann-to-matrix route.

These elementary results do not assume a shortcode inverse theorem. They separate the equality test, the retention of a nonempty output fiber, and the subsequent removal of zero perturbation factors. The dimension-uniform Grassmann expansion theorem remains a separate mathematical obligation.
