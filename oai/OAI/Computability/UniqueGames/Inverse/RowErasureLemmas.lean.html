The row-erasure union bound is eventually strictly below one. The number of descriptions grows exponentially in m, while the tail exponent grows as 2 ^ ((ell-r)*(m-r)). The only asymptotic input is the standard proved limit that a fixed polynomial divided by 2^n tends to zero.
