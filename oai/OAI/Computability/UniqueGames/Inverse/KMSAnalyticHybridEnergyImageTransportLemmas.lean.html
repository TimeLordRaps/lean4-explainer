Product Hybrid energy from actual mixed slices

The derivative's exact rank support is partitioned by its actual compressed image. Each image is put in complement coordinates, and the proved coefficient factorization bounds that contribution by an actual mixed slice. The finite image, extension, and complementary-map counts fit the mixed denominator. The sole analytic premise is the displayed uniform bound on actual slices.
