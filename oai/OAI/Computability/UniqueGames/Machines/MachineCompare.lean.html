A destructive equality test on two actual Bool stacks

Each loop transition pops both stacks and compares only the two finite head registers. A sticky mismatch flag makes the loop continue after disagreement. The final empty/empty test selects an equal or different continuation and resets all comparison registers. The ambient state and every other tape are preserved.
