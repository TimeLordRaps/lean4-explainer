source

Finiteness of every alphabet of the actual machines used by the reduction. FinTM2 already bundles finitely many tapes, control labels and register states, but its alphabet field only requires the input tape alphabet to be finite. This additional property is preserved by the actual sequential and repetition constructors; it does not replace or alter their execution certificates.
