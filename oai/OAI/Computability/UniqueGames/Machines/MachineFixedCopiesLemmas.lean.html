A fixed number of copies of an arbitrary bit stream, by a real finite-alphabet machine. This is the uniform-rounding stream primitive: copying a header-free occurrence stream C times gives multiplicity C to every listed occurrence. The input is preserved during the copy phase, physically drained, and replaced by the forward-order result. No unbounded word is stored in finite control.
