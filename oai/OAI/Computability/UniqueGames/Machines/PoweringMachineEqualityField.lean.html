Actual producer for equality of two bounded walk endpoints

The two port words are fixed finite control. Each executes against the live rotor table from the same preserved starting vertex. Their endpoint fields are compared by the physical unary equality primitive. The emitted field is the Boolean equality of the two actual walk endpoints.

Work tapes may retain arbitrary histories. The table, start, scratch and two comparison copies satisfy exact frame theorems. There is no execution or running-time premise in the composite trace theorem.
