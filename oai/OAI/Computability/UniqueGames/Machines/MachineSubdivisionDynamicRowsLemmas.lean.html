Four-edge subdivision row emission with a physical permutation-table field.

The program depends only on the fixed identity table. Five saved endpoint words and the entire final forward permutation table are physical tape contents. In particular an input-dependent permutation is not inserted into a program label or literal instruction. The last row is oriented v → r; for binary-vector translations its table equals the inverse table of the original u → v edge.
