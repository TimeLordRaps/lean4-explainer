Complete execution of one expander-table row

The actual finite program performs both stored-table lookups, splits their results by its fixed degree, emits the resulting square/zigzag reverse index, and clears its work tapes. Its transition bound is linear in the unary input table length. The output accumulator and finite caller state are arbitrary.
