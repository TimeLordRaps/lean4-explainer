Compile bounded-stack algorithms whose steps may themselves be space-bounded computations. A Flow is just semantics, no computability in its definition; the compilation criterion requires a uniformly computable step on the packed code, in the same unit being bounded. The interpreted machine manipulates ONE GLOBAL word, outside the activation stack. To charge the stack we measure the sizes of suspended (continuation) frame codes.
