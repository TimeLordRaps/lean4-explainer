Exact uniform Fourier transforms and convolution.

The construction formalizes the exact arithmetic algorithms of An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform (OpenAI). The finite phase network originates in the complex construction of Integer multiplication below n log n.

transform_main and convolution_main give fixed finite RAM programs, including preparation and index work, polynomial storage bounds, and a canonical root whose order is computed from the input length. Each includes the paper exponent, decimal power saving, and little-o bound. The _order variants also specify the root order.

The implementation uses a smaller concrete tensor network (32 binary coordinates per factor, 4060 triples, and a 2^50 role envelope) with a stronger saving that implies the stated paper constants. The general rational-algebra extraction, symbolic certificate search, circuit printer, and bounded-printer uniformization from the synthesis appendix are outside this formalization.
