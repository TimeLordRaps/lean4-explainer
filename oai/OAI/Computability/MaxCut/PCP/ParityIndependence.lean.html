Separation of polynomial parity classes

The genericity argument uses monomials with distinct squarefree exponent vectors. Polynomial coefficients in the original «variables» become polynomials in squares after adjoining their square roots. The lemmas below prove the resulting coefficient separation directly, without assuming an algebraic independence witness.
