Exact row enumeration and accumulator contents for an expander-table step

The outer loop enumerates original vertices, and the inner loop enumerates cloud/port positions in increasing mixed-radix order. The resulting list is the actual next table's stored rotation-word list. Prefix and reverse-encoding identities describe data accumulated by a row loop; they assert no runtime.
