Walsh diagonalization of actual Boolean-cube Cayley graphs

Every port is an indexed generator. The rotation keeps the port and adds its generator by pointwise xor, so loops and repeated generators keep their full multiplicity. The Fourier multiplier and energy estimate are proved for this actual averaging operator; no Fourier/operator compatibility is assumed.
