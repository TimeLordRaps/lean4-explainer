A concrete finite family from the Cayley base

The initial graph has one vertex and the fixed number of loop ports. Each subsequent graph is the actual square-and-zigzag construction. Consequently the vertex sizes are exact powers of one fixed integer, including size one. The finite base is selected from the proved existence theorem.
