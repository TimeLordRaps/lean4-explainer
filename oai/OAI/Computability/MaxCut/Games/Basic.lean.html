Finite two-player games with actual joint question distributions.

An answer function receives only its own player's question. In a repeated game, that question is the entire tuple, so a repeated strategy need not act separately on coordinates. The predicate may reject invalid answers depending on questions. All probabilities below are actual finite sums of real weights.
