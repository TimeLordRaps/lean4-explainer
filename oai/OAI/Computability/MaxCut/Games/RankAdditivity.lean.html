Rank-additive pairs of linear maps

The rank-additive selector in Appendix A.1 has a concrete geometric meaning: the two image spaces are disjoint and the two kernels span the domain. These lemmas apply over any field and arbitrary finite dimensions, in particular over ZMod 2 with the second map equal to Y + X.
