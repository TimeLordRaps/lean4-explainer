The independent graph product in the last step of the v2 reduction.

Edges are tuples of actual base edges. The endpoint maps remain injective, the edge law is exactly uniform, and the associated game agrees with ordinary parallel repetition for every pair of local strategies, including strategies depending on their whole question tuples. Only the completeness lower bound uses coordinatewise strategies; no repetition soundness bound is assumed here.
