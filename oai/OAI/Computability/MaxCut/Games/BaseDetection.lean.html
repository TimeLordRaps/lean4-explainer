From full character lifts to linear-map detection

If a target linear map is injective on the logical alphabet, a linear left inverse of that restriction turns all logical characters into a full lift. Detection by this lift implies that the target map does not annihilate the noise. The left inverse is proved to exist by the vector-space splitting theorem; it is not an additional premise.
