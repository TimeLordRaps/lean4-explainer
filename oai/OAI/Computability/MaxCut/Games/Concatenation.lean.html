Common-parent factorization before fresh orientation

This is the linear algebra in the lift-factorization lemma of v2 Section 2. The map gamma is obtained from the whole parent family and the first aggregate coordinate. It is chosen once, before any child is selected. No rank, genericity, or independence assumption is needed for this step.
