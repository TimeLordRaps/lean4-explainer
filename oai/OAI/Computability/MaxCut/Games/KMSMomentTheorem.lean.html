The actual upper fourth-moment theorem for the full-rank lift of a Grassmann set. Its hypotheses are literal tuple probabilities. The affine sampling law, mixed estimate and hybrid derivative estimate are all discharged by proved lemmas; there is no analytic estimate among the hypotheses.
