Affine word emission in the actual clone-table machine

The shared machine keeps its context fixed while copying one saved word. Its finite transducer therefore acts by a fixed symbol substitution. The lemmas below identify that substitution with the established unary encoding of offset + scale * value, including all header and equation-field contexts. Scale, offset, and the triple-table length remain symbolic.
