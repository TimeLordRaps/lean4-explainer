Canonical output for finite Boolean machines

A raw machine may halt with retained metadata and a changed finite state. This wrapper redirects its halt to a fixed list of actual tape drains, then executes one state-reset instruction. The output tape is preserved and the result is literally haltList. Its cost follows from the raw execution and the maximum number of pushes in a raw statement.

The cleanup list is fixed with the program, not selected from the input. It may contain repetitions and may be empty when output is the only tape.
