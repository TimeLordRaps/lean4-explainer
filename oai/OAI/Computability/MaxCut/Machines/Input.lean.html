Serialization boundaries and direct row lookup for the shared executable fixed-alphabet graph codec. The alphabet is a fixed parameter. Rows remain in their stored order, including repeated darts and loops. Word offsets are fixed; bit offsets are represented by actual encoded prefixes because unary fields have data-dependent lengths. These are data identities, not runtime certificates.
