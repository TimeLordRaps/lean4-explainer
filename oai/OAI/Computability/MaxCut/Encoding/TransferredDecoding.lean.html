Decoding an actual transferred column slice

The point Mstar used to parametrize a transferred slice is distinct from the canonical visible row base observedBase A rows. The right strategy uses only the latter. Their kernel-valued difference appears solely in the analysis. The exact quotient equivalence transports uniform slice agreement, including the affine offset, to the conditional private-decoder theorem.
