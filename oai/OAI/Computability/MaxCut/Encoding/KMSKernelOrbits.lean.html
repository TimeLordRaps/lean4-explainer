Equal kernels give the same orbit under invertible postcomposition. The canonical range equivalence comes from the first isomorphism theorem; Mathlib's finite-dimensional subspace extension theorem then extends it to an automorphism of the ambient codomain. No orbit or counting premise is assumed.
