    Source documentation

    Strip a given prime multiset, updating Euler's totient one prime factor at a time. The supplied list may contain redundant factors.

    def totientScan : List ℕ → ℕ → ℕ
  | [], _ => 1
  | p::ps, v => if p ∣ v then
      (if p ∣ v/p then p else p-1)*totientScan ps (v/p)
    else totientScan ps v
