Five-ball Hausdorff comparison for integral varifolds

Integral varifolds are modeled by rectifiable weighted measures and tangent projections. Smooth graphs provide the regular-locus model. The supporting results establish local finiteness, Hausdorff domination, first-variation integrability, and smooth graph embeddings.

A five-ball covering gives quantitative Hausdorff comparison from local lower ball-mass bounds, and localization gives the corresponding null-set comparison for integral varifolds. The lower bounds and weight-nullness are hypotheses: stationary monotonicity, weighted almost-everywhere regularity, and the geometric-measure bridge are not proved here. Thus neither stationary integral-varifold almost-everywhere regularity nor its round-sphere corollary is established.
