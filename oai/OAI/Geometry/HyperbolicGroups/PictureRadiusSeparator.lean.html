  1. theorem OAI.HyperbolicObstruction.PictureSwitch.PictureMap.bounded_radius_weighted_separator line 28

    Source documentation

    Weighted bounded-radius planar separator for LITERAL genus-zero rotation systems. If faces have at most k sides and a spanning tree has radius R, at most k*(R+1) actual vertices separate every connected remainder into mass at most one half. Loops and parallel edges are allowed in the original map. No planar drawing/separator conclusion is assumed.
