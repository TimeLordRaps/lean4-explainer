    Split repeated vertex visits in selected kinds of closed polygon walks. Only face permutations change; all oriented steps, occurrence indices, edge pairings and therefore every additive cone-step chain are retained literally. Unselected faces retain their entire cyclic parametrization. The normalization is a finite maximization, not an assumed simple-polygon representative.
