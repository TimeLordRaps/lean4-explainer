Deleted-face retractions and preliminaries for anticanonical indices

The coordinate invariant Dolbeault index is defined, with high-degree vanishing proved. The deleted-face deformation retraction and contractibility are supporting results, alongside positive-weight lattice fibers, compactness, polarization and polynomial continuation. No theorem identifies this geometric index with a polynomial: Euler-characteristic cancellation, equivariant Dolbeault localization and the weighted Ehrhart step remain unproved. The metric and direct-image arguments for pseudoeffective-error conversion are also absent.
