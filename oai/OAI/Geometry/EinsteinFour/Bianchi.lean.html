Concrete four-vector coordinates for a Bianchi gradient

The three quaternion actions are left multiplication by i,j,k on the real four-dimensional Euclidean space. A symmetric matrix of four-vectors is encoded by its three diagonal and three off-diagonal entries. Its divergence constraint is the actual quaternionic Bianchi constraint of Section 2.
