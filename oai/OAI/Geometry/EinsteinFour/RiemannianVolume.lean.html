The measure in the geometric application is fixed by the metric. In four Euclidean dimensions the diameter-normalized Hausdorff measure is multiplied by the unit-ball factor (pi^2 / 2) / 2^4. Positivity and finiteness are kept separate from the elementary probability normalization.
