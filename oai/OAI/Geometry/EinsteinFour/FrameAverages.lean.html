Spherical disintegration of the actual frame-curvature energies.

The explicit published input BrinKarcherAveraging is the specialization of the Riemannian-base times Stiefel probability measure in Brin--Karcher (1984), Section 1, p.275, to functions depending only on the first frame direction. It is a generic integration statement and contains no curvature estimate. The angular curvature identities used below are proved separately.
