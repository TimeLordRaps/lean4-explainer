Positive dual cones and their Laplace integrals (paper Piece 2, §01)

The measure is ordinary Lebesgue (statements proved for any compatible Lebesgue normalization where useful). "ProperCone" here is Mathlib's closed cone containing zero; the extra paper hypotheses (solid and contains no line) are imposed as needed. In the main application both C and its positive dual have specified interior points.
