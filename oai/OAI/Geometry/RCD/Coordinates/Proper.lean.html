Properness from the actual curvature-dimension volume bound

The existing FullCD.ball_ratio theorem bounds the mass of every small ball centered in a bounded region from below. Disjoint small balls therefore bound every separated set. The resulting finite nets prove total boundedness; completeness then gives properness. No compactness hypothesis is added.
