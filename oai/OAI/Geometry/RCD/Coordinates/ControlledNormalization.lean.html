Exact normalization retaining arbitrarily small splitting error

The two published harmonic inputs are applied with compatible thresholds. Metric flatness supplies the no-extra-factor hypothesis by a proved finite configuration argument. The resulting normalization acts on the actual Sobolev column, and its pointwise coordinate map approximates the metric.
