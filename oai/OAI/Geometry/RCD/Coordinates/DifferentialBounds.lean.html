Quantitative bounds for the differential update

The vectors v, v1, and v2 stand for a field and its first and second directional derivatives. The operators K, K1, and K2 stand for the inverse transpose and its directional derivatives. Their stated bounds are used directly, with an arbitrary real-valued dual-size function. The algebra does not assume the desired increment or recurrence estimates.
