Exact mean normalizations and their metric consequences

An exact normalization is the identity for the mean Sobolev Gram operator, as in Huang--Huang, Theorem 4.1(2). All conclusions below are derived from that identity. In particular, nondegeneracy of the original Gram operator is not separately assumed.
