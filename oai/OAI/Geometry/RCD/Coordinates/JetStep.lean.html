Scalar all-order jet step

At Gevrey exponent σ = 2, the factorial weights in an increment leave only the factor (m + 1)^3 relative to the allowed jet size. The next dyadic scale contributes 2 / 2^m. A single small parameter controls every order m ≥ 2. The differential estimates producing the old jet and increment are explicit premises of the scalar theorem below.
