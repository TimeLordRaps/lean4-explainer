Localization of active coordinate patches

A cutoff support bounds the finely normalized displacement. Coarse harmonic separation and sublinear growth of the normalizations then bound the original metric distance. The squared growth hypothesis below is the exponent-one bound for squared norms (equivalently, exponent one-half for norms).
