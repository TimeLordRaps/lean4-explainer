Directional estimates for finite patch fields

The finite set is the set of active patches at the point under consideration. All metric norms are frozen using a continuous linear equivalence T. The hypotheses are the individual displacement energies, cutoff derivative bounds, overlap bound, and positive mass bounds; no field estimate is assumed.
