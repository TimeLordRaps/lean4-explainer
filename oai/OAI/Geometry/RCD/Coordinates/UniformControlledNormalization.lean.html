Uniform normalization at nearby centers

A single harmonic map on the enlarged initial ball supplies the maps at all nearby centers. Only the volume-ratio cost is paid when its first-order splitting data are restricted. The resulting normalizers and approximation bounds are uniform in the center and in every smaller scale.
