Patch fields as input to the analytic iteration

The frozen covector transformation sends the actual patch field to an actual cutoff-affine patch field with conjugated positive operators. The estimates here use only patch geometry and cutoff derivatives; no coordinate iterate or estimate for its derivatives is an input.
