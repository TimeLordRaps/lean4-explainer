Absolute continuity of the Euclidean-normalized volume profile

Bishop--Gromov and a positive Lipschitz comparison model make ball volume Lipschitz on every compact interval of positive radii. Multiplication by the smooth Euclidean normalization then gives the absolute continuity needed by the radial source budget.
