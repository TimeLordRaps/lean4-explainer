Euclidean Gevrey cutoffs of order two

L. Hörmander, Between distributions and hyperfunctions, Astérisque 131 (1985), pp. 89–106, §2, equations (2.2) and (2.6), p. 90, describes nonquasianalytic Gevrey classes containing compactly supported functions. Squaring a nonzero bump and convolving its normalized dilation with a ball indicator gives a fixed core and support. Equivalence of coordinate and finite-dimensional multilinear derivative bounds changes only the exponential constant.
