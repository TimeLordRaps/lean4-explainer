Frozen primal and dual norms

A continuous linear equivalence T represents the frozen square root of the source metric. The primal norm is ‖T x‖, and the dual norm is ‖(T.symm).adjoint w‖. Congruence by the inverse of T transports the positive-form bounds to Euclidean norms without a condition-number loss. No construction or differentiation of a matrix square root is required.
