The local measure Laplacian of a regular Gram entry

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.regularBochner_measure_laplacian line 21

    Source documentation

    Local Sobolev representatives of the Laplacians turn the common Bochner identity into the full distributional equation for each Gram entry. Global derivatives of the chosen extensions are not required.
