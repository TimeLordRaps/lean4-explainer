Fixed centers for directional patch energies

This is the selection in equation re:fixed-center: a positive lower energy floor permits one fixed point in each patch with energy at most twice every active point's energy. No continuity or measurability of the energy function is used. Directional pair bounds then give the actual fixed-center bound used to construct the correction fields.
