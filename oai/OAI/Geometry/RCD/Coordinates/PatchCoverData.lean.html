Localized finite patch data from a uniform volume regime

The centers form a maximal finite net. Cutoffs are affine pullbacks of a fixed Euclidean cutoff. The half-error volume regime, coarse separation, and slow normalization growth bound the overlap. The smooth cutoff has core radius two and support radius three.
