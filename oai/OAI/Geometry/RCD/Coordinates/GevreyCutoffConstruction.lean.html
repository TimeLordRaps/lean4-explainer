Construction of a Euclidean Gevrey cutoff

Convolve the normalized small Gevrey bump with the indicator of the radius-5/2 ball. The resulting function is one on the radius-two ball, vanishes outside radius three, and retains the order-two derivative bound. This constructs the exact cutoff used in the chart iteration.
