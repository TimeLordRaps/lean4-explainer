Pair segments from harmonic-coordinate ellipsoids

The final analytic argument only needs a coordinate segment to stay in the larger working region, with comparable frozen metrics along it. Central ellipsoid containment and the nearby-center comparison imply precisely those facts. The source of the final chart may be a smaller open subset.
