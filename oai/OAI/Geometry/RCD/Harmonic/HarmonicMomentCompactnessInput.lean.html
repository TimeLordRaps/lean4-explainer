The local harmonic-compactness input, restricted to finite moments

Ambrosio--Honda (2018), Local spectral convergence in RCD*(K,N) spaces, Theorem 4.2, Theorem 4.4 and Corollary 4.5, specialized to a Euclidean measured limit. Strong local L² convergence implies convergence of the pairings with constants and the bounded coordinate functions. The hypothesis below retains the local rescaled H¹ bound needed by those theorems. It asserts neither an affine approximation nor a chart.
