Uniform affine improvement from published harmonic compactness

The threshold is uniform in the center, radius, coordinates and harmonic function. All inputs are measured approximation, a rescaled curvature bound, harmonicity and an actual normalized L² bound. The affine estimate itself is proved by a compactness contradiction.
