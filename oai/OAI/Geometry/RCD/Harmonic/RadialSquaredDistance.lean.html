Applying the radial test to an actual squared-distance representative

The squared-distance function is cut off only at the larger working radius. Locality transfers its radial pairing to each smaller ball. The resulting mass estimate takes precisely the local weak Laplacian-defect identity and the eikonal input, rather than an assumed annular source formula.
