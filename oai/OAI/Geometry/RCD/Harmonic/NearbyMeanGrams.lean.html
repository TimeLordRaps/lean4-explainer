Comparing Gram means inside a common normalized region

Averaged Gram errors on an enlarged ball restrict to nearby balls with only the volume-ratio cost. If one coordinate normalization makes both resulting means close to the identity, their original quadratic forms are close to each other. These are the comparison steps after harmonic transformation.
