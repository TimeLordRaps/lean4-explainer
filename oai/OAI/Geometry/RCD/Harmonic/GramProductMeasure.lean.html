The full measure equation for a product Gram entry

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.gram_product_measure line 21

    The Gram entry of a product is represented by genuine Sobolev products. Its local Laplacian keeps both weighted full measures and the ordinary cross terms.
