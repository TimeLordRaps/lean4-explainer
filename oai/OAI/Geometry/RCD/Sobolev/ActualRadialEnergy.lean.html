Interior energy of the actual Poisson radial fits

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.actual_radial_fit_energy line 22

    The actual Poisson radial derivative has an interior gradient fit controlled by the distance value fit and the original Poisson defect. The harmonic gradient constant is chosen before all centers and scales.
