Recovering the dimensional density from the weak Bochner test inequality

The only analytic input here is the scalar finite-dimensional Bochner test inequality, as in Han, JGA 28 (2018), Theorem 3.3. The proof identifies its absolutely continuous density while retaining the full positive measure.
