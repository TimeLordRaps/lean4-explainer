Weak radial cancellation and its harmonic correction

The local Hessian test formula is equation (3.1.10) in Gigli (2018). The cancellation itself is proved here: constant Laplacian and harmonicity make the two integration-by-parts terms vanish, and continuity extends the identity from the regular test core to the actual Dirichlet space.
