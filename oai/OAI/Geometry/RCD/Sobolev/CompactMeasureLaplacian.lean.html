Compactly supported measure Laplacians and continuous Sobolev tests

IsMeasureLaplacian records the distributional integration-by-parts identity on continuous compactly supported Sobolev tests. It is a definition, not an existence assumption. The cutoff argument extends this identity to arbitrary continuous Sobolev representatives when the function and its full signed Laplacian have compact support. In particular it applies to positive-time kernel rows without removing singular mass.
