Quantitative cutoff input and its canonical Bochner application

The existence input is the fixed radius-ratio specialization of Mondino-- Naber (2019), Lemma 3.1 (arXiv:1405.2222v3, pp.13--14), also stated in Connell et al. (2021), Lemma 2.6. Scaling to unit radius and lowering the curvature bound to -1 makes its constant dimensional. Support of the Laplacian off the transition region is the locality of the weak Laplacian.
