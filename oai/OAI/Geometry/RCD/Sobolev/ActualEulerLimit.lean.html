Passing the actual Sobolev Euler equation to the Euclidean limit

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.PoissonHarmonicLimit.euler_equation line 20

    Joint compactness, the actual projected Sobolev equation, and a vanishing normalized error imply the limiting Euler equation. There is no assumption of an Euler equation for the limiting model.
