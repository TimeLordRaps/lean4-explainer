Shifted Hessian domination from the scalar dimensional Bochner bound

The same quadratic perturbation used for the unshifted Hessian works in the positive form augmented by a scalar metric shift. Thus the scalar finite-dimensional Bochner inequality suffices; a separate identification of the local dimension is not needed for this step.
