  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.no_euler_countersequence line 23

    There is no normalized orthogonal Euler countersequence satisfying the actual analytic preconditions of Ambrosio--Honda compactness. The coefficient subsequence is extracted after the joint function limit, so all pairings and the nonzero norm refer to one and the same limit.
