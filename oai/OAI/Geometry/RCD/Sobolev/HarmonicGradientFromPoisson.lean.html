The harmonic L² gradient input from the existing Poisson and mean inputs

A fixed small ball around each interior point has uniformly comparable volume. The harmonic mean estimate bounds its values, and the constant-zero case of the Poisson gradient estimate bounds its gradient. A countable open subcover combines the local almost-everywhere estimates.
