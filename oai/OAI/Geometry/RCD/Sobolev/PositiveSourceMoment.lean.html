The moment estimate used for positive Poisson sources

A weak power-tail bound of exponent d gives every smaller positive moment. In the Poisson argument the tail follows by testing with a truncation and applying the Dirichlet Sobolev inequality. The constants below do not depend on an upper bound for the solution.
