The Hessian input for the finite Poisson span

Gigli (2018), equation (3.1.10) and Theorem 3.3.8, give the local Hessian pairing and its Bochner bound. Han (2018), Proposition 3.2 and Theorem 3.3, give the top-dimensional trace identity and the lower-dimensional trace defect. Completing the trace-defect square and applying integral Cauchy--Schwarz gives the displayed bound on the integrated pairing on every concentric inner ball, using the same canonical measure. This localization is needed to apply cutoff mass bounds before constructing the harmonic correction. The tensor pairing is restricted to the finite harmonic and Poisson span.
