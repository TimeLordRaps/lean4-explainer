L² Laplacians of products of bounded Sobolev functions

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.IsWeakLaplacian.product line 18

    The product rule retains the mixed gradient term. A bounded gradient of one factor suffices to make the resulting weak Laplacian belong to L².
