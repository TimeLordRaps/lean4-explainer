The local L2 Poincare input used by the Euler forcing

Rajala (2012), Calc. Var. PDE 44, 477--494, Theorem 1.2 supplies the weak local Poincare inequality. The local doubling bound and the Sobolev--Poincare consequence in Hajlasz--Koskela (2000), Theorem 5.1, give this L2 specialization on a smaller ball. Eightfold interior room is retained; only bounded rescaled curvature is used. The extension from Lipschitz functions to the canonical Sobolev space is by relaxation.
