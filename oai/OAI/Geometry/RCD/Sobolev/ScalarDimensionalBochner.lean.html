The scalar finite-dimensional Bochner inequality and its measure form

Han, JGA 28 (2018), Theorem 3.3, gives the scalar Bakry--Émery inequality on compact Lipschitz functions with L² Laplacian. The finite-family measure identities use the full positive regular Bochner measure.
