Absorbing both errors in the weak transport balance

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.weighted_transport_energy line 17

    Integration and two weighted Young inequalities give a quadratic bound. All weights and the weak balance are explicit; the radial cutoff supplies them in the application. In particular no division by the inner norm occurs.
