The quadratic remainder for finite Rényi reweighting

For the negative power of a positive density, its matching primitive is (q-1)/q * r^q. Multiplying the power derivative by the initial density leaves only a quadratic remainder. This is the scalar estimate needed to identify the transport first variation.
