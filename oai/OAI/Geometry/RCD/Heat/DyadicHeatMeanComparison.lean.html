Uniform comparison along one fixed-root dyadic family

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.dyadic_heat_mean_comparison line 20

    A single fixed coordinate map and its exact dyadic normalizers give uniform heat comparisons at all sufficiently fine scales. The outer normalizer is used only for the working-frame calculation.
