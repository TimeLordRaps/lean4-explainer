Canonical bounded-data heat representatives

The actual Cheeger semigroup and the proved reverse Poincare estimate give continuous representatives on every RCD space in this application. Full support makes the representative unique; positivity, linearity and the Markov amplitude bound therefore hold at every point. No heat kernel is assumed in this construction.
