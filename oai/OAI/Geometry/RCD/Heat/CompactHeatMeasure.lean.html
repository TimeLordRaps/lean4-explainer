A Radon measure representing each bounded heat evaluation

The positive functional is the actual heat semigroup on compact continuous data. Riesz--Markov constructs its representing measure, without assuming a kernel or a density. Subsequent density arguments can therefore work with the canonical measure rather than adding an existence premise.
