Interior Lipschitz control for heat flow of an annular source

  1. theorem OAI.RegularPointCoordinates.NoncollapsedRCD.annular_heat_difference line 19

    A source supported away from an interior ball has a uniformly Lipschitz heat evolution there, all the way down to arbitrarily small positive time.
