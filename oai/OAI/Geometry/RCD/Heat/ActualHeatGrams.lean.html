Heat averages of the canonical Sobolev Gram form

The published kernel input is the specialization of Jiang--Li--Zhang, Heat kernel bounds on metric measure spaces and some applications (2016), Section 2.2, printed p. 7, to L¹ ∩ L² data and a fixed positive time. See https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.5289v2 . The Gaussian and gradient bounds of Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 1.2 are separate quantitative inputs, not part of the definition below.
