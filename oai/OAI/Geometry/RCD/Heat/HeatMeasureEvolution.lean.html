The heat identity for a full compactly supported signed Laplacian

The distributional source is paired with continuous positive-time kernel rows. Locality supplies the compact test, and the actual L2 heat equation supplies the derivative. This proves the positive-endpoint measure-source identity without assuming absolute continuity of the source.
