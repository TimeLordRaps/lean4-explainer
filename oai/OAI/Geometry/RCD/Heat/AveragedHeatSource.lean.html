Averaged measure-source heat identity including time zero

The average of the positive kernel over a fixed finite-volume set is bounded by its reciprocal volume, uniformly at all positive times. This bounds its pairing with any finite signed measure. The genuine L2 heat flow gives continuity at zero, so the source identity extends to the initial endpoint without a pointwise kernel limit.
