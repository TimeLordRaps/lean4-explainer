The dyadic volume-defect budget

Successive volume increments telescope, while the rescaled curvature contribution is a geometric series. Both monotone bounded profiles and raw profiles converging to a bounded density are treated. This is the scalar summation argument in the proof of the manuscript's Poisson pinching lemma.
