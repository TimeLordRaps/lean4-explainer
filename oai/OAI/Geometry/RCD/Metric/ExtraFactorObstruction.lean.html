An extra Euclidean factor contradicts a sufficiently flat n-dimensional ball

This uses the actual pointed approximation predicate: lift n+1 orthogonal unit vectors through its density clause, then apply the finite-dimensional distance obstruction. No topological dimension theorem is assumed.
