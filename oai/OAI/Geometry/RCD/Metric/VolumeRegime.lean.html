Volume convergence and the all-center volume regime

The volume-convergence hypothesis is the fixed-space rescaling specialization of De Philippis--Gigli (2018), Theorems 1.2 and 1.3. It concerns ball volumes in a Euclidean pointed limit. Bishop--Gromov comparison and the density upper bound then control all smaller balls around every nearby center.
