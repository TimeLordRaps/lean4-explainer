Uniformly flat negative-curvature comparison volumes

The dimension is m + 1. The radial factor for curvature -m * a ^ 2 is written as the primitive of cosh (a * t), which also covers a = 0 without a removable singularity. Its volume is normalized by the volume of the Euclidean unit ball. The bounds below are uniform down to radius zero, as required before applying almost-volume rigidity.
