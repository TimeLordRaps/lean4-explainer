Compact inward distance and its squared-distance primitive

The inward distance to the boundary of a metric ball is a compactly supported Lipschitz function. Its quadratic primitive agrees with squared distance, up to a constant, on the ball. This realizes radial tests in the global Sobolev space without pretending the unbounded distance itself is in L2.
