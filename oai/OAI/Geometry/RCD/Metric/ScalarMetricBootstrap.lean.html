Finite-scale scalar metric bootstrap

Fixing a testing point and a direction turns the manuscript's matrix estimates into real sequences. Here z is the local mass in that direction, d is the weighted defect, and b is the normalized diagonal second derivative. The signed first-order errors and nonnegative squared increment are kept explicit.

Finite summation and Cauchy--Schwarz estimates close the bootstrap from these scalar premises, without further geometric assumptions.
