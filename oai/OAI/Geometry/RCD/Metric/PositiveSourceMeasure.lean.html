Positive measure sources and bounded Dirichlet tests

Bounded clipping of the Lipschitz core converges in L2 with uniformly bounded Sobolev norm. Weak energy convergence therefore extends a source mass bound to every bounded nonnegative Dirichlet test. This argument needs no values of an arbitrary Sobolev representative on a singular source measure.
