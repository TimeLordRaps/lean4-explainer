Shared coarse array evaluation

A missing array is a constant-zero marker. An expanded child is evaluated once, then transformed by the two linear sweeps, thresholded, and added to its siblings. The instrumented counter charges rational arithmetic and comparisons; allocation, source-width preprocessing and integer addressing are separate parts of the machine implementation.
