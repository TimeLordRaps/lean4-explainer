An edit-distance approximation scheme conditional on finite scalar Bernstein concentration (Tropp, ACM217, corrected 2023, Theorem 4.16). The same uniform binary algorithm is used in all four clauses.

  1. theorem OAI.EditApproximation.almostLinearEditDistance_conditional line 18

    For each fixed binary accuracy in (0,1), the concrete uniform algorithm has success probability at least 2/3, deterministic zero on equal inputs, worst-case expected almost-linear time including preprocessing and sampling, and polynomial space on every outcome. Alphabet bounds are analysis parameters, not inputs to the algorithm. The finite initial range uses exact suffix DP.
