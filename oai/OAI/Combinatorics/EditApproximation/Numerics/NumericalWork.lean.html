Concrete numerical representation and arithmetic work

These are integer bit-length and exact rational-operation bounds for the finite optimizer. They are not a RAM theorem: the costed evaluator below counts rational arithmetic operations, while bit-operation costs also require the charged evaluation model used by the surrounding algorithm.
