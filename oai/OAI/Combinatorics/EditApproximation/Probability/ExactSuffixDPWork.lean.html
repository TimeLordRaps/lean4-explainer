A costed implementation of the exact cut dynamic program. The counter counts symbol comparisons and list-cell visits, with explicit outer-loop overhead. Each counted transition uses a fixed number of word operations; the surrounding RAM realization supplies that constant. Suffix lookups and occurrence scans are charged rather than treated as constant-time arrays.
