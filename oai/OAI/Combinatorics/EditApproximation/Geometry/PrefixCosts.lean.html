Finite prefix costs used by the predicted bands

A prefix is a restriction of an enumerated wide action. The last jump is evaluated at the proposed real shift. Taking the minimum over the actual finite list, with its first member duplicated as the fallback, gives the exact prefix minimum and its 1-Lipschitz property.
