Executable shared group estimates

All dyadic powers in the prescribed closed range are enumerated, including negative exponents. A scale is evaluated exactly when its sample count reaches the child count; otherwise one shared array of uniform labels is used for every member of the group. The selected member is the first minimizer in the fixed list order.
