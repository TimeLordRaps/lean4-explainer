Connection costs with arbitrary gaps and overlaps

This proves the edit-cost part of the connection inequality of Section 2 for actual substrings and edit scripts. Prefixes telescope across the child intervals; common-prefix cancellation then removes the outside prefix. The children need not have ordered or shared target endpoints.
