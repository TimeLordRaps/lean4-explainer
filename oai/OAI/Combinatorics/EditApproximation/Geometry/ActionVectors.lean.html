The actual fixed-block action hull

Coordinates are precisely the child/state pairs occurring in a finite band. Each action assigns its fixed rational child mass to one state per child. This verifies the probability, coverage and block-mass hypotheses used by the numerical optimizer and optimistic entropy regret.
