Actual comparison prefixes in the finite wide action family

The cut is the start of the remaining comparator suffix, or the parent's end when that suffix is empty. Splitting at this cut preserves the literal connection budget. Splicing any enumerated prefix to the comparator suffix remains an enumerated wide action.
