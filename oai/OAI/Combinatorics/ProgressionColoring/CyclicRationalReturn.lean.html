Primitive rational paths for the cyclic representatives

A closest positive return to a rectangle in the two representative systems gives a primitive integer return vector. When the two displacements satisfy the dilation relation, the first numerator is already primitive. Reducing it modulo the return time gives the finite rational label and the two full paths.
