Literal support of recorded patterns

A pattern records either a label or none at each position. Its support is exactly the set of recorded labels. If no recorded label occurs twice, counting labels is equivalent to counting regular positions, also after restricting to one color. Thus quarter-balancing the support balances the actual positions.
