The actual outer-coloring dichotomy

The coloring is the selected coloring of literal full labels. A progression with many light positions is balanced by its global word test. Otherwise its actual closest heavy-label return supplies a primitive rational path. Short periods lift affinely; long periods satisfy the actual eligible block tests.
