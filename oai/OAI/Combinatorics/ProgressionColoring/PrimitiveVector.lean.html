Primitive rational vectors

A denominator and an integer coordinate vector are primitive if they have no common natural divisor greater than one. Multiplying such a vector by an integer not divisible by its denominator leaves a nonintegral coordinate. Every such coordinate stays at least the reciprocal denominator from every integer.
