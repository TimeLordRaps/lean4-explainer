Affinity of representatives inside a small box

The input is the integer-valued cross difference of three representatives of an arithmetic progression modulo one. A box of width 2 * H makes this integer strictly smaller than one in absolute value, so it vanishes. Interpolation then produces one real affine lift on all selected indices.
