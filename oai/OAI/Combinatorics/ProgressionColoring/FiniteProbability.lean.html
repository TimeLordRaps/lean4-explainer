Finite weighted probability and independent Bernoulli coordinates

Events are finite sets and their weights are finite sums. Normalization and nonnegativity are explicit hypotheses for the generic probability inequalities. The product identities are algebraic, so their statements also hold at the boundary parameters zero and one without dividing by any point weight.
