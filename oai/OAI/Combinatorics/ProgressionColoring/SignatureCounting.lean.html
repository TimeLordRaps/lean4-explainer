Counting literal signatures on affine progressions

Only coefficient triples whose evaluations lie in the specified bounded interval are counted. Integer threshold comparisons, including equality, determine their floor words. The proved arrangement estimate in dimension three gives 25 * (k * (B + 1) + 1)^8; no cubic arrangement bound is asserted.

The full label word and the floor word determine each original color and literal (box, integer band) key. The product count never renames keys.
