Traces of actual orthogonal projections

The trace calculation uses the algebraic theorem for a projection onto a submodule. In particular, no spectral decomposition or matrix norm instance is needed. Matrix transport is valid in every basis, and hence in the orthonormal basis used for the weighted grading.
