Scalar inequalities for the one-sided coupling estimate

The increasing function couplingPotential m t = (t-m)|t-m|/2 controls squared distances from below and has a trapezoidal increment bound from above. All arguments here are algebraic, by splitting according to the positions of the endpoints relative to the center m.
