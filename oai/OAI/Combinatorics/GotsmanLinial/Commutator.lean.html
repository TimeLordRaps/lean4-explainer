Entry bounds for a tridiagonal grading commutator

All norms here are norms of individual complex entries. No default norm on a matrix is used. A complete orthogonal family defines a phase unitary with eigenvalues I^k. For a tridiagonal operator the commutator is the difference of two unitary conjugates divided by 2 I.
