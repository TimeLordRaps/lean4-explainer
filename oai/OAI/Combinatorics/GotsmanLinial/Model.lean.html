Polynomial thresholds and average sensitivity

The Boolean cube encodes the sign cube {−1,1}^n: false represents −1 and true represents 1. The public statement quantifies directly over real multilinear polynomials and uses uniform counting probability, the convention sign(0) = 1, and the exact constant 8.

GotsmanLinialStatement is a proposition definition, not a proof of the bound.
