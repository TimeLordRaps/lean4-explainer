Exterior monomials, color counts and finite stable-graph bounds

The color-incidence set indexes slots in triples of colors. Its lexicographic enumeration defines the color word and is used to count occurrences and word-preserving permutations. The exterior-algebra constructions prove nonvanishing of a factorial-scaled monomial, not a detector evaluation.

The strata class Y, its realizations, Pixton relation span, detector pairing and geometric vanishing are not defined here. These combinatorial results therefore do not assert the geometric main theorem or cohomological corollary.
