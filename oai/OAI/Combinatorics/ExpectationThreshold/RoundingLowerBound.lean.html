The deterministic lower bound for rounding

A weighted average at most two places at least half the mass on subsets with average at most four. On those subsets the quotient-of-powers product is at least one, yielding the lower bound one quarter for the full product score. This statement is pointwise in the coloring and needs no independence premise.
