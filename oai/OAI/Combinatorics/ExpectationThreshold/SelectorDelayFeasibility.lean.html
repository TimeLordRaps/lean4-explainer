Feasibility after delaying one color

Removing a point from a tested prefixSet subtracts exactly its truncated weight from the excess. If that point is not heavy, the heavy-set condition survives; the crossing count decreases by one, so the same cutoff remains admissible. The other tests and their cutoffs are unchanged.
