A finite geometric bound for nonzero tuples

Every finite family of tuples lies in a finite coordinate box. Factoring the sum over this box reduces the estimate to ordinary finite geometric sums. Adding the zero tuple contributes exactly one, so removing it gives the selector's budget of strictly less than 1 / 20.
