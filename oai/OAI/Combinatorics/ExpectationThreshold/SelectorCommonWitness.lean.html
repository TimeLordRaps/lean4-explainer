One witness for an entire selector profile group

A nonempty group contains an actual selected move, hence an actual feasible family member. Its admissibility tests depend on the original coloring only through the full profile. Maximizing each cutoff for this one member therefore produces common heavy sets for the entire group. Selected-move minimality puts every moved row inside its corresponding common heavy set.
