The right-hand slope at a maximal admissible cutoff

A finite minimum provides an actual larger cutoff below every currently heavy weight. Between these cutoffs the truncated excess has the exact affine increment contributed by the heavy coordinates. Weights equal to the original cutoff remain saturated, including when the cutoff is zero.
