Containment covers and expectation thresholds

Both costs use budget one half. Fractional coefficients are indexed by every subset of the ground set, without a support-size bound. The empty subset is included, and ordinary natural powers give p^0 = 1, including at zero. The thresholds are real suprema of feasible densities in the closed unit interval.
