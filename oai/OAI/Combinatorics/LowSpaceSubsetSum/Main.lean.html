Worst-case Subset Sum in one-fifth-exponential space

The threshold is chosen once from its proved existence theorem. No solution, deficiency statistic, or bit-length exponent is an input. The finite random-word experiment has one-sided correctness, success probability at least two thirds, and bounds on its operation and writable-storage counts on every outcome.
