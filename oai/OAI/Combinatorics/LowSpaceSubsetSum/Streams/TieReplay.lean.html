Bounded replay of one tie suffix

The reporter can stop after any matching occurrence. The replay retains only its cursor, callback state, counters, and fixed key. Its recursion is tail recursion. All attempted reports are bounded by the complete suffix length, even if the callback or fuel stops execution early.
