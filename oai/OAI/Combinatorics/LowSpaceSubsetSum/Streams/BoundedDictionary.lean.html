Streaming construction of a hard-capacity dictionary

The input is a list of occurrences; equal keys are tested and skipped. The builder stops at the first new key beyond capacity. Its tree includes the possibly allocated rejected key, as in the manuscript's implementation. No Cartesian product or ambient entry set is stored by this procedure.
