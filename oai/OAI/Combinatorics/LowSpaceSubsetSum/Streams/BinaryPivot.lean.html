Bounded binary pivot search

A search state consists of two endpoints of a half-open interval. Each step performs at most one indexed predicate query and halves the interval. The predicate is true on a prefix, including the empty and full prefixes. After k steps with array length less than 2^k, the endpoints agree and identify the exact boundary. This is the pivot rule needed by cyclic modular rows.
