The modular pivot order of a pair-stream row

These are the two cyclic orders in lem:streams. The list is an analysis view of one sorted slice: implementation can traverse the slice by its pivot and cursor, rather than allocating the reordered list. The permutation theorems preserve occurrence multiplicity, including repeated residues.
