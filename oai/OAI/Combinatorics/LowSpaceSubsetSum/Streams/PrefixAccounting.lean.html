Deterministic accounting for right-prefix refinement

The tree depends on distinct entries for overflow, while raw tuple occurrences are charged separately. The finite sets in this mathematical accounting are ambient analysis sets, not arrays that the implementation is allowed to store. The resulting combinatorial bounds give the accounting in lem:prefix.
