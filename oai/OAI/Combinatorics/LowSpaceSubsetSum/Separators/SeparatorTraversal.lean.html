Depth-first separator interval traversal

Only the received arrays, their filtered children and a scalar result are retained. A branch whose filtered arrays are empty ends immediately. The binary interval queries refer to the precomputed incidence arrays. The terminal callback is the next block, or the final exact-weight search.
