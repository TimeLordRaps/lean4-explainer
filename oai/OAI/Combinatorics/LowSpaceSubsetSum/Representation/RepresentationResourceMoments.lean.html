Raw representation universes before prefixes or the extra prime

A raw leaf occurrence records its three actual subset choices. Products of these occurrences retain repeated weights and locally overlapping masks. Only the valid-entry universe removes overlapping local masks and deduplicates by exact weight and top mask. These are the universes counted by G, J, U.
