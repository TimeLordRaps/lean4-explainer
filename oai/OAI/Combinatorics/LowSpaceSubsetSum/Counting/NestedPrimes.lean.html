The at-most-three-factor nested prime construction

An increment below four contributes the factor one. Larger increments use the actual prime range. Seed identities are retained in the product sampling space even when numerical factors coincide. This proves the size and collision bounds used in alg:filter-bounds without a coprimality premise.
