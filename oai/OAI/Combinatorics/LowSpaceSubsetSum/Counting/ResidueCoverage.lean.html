Collision energy and occupied residue classes

The collision-to-occupancy argument is used in §6 of A Low-Space Algorithm for Worst-Case Subset Sum. Its published antecedents are Austrin, Kaski, Koivisto and Nederlof, Dense Subset Sum May Be the Hardest, STACS 2016, Proposition 3.5, and Nederlof and Węgrzycki, Improving Schroeppel and Shamir's Algorithm for Subset Sum via Orthogonal Vectors, arXiv:2010.08576v2, Lemma 3.2, especially equation (1) and the following Cauchy–Schwarz argument.

Those published statements assume exponentially bounded integer weights. The finite collision-to-occupancy bound instead takes the collision estimate as an explicit hypothesis. The manuscript's prime-factor estimate supplies that hypothesis for polynomial-bit inputs.
