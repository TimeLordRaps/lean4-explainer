Orienting three pairs of mixer halves

The orientations here describe the conditional sign experiment; they are analysis variables, not extra random draws made by the algorithm. Every unordered pair of halves has a good orientation by deficiency superadditivity. A uniform vector of three orientations therefore succeeds with probability at least one eighth. The product-space bound also permits an arbitrary preceding event depending on the unordered pairs.
