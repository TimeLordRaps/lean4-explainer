Deficiency under sampling without replacement

The variance estimate in the paper's deficiency lemma is a specialization of the finite completion-coupling theorem Balanced.WithoutReplacement.finiteVariance_powersetCard_le. Its probability space is exactly the uniform law on all size-v subsets of the supplied domain U, including the cases v = 0 and v = |U|.
