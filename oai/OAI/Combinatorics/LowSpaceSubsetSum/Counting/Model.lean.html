Indexed Subset Sum and the random sign transformation

The input is indexed: equal numerical values at different indices remain different choices. The empty subset is included. These definitions and lemmas implement the sign transformation in §3 of A Low-Space Algorithm for Worst-Case Subset Sum (equation alg:sign-flip). No distributional assumption on the input weights is made.
