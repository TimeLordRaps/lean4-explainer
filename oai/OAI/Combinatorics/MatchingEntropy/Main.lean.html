Sharp analytic bounds for perfect-matching entropy

For a finite loopless labelled multigraph on 2m vertices, with m ≥ 1 and a perfect matching, every point of the matching polytope satisfies F - (2 - 2 / m) * B ≤ H ≤ F. Here F and B are the coordinate entropy and complement entropy with natural logarithms, and H is the attained supremum of entropy over all perfect-matching laws with the prescribed mean.

The development includes the odd-cut description of the matching polytope, sharp face rank bounds, minimal faces, fractional and connected-component reductions, attained entropy and weighted partition objectives, matching counts, degenerate cases, colored triangle-expansion families, and concrete coefficient obstructions.
