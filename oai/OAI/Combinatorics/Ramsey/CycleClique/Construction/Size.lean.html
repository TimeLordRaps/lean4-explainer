The manuscript's size test, conditional on Chvátal–Erdős

An induced vertex set larger than both k and twice the clique number has an independent triple in a graph with no exact (k + 1)-cycle. The only published hypothesis is CEAlphaTwo; the disconnected and cutvertex cases and exact-cycle shortening are proved here.
