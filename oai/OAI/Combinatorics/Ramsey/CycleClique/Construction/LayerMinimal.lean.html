Expansion inside a minimum-order dense layer subgraph

These are the independent-set deletion calculations in manuscript Proposition clq:layer-interface. Both parity cases are stated: for an even parameter the order inequality is weak and minimality is strict; for an odd parameter the order inequality is strict and minimality weak.
