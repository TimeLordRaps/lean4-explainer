Expanded path systems on a clique

Each nontrivial chain is stored as its actual simple vertex list. Distinct chains are disjoint, both endpoints belong to the clique, and successive clique vertices on a chain are separated by nonempty outside interiors. Thus the assigned paths are recovered by splitting at clique vertices.
