Talagrand discrete convexity at the original density

Independent coordinate choices produce dependent rows with full Bernoulli subset laws. The label construction raises the union density when L p < 1; the mandatory-row construction covers every coordinate when 1 ≤ L p. Both cases use the same row-failure budget, without independence between rows.
