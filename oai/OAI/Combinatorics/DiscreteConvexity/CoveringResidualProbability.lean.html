Exact survival probability of a tuple of distinct edges

The sampling coordinates are ordered pairs of edges, not their unions. Every ordered pair of positions contributes a distinct coordinate, including each diagonal position. The product Bernoulli law therefore assigns exactly (1 - p) ^ (m * m) to the event that all these coordinates are absent.
