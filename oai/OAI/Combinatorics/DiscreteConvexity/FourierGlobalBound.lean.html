Biased Parseval and the global weight bound

The exact Parseval multiplier for the unnormalized coefficients is (q / (1-q))^|U|. The desired multiplier q^|U| is smaller for 0 < q < 1. The actual product-kernel identity proves Parseval here; it is not a hypothesis of the global bound or its family-indicator specialization.
