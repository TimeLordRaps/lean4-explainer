The total weight of residual tuples

We bound the sum over injective edge tuples by the sum over all edge tuples. Splitting an unrestricted tuple into its first edge and its tail gives an exact weighted recurrence. The per-union extension bound then controls every step. No nonemptiness assumption on the edge family is needed.
