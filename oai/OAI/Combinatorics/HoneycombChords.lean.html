Critical honeycomb bridge mass

For simple honeycomb paths weighted by the critical activity per visited center, the strict bridge mass at height h is comparable to (1 + h)^(-1/4), with height-zero mass equal to one. Its height Laplace transform is comparable to s^(-3/4) for 0 < s ≤ 1.

The geometric port convention and finite strip transfer estimates follow OpenAI, Critical honeycomb chords with prescribed boundary endpoints and its companion Uniform marked-polygon estimates and sharp finite bridge moments (September 26, 2026).
