A common explicit encoding for initialization and proposals

Cells follow cellEnum, their copies follow increasing finite indices, and each copy contributes its false and true coordinates consecutively. The polynomial initializer computes exactly this encoding of the actual initial draw. The same order may therefore be used by the proposal scan.
