A finite factorial-lift counterexample

The ten labels are the first cell's rows 0, 1, 2 and columns 3, 4, 5, and the second cell's rows 6, 7 and columns 8, 9. Conditioning on the first three rows gives a symmetric addition matrix with two distinct positive eigenvalues. This finite counterexample does not formalize a randomized approximation algorithm for contingency tables.
