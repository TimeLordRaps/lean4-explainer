Balanced marginalization of actual integer two-removal matrices

The new pair is summed subject to its exact total M. We identify every old, fresh and cross entry with the balanced summation theorem; thus the signature of the marginal is derived from signatures of the actual unsummed integer matrices, rather than assumed for a binary encoding.
