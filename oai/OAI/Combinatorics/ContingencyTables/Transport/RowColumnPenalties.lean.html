Literal doubled row and column penalties

The false coordinate of a cell is its row value x. Its true coordinate is y, with physical column value q=B-y. Thus row and column factor groups are disjoint, while their small-cell subsets are nested inside them. Offsets below are the actual full margins and capacity sums of the paper.

    (r cR : I → ℕ) (c cC : J → ℕ) (z : ((I × J) × Bool) → ℕ)
    (hz : InBox (fun u => B u.1.1 u.1.2) z) :
    penalty cellLine (smallCoordinate small) (fullOffset B cR cC) viewSlope
