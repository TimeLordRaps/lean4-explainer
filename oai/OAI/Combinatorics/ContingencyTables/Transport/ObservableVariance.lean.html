Two-lift transversal variance

The adaptive signed-transport proof from paper 200-02, with its comparison factor q². The transport weight has addition signatures in complementary coordinates; the other lift has addition signatures in the original coordinates. Complement identities, rather than an interchange of those two signature roles, supply the defect score inequalities.
