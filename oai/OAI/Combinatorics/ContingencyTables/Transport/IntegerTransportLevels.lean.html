Summing the actual integer-branch recursion by exposure level

The branches are the U+1 balanced profiles of each ordinary slot. Local potential increments and future-form monotonicity telescope to the root bound used before the softened limit. No positivity of future forms or signature property of their weighted sums is assumed.
