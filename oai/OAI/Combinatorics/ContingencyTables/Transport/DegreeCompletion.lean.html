Positive completion of a single valid degree

A fixed-degree weight is extended by the constant weight one on every other degree. All off-degree signatures are proved, and all coefficient sums at the original degree are unchanged. This lets the existing general nonnegative-derivative theorem retain contracted coordinates without assuming positivity or signatures of the original weight at other degrees.
