Root penalties and exact nonroot equations

Root lines carry their actual absolute-deviation penalty. Nonroot lines carry a positive equality softener; its limit is the exact prescribed-line indicator. Unary interval penalties include the companion's tree factors. All balanced large-cell sums are performed before this finite limit.
