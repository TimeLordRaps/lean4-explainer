Shortest-augmenting-path bound

The finite integral-network specialization of Edmonds and Karp, Theoretical Improvements in Algorithmic Efficiency for Network Flow Problems, J. ACM 19(2) (1972), 248–264, Theorem 1 (printed page 251). The bound (n³−n)/4 implies the n³ bound for shortest residual augmentations.

Primary text: https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~pettie/matching/Edmonds-Karp-network-flow.pdf
