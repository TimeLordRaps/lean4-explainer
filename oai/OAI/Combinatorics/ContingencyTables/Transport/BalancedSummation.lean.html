Balanced-pair summation of two-removal forms

This is the finite unweighted closure step in paper 200-01, Lemma lem:pair-summation (D'=B), and paper 200-02, Lemma lem:integer-signature. The hypotheses describe the actual old/fresh blocks and their adjacent cross entries. Cumulative coefficients are constructed from those cross entries; no transport or closure conclusion is assumed. In particular the zero-diagonal capacity-two case is included.
