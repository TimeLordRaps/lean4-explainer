Actual paired states and the two factorial weights

Every physical cell contains an arbitrary finite bundle of paired labels. The plain and one-hole/one-full predicates imply the exact cell count identities. Thus the literal subset lifts agree on plain states and obey the manuscript's W² comparison on every actual chain state.
