The two-level laminar kernel in contingency-table weights

The factor groups in the sampling paper's laminar-signature lemma are line coordinate sets, with one nested small-slot subset in each line. Row and column coordinates belong to separate lines. The normalized kernel decomposition gives the quadratic one-positive-direction property.
