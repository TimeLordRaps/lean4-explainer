Raw integer signatures of the actual line and unary product

The line groups are disjoint. Integer counts at zero are masked; a repeated removal from a count below two uses a zero second-update factor. This identifies the actual integer matrix, including its box boundaries, with a congruence of the proved line/unary update matrix.
