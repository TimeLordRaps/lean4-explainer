Finite softened-weight transport limits

The explicit root coefficient has only the two endpoint masses in its weight-dependent denominators. All puncture masses and edge energies are finite sums of continuous functions of weights. Thus the actual proved one-hole transport survives zero internal weights when the two limiting endpoint masses are positive.
