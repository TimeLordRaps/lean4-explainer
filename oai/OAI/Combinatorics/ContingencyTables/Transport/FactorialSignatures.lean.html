Factorial congruence for the two binary lifts

The integer-to-binary signature transfer in paper 200-02, Lemma lem:binary-signatures. Coordinates with zero remaining copies are allowed; a single remaining copy gives an invalid repeated removal. The copy matrix is proved to be dominated by the integer form compressed to group means. The difference is the nonnegative within-group variance.
