Reflected actual penalties and the complementary factorial lift

Reflecting a physical box changes affine offsets and reverses their slopes. The root/equality and unary interval factors therefore have the same proved scalar signatures. All finite balanced pairs are reflected as well, before transferring the resulting addition signature to labels.
