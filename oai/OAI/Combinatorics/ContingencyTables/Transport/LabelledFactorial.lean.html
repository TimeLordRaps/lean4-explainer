Literal subset factorial lifts and their conditioned copy displays

A display is the actual subtype of available labelled elements. Its group fibres are reindexed by Fin of their exact cardinalities. Removing or adding actual labels changes the group counts by the exact integer updates, so the conditioned subset matrices are congruent to the proved factorial copy matrices. No binary-lift signature is assumed.
