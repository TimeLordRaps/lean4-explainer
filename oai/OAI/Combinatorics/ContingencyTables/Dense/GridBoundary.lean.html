Cut faces of a unit grid

The geometric step in the dense sampler's conductance proof: two points in differently labelled open bins at sup distance less than r ≤ 1 are within r of a common closed face of oppositely labelled axis neighbours. A coordinate path avoids any general-position assumption at simultaneous grid crossings.
