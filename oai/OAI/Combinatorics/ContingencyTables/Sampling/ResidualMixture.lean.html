Exact correction by a finite residual mixture

Finite probability arithmetic gives the residual correction used in exact sampling. The construction is that of Göbel, Liu, Manurangsi and Pappik, Perfect sampling from rapidly mixing Markov chains, arXiv:2410.00882v2 (2024), §3.1, Algorithm 1 and Theorem 6.

All probabilities are rational, as required for the finite fair-bit sampler.
