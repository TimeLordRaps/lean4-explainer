Zero penalties are exactly padded residual completions

Retain the small x,y views and fill every large cell with the balanced pair (A,B-A). For an assignment supported on large cells, the full margin equations are precisely the padded residual margin equations. The small positive-part penalties enforce the original nonnegative residual tests.
