The companion's maximum-capacity forest

Among finite acyclic subgraphs choose one of maximum total capacity. Positive capacities make it spanning in each large-cell component. A free edge can replace every tree edge on its path; optimality therefore gives the exact path-capacity inequality used in the forest estimates.
