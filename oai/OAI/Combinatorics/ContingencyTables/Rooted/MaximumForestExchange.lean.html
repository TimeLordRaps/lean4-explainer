The safe-edge invariant for the executed maximum-forest algorithm

At every step, choose a largest-capacity edge whose endpoints lie in different current components. It extends the current forest inside a maximum-weight forest. This is the exchange proof behind the binary component-search implementation; no forest is enumerated by that program.
