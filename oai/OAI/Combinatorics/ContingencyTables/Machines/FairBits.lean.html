Bounded fair-bit scaling

Both papers for result 200 implement a bounded approximately uniform integer choice by drawing z uniformly from 0,...,Q-1 and returning ⌊M*z/Q⌋. When Q = 2^k, the input choice uses exactly k fair bits.

We prove the exact integer fiber formula in 200-01, equation eq:dense-offset-count, and the pointwise and total-variation error estimates used in 200-02, Lemma lem:dyadic-integer-draw. The arguments are direct finite arithmetic and have no published-input hypotheses.
