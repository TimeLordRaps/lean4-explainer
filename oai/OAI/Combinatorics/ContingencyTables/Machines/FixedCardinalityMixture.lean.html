The uniform-size mixture realizes the initial factorial weights

A size is drawn uniformly from 0,...,n, followed by the sequential fixed-cardinality draw. Its subset mass is the literal local factorial weight divided by n+1. The bounded Boolean implementation uses a fresh word for the size and for each inclusion decision.
