Fixed-degree binary cubic contraction

The full-matrix cubic contraction in paper 200-02, Lemma lem:binary-contraction, follows from the generic spectral theorem CommonBasesFPRAS.cubic_signature and its closed-limit lemma. A symmetric cubic tensor is positive on distinct triples and zero on repetitions; its coordinate slices have the quadratic signature; contraction by an arbitrary nonnegative vector, in particular a pair indicator, preserves that signature on the full remaining index set.

There is no assumption of positivity on unrelated subset cardinalities.
