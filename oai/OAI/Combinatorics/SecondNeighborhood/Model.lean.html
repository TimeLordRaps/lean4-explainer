  1. structure OAI.SeymourSecondNeighborhood.IsOriented The loopless, asymmetric relation underlying an oriented graph line 10

    L4DEL explanation

    An oriented graph is a finite directed graph with no loops, no multiple arcs, and no pair of opposite arcs.

    — OpenAI Seymour second-neighborhood manuscript

    IsOriented r packages the two relation-level conditions needed throughout the development: no vertex points to itself, and an arc u → v rules out the opposite arc v → u. The Lean model uses a relation rather than a graph structure, so finiteness and decidable equality are supplied separately where needed.

    1. loopless excludes r v v for every vertex.
    2. asymmetric excludes a two-cycle formed by opposite arcs; it is stronger than merely having no duplicate representation of an arc.

  3. def OAI.SeymourSecondNeighborhood.secondNeighbors Vertices at directed distance exactly two line 22

    L4DEL explanation

    Thus N2+ (v) consists of the vertices at directed distance exactly two from v.

    — OpenAI Seymour second-neighborhood manuscript

    secondNeighbors r v contains a vertex w when some u gives a two-step path v → u → w, while explicitly excluding v itself and every first out-neighbor of v. This is exact distance two, not merely reachability within two steps.

    1. The existential witness supplies a directed walk of length two.
    2. The two negative conditions remove the starting vertex and all vertices already reached in one step; the surrounding Finset deduplicates multiple paths to the same endpoint.
