Arbitrary-repair lower bound for the actual canonical host

The concrete common-leaf selection satisfies both independent certificates: its guarded path is valid, and its chosen coordinates bound each guard cell's sampling multiplicity. Together these prove the integer repair lower bound. No assumption about preservation of arbitrary variable-variable cells is made.
