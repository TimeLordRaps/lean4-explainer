source

A direct qualitative proof of the non-anchor trace obstruction. No enumeration of suffix generators is needed.

If all binary words occur, any two selected columns in the same class can be separated by a singleton word, contradicting their suffix monotonicity. Thus all five classes are distinct. The word 11110 then uses four classes, while a variable row uses at most three and a dummy row is all ones.
