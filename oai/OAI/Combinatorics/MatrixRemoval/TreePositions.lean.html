Concrete depth-first orders for variable positions. Each class has 2^h positions. Classes 2*i and 2*i+1 are respectively plus and minus at i<h; class 2*h is the shared leaf class. The final two key components retain the original coordinates, making injectivity immediate without uniqueness assumptions on dyadic data.
