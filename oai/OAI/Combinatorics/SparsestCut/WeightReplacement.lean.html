Replacing vertices of zero retained demand

The replacement coupling of Section 7 of OpenAI's Constant-factor hardness of uniform sparsest cut keeps a draw of positive retained demand and replaces every other draw by an independent retained-demand draw. Its color-change probability controls the change in Boolean variance.
