Replication of finite weights

Ceilings produce integer cluster sizes while giving one copy to every key of zero weight. The resulting counting law satisfies the multiplicative and auxiliary-mass estimates from Section 7 of OpenAI's Constant-factor hardness of uniform sparsest cut.
