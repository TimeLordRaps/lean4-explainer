Finite-group displacement for arbitrary maps to ℓ₁

The finite-group displacement identity lem:finite-fourier holds for maps from any finite abelian group into TreeEdit.RealL1, the full space of absolutely summable real sequences. No injectivity or finite-support assumption is needed.

The proof combines finite scalar cuts, summable-coordinate aggregation, Parseval's identity and finite nonnegative summation.
