Counting active leaves in a full ordered tree

A node is its path, a list of child labels. activeLeafCount A v h counts the active descendants exactly h edges below v. The condition BranchesAtLeast A k v h concerns only internal nodes of that finite subtree; it makes no assertion about children below its leaves.
