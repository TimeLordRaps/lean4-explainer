Binary edit-distance distortion from a finite tree

The finite group of leaf coordinates carries recursively concatenated words. Tagged subsequence matchings give separation, while cheap translations and nonnegative Fourier weights bound averaged ℓ₁ displacements. A binary block code of width M² and distortion at most 804 produces equal-length binary witnesses. Comparing the word-length and distortion scales gives the lower bound for every sufficiently large cap.

The construction and binary conversion formalize OpenAI's September 27, 2026 manuscript, Tree Constructions for the ℓ₁ Distortion of Binary Edit Distance.
