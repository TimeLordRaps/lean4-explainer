Exact rank of polynomial convolution

Each output coefficient of polynomial multiplication occurs in some product of two input monomials. Choosing one such pair for each output gives an identity minor in the output flattening. Together with evaluation and interpolation, this proves the exact rank of C(a,b) for positive dimensions.
