Comparing power growth

The last scalar step of the auxiliary-separation argument compares a fourth-power lower bound for the cube of the diagonal profile with its dimension upper bound. Powers of two suffice to compare the exponents, so the argument needs no asymptotic estimates or approximation of constants.
