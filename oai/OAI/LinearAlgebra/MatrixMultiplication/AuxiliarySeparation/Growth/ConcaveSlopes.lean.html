Slopes of positive discretely concave sequences

The row sequences in Section 6 have decreasing consecutive increments. A nonnegative sequence cannot have a negative increment of this kind: every later increment would be at least as negative. Consequently the increments converge to a nonnegative limit, and the sequence divided by its index has the same limit.
