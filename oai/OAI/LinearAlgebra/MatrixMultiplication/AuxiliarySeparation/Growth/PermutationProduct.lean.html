Products over the six permutations of three tensor legs

The symmetrized quantity in Section 5 uses every permutation of the three tensor legs. Composing by a fixed permutation preserves this product. For the exponent calculation, each original leg occurs twice in any fixed slot. These are finite identities; they do not assume that an individual tensor character is invariant under exchanging its legs.
