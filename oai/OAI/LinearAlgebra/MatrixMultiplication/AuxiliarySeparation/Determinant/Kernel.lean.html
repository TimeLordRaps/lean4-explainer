The determinant kernel of diagonal substitution

A form A(u,v)s + B(u,v)w of bidegree (e,1) is represented on the affine chart u = 1 by the polynomial pair (A(1,X), B(1,X)). Diagonal substitution becomes (A,B) ↦ A + X * B, and multiplication by uw - vs becomes p ↦ (-X * p, p).

The identities here hold over every commutative ring. They include the fixed quotient/kernel coordinates and the monomial identities underlying the simultaneous triangularization in Section 5.1 of Matrix Multiplication via Auxiliary Separation and Polynomial Multiplication.
