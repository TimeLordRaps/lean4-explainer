Concrete tensor characters and the tensor semiring

The coefficient-level character interface and monotone real-valued semiring homomorphisms on mutual-restriction classes describe the same valuations. The map classOf uses finite natural-number coordinate presentations, so the construction applies to all finite coordinate types in the character interface.
