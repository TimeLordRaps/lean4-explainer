The character inequality from the three-sector degeneration

The retained tensor has three branches sharing its actual first-input space. Their two remaining coordinate intervals determine matching branch labels. The uniform three-branch tag inequality and polynomial degeneration then yield the character inequality of Section 5.2 before symmetrization.
