Entropy optimization for finitely many sectors

The normalized weights q = A / (A + B) and 1 - q = B / (A + B) make the entropy-weighted geometric mean equal to A + B. This is the scalar identity used in Section 5.1 of the auxiliary-separation argument. The same calculation also applies to an arbitrary nonempty finite family.
