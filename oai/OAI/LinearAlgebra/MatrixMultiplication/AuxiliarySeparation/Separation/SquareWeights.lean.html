+# The square weights in auxiliary separation

Sections 4.1–4.3 use the phase u - v + 2 * (g - h) to select monomials. On a selected monomial, the three integer variable weights add to (g - h)^2. Thus the surviving monomials have nonnegative total degree, although individual variable weights may be negative. Their degree is zero precisely when the guess g matches the sector h and the auxiliary labels agree.
