Exact finite Fourier projection and square filtering

The 5 * M-term Fourier average is applied to an arbitrary branch coefficient. The label bounds eliminate modular wrap. The weights on the three tensor legs then give a nonnegative square degree on every surviving branch. Taking the constant coefficient retains exactly the branches with g = h and u = v.

The finite-family statements permit arbitrary coefficients and arbitrary repetitions of labels; they use only linearity of the Fourier projection and coefficient extraction, without assumptions on an ambient tensor.
