Exact interpolation away from zero

The coefficient-extraction theorem already proved for matrix multiplication is used here with the nonzero nodes 1, ..., D + 1. In particular a polynomial degeneration can be recovered by a finite sum of its ordinary restrictions at nonzero parameters, with the leading power divided out at each parameter.
