The unramified cyclotomic coefficient ring

We use the actual subring ℤ[eta] of ℂ. At a maximal ideal above a rational prime not dividing the order of eta, its localization has maximal ideal generated by that prime. The central argument proves the vanishing of the universal derivation by differentiating eta ^ L = 1.
