Cyclotomic conjugation and norm divisibility in a DVR

The conjugation fixes the coefficient ring and sends the actual adjoined root to its multiplicative inverse. In a DVR every ring automorphism preserves the canonical additive valuation, because it sends units to units and uniformizers to uniformizers. Consequently a norm equal to a unit times an even scalar power forces the corresponding half-power to divide the element.
