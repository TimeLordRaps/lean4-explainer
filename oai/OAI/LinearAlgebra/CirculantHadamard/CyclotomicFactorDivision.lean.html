A projected factor is integrally divisible by the residue prime

The primitive-character values live in the actual cyclotomic quotient. Their product is evaluated from the actual group-ring product. The DVR inequality chooses a factor, and the monic remainder theorem transfers its divisibility to the smaller cyclic group ring.
