Association by a unit with residue one

This algebraic relation expresses the local comparison conclusion without introducing division in the coefficient ring. The scalar-cancellation lemmas require regularity explicitly; they do not give group rings a domain instance. For a local ring, the residue map is the quotient by its maximal ideal.
