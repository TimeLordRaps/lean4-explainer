The odd-coefficient obstruction modulo eight

The identity coefficient of an integer cyclic norm is the sum of the actual coefficient squares. Every odd square is one modulo eight. Consequently an odd-coefficient element in a cyclic group of order divisible by eight cannot have norm scalar 4 * u^2 with u odd. The divisibility hypothesis excludes order four, so the explicit order-four example is not ruled out.
