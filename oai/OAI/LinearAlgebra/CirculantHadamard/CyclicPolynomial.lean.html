The polynomial presentation of the actual cyclic group algebra

For a commutative coefficient ring and a positive integer n, the cyclic group algebra is the quotient of R[X] by X ^ n - 1. The two maps are defined by their universal properties, and both inverse identities are proved explicitly. No domain property of the finite group algebra is used.
