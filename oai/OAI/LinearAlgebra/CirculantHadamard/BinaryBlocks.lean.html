The actual four-block decomposition and partial evaluations

The Chinese remainder equivalence transports the actual cyclic group ring, not just a coefficient count. Coefficients of its four slices are the original signs. Partial evaluation is a ring homomorphism defined on the product group algebra and intertwines coefficient conjugation with index reversal.
