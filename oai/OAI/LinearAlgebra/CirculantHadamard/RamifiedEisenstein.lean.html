Eisenstein polynomials for the ramified cyclotomic extension

The maximal ideal is generated by the actual natural prime p. We transport the integer coefficient divisibilities and prove separately that the constant term p is outside the square of the maximal ideal. This yields irreducibility over the local base, and hence the minimal polynomial of an actual primitive root in any faithful domain extension.
