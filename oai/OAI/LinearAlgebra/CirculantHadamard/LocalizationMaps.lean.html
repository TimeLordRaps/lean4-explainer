Maps between coefficient localizations and maximal ideals over primes

The localization map uses the actual contraction of the target prime ideal. Its denominator condition follows from the definition of contraction; no integrality is needed for this map. Integrality is used separately to prove maximality of contractions and existence of maximal ideals over rational primes.
