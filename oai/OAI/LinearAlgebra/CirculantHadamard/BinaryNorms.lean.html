Cancelling the factor four in the binary norm identities

The coefficient ring may be a domain; the finite cyclic group algebra is not assumed to be a domain. Cancellation is performed separately at every coefficient. In particular these statements apply both to complex coefficients and to the actual Gaussian subring of ℂ.

CyclicRing.ringStar conjugates coefficients as well as reversing exponents. The evaluation bridge requires compatibility with this full operation, including at the fourth root i; an inversion-only operation would not suffice.
