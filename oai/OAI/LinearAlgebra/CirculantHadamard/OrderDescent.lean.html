Finite descent of an odd dyadic cyclic norm

The invariant 1 ≤ t and t + 2 ≤ k records exactly the range needed to halve an odd row until its norm is 4*u^2 while retaining a group order divisible by eight. Every step invokes the concrete cyclotomic halving theorem. The final step is the coefficient-square contradiction modulo eight.
