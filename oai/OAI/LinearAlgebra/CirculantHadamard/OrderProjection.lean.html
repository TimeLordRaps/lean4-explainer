The first order projection

The quotient sends the original cyclic generator to the cyclic generator of the smaller order. Its coefficients are the sums over the actual reduction fibers. When n = m * d, every fiber contains exactly d original indices; an odd number of signs therefore produces an odd integer coefficient.

The norm scalar is preserved by the quotient map. In particular, projecting from 2^(2*s) * u^2 to 2^(2*s) does not divide the original norm scalar.
