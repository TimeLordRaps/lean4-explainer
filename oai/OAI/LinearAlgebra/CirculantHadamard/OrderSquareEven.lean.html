Square and even order from actual circulant sign rows

Augmentation of the cyclic norm identity gives an integer square. A nonidentity correlation is a zero sum of signs; reduction modulo two makes its number of summands even. Both conclusions follow from the actual real circulant Hadamard matrix predicate.
