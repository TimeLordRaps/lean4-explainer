Integer cyclic evaluation and actual cyclotomic conjugation

The coefficient map is the integer cast into the cyclotomic quotient. The character sends the cyclic generator to its adjoined root. The root-inverting automorphism therefore carries evaluation to evaluation of the actual cyclic group-ring involution.
