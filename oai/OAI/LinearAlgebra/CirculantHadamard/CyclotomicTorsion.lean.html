Kronecker's criterion in the actual alternating coefficient ring

The finite generator and root-of-unity hypotheses are discharged for the literal ring B u = ℤ[i, rho_p : p ∣ u]. The modulus-one hypothesis follows for alternating products by clearing the actual product denominator and evaluating the norm identities.
