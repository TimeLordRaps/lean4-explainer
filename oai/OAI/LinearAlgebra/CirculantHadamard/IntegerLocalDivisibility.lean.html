Reflecting prime-power divisibility from the localized integers

A denominator inverted in the localization at (p) is not divisible by p. Clearing that denominator and using primality therefore reflects divisibility by every power of p back to the integers. The argument includes the zeroth power and the zero numerator, without any nonzero numerator hypothesis.
