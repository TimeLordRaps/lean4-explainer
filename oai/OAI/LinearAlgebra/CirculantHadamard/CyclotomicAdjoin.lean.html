The integral algebra and number field generated by roots of unity

Both objects are concrete subobjects of ℂ. The integer algebra includes literally into the rationally generated intermediate field. Integrality is proved from positive power-equals-one witnesses for the generators; finite generation then supplies the finite-dimensional and number-field structures.
