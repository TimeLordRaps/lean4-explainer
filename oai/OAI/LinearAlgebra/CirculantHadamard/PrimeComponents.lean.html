Actual primary components of the cyclic group of order u²

The prime set is the literal finite set of prime factors of u. The exponent of each component is (u ^ 2).factorization p, hence twice the exponent in u. Factorization and coprimality supply the hypotheses of the finite Chinese remainder theorem. Its coordinates are the canonical reduction maps.

The generator of each primary component is the inverse image of its unit coordinate vector. Its additive order is proved to be the full prime power, and the complementary integer factor of u² is proved prime to p.
