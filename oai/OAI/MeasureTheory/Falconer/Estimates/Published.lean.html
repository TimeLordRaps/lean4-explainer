Published radial input for the planar Falconer application

The only input isolated here is the planar specialization of Orponen, On the dimension and smoothness of radial projections, Analysis & PDE 12 (2019), 1273–1294, proof of Theorem 1.13, equations (3.5)–(3.6). The parameters used by the application are s = 1 + δ / 2, t = 1 - δ / 4, and p = 1 + δ / 8. They satisfy the strict inequalities in (3.5). Orponen uses a distance weighted source measure; on disjoint compact supports that weight and its inverse are bounded, so finiteness of the moment is unchanged.

Prepared root measures follow from this radial moment estimate.
