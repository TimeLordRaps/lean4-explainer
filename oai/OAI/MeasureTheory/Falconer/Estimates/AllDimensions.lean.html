The all-dimensional Falconer statement and the final measure argument

The Falconer distance conjecture concerns compact subsets of Euclidean space with Hausdorff dimension greater than half the ambient dimension. The dimension-independent final measure argument reduces positivity of the original distance set to absolute continuity of a weighted distance pushforward.
