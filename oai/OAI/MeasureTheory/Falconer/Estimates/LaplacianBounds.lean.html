Quantitative finite-derivative bounds for stationary jets

The Laplacian is a fixed finite sum of second directional derivatives. Expressing that contraction as a continuous linear map gives bounds for all mixed Fréchet derivatives, without replacing them by separate bounds on a single chart coordinate. Vertical unit vectors in a parameter/tangent product give the partial Laplacian used by the actual stationary operators.
