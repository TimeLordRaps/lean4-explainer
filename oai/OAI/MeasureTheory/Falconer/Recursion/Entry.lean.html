Entry depth for the all-dimensional assembly

This is the numerical content of asm:entry. The dimension enters through the Lipschitz constant of the two excess profiles. The envelope and dimension constants are independent.

  1. theorem OAI.Falconer.exists_assembly_entry line 21

    A common entry depth with a small graph cost and two positive tail potentials. The large-scale hypothesis is explicit; the manuscript uses this only for all sufficiently large N.
