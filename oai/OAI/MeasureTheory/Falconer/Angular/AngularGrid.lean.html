A common line grid for the planar incidence obstruction

Each original strip gives a bounded list of common-grid line parameters. The list is attached to its original direction label. Rounding therefore turns the manuscript's covering-number upper bound into the cardinality upper bound needed by published_furstenberg_labelled_obstruction.
