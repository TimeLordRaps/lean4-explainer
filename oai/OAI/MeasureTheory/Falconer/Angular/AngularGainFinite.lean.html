Finite labelled incidence obstruction

The sampling argument in ang:planar-measures ends with finite point and line families. Their counting contradiction follows from the precise published Furstenberg input. All line families must use a common separated grid before cardinalities replace covering numbers. Direction labels remain distinct throughout the upper counting estimate.
