Finite algebra of individual ambient mask jets

A term contains each original mask exactly once, evaluated at one smooth angular chart point, with a fixed finite jet on each occurrence. The coefficient contains only the fixed chart geometry. Differentiation can therefore distribute to one mask without deleting any other occurrence.
