Actual stationary operators on a smooth pole patch

The operators are the quadratic Laplacian jets of the explicit chart amplitude. They depend smoothly on the evaluation direction away from the opposite pole. Their equality with the coefficients in the local sphere expansion follows by locality, including the fixed chart cutoff.
