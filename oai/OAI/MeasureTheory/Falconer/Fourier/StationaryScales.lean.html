Absorbing the finite stationary remainders

These are the exact scale calculations in osc:stationary. The ratio A^2 / Λ pays for each extra stationary term. The remaining d+2 derivatives are absorbed using A ≤ R^2. No stationary-phase estimate is assumed here.
