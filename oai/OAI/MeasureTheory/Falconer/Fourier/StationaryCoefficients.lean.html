Identification of the outgoing stationary coefficients

The zeroth coefficient is evaluated on the chart amplitude. The outgoing Helmholtz recurrence uniquely determines the manuscript's polynomial coefficients. The identification for local quadratic jets is conditional on their satisfying this recurrence.
