Applying the quadratic expansion to the actual sphere integral

The amplitude is the explicitly computed surface density times the spherical amplitude, extended by zero from the fixed unit chart. Smoothness and its quantitative derivative bounds are retained as local obligations. The only published inputs are the gnomonic measure formula and the fixed Euclidean quadratic stationary-phase theorem.
