Coordinate integration by parts for the angular localization

For an arbitrary smooth real phase in a sphere chart, coordinate integration by parts gives a scale bound through the reciprocal phase derivative. The transport derivative identities concern functions on the real line and are independent of the ambient sphere dimension.
