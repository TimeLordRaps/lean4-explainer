The actual sphere integral in fixed quadratic coordinates

The only measure input is the already recorded gnomonic surface formula. The quadratic coordinate Jacobian is proved locally. Its product with the gnomonic density is exactly the manuscript's (1-‖u‖²/4)^((n-2)/2).
