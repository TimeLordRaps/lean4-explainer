A concrete finite family of separated internal dyadic intervals

At depth 2D use the cells with indices 2i+1. The bound 2D < 2^(2D) leaves room at the right endpoint. Each adjacent selected pair has a positive gap. The finite occurrence type remembers parents and their children without requiring an injectivity premise for the family.
