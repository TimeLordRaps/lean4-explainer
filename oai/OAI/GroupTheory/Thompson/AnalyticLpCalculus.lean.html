Uniform estimates in the concrete Hilbert space

The restricted Lebesgue measure has mass one. Consequently, an almost-everywhere uniform bound on a representative gives the same bound on its L² norm. The derivative criterion below turns a quadratic norm remainder into a genuine derivative in any real normed space, including this Hilbert space.
