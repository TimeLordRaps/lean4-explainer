Scalar cutoffs for the Hilbert displacement construction

The inner cutoff beta is one through radius ρ / 4 and vanishes from ρ / 2. The outer cutoff chi is one through radius ρ / 2 and vanishes from ρ. Both formulas take values in [0, 1] even outside this domain.
