Simultaneous affine transport of two separated basic intervals

Each of the three complementary gaps is partitioned and equalized separately. Consequently the selected source and target cells occupy matching positions in the two full ordered partitions. The interpolation theorem constructs an actual increasing dyadic PL homeomorphism on those partitions.
