The Euclidean planar oscillator

The ordinary sine/cosine circle and its tangent have unit Euclidean norm. The derivative of the tangent is the negative circle vector. The mean value inequality therefore gives Lipschitz constant one for both functions, and a quadratic first-order remainder estimate with constant one.
