Exact finite correlation and variance estimates

The diagonal and off-diagonal coefficients are exact finite counts. The mixed sum has one exceptional parent column, hence D nested terms and D(D-1) separated terms. Every count identity permits arbitrary signed coefficients. The final cancellation imposes no positivity assumption on the common correlation alpha.
