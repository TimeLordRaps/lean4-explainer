Lower packing on a fixed Cartan slice

A finite net in the ambient group pulls back to a cover of the nilpotent parameter set. Conjugation preserving Haar makes the mass of every pulled-back ball independent of the slice position. This is the fixed-position part of the manuscript's lower packing argument, with no assumption on an image's absolute continuity or multiplicity.
