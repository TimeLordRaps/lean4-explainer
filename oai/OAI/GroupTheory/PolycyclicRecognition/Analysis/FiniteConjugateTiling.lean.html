Measurable tilings by conjugated small boxes

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.finite_conjugate_tiling line 20

    A measure-preserving group automorphism can distort tiles without altering the cardinality bound. The doubled small box suffices for a measurable partition of the large box.
