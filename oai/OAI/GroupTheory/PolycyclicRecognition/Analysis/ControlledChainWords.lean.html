Bounded-step words near a coarse chain

Sampling a coarse path gives finitely many jumps. Replacing each jump by a short word in the unit ball gives a controlled word whose every prefix stays near a sampled vertex. The minimizing-path hypothesis is the same concrete metric hypothesis used by GeodesicWords and the stationary sublinearity bridge.
