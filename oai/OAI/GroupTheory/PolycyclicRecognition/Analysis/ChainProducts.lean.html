Polynomial coordinates for nilpotent chains

Every bounded-step chain is an actual product of its group increments. Compactness bounds the increment logarithms in a global exponential chart. The finite Dynkin identity then bounds all endpoint coordinates polynomially in the number of steps. No geometric growth estimate is assumed.
