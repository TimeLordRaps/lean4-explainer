Right Haar measure in quotient-section coordinates

Right translations translate each kernel fiber on the right. Consequently the product of right Haar measures gives a right-invariant measure without assuming that conjugation preserves the kernel measure. In a unimodular ambient group, Haar uniqueness then gives left invariance as well.
