Packing estimates and intrinsic word balls

This is the finite counting step in Lemma height-kernel-growth of Quasi-isometric recognition of virtually polycyclic groups. A metric cover of orbit points and a bound on the number of source points in a small target ball give a cardinal bound on the original finite set. Applying this to intrinsic word balls does not require the subgroup to be undistorted in an ambient group.

  4. def OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.wordBall line 78

    Products of at most n letters from T. Choosing T symmetric gives the usual intrinsic word ball of radius n in the subgroup generated by T.

    def wordBall (T : Finset H) : ℕ → Finset H
  | 0 => {1}
  | n + 1 => wordBall T n ∪ T.biUnion (fun t => (wordBall T n).image (t * ·))
