Exponential-coordinate Haar measure

E. Le Donne, Metric Lie Groups, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 306, Springer (2025), Theorem 9.4.7 (exponential coordinates) and Corollary 9.4.21. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98832-5_9

A global exponential chart identifies Haar measure on a simply connected nilpotent Lie group with Lebesgue measure. The Euclidean determinant formula then gives the volume of weighted boxes, including their scale factors.
