The three measurable samplers from an actual ideal tiling

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.exists_measurable_tiled_probe_samplers line 24

    A finite measurable tiling supplies the actual first-sheet, reverse-anchor, and within-tile samplers. Their group increments are the original endpoints, so choosing a tile never replaces a counted point by another representative.
