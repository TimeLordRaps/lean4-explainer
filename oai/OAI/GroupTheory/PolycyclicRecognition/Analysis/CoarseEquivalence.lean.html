Coarse equivalences of discrete groups

Finite subsets are the bounded sets in a word metric on a finitely generated group. Controlling all finite sets of differences is the metric-free form of being bornologous. For finitely generated groups, a coarse equivalence in this sense is a quasi-isometry. It also matches the coarse-equivalence formulation of Li's finiteness and duality theorems.
