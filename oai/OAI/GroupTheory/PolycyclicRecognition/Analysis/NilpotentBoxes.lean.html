Products in actual nilpotent exponential boxes

The published finite BCH identity is transported to a chosen Lie-algebra model. The uniform normalized structure constants then give the polynomial product bound in the manuscript's box calculus, for every height scale and letter support. No coordinate growth conclusion is assumed as an input.
