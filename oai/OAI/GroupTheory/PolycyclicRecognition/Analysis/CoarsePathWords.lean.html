Subdividing coarse paths into bounded-increment words

A path with speed L and additive error κ admits a unit-step word of length at most (L + 1) * (κ + 2). Its prefixes remain within κ + 2 of points on the path. In the application L = O(r) and κ = O(log r), so this loss is polynomial and its extra height error is sublinear.
