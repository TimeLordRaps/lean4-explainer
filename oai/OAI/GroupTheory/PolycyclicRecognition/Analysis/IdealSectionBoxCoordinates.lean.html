Exact linear coordinates for an ideal and its quotient

Pure quotient lifts have weight zero. In the adapted basis, projection retains exactly those coordinates, and inclusion of the ideal retains exactly the weighted ideal coordinates. This is the linear part of the section-coordinate box comparison.
