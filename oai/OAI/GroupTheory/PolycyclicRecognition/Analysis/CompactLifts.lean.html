Compact lifts under an open quotient

A compact target set has lifts in one compact source set for an open surjection from a locally compact space. This is the precise bounded-step lifting consequence needed by the Cartan multiplication map.

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.exists_compact_lifts_of_open_surjection line 21
