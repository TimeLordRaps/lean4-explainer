Counting separated endpoints using Haar volume

Disjoint fixed-radius balls around separated endpoints have the same positive Haar mass. A polynomial-volume region containing their thickening therefore gives a polynomial cardinal bound. This is the counting step of the path packing lemma, separated from its Lie-coordinate estimate.
