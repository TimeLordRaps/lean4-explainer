Measurable finite labels along converging measure-preserving maps

This is the local transport step in the profile affinity argument. A common finite-measure carrier lets continuity of measurable-set preimages control every label fiber. Relations between two transported labels then pass to the limit directly, without selecting pointwise values on exceptional sets.
