Metric quasi-isometries and the discrete group coarse structure

The finiteness and duality endgame uses finite sets of group differences. Here an actual metric quasi-isometry supplies that data. The metrics are left invariant and their bounded identity balls are finite, as for word metrics on finitely generated groups. No recognition theorem is assumed.
