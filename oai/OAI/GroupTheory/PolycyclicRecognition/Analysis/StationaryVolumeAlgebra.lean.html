Algebraic consequences of stationary volume comparison

Once the forward volume comparison gives domination of segment supports, the averaged homomorphism factors through canonical height by an invertible linear map. The reverse comparison for the coupled inverse then makes this map a support isometry. Neither conclusion is taken as a new hypothesis.
