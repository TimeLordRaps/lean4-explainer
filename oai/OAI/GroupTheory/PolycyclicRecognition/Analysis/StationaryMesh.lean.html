Selecting one map that is good on a finite mesh

The stationary volume comparison samples endpoints independently of the stationary map. Uniform L² bounds at finitely many sampled path points give a single map for which most endpoints have every sampled error small. The finite mesh is fixed before the scale tends to infinity.
