Averaging the stationary height cocycle

The normalized pair space carries a measure-preserving action. Its height evaluation is a cocycle for inverse pullback. We construct that action on the actual space L², and show that integration gives an additive slope. No reduced-cohomology assertion is assumed in this construction.
