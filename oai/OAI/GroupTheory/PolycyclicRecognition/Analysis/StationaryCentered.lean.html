The zero-mean Hilbert representation and stationary sublinearity

The closed kernel of integration is invariant under inverse pullback. Ergodicity removes its invariant vectors. This constructs the representation to which the published reduced-cohomology input is applied; the conclusion is the manuscript's averaged sublinear estimate for a fixed measure.
