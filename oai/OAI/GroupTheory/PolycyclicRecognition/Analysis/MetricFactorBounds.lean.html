Logarithmic factor bounds for short ambient bridges

Unit words and the actual Cartan factorization give logarithmic bounds for every ambient element and its fixed compact right thickening. The nilpotent factor grows exponentially in ambient distance; the Cartan logarithm grows only polynomially.
