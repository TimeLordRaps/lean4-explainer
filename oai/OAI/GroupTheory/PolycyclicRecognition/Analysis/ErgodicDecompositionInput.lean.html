Ergodic decomposition of invariant probabilities

Greschonig--Schmidt, Ergodic decomposition of quasi-invariant probability measures, Colloquium Mathematicum 84/85 (2000), 495--514, Theorem 5.2(1), specialized to the zero Radon--Nikodym cocycle. Components are invariant ergodic probabilities and their barycenter is the original measure. The formalization records setwise measurability, the barycenter identity, and invariance of the component map.

Source: https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~kschmidt/Publications/decomposition.pdf
