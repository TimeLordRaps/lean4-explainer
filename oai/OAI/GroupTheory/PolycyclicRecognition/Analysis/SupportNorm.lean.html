The norm obtained from weighted nilpotent boxes

For a bracket-word basis, each generator occurs with a positive integral multiplicity. Unimodularity says that the corresponding weighted sum of characters is zero. The support exponent of a segment is the maximum, over these bases, of the weighted positive parts of the characters. This finite data yields the support norm identities.
