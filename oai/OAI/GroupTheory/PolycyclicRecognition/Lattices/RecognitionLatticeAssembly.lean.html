Recognition from bounded-height hypotheses

Metric comparison with the virtually polycyclic group supplies the invariant mean and the cohomological coarse comparison.

The conditional recognition theorems retain polynomial bounded-height packing and a uniform affine height estimate as explicit geometric hypotheses.
