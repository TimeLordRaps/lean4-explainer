Finite generation of a uniform lattice from metric chains

The bounded-distance lattice section sends ambient chains to bounded lattice steps. The steps belong to one finite ball, which therefore generates the lattice. This is the finite-generation part of the Švarc argument, proved from compactness and explicit chains.
