Polycyclicity of lattices in simply connected solvable Lie groups

Complex adjoint weights include rotations. Their normalized additive lifts have nilpotent Lie kernel and a real vector-space quotient. Discrete subgroups of the kernel are polycyclic, and finite generation controls the abelian image.
