The algebraic endgame for recognition of solvable Lie lattices

B. Farb and S. Weinberger, Isometries, rigidity and universal covers, Annals of Mathematics 168 (2008), 915–940, Proposition 3.4, p.932, states that every lattice in a connected solvable Lie group is polycyclic. The lattice-polycyclicity predicate specializes this statement to uniform lattices in connected simply connected solvable Lie groups.

Elementary amenability and the finiteness and duality theorems give virtual polycyclicity. Dekimpe's realization then produces a finite-index lattice in a possibly different solvable Lie group. Elementary amenability remains an explicit premise of these conditional recognition theorems.
