Constructing the nilshadow of an integral abelian extension

Both the section and the removed action are constructed from the original extension. The twisted group has a nilpotent real completion of the correct dimension. Its removed action extends, and the original group embeds faithfully in the corresponding semidirect product. The quotient here is still integral.
