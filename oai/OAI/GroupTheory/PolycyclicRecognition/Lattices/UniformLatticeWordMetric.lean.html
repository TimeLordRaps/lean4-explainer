Comparing a lattice word metric with its ambient metric

The inclusion of a uniform lattice, equipped with any finite word metric, is continuous and proper. Applying the proved chain argument therefore gives both quasi-isometry inequalities and coarse surjectivity.

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.IsUniformLattice.wordMetric_quasiIsometry line 27

    A uniform lattice with any finite word metric is quasi-isometric to its ambient group, provided that the ambient metric has linear chains.
