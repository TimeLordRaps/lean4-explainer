A small ring of bounded functions with finite-subgroup projectivity

For each finite subgroup choose its right-coset transversal. Take the Boolean algebra generated by every left translate of these transversals and of subgroup coset partitions inside finitely generated subgroups. When the group is countable, this uses countably many coordinates. Its step-function ring is projective over the integers and has a trace-one element for every finite subgroup.
