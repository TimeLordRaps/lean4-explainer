The stable identity of a resolution syzygy

Maps factoring through projective representations form the image of a chosen projective cover. The identity is zero in that quotient precisely when the representation is projective. Applied to a resolution syzygy this detects finite integral cohomological dimension, without assuming any stable vanishing theorem.
