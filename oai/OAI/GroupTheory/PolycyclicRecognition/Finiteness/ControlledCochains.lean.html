Cochains with finite support on controlled tuples

These are the scalar homogeneous cochains used to compare group-ring cohomology under a coarse equivalence. Support is finite after restricting all pairwise differences to any finite set. The pullback below uses both bounded-difference control and finite fibers of the coarse map.
