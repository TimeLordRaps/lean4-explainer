The duality comparison from an actual uniform lattice

The metric lattice comparison gives a Robinson/Bieri duality comparison. Polycyclicity of the comparison lattice is retained explicitly; for solvable Lie lattices it is the conclusion of Farb--Weinberger (2008), Proposition 3.4.
