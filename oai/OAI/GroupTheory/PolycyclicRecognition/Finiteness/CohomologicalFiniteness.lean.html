The finiteness and duality properties used in recognition

These are concrete properties of the trivial integral representation and its projective resolutions. A finite classifying space supplies a finite resolution of this kind, but no predicate for an unspecified classifying space is used. The cohomology condition includes the possibly nontrivial orientation action: only the underlying integral module in top degree is required to be ℤ.
