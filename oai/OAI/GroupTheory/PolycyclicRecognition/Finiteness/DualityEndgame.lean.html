The conditional finiteness and duality endgame

Elementary amenability is an explicit premise. Finiteness transfer, elementary amenable finiteness, duality transfer, and solvable duality are retained as separate parameters with the following mathematical sources.

  • Xin Li, Dynamic characterizations of quasi-isometry and applications to cohomology, AGT 18 (2018), Corollaries 4.47 and 4.48.
  • Kropholler–Martínez-Pérez–Nucinkis, Cohomological finiteness conditions for elementary amenable groups, J. reine angew. Math. 637 (2009), Theorem 1.1 (viii) ⇒ (v) and the structural conclusion in §2. We use only their consequence that an elementary amenable group of type FP∞ has a finite-index solvable subgroup of finite integral cohomological dimension: intersect the solvable subgroup with the type-F subgroup.
  • R. Bieri, Homological dimension of discrete groups, 2nd ed. (1981), Theorem 9.23: solvable integral Poincaré duality groups are polycyclic.

The propositions concern group-ring resolutions, group cohomology, and finite-index subgroups.
