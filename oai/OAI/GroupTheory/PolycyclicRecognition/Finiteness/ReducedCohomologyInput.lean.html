The precise published reduced-cohomology input

Fourier 70 (2020), 1951--2003, Corollary 1.9, together with Definition 1.1: real-triangulable Lie groups have WAP_t. The proposition below is only its real Hilbert-space consequence, for the particular representation used by the application. Isometric Hilbert representations are weakly almost periodic. The topology on cocycles is uniform norm convergence on compact subsets, as defined in §1.1.
