The algebraic Poincaré-duality comparison group

Only two standard published consequences are needed here:

  • D. J. S. Robinson, A Course in the Theory of Groups, second edition, GTM 80, Springer (1996), Theorem 5.4.15(i), p.153: every polycyclic group has a finite-index poly-infinite-cyclic subgroup. We use only its torsion-free consequence, not normality or a chosen infinite-cyclic series.
  • R. Bieri, Homological Dimension of Discrete Groups, second edition (1981), Theorems 9.9 and 9.10, and their explicit consequence on p.176: every torsion-free polycyclic group is an integral Poincaré-duality group.

These are explicit conditional inputs. The resulting recognition theorem retains elementary amenability as a hypothesis.
