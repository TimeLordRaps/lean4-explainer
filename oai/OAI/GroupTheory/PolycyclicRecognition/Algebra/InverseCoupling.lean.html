Inverse coupling with the prescribed inverse coefficient

Compact-uniform approximation of the forward height cocycle transfers to the inverse section, with precisely the inverse linear coefficient. The measure and the square-integrable transfer functions are constructed from the pair-space charts and whole-fiber averages.
