+# The normalized translation action on pairs

For a pair (F,B), normalization of domain translation by x has forward map F(x)⁻¹ F(xz) and inverse map x⁻¹ B(F(x)z). These are actual maps, with all Lipschitz and quasi-inverse bounds retained. Inverting the parameter converts the manuscript's right action into a left action.
