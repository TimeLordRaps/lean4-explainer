Nilpotent rational rank from a homology bound

Finite-index torsion-free subgroups of finitely generated nilpotent groups have a top orientation class. Detecting this class after rationalization bounds every lattice quotient. Lifting finite independent families then bounds abelian quotients of arbitrary nilpotent groups, including torsion.
