Conditional measures over an invariant measurable factor

Disintegrating the graph of an invariant measurable map gives a measurable probability kernel with the original measure as barycenter. Uniqueness of conditional kernels proves invariance of its components almost everywhere, for each acting element. No ergodicity is assumed here.
