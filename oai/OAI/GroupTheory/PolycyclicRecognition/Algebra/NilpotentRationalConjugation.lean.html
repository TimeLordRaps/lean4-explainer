Nilpotent conjugation has a dimension-bounded unipotence exponent

On an abelian normal subgroup, conjugation minus the identity is the commutator map. Its successive powers lie in successive lower-central terms. Rationalization therefore gives a nilpotent operator, whose exponent is bounded by the rational rank rather than by a chosen group nilpotency class.
