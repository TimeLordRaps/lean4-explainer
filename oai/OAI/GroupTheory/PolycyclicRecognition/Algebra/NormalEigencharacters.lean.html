Finite-index stabilizers of normal-subgroup eigenspaces

Conjugation permutes the common eigenvalue functions of a normal subgroup. There are only finitely many nonzero common eigenspaces. Consequently every one of them is invariant under a subgroup of finite index in the ambient group. This separates the finite permutation from the linear action.
