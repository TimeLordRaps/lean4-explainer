Gromov's theorem from Aaron Hill's formalization

Aaron Hill's FormalConjecturesCheck.gromov_forward proves Gromov's polynomial-growth theorem for arbitrary groups. Its list-defined Cayley balls agree with the finite word balls used here, and their polynomial bounds are equivalent without an additional finite-generation or countability hypothesis.
