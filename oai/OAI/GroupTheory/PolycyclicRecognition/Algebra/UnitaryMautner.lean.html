Fixed vectors and contracted elements

The fixed-vector case of Cornulier--Tessera, Lemma 5.2. The proof uses only strong continuity and the isometry identity, with explicit contracting sequences. The closed subgroup generated by these elements then has the same fixed vectors as the contracting subgroup.
