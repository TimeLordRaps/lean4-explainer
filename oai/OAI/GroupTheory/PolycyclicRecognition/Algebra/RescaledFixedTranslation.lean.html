Fixed group moves disappear in positively weighted word charts

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.rescaled_fixed_translation_tendsto line 21

    The coordinate maps of every fixed right move tend to the identity in the compact-open topology. This is the part of Cartan scaling needed by the density sampling argument; it does not require a preconstructed graded Lie group or any metric scaling premise.
