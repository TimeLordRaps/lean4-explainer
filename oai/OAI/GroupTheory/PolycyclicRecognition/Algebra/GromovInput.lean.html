The polynomial-growth input in height-kernel recognition

Published input: M. Gromov, Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps, Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS 53 (1981), 53–78, Main Theorem, p. 54, DOI: 10.1007/BF02698687.

GromovPolynomialGrowthInput J is precisely the implication needed here: a finite symmetric generating set with polynomially bounded intrinsic word balls gives a nilpotent subgroup of finite index. The supporting covering-to-cardinality argument uses concrete finite word balls, with this implication as an explicit hypothesis.
