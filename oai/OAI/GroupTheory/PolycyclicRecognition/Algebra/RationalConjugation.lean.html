Rational conjugation on an abelian normal subgroup

Conjugation extends to the rationalization. When the subgroup is torsion free, the extended action has exactly the same kernel, namely its centralizer. Together with the rank bound this is a finite-dimensional representation of the centralizer quotient, without a finite-generation assumption on the abelian subgroup.
