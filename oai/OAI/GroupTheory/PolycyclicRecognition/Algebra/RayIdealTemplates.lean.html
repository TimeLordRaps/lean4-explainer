+# Negative-height templates for the actual ray ideal

The halfspace construction is applied to the actual Cartan weights on canonical height. Every word in the finite mixed-word spanning pool then has positive width. Consequently a fixed ideal displacement shrinks in every maximizing full word box, even after a polynomial loss.+
