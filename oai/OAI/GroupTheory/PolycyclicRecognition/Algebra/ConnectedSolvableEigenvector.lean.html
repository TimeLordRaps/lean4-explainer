A common eigenvector for a connected solvable matrix action

Induct on the derived length. The connected derived subgroup has a common eigenspace. The whole group preserves it, and acts there with scalar commutators. Those commutators are trivial by connectedness and determinant one, leaving a commuting action on this nonzero subspace.
