Additive lifts of complex characters

Rotational diagonal characters in a simply connected solvable group have continuous logarithms. Uniqueness of covering lifts makes the normalized logarithm additive; its value on an exponential curve is the prescribed linear weight, with no choice of a global principal logarithm.
