Finite BCH products controlled by the sum of input sizes

The coefficient bound in the existing finite Dynkin input also controls products by the sum of the logarithmic input norms. In particular the bound tends to zero with that sum, independently of the number of factors. This is the estimate needed for contracting quotient trajectories in exponential coordinates; no Riemannian metric comparison is assumed.
