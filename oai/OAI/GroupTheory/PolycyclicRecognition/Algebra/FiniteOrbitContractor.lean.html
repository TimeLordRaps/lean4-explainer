A contractor fixed by a finite group

Products of commuting ascending actions retain an ascending base. Consequently the product of a finite orbit of contracting elements is invariant and remains contracting. This is the finite conjugation step in KMN, Lemma 2.2; no assertion that an FP-infinity group supplies an initial contractor is made here.
