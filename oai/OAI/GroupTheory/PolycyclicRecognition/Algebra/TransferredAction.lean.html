Transferring translations across a coarse inverse pair

For maps f : H → X and r : X → H, the maps x ↦ f (h * r x) form the uniform quasi-action used in Proposition recognition-from-height of the manuscript. These estimates retain the errors of the coarse inverse pair and require only the stated metric inequalities.
