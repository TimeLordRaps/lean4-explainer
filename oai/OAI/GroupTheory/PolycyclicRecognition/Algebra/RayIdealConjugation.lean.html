+# Fixed ideal displacements along a Cartan ray

The residual adjoint part costs a polynomial, which is absorbed by the strict mixed-word widths. Exact weight dilation translates the template. This gives small coordinates for the actual conjugated displacement in the template translated to contain zero, as required by Cartan paths.
