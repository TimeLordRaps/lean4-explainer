Polynomial residual bounds on the actual cone quotient

The nilpotent residual part of the Cartan action has polynomial coordinate growth. Passing to a quotient costs a fixed coordinate constant, obtained from lifts of a basis. These are estimates for the constructed action, with no quotient-contraction hypothesis.
