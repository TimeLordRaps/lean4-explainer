Uniform local images for nearby parameters

Apply the ordinary submersion theorem to the joint map (p,x) ↦ (p,f(p,x)). A surjective derivative in the second variable then gives a common image neighborhood for all nearby parameters. This is the persistence step for normalized generator product schemes.
