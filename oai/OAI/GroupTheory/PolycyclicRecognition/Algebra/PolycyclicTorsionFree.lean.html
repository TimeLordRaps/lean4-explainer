Torsion-free finite-index subgroups of polycyclic groups

This proves the torsion-free consequence of Robinson, A Course in the Theory of Groups, second edition, Theorem 5.4.15(i), needed by the duality comparison. The proof is induction along the actual cyclic subnormal series.
