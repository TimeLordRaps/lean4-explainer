Polynomial group laws in a fixed coordinate space

The binary Dynkin coefficients are fixed once in the original nilpotent group. Changing its basis changes only the bracket. The group identities and the straight-line one-parameter identities are closed conditions on this finite-dimensional bracket parameter.
