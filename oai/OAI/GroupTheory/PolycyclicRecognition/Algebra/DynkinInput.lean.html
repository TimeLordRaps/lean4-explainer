The precise finite BCH input

Published source: F. Baudoin, An Introduction to the Geometry of Stochastic Flows (Imperial College Press, 2004), Appendix B.5, equation (B.2) and the multiple-exponential formula on p.124; Appendix B.6, p.126, makes the formula global for nilpotent groups. Author's copy: https://fabricebaudoin.blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/source.pdf

We use only its finite-degree consequence. Grouping the terms of the multiple-exponential formula by their ordered letter word leaves at most 2^(d-1) cuts of a degree-d word. Each coefficient before grouping has absolute value at most one. Thus 2^c bounds each grouped coefficient for all degrees at most c. The actual exponential is characterized by its one-parameter subgroups and their derivatives, not by an arbitrary chart. This is an explicit published-input parameter, never a global axiom.
