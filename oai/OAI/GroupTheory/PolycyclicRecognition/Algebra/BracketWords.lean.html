Actual bracket words for the nilpotent box construction

The linear span of evaluated bracket words is exactly the generated Lie subalgebra. A finite-dimensional generated algebra therefore has a basis chosen from actual words. Letter counts give the weights of these words, including for generalized weight vectors.
