  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.CartanFactorModel.normalized_pair_inverse_coefficients line 25

    The inverse normalization only left-translates the inverse map, which cancels in two-point height differences. Thus the proved sublinear labels of the normalized pairs are genuinely inverse coefficients.
