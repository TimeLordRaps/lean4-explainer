Closed linear stabilizers in the polynomial matrix topology

Membership of a matrix translate in a subspace is a family of polynomial equations, obtained from its annihilating linear functionals. Consequently the finite-index common eigenspace stabilizer is dense and fixes that eigenspace under a connected matrix group.
