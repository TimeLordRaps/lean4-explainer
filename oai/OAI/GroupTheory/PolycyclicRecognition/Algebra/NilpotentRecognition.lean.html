The nilpotent comparison branch

A coarse embedding into a simply connected nilpotent Lie group gives polynomial intrinsic word growth, by the proved nilpotent chain-packing estimate. The final passage to virtual nilpotence uses exactly Gromov's published polynomial-growth theorem. This settles the nilpotent branch; the general solvable height argument remains separate.
