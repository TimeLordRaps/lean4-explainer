Polynomial topology on invertible matrices

Both a matrix and its inverse are retained as affine coordinates. Inversion then exchanges coordinates, and translations are polynomial coordinate maps. The resulting topology is Noetherian and T1. Only separate continuity of multiplication is asserted; the product topology need not be the affine polynomial topology on a product.
