Polynomial bounds for products with nilpotent increments

The finite-degree product estimate in the nilpotent-coordinate argument is proved here in a normed associative algebra. The factors need not commute. Homogeneous terms are sums over ordered sublists, so nilpotence truncates the expansion before a fixed degree, independently of the number of factors.
