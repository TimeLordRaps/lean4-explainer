Finiteness of linear height symmetries

The finite-symmetry step used in Lemma support-symmetries reduces to permuting a finite spanning collection of covectors. The elementary linear algebra fact proved here applies equally to vectors in the dual height space. It does not assume that the permutation of covectors determines a symmetry: it proves uniqueness from the spanning hypothesis.
