Diagonal adjoint characters and the zero-height case

Starting from an actual simultaneous upper triangular basis for the adjoint action, the diagonal entries are linear characters and kill every bracket. The resulting canonical height space is zero exactly when the Lie algebra is nilpotent. This proves the algebraic zero-height branch used in the manuscript; it does not assume nilpotence from vanishing height.
