Controlled words in the actual semidirect product

Nilpotent Cartan increments give polynomial bounds on every Cartan prefix. The exact semidirect-product formula then reduces its nilpotent coordinate to the controlled product estimate. No polynomial bound on prefixes is left as an assumption of this theorem.
