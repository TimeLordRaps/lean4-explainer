Limits of contracting boundary trajectories

Polynomial times exponential decay is summable. Metric trajectories with a common increment bound converge uniformly, with a common tail bound and compact normalized shadow. Asymptotic representatives of a quotient point have equal limits and determine a continuous boundary map.
