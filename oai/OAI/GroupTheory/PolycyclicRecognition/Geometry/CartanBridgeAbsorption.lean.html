Absorbing the conjugated bridge into the second-sheet ideal box

The logarithm of the second Cartan factor can be subexponentially large. Its small height, rather than a bound on its full logarithm, controls the exponential size of the conjugated bridge. Strict ideal widths absorb it.

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.CartanWordModel.exists_conjugated_bridge_ideal_bound line 35

    A single subexponential ideal box contains the residual ideal coordinates after every sufficiently small bridge and every allowed second-sheet Cartan factor. The threshold is independent of the scale and of the chosen endpoints.
