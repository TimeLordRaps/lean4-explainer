Closed embeddings from compatible global exponential charts

An injective map of finite-dimensional real Lie algebras is closed in linear coordinates. Compatible global exponential charts transfer that property to the integrated subgroup map. This supplies the properness required for fixed-slice packing, without adding a closed-subgroup hypothesis.
