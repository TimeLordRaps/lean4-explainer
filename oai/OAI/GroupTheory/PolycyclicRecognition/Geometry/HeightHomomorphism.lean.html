The corrected height homomorphism

An amenable group acting with bounded height error and finitely many linear height parts has a normal finite-index subgroup with a genuine homomorphism to the height space at bounded distance from the height shifts. In particular the shifts are uniformly bounded on the kernel. The construction combines the exact linear action with an invariant-mean correction.
