Polynomial bounds for products of controlled Cartan increments

Each increment begins in a fixed nilpotent coordinate box. Its Cartan height lies in a scaled template, and its Cartan logarithm has a polynomial bound. The resulting product remains in the template box with polynomial radius. All constants are uniform over templates, maximizing bases and scales.
