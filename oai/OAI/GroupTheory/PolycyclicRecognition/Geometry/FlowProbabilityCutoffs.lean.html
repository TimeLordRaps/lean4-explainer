Fixed probability cutoffs for converging horizontal segments

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.exists_flow_probability_cutoffs line 19

    A segment in the open chart admits one starting density and one occupation density. The domination constant works for every time on the segment and all sufficiently late scaled flows, before choosing a mesh.
