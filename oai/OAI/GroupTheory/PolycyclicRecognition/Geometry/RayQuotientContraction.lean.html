Contraction on the constructed positive-ray quotient

This proves the linear logarithmic estimate underlying boundary.tex, equation (quotient-contraction). The basis is made of actual pure bracket words in the quotient by the mixed-word ideal. Its scalar weights are positive multiples of the chosen ray, while its residual Cartan action has the already-proved polynomial bound.
