Exponential compatibility in the published faithful realization

Onshuus–Post (2022), Proposition 2.4 and proof, give a faithful inducing linear Lie realization of a simply connected real triangular group. Lee, Introduction to Smooth Manifolds, second edition (2013), Theorem 8.44 (p.195) and Proposition 20.8(g) (p.520), give its Lie algebra map and exponential compatibility. Only these properties are retained below.
