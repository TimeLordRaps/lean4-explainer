A faithful triangular group representation without Dixmier

The intrinsic height kernel makes the Cartan construction and its overlap faithful. A kernel element of the resulting representation acts trivially on the Lie algebra, hence has zero intrinsic height and lies in the known nilpotent kernel, where faithfulness follows from the finite logarithm.
