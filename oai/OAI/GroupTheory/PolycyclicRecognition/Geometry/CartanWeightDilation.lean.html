Exact dilations of generalized Cartan weight spaces

Scalar multiplication by exp (χ h) on every generalized root space defines an actual Lie automorphism. The nilpotent parts of the Cartan action do not enter this construction. These are the exact weight dilations used in the box-translation argument.
