Polynomial bounds for the residual Cartan action

On an actual generalized root space, subtracting its scalar character leaves an operator nilpotent in a uniform dimension bound. Its exponential therefore has polynomial growth in the Cartan parameter. This supplies the residual operator estimate used in box conjugation.
