Root-space widths inside actual word boxes

A nonzero coordinate of a root vector can occur only at a word of the same weight. Its word exponent dominates the support of that root. Consequently rescaling a letter-root vector never enlarges its coordinates in the scaled word box. This includes zero-weight bracket directions.
