A triangular adjoint basis from a faithful triangular representation

Order matrix units by increasing row and decreasing column. Commuting with an upper triangular matrix then gives an upper triangular operator. Restricting this action to a faithfully embedded Lie algebra supplies its actual adjoint basis.
