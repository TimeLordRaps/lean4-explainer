Removing the polar cone vertex without changing the mixed ideal

The polar template uses a closed cone containing zero. The cone quotient must kill the zero generalized weight space to descend the overlapping Cartan factors. Salience and the nonzero generator weights supply this bridge for the same mixed ideal.
