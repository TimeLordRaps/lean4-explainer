Polynomial coordinate bounds for bounded-height triangular words

We use the actual prefix diagonals of a word in positive triangular matrices. Every normalized increment is unipotent. Thus bounded heights at every prefix, with bounded step matrices, imply a polynomial endpoint bound. No endpoint-only height assumption is substituted for the path condition.
