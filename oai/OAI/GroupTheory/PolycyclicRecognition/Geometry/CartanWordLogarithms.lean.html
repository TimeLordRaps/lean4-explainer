Cartan logarithms of bounded words

The Cartan group is nilpotent. The finite Dynkin identity therefore gives a polynomial bound on the logarithm of every prefix of a bounded word. We also record the exact semidirect-product word formula used to apply the controlled Cartan estimates to such prefixes.
