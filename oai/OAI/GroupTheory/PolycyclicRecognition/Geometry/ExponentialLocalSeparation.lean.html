Separation of arbitrary exponential preimages near a regular logarithm

If the exponential has trivial kernel, a local injectivity neighborhood cannot have an additional logarithm outside that neighborhood. Conjugating the small logarithm by the one-parameter subgroup of the other logarithm first forces the two logarithms to commute.
