Cartan actions whose heights stay in a template

The normalized width of a root controls every exponential whose exponent is below that width. Consequently a Cartan action with height in the scaled template sends a fixed coordinate box into the template box with polynomial loss in its Cartan logarithm. This is the increment estimate for controlled paths.
