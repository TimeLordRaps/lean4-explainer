Local product charts from the finite Dynkin identity

Products of exponentials in a basis have a locally surjective logarithm map. The proof gets smoothness from the finite Dynkin polynomial and its derivative from the exact one-coordinate restrictions. No smooth-logarithm or local product assertion is a separate input.
