Propagating root-action bounds through Cartan boxes

A Lie map which preserves the letter root spaces has a polynomial bound in every maximizing box. The proof propagates the bounds on letters through actual bracket words, then through their coordinate basis. This is the algebraic step in the box-conjugation estimate.
