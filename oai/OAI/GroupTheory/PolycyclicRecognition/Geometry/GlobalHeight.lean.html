Assembling the scalar boundary bounds into global height bounds

The scalar estimates are uniform over all base points. Separating boundary traces then control the full height on a transversal, and the leaf estimate extends the same bound to the whole group. The existence of the boundary maps and their shadow estimates remains the geometric obligation.
