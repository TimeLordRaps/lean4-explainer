Polynomial products with bounded diagonal prefixes

For an upper triangular matrix path, remove the diagonal of each prefix. The resulting increments are unipotent. If the original increments are bounded and all diagonal prefixes and their inverses are bounded, the unipotent increments are uniformly bounded. Their products therefore have polynomial size. This is the coordinate estimate behind bounded-height path packing.
