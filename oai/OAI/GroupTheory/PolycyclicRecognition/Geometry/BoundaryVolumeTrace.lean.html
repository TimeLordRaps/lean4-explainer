Boundary volume sandwiches bound trace-height differences

  1. theorem OAI.PolycyclicRecognition.boundary_trace_bound_of_sandwich line 17

    Source documentation

    Equal source boundary sets have equal images. A fixed positive lower volume and finite upper volume then bound the difference of the two logarithmic Haar characters. The constant is independent of the map.
