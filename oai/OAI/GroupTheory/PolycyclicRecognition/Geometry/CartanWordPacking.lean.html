Ambient packing for controlled Cartan word endpoints

A positive cell in the product of the actual factors is translated by fixed word lifts. These cells are disjoint after testing their projections in the ambient metric. The word-thickening theorem encloses them in one product of boxes, with the exact support exponential and polynomial radii.
