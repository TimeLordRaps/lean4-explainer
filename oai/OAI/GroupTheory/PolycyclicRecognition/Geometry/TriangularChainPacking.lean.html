Polynomial path packing for a concrete triangular representation

A proper left invariant metric compatible with an actual positive triangular matrix embedding has polynomial packing for paths of bounded logarithmic height. All coordinate and inverse estimates are proved here. No polynomial packing hypothesis is imposed on this representation.
