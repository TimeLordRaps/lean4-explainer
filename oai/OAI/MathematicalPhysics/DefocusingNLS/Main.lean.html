Stable blowup for the defocusing Schrödinger equation

Arbitrarily large odd powers admit a nonempty open family of classical solutions on the twelve-dimensional torus with finite-time self-similar blowup. For the same power and integer Sobolev order above eight, every Gaussian decay exponent above that order plus six has positive blowup probability.

The construction combines radial profiles, spectral classification, decay on expanding tori, nonlinear stable graphs, physical-data openness and Gaussian full support.
