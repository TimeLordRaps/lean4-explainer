Gluing the finite trajectories selected by a stable endpoint sequence

Adjacent closed slabs agree at their endpoints. Their locally finite cover therefore gives one continuous trajectory, with the same geometric decay between the discrete times. This is a supporting construction; its inputs are actual finite trajectories and their proved endpoint identities.
