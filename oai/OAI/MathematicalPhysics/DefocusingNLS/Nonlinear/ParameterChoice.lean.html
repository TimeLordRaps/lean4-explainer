Finite choices of the nonlinearity power and Sobolev index

The manuscript first constructs profiles for all sufficiently small positive a, then chooses an odd integer power with a = 1 / (p - 1), and only afterward chooses the finite Sobolev index. These lemmas justify that order of choices.
