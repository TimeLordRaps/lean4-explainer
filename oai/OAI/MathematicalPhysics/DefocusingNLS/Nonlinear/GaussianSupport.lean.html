Supporting probability lemmas for stable defocusing NLS blowup

These lemmas formalize the measure and quantifier steps in the proof of Corollary 1.2 of Stable self-similar blowup for a supercritical defocusing Schrödinger equation on the torus (September 2026). The independent head/tail argument uses Mathlib's measure-theoretic independence theorem.
