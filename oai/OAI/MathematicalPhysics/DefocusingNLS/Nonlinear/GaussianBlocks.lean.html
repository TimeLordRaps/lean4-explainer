Independent finite heads and infinite tails

The restrictions of a product sample to disjoint index sets are independent. For complex Gaussian coordinates, every nonempty open weighted finite-head event has positive probability. These facts apply to any countable Fourier indexing, including the twelve-dimensional frequency lattice.
