The forward part of the weighted graph map

In weighted coordinates the stable recurrence has a factor two. Rescaling the unstable coordinates by a fixed constant makes the mixed block small. The resulting estimates use the product sup norm, equivalent to the sum norm in the manuscript and complete on the same weighted sequences.
