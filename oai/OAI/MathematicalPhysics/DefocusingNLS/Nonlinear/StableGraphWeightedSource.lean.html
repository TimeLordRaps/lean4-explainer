Endpoint remainders define bounded weighted sequence sources

The zero-data remainder decays geometrically along the expanding radii. Pointwise Lipschitz bounds on a small state ball therefore give a bounded Lipschitz map on the weighted sequence ball. The extension outside that ball is used only to supply a total function to the local contraction.
