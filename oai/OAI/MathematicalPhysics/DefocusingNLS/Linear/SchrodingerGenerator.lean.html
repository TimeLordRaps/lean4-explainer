Two-derivative loss for the free Schrödinger evolution

In the common weighted-ℓ² model, the inclusion from H^k to H^(k-2) multiplies by (1+|n|²)⁻¹. The time derivative is the bounded multiplier -i |n|²/(1+|n|²). Finite Fourier sums give the derivative of the full flow by uniform convergence of their derivatives.
