Joint scale and forcing continuity of the finite-slab Duhamel operator

The operator has a uniform finite-slab bound and is strongly continuous in the starting radius. These two properties give joint continuity in the radius and the forcing path, without operator-norm continuity of the free Schrödinger propagator.
