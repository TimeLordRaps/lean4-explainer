Inhomogeneous propagation in the exact expanding Sobolev norms

The forcing at time τ is represented in Y_(L exp(τ/2)). Its free transport therefore lands in the same final space Y_(L exp(t/2)) for every τ ∈ [0,t]. The estimate keeps the exact exponential kernel and is uniform in L ≥ 1.
