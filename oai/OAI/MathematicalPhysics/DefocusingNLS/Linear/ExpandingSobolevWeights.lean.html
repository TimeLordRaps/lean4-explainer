Exact weights of the expanding-torus Sobolev norm

The norm from manuscript (lin:YL), after extracting (2π)^12, has squared Fourier weight L^(2a) ⟨n⟩^(12-2a) + L^(12-2k) |n|^(2k). The auxiliary one-dimensional weights will control its inverse sum.
