Joint continuity of the actual moving-scale Picard operator

The reaction is allowed to vary with the parameter in the topology of joint point evaluation. Compactness of the time slab turns this into continuity of its forcing history. Together with the strongly continuous free family and the uniformly bounded Duhamel operator this gives the required path continuity without an operator-norm assertion for Schrödinger evolution.
