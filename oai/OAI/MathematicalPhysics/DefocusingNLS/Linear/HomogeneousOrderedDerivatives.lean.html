Ordered Cartesian derivatives and the high homogeneous energy

The sum over all ordered k-tuples of Cartesian directions is exactly the Fourier weight |ξ|^(2k). This supplies the high-energy interpretation needed to apply one pointwise principal potential to each derivative component.
