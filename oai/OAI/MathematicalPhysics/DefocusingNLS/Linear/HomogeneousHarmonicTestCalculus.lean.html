Cartesian calculus for a radial test times a spherical harmonic

The degree-zero extension of the spherical harmonic is kept explicit: its Euler derivative is zero and its Cartesian Laplacian is the spherical eigenvalue divided by the squared radius. All formulas are local away from zero.
