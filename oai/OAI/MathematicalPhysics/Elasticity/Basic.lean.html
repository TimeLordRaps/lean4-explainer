Variational isotropic elasticity

H¹ is the closure of smooth value/gradient jets in L². Boundary data are the quotient by the closure of compactly supported smooth jets, and the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map is the physical variational displacement-to-traction pairing. Arbitrary-data Dirichlet well-posedness and independence of representatives are proved, not assumed. Identification of the quotient norm with boundary-chart fractional Sobolev norms is not established here.
