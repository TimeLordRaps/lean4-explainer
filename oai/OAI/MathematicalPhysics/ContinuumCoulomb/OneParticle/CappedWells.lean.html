The explicit cap in the isolated-well construction

The quintic transition is the normalized integral of a positive multiple of s²(1-s)². Capping removes a whole neighbourhood of both nuclear poles. The resulting pointwise bound is uniform in the secondary displacements, as required before any energy tuning is carried out.
