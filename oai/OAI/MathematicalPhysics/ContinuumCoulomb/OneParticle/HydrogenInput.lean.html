Minimal published hydrogen input for isolated-well energies

Gerald Teschl, Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics, second edition, AMS Graduate Studies in Mathematics 157 (2014), Theorem 10.10, equations (10.5) and (10.64): the lowest eigenvalue of -Δ-γ/|x| is -γ²/4. Set γ=2, divide the operator by two, and translate the centre. The exact specialization used below is PublishedHydrogenBottom. Spin contributes two identical summands and does not alter the bottom. https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-schroe/schroe2.pdf .

This conditional interface assumes the unperturbed hydrogen bottom. The isolated-well comparison follows by a variational perturbation argument.
