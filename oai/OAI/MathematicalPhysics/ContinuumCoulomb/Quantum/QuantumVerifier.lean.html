source

The promise-QMA interface for the Heisenberg source. The verifier uses the fixed H,T,CNOT gate set, complex quantum witnesses, ancillas initialized to zero, one output measurement and a polynomial-time TM2 circuit generator. There is no condition outside either promise set.
