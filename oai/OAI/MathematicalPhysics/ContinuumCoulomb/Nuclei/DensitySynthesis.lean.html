Nonnegative continuous-density synthesis for the unit-charge construction

The density is the uniform slab plus ΔV/(4π). Compact support of V inside the slab makes its attractive Coulomb field exactly the slab field plus V. A pointwise Laplacian bound makes the density nonnegative. Only C² regularity is required, as the manuscript's manufactured well is finitely smooth.
