Stability under rounding a nuclear position

Translated Hardy estimates uniformly control rational rounding on arbitrary form-domain states. The constant eight permits a pointwise kernel inequality also at the poles, using Lean's convention 0⁻¹ = 0, and suffices for the polynomial precision budget.
