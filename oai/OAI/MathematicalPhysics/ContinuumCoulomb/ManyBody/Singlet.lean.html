Exact singlet mediator calculations

The positive-spin conversion uses the singlet mediator of Piddock–Montanaro, The complexity of antiferromagnetic interactions and 2D lattices, Quantum Information and Computation 17 (2017), Section 2.4 and Lemma 5. The Pauli contractions and penalty identities use an unnormalized singlet vector, whose expectation values are twice the normalized values.
