Coulomb repulsion as a form-domain to L² multiplier

Both continuum comparisons need control of the repulsion applied to a localized many-electron vector, including its mixed block with the continuum complement. Translated pair Hardy and finite Cauchy--Schwarz prove a bound on the actual full repulsion, without summing over an exponentially large spin operator matrix.
