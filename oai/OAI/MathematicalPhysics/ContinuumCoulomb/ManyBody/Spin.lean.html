The two-spin Heisenberg interaction

The basis order is ↑↑, ↑↓, ↓↑, ↓↓. The matrix below is X ⊗ X + Y ⊗ Y + Z ⊗ Z = 2 SWAP - I in that basis. The exact singlet/triplet computation supplies the interaction bound used when rounding or deleting source coefficients in both continuum Coulomb manuscripts (their sections on rational source normalization).
