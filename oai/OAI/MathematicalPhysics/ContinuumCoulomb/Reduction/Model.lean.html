Full spinful continuum Coulomb energy

Nuclear data and electronic states use the weak H¹ domain and translated Hardy bounds. Antisymmetry permutes spin and position together. No orbital projection or spin sector is imposed. The bottom takes values in EReal so its definition does not assume existence of a normalized state or a minimizing eigenvector.
