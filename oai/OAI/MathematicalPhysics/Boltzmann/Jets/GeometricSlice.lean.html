Degenerate slices and nonnegative singular integrals

The line integral through the velocity axis is infinite. Its exceptional transverse parameters form an affine line of two-dimensional measure zero. These results keep that extended value explicit and justify the almost-everywhere scalar estimates, including products with arbitrary axial probability measures.
