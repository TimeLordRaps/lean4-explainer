The scalar contradiction in the branching argument

At the end of Section 9 of the supplied manuscript, the mass in the moving box is bounded below by 3^n * m₀ and above by C * (1 + (1+a)^n * t₀)^4. The following argument proves that those estimates cannot both hold when (1+a)^4 < 3. It assumes no kinetic equation, branching kernel, or existence statement.
