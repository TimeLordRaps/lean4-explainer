Summable cold-jet datum bounds

Section 4 of the supplied manuscript bounds the mass and energy of each annulus by a constant times s_j², and its absolute entropy by a constant times s_j² (1 + |log s_j|). Those scalar bounds are summable for the stated geometric scales, and their tails converge to zero. Indices start at zero; the manuscript's indices starting at one are obtained by removing the first term.
