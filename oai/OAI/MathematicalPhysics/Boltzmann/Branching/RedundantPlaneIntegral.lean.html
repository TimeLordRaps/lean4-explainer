A nonsingular three-dimensional coordinate map for a collision plane

The third coordinate is the coefficient of the normal, rather than distance along the unit normal. This normalization cancels the length of the normal in the redundant-parameter density. The transverse normal must be nonzero, just as in the graph chart used by the actual gain kernel.
