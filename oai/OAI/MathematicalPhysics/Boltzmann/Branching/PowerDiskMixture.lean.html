The positive disk mixture for the truncated transverse power

The hot-gain estimate uses the finite measure with density (1 + ‖p‖⁻ᑫ) 1_{‖p‖ < D}. Its singular part is a positive radius mixture of centered disks. Values at the origin are irrelevant for this identity.
