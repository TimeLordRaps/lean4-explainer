The graph chart for a collision plane

The chart records actual three-dimensional velocities. Its displacement satisfies the collision-plane equation, and the three-dimensional ball restriction is precisely the transverse-plus-axial restriction used in the geometric slice estimate. Identifying its measure with the Carleman plane measure remains a separate change-of-variables theorem.
