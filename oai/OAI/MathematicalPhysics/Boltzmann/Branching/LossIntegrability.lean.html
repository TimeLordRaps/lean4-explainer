Loss frequency and its first-moment bound

The angular normalization gives the loss-frequency formula from Section 2, equation eq:frequency. Fubini is used only with an explicit integrability hypothesis for the original collision integrand. Separate first-moment conditions ensure that the frequency integral itself is finite.
