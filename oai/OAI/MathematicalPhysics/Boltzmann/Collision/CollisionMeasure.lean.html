Measure preservation for a hard-sphere collision

For each fixed unit normal, the pre/post-collisional map is an orthogonal involution on the six-dimensional velocity-pair space. Its Euclidean norm is implemented with WithLp 2; the final measure statement uses the ordinary product of the two three-dimensional Lebesgue measures.
