Grand-canonical hard-sphere probability and kinetic fluctuation preliminaries

This development proves microscopic law normalization, scattering and covariance algebra, exact centering, and compact and Gaussian-growth test estimates. The dynamics, backward Gaussian field, and finite-dimensional limit are specified, but their existence or convergence is not proved. The almost-everywhere deterministic flow and backward Gaussian field constructions, connected-history estimates, sharp cumulant bounds, discrepancy and centering transfer, and finite-dimensional convergence remain open here.
