The isotropic two-dimensional periodic heat kernel and its Gaussian bound.

P. Maheux, Notes on Heat Kernels on Infinite dimensional Torus (16 February 2008), Sections 2.1 and 3.1, and Theorem 3.1, equation (3.15), pp. 5, 10--11. The isotropic two-dimensional kernel is rescaled from period 2π to period one. Its generator is the coordinate Laplacian, and its Gaussian estimate is the first upper bound in (3.15). Source: https://idpoisson.fr/maheux/InfiniteTorusV2.pdf
