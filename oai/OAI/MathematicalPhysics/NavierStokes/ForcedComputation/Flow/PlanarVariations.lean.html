The variational equations used for the planar quantitative estimates. The regularity input is the finite-dimensional smooth-dependence theorem: Teschl, Ordinary Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems (2012), Theorem 2.10, equations (2.49)--(2.51). The quantitative estimates follow from these equations by Gronwall inequalities.
