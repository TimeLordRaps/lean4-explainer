The seven-row reversible recording table in Section 2 of Finite Instructions and Solenoidal Shear Flows (2026), Lemma lem:recorder.

The inverse below is valid on the entire guarded domain, including tapes that do not satisfy the checkpoint invariant. The record is the pair consisting of the original state and the symbol read, as in Bennett, Logical reversibility of computation (1973), equations (9)--(11); the concrete seven-row construction and its proof are supplied here rather than assumed from that reference.

  | checkpoint q, (c, l, _) => some (scanLeft q, (c, l, true))
  | mark r, (_, l, _) => some (checkpoint r.1, (r.2, l, false))
  | scanRight r, (c, l, true) => some (mark r, (c, l, false))
  | scanRight r, (c, l, false) => some (scanRight r, (c, l, false))

  | scanLeft q, (c, l, _) => some (scanLeft q, (c, l, false))
