International Mathematical Olympiad 2002, Problem 1

Let n be a positive integer. Let T be the set of points (x, y) in the plane where x and y are non-negative integers and x + y < n. Each point of T is coloured red or blue, subject to the following condition: if a point (x, y) is red, then so are all points (x', y') of T with x' ≤ x and y' ≤ y. Let A be the number of ways of choosing n blue points with distinct x-coordinates, and let B be the number of ways of choosing n blue points with distinct y-coordinates. Prove that A = B.
