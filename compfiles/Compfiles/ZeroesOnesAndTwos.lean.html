(From Mathematical Puzzles: A Connoisseur's Collection by Peter Winkler.)

Let n be a natural number. Prove that

(a) n has a (nonzero) multiple whose representation in base 10 contains only zeroes and ones; and (b) 2^n has a multiple whose representation contains only ones and twos.
