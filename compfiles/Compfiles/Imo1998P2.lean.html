International Mathematical Olympiad 1998, Problem 2

In a competition, there are a contestants and b judges, where b ≥ 3 is an odd integer. Each judge rates each contestant as either "pass" or "fail". Suppose k is a number such that, for any two judges, their ratings coincide for at most k contestants.

Prove that k / a ≥ (b - 1) / (2b).
