USA Mathematical Olympiad 2020 Problem 6

Let n ≥ 2 be an integer. Let x₁ ≥ x₂ ≥ ⋯ ≥ xₙ and y₁ ≥ y₂ ≥ ⋯ ≥ yₙ be 2n real numbers such that

0 = x₁ + x₂ + ⋯ + xₙ = y₁ + y₂ + ⋯ + yₙ,

and

1 = x₁² + x₂² + ⋯ + xₙ² = y₁² + y₂² + ⋯ + yₙ².

Prove that

∑_{i=1}^{n} (xᵢyᵢ − xᵢyₙ₊₁₋ᵢ) ≥ 2/√(n−1).
