International Mathematical Olympiad 1990, Problem 5

Given an initial integer n₀ > 1, two players A and B choose integers n₁, n₂, n₃, ... alternately according to the following rules:

Knowing n₂ₖ, A chooses any integer n₂ₖ₊₁ such that n₂ₖ ≤ n₂ₖ₊₁ ≤ n₂ₖ².

Knowing n₂ₖ₊₁, B chooses any integer n₂ₖ₊₂ such that n₂ₖ₊₁/n₂ₖ₊₂ = p^r for some prime p and integer r ≥ 1.

Player A wins the game by choosing the number 1990; player B wins by choosing the number 1.

For which n₀ does (a) A have a winning strategy? (b) B have a winning strategy? (c) neither player have a winning strategy?

Formalization notes

The proof follows a solution sketch after kalva.
