International Mathematical Olympiad 1999, Problem 2

Let n ≥ 2 be a fixed integer. Find the least constant C such that

∑_{i < j} xᵢxⱼ(xᵢ² + xⱼ²) ≤ C (∑ i, xᵢ)⁴

for all non-negative real numbers x₁, ..., xₙ.
