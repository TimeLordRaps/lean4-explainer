International Mathematical Olympiad 2023, Problem 4

Let x₁, x₂, ... x₂₀₂₃ be distinct positive real numbers. Define

aₙ := √((x₁ + x₂ + ... + xₙ)(1/x₁ + 1/x₂ + ... + 1/xₙ)).

Suppose that aₙ is an integer for all n ∈ {1,...,2023}. Prove that 3034 ≤ a₂₀₂₃.
