International Mathematical Olympiad 2020, Problem 3

There are 4n pebbles of weights 1,2,3,...,4n. Each pebble is colored in one of n colors and there are four pebbles of each color. Show that we can arrange the pebbles into two piles such that the total weights of both piles are the same, and each pile contains two pebbles of each color.
