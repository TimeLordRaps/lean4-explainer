International Mathematical Olympiad 1990, Problem 2

Take n ≥ 3 and consider a set E of 2n - 1 distinct points on a circle. Suppose that exactly k of these points are to be colored black. Such a coloring is said to be "good" if there is at least one pair of black points such that the interior of one of the arcs between them contains exactly n points from E. Find the smallest value of k so that every such coloring of k points of E is good.
