International Mathematical Olympiad 1961, Problem 1.

Given constants a and b, solve the system of equations

x + y + z = a x² + y² + z² = b² xy = z²

for x,y,z. Give the conditions that a and b must satisfy so that the solutions x,y,z are distinct positive numbers.
