Iranian Mathematical Olympiad 1998, problem 3

Let x₁, x₂, x₃, x₄ be positive real numbers such that

x₁ ⬝ x₂ ⬝ x₃ ⬝ x₄ = 1.

Prove that x₁³ + x₂³ + x₃³ + x₄³ ≥ max(x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + x₄, 1/x₁ + 1/x₂ + 1/x₃ + 1/x₄).
