USA Mathematical Olympiad 1999, Problem 4

Let a₁, a₂, ..., aₙ be a sequence of n > 3 real numbers such that

a₁ + a₂ + ⋯ + aₙ ≥ n

and

a₁² + a₂² + ⋯ + aₙ² ≥ n².

Prove that max(a₁, a₂, ..., aₙ) ≥ 2.
