USA Mathematical Olympiad 2016, Problem 1

Let X₁, X₂, …, X₁₀₀ be a sequence of mutually distinct nonempty subsets of a set S. Any two sets Xᵢ and Xᵢ₊₁ are disjoint and their union is not the whole set S, that is, Xᵢ ∩ Xᵢ₊₁ = ∅ and Xᵢ ∪ Xᵢ₊₁ ≠ S, for all i ∈ {1, …, 99}. Find the smallest possible number of elements in S.
