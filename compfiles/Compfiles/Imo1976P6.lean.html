International Mathematical Olympiad 1976, Problem 6

The sequence u_0, u_1, u_2, ... is defined by: u_0 = 2, u_1 = 5/2, u_{n+1} = u_n(u_{n-1}^2 - 2) - u_1 for n = 1, 2, ... . Prove that ⌊u_n⌋ = 2^(2^n - (-1)^n)/3 for all positive n, where ⌊x⌋ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to x.
