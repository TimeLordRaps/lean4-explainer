USA Mathematical Olympiad 2023, Problem 2

Let ℝ+ be the set of positive real numbers. Find all functions f: ℝ+ → ℝ+ that satisfy the equation

f(x⬝y + f(x)) = x⬝f(y) + 2

for all x,y ∈ ℝ+.
