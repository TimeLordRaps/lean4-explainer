USA Mathematical Olympiad 1972, Problem 4

Let k be the real cube root of 2. Find integers A, B, C, a, b, c such that

|(Ax² + Bx + C)/(ax² + bx + c) − k| < |x − k|

for all non-negative rational numbers x.
