USA Mathematical Olympiad 1980, Problem 5

Let x,y,z be real numbers in the closed interval [0,1]. Show that

x/(y + z + 1) + y/(z + x + 1) + z/(x + y + 1) ≤ 1 + (1 - x)(1 - y)(1 - z).
