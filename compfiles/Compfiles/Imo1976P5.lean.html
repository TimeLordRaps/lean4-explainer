International Mathematical Olympiad 1976, Problem 5

n is a positive integer and m = 2n. aᵢⱼ = 0, 1 or -1 for 1 ≤ i ≤ n, 1 ≤ j ≤ m. The m unknowns x₁, x₂, ... , xₘ satisfy the n equations:

aᵢ₁x₁ + aᵢ₂x₂ + ... + aᵢₘxₘ = 0,

for i = 1, 2, ... , n. Prove that the system has a solution in integers of absolute value at most m, not all zero.
