USA Mathematical Olympiad 1983, Problem 5

Consider an open interval of length 1/n on the real number line, where n is a positive integer. Prove that the number of irreducible fractions p/q, with 1 ≤ q ≤ n, contained in the given interval is at most (n + 1) / 2.
