International Mathematical Olympiad 1991, Problem 6

An infinite sequence x₀,x₁,x₂,... of real numbers is said to be bounded if there is a constant C such that |xᵢ| ≤ C for every i ≥ 0.

Given any real number a > 1, construct a bounded infinite sequence x₀,x₁,x₂,... such that

|xᵢ - xⱼ|⬝|i - j|ᵃ ≥ 1

for every pair of distinct nonnegative integers i, j.
