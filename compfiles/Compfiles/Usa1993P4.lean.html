USA Mathematical Olympiad 1993, Problem 4

The sequence aₙ of odd positive integers is defined as follows: a₁ = r, a₂ = s, and aₙ is the greatest odd divisor of aₙ₋₁ + aₙ₋₂. Show that, for sufficiently large n, aₙ is constant and find this constant (in terms of r and s).
