USA Mathematical Olympiad 2018, Problem 6

Let $a_n$ be the number of permutations $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)$ of the numbers $(1, 2, \ldots, n)$ such that the $n$ ratios $\frac{x_k}{k}$ for $1 \le k \le n$ are all distinct. Prove that $a_n$ is odd for all $n \ge 1$.
