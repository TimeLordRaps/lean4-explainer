International Mathematical Olympiad 2001, Problem 3

Twenty-one girls and twenty-one boys took part in a mathematical competition. It turned out that each contestant solved at most six problems, and for each pair of a girl and a boy, there was at most one problem solved by both the girl and the boy. Show that there was a problem solved by at least three girls and at least three boys.
