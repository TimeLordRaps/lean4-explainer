USA Mathematical Olympiad 1977, Problem 1

Determine all pairs of positive integers (m, n) such that

1 + x ^ n + x ^ (2 * n) + ... + x ^ (m * n)

is divisible by

1 + x + x ^ 2 + ... + x ^ m.
