International Mathematical Olympiad 2006, Problem 3

Determine the least real number $M$ such that $$ \left| ab(a^2 - b^2) + bc(b^2 - c^2) + ca(c^2 - a^2) \right| ≤ M (a^2 + b^2 + c^2)^2 $$ for all real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$.

    The least M with |ab(a^2-b^2) + bc(b^2-c^2) + ca(c^2-a^2)| at most M(a^2+b^2+c^2)^2 for all reals is 9 sqrt(2) / 32.

    1. Problem: determine the least real M such that the stated inequality holds for all real a, b, c. The statement is IsLeast of the set of such M with the determined answer solution = 9 * sqrt 2 / 32.
    2. Solution (complete, no sorry): substitute x = a - b, y = b - c, z = c - a, s = a + b + c, following Evan Chen's notes cited in the file.
    3. Part one (proof₁) shows the inequality holds for this M, using auxiliary inequalities such as lhs_ineq, mid_ineq and rhs_ineq.
    4. Part two (proof₂) shows every valid M is at least 9 sqrt(2) / 32 by plugging in a = 2 - 3 sqrt 2, b = 2, c = 2 + 3 sqrt 2, where the inequality is an equality.
