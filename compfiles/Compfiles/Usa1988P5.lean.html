USA Mathematical Olympiad 1988, Problem 5

Let p(x) be the polynomial (1 - x)ᵃ (1 - x²)ᵇ (1 - x³)ᶜ ... (1 - x³²)ᵏ, where a, b, ..., k are integers. When expanded in powers of x, the coefficient of x¹ is -2 and the coefficients of x², x³, ... , x³² are all zero. Find k.
