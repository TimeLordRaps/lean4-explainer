International Mathematical Olympiad 2016, Problem 5

The equation

(x - 1)(x - 2) ... (x - 2016) = (x - 1)(x - 2) ... (x - 2016)

is written on the board. What is the least possible value of k for which it is possible to erase exactly k of these 4032 factors such that at least one factor remains on each side and the resulting equation has no real solutions?
