International Mathematical Olympiad 2021, Problem 3

Let D be an interior point of the acute triangle $ABC$ with AB > AC so that ∠DAB = ∠CAD. The point E on the segment AC satisfies ∠ADE = ∠BCD, the point F on the segment AB satisfies ∠FDA = ∠DBC, and the point X on the line AC satisfies CX = BX. Let O₁ and O₂ be the circumcenters of the triangles ADC and EXD, respectively. Prove that the lines BC, EF, and O₁O₂ are concurrent.
