    The fraction (21n+4)/(14n+3) is in lowest terms for every natural number n.

    1. Problem: prove that (21n+4)/(14n+3) is irreducible for all natural n. The formalization states this as Nat.Coprime (21 * n + 4) (14 * n + 3), since Lean has no notion of an irreducible fraction.
    2. Solution (complete, no sorry): if k divides both numbers then it divides 3 * (14n+3) - 2 * (21n+4), and that difference equals 1, so k divides 1.
    3. The file says the solution is imported from the Mathlib Archive (solutionImportedFrom).
