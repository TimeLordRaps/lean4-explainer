USA Mathematical Olympiad 1997, Problem 1

Let p₁, p₂, p₃, ... be the prime numbers listed in increasing order, and let x₀ be a real number between 0 and 1. For positive integer k, define

xₖ = 0, if xₖ₋₁ = 0, xₖ = {pₖ / xₖ₋₁}, if xₖ₋₁ ≠ 0,

where {x} denotes the fractional part of x. (The fractional part of x is given by x - ⌊x⌋, where ⌊x⌋ is the greatest integer less than or equal to x.)

Find, with proof, all x₀ satisfying 0 < x₀ < 1 for which the sequence x₀, x₁, x₂, ... eventually becomes 0.
