USA Mathematical Olympiad 2018, Problem 3

Let n ≥ 2 be an integer, and let {a₁, ..., aₘ} denote the m = φ(n) integers less than n and relatively prime to n. Assume that every prime divisor of m also divides n. Prove that m divides a₁ᵏ + ⋯ + aₘᵏ for every positive integer k.
