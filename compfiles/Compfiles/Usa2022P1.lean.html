USA Mathematical Olympiad 2022, Problem 1

Let a and b be positive integers. The cells of an (a+b+1) × (a+b+1) grid are colored amber and bronze such that there are at least a² + ab - b amber cells and at least b² + ab - a bronze cells. Prove that it is possible to choose a amber cells and b bronze cells such that no two of the a + b chosen cells lie in the same row or column.
