International Mathematical Olympiad 1989, Problem 3

Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers and let $S$ be a set of $n$ points in the plane such that (i) no three points of $S$ are collinear, and (ii) for each point $P$ of $S$ there are at least $k$ points of $S$ equidistant from $P$. Prove that $k < \frac{1}{2} + \sqrt{2n}$.
