International Mathematical Olympiad 2017, Problem 6

A point (x,y) ∈ ℤ × ℤ is called primitive if gcd(x,y) = 1. Let S be a finite set of primitive points. Prove that there exists n > 0 and integers a₀,a₁,...,aₙ such that

a₀xⁿ + a₁xⁿ⁻¹y + a₂xⁿ⁻²y² + ... + aₙ₋₁xyⁿ⁻¹ + aₙyⁿ = 1

for each (x,y) ∈ S.
