International Mathematical Olympiad 1961, Problem 2

Let $a, b, c$ be the sides of a triangle, and $T$ its area. Prove: $$ a^2 + b^2 + c^2 \ge 4\sqrt{3} T $$ Equality holds if and only if the triangle is equilateral.
