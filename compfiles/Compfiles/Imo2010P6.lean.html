International Mathematical Olympiad 2010, Problem 6

Let a₁, a₂, a₃, ... be a sequence of positive real numbers. Suppose that for some positive integer s, we have aₙ = max{aₖ + aₙ₋ₖ | 1 ≤ k ≤ n − 1} for all n > s. Prove that there exist positive integers l and N, with l ≤ s and such that aₙ = aₗ + aₙ₋ₗ for all n ≥ N.
