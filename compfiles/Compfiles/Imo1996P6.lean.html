International Mathematical Olympiad 1996, Problem 6

Let p, q, n be three positive integers with p + q < n. Let (x₀, x₁, . . . , xₙ) be an (n + 1)-tuple of integers satisfying the following conditions: (a) x₀ = xₙ = 0. (b) For each i with 1 ≤ i ≤ n, either xᵢ − xᵢ₋₁ = p or xᵢ − xᵢ₋₁ = −q. Show that there exist indices i < j with (i, j) ≠ (0, n), such that xᵢ = xⱼ.
