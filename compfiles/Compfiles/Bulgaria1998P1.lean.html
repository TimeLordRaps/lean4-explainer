Bulgarian Mathematical Olympiad 1998, Problem 1

We will be considering colorings in 2 colors of n (distinct) points A₁, A₂, ..., Aₙ. Call such a coloring "good" if there exist three points Aᵢ, Aⱼ, A₂ⱼ₋ᵢ, 1 ≤ i < 2j - i ≤ n, which are colored the same color.

Find the least natural number n (n ≥ 3) such that all colorings of n points are good.
