International Mathematical Olympiad 2003, Problem 5

Given n > 2 and reals x₁ ≤ x₂ ≤ ... ≤ xₙ, show that

(∑ᵢⱼ |xᵢ - xⱼ|)² ≤ (2/3)(n² - 1) ∑ᵢⱼ (xᵢ - xⱼ)².

Show that we have equality iff the sequence is an arithmetic progression.
