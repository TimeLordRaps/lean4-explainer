USA Mathematical Olympiad 1985, Problem 5

0 < a₁ ≤ a₂ ≤ a₃ ≤ ⋯ is an unbounded sequence of integers. Let bₙ = m if aₘ is the first member of the sequence to equal or exceed n. Given that a₁₉ = 85, what is the maximum possible value of a₁ + a₂ + ⋯ + a₁₉ + b₁ + b₂ + ⋯ + b₈₅?
