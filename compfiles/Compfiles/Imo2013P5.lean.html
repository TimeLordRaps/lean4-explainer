International Mathematical Olympiad 2013, Problem 5

Let ℚ>₀ be the set of positive rational numbers. Let f: ℚ>₀ → ℝ be a function satisfying the conditions

(1) f(x) * f(y) ≥ f(x * y) (2) f(x + y) ≥ f(x) + f(y)

for all x,y ∈ ℚ>₀. Given that f(a) = a for some rational a > 1, prove that f(x) = x for all x ∈ ℚ>₀.
