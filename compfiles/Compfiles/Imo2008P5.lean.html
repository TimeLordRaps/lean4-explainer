International Mathematical Olympiad 2008, Problem 5

Let n and k be positive integers with k ≥ n and k - n an even number. There are 2n lamps labelled 1, 2, ..., 2n each of which can be either on or off. Initially all the lamps are off. We consider sequences of steps: at each step one of the lamps is switched (from on to off or from off to on). Let N be the number of such sequences consisting of k steps and resulting in the state where lamps 1 through n are all on, and lamps n + 1 through 2n are all off. Let M be the number of such sequences consisting of k steps, resulting in the state where lamps 1 through n are all on, and lamps n + 1 through 2n are all off, but where none of the lamps n + 1 through 2n is ever switched on.

Determine N/M.
