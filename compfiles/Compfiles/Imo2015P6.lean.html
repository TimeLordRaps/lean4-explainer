International Mathematical Olympiad 2015, Problem 6

The sequence $a_1, a_2, \dots$ of integers satisfies the conditions

  1. $1 ≤ a_j ≤ 2015$ for all $j ≥ 1$,
  2. $k + a_k ≠ l + a_l$ for all $1 ≤ k < l$. Prove that there exist two positive integers $b$ and $N$ for which $$\left|\sum_{j=m+1}^n (a_j-b)\right| ≤ 1007^2$$ for all integers $m,n$ such that $N ≤ m < n$.
