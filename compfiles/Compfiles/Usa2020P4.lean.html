USA Mathematical Olympiad 2020, Problem 4

Suppose that (a₁, b₁), (a₂, b₂), ..., (a₁₀₀, b₁₀₀) are distinct ordered pairs of nonnegative integers. Let N denote the number of pairs of integers (i, j) satisfying 1 ≤ i < j ≤ 100 and |aᵢbⱼ − aⱼbᵢ| = 1. Determine the largest possible value of N over all possible choices of the 100 ordered pairs.
