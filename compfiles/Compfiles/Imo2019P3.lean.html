International Mathematical Olympiad 2019, Problem 3

A social network has 2019 users, some pairs of which are friends (friendship is symmetric). If A, B, C are three users such that AB are friends and AC are friends but BC is not, then the administrator may perform the following operation: change the friendships such that BC are friends, but AB and AC are no longer friends. Initially, 1009 users have 1010 friends and 1010 users have 1009 friends. Prove that the administrator can make a sequence of operations such that all users have at most 1 friend.
