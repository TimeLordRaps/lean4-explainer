USA Mathematical Olympiad 1995, Problem 5

A graph with n points and k edges has no triangles. Show that it has a point P such that there are at most k(1 - 4k/n²) edges between points not joined to P (by an edge).

    Double counting: the sum of the degrees of the neighbors of P equals the number of edges of G having an endpoint adjacent to P. Indeed, every edge incident to a neighbor Q of P has Q as its unique endpoint adjacent to P, since the graph is triangle-free.
