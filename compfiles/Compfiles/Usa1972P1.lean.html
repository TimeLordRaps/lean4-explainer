USA Mathematical Olympiad 1972, Problem 1

Let (a, b, ... , k) denote the greatest common divisor of the integers a, b, ... k and [a, b, ... , k] denote their least common multiple.

Show that for any positive integers a, b, c we have (a, b, c)² [a, b] [b, c] [c, a] = [a, b, c]² (a, b) (b, c) (c, a).
