USA Mathematical Olympiad 1990, Problem 3

Show that for any odd positive integer n, we can always divide the set {n, n+1, n+2, ... , n+32} into two parts, one with 14 numbers and one with 19, so that the numbers in each part can be arranged in a circle, with each number relatively prime to its two neighbours.
