USA Mathematical Olympiad 2009, Problem 4

For n ≥ 2, let a₁, a₂, ..., aₙ be positive real numbers such that

(a₁ + a₂ + ... + aₙ) (1/a₁ + 1/a₂ + ... + 1/aₙ) ≤ (n + 1/2)².

Prove that max(a₁, a₂, ..., aₙ) ≤ 4 min(a₁, a₂, ..., aₙ).
