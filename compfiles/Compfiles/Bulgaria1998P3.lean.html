Bulgarian Mathematical Olympiad 1998, Problem 3

Let ℝ⁺ be the set of positive real numbers. Prove that there does not exist a function f: ℝ⁺ → ℝ⁺ such that

(f(x))² ≥ f(x + y) * (f(x) + y)

for every x,y ∈ ℝ⁺.
