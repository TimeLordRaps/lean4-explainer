USA Mathematical Olympiad 2014, Problem 1

Let a, b, c, d be real numbers such that b-d ≥ 5 and all zeros x₁, x₂, x₃, x₄ of x⁴+ax³+bx²+cx+d are real. Find the smallest value the product $(x₁²+1)(x₂²+1)(x₃²+1)(x₄²+1)$ can take.
