USA Mathematical Olympiad 2008, Problem 3

Let n be a positive integer. Denote by Sₙ the set of points (x, y) with integer coordinates such that

|x| + |y + 1/2| < n.

A path is a sequence of distinct points (x₁, y₁), (x₂, y₂), ..., (x_ℓ, y_ℓ) in Sₙ such that, for i = 2, ..., ℓ, the distance between (xᵢ, yᵢ) and (xᵢ₋₁, yᵢ₋₁) is 1. Prove that the points in Sₙ cannot be partitioned into fewer than n paths.
