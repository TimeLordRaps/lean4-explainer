International Mathematical Olympiad 2003, Problem 1

Let A be a 101-element subset of S = {1,2,...10⁶}. Prove that there exist numbers t₁, t₂, ..., t₁₀₀ in S such that the sets

Aⱼ = {x + tⱼ | x ∈ A}, j = 1,2, ..., 100

are pairwise disjoint.
