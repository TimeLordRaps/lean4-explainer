International Mathematical Olympiad 1981, Problem 6

Suppose that f : ℕ × ℕ → ℕ satisfies

  1. f (0, y) = y + 1
  2. f (x + 1, 0) = f (x, 1),
  3. f (x + 1, y + 1) = f (x, f (x + 1, y))

for all x y ∈ ℕ.

Determine f (4, 1981).
