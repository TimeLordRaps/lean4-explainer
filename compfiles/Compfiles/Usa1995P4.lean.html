USA Mathematical Olympiad 1995, Problem 4

a₀, a₁, a₂, ... is an infinite sequence of integers such that aₙ - aₘ is divisible by n - m for all (unequal) n and m. For some polynomial p(x) we have p(n) > |aₙ| for all n. Show that there is a polynomial q(x) such that q(n) = aₙ for all n.
