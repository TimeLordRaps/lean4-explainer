USA Mathematical Olympiad 1979, Problem 3

$a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ is an arbitrary sequence of positive integers. A member of the sequence is picked at random. Its value is $a$. Another member is picked at random, independently of the first. Its value is $b$. Then a third, value $c$. Show that the probability that $a + b + c$ is divisible by $3$ is at least $1 / 4$.
