International Mathematical Olympiad 2004, Problem 4

Let n ≥ 3 be an integer. Let t₁, t₂, ..., tₙ be positive real numbers such that

n² + 1 > (t₁ + t₂ + ⋯ + tₙ) (1/t₁ + 1/t₂ + ⋯ + 1/tₙ).

Show that tᵢ, tⱼ, tₖ are the side lengths of a triangle for all i, j, k with 1 ≤ i < j < k ≤ n.
