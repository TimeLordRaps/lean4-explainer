USA Mathematical Olympiad 1999, Problem 3

Let p > 2 be a prime and let a, b, c, d be integers not divisible by p, such that

{ra/p} + {rb/p} + {rc/p} + {rd/p} = 2

for any integer r not divisible by p. (Here, {t} = t − ⌊t⌋ is the fractional part.) Prove that at least two of the numbers a + b, a + c, a + d, b + c, b + d, c + d are divisible by p.
