International Mathematical Olympiad 2001, Problem 5

Let ABC be a triangle. Let AP bisect ∠BAC and let BQ bisect ∠ABC, with P on BC and Q on AC. If AB + BP = AQ + QB and ∠BAC = 60°, what are the angles of the triangle?
