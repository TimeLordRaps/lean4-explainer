USA Mathematical Olympiad 1983, Problem 3

S₁, S₂, ..., Sₙ are subsets of the real line. Each Sᵢ is the union of two closed intervals. Any three Sᵢ have a point in common. Show that there is a point which belongs to at least half the Sᵢ.
