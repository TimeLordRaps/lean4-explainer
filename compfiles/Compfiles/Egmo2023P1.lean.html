European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad 2023, Problem 1

There are n ≥ 3 positive real numbers a_1, a_2, . . . , a_n. For each 1 ≤ i ≤ n we let b_i = (a_(i-1) + a_(i+1)) / a_i (here we define a_0 to be a_n and a_(n+1) to be a_1). Assume that for all i and j in the range 1 to n, we have a_i ≤ a_j if and only if b_i ≤ b_j. Prove that a_1 = a_2 = ... = a_n.
