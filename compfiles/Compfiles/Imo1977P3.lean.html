International Mathematical Olympiad 1977, Problem 3

Given an integer n > 2, let Vₙ be the set of integers 1 + kn for k a positive integer. A number m in Vₙ is called indecomposable if it cannot be expressed as the product of two members of Vₙ. Prove that there is a number in Vₙ which can be expressed as the product of indecomposable members of Vₙ in more than one way (decompositions which differ solely in the order of factors are not regarded as different).
