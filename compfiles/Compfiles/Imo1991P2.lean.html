International Mathematical Olympiad 1991, Problem 2

Let n > 6 be an integer and a₁, a₂, ..., aₖ be all the natural numbers less than n and relatively prime to n. If

a₂ - a₁ = a₃ - a₂ = ... = aₖ - aₖ₋₁ > 0,

prove that n must either be a prime number of a power of 2.
