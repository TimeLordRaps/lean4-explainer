USA Mathematical Olympiad 2000, Problem 6

Let a₁, b₁, a₂, b₂, ..., aₙ, bₙ be nonnegative real numbers. Prove that

∑ᵢⱼ min(aᵢaⱼ, bᵢbⱼ) ≤ ∑ᵢⱼ min(aᵢbⱼ, aⱼbᵢ),

where each sum is taken over all n² pairs (i, j).

    The generalized min kernel Mᵢⱼ = min (u i * w j) (u j * w i), with u and w nonnegative, is positive semidefinite. On the support of w it equals w i * w j * min (u i / w i) (u j / w j), so this reduces to min_kernel_nonneg.
