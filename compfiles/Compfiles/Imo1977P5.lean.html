International Mathematical Olympiad 1977, Problem 5

Let $a,b$ be two natural numbers. When we divide $a^2+b^2$ by $a+b$, we get the remainder $r$ and the quotient $q$. Determine all pairs $(a, b)$ for which $q^2 + r = 1977$.
