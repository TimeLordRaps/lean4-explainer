International Mathematical Olympiad 2025, Problem 4

A proper divisor of a positive integer N is a positive divisor of N other than N itself. The infinite sequence a₁, a₂, ... consists of positive integers, each of which has at least three proper divisors. For each n ⩾ 1, the integer aₙ₊₁ is the sum of the three largest proper divisors of aₙ. Determine all possible values of a₁.
