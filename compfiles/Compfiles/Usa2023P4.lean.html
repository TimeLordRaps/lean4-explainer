USA Mathematical Olympiad 2023, Problem 4

Positive integers a and N are fixed, and N positive integers are written on a blackboard. Alice and Bob play the following game. On Alice's turn, she must replace some integer n on the board with n + a, and on Bob's turn he must replace some even integer n on the board with n/2. Alice goes first and they alternate turns. If Bob has no valid moves on his turn the game ends.

After analyzing the N integers on the board, Bob realizes that, regardless of what moves Alices makes, he will be able to force the game to end eventually. Show that, in fact, no matter what either player does, for these values of a and N and these particular N integers, the game is guaranteed to end, regardless of either player's moves.
