International Mathematical Olympiad 1981, Problem 2

Let $1 \le r \le n$ and consider all subsets of $r$ elements of the set $\{ 1, 2, \ldots , n \}$. Each of these subsets has a smallest member. Let $F(n,r)$ denote the arithmetic mean of these smallest numbers; prove that [ F(n,r) = \frac{n+1}{r+1}. ]
