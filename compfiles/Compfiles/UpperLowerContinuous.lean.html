Suppose f : ℝ -> ℝ is continuous in both the upper topology (where the basic open sets are half-open intervals (a, b]) and lower topology (where the basic open sets are half-open intervals [a,b)). Then f is continuous in the usual topology (where the basic open sets are open intervals (a,b)) and also monotone nondecreasing.
