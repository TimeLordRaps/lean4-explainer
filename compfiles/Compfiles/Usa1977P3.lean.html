    Suppose that x ^ 4 + x ^ 3 - 1 is divisible by the quadratic x ^ 2 - s * x + p, i.e. both coefficients of the remainder (s ^ 3 + s ^ 2 - 2 * p * s - p) * x + (p ^ 2 - p * (s ^ 2 + s) - 1) of the long division vanish. Then p is a root of the sextic x ^ 6 + x ^ 4 + x ^ 3 - x ^ 2 - 1.

    This is the Vieta elimination step of the problem: writing the four roots of the quartic as a, b, c, d with s = a + b and p = a * b, the two remainder relations are exactly the third and fourth elementary symmetric relations between the roots.

    Suppose that `x ^ 4 + x ^ 3 - 1` is divisible by the quadratic `x ^ 2 - s * x + p`, i.e. both coefficients of the remainder `(s ^ 3 + s ^ 2 - 2 * p * s - p) * x + (p ^ 2 - p * (s ^ 2 + s) - 1)` of the long division vanish. Then `p` is a root of the sextic `x ^ 6 + x ^ 4 + x ^ 3 - x ^ 2 - 1`. This is the Vieta elimination step of the problem: writing the four roots of the quartic as `a, b, c, d` with `s = a + b` and `p = a * b`, the two remainder relations are exactly the third and fourth elementary symmetric relations between the roots.
