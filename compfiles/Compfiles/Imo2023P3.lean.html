International Mathematical Olympiad 2023, Problem 3

For each integer k ≥ 2, determine all infinite sequences of positive integers a₁, a₂, ... for which there exists a polynomial P of the form

P(x) = xᵏ + cₖ₋₁xᵏ⁻¹ + ... + c₁x + c₀,

where c₀, c₁, ..., cₖ₋₁ are non-negative integers, such that

P(aₙ) = aₙ₊₁aₙ₊₂⋯aₙ₊ₖ

for every integer n ≥ 1.
