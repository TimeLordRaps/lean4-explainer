USA Mathematical Olympiad 1998, Problem 3

Let a₀,a₁,...,aₙ be real numbers from the interval (0,π/2) such that

tan(a₀ - π/4) + tan(a₁ - π/4) + ... + tan(aₙ - π/4) ≥ n - 1.

Prove that

tan(a₀)tan(a₁)...tan(aₙ) ≥ nⁿ⁺¹.
