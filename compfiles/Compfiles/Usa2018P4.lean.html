USA Mathematical Olympiad 2018, Problem 4

Let p be a prime, and let a₁, ..., aₚ be integers. Show that there exists an integer k such that the numbers

a₁ + k, a₂ + 2k, ..., aₚ + pk

produce at least ½p distinct remainders upon division by p.
