USA Mathematical Olympiad 1991, Problem 2

For each non-empty subset of {1, 2, ... , n} take the sum of the elements divided by the product. Show that the sum of the resulting quantities is n² + 2n - (n + 1)sₙ, where sₙ = 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + ... + 1/n.
