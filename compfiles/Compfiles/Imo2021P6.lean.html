International Mathematical Olympiad 2021, Problem 6

Let m ≥ 2 be an integer, A a finite set of integers (not necessarily positive) and B₁, B₂, ... Bₘ subsets of A. Suppose that for every k = 1, 2, ..., m, the sum of the elements of Bₖ is m^k. Prove that A contains at least m/2 elements.
