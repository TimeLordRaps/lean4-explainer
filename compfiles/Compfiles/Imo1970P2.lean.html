International Mathematical Olympiad 1970, Problem 2

Let a, b and n be integers greater than 1, and let a and b be the bases of two number systems. A_{n-1} and A_n are numbers in the system with base a, and B_{n-1} and B_n are numbers in the system with base b; these are related as follows:

A_n = x_n x_{n-1} ⋯ x_0, A_{n-1} = x_{n-1} x_{n-2} ⋯ x_0, B_n = x_n x_{n-1} ⋯ x_0, B_{n-1} = x_{n-1} x_{n-2} ⋯ x_0,

(written as digit strings in the respective systems) with x_n ≠ 0 and x_{n-1} ≠ 0.

Prove that A_{n-1}/A_n < B_{n-1}/B_n if and only if a > b.
