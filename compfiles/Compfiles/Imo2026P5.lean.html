International Mathematical Olympiad 2026, Problem 5

Let ℝ_{>0} be the set of positive real numbers. Determine all functions f : ℝ_{>0} → ℝ_{>0} such that

√((x² + f(y)²)/2) ≥ (f(x) + y)/2 ≥ √(x·f(y))

for every x, y ∈ ℝ_{>0}.

Statement formalization adapted from AxiomMath/IMO2026; proof adapted from Humanfia's Kimi-K3 solutions (https://github.com/humanfia/imo2026).
