International Mathematical Olympiad 2023, Problem 6

Let ABC be an equilateral triangle. Let A₁,B₁,C₁ be interior points of ABC such that BA₁=A₁C, CB₁ = B₁A, AC₁=C₁B, and

∠BA₁C + ∠CB₁A + ∠C₁B = 480°.

Let BC₁ and CB₁ meet at A₂, let CA₁ and AC₁ meet at B₂, and let AB₁ and BA₁ meet at $C₂.

Prove that if triangle A₁B₁C₁ is scalene, then the three circumcircles of triangles AA₁A₂, BB₁B₂ and CC₁C₂ all pass through two common points.
