USA Mathematical Olympiad 2007, Problem 4

An animal with n cells is a connected figure consisting of n equal-sized square cells (equivalently, a polyomino with n cells). A dinosaur is an animal with at least 2007 cells. It is said to be primitive if its cells cannot be partitioned into two or more dinosaurs. Find with proof the maximum number of cells in a primitive dinosaur.
