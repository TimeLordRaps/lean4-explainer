International Mathematical Olympiad 1971, Problem 6

Let $A = (a_{ij})$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with non-negative integer entries. Suppose that for all $i, j$, if $a_{ij} = 0$, then the sum of the elements in the $i$-th row plus the sum of the elements in the $j$-th column is at least $n$. Prove that the sum of all elements in the matrix is at least $n^2 / 2$.
