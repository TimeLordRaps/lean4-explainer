International Mathematical Olympiad 1966, Problem 4

Prove that for every natural number n and for every real number x that is not of the form kπ/2ᵗ for t a non-negative integer and k any integer,

1 / (sin 2x) + 1 / (sin 4x) + ... + 1 / (sin 2ⁿx) = cot x - cot 2ⁿ x.
