International Mathematical Olympiad 2022, Problem 2

Let ℝ+ be the set of positive real numbers. Determine all functions f: ℝ+ → ℝ+ such that for each x ∈ ℝ+, there is exactly one y ∈ ℝ+ satisfying

x·f(y) + y·f(x) ≤ 2
