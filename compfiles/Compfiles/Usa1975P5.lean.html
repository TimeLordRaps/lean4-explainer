USA Mathematical Olympiad 1975, Problem 5

A pack of n cards, including three aces, is well shuffled. Cards are turned over in turn. Show that the expected number of cards that must be turned over to reach the second ace is (n+1)/2.

Formalization notes

We model the well-shuffled pack by the set of positions occupied by the three aces, which is uniformly distributed over the 3-element subsets of {1, ..., n}. The number of cards that must be turned over to reach the second ace is then the middle (second-smallest) of the three ace positions, and the expected number of cards is the average of this quantity over all 3-element subsets.
