USA Mathematical Olympiad 2011, Problem 6

Let A be a set with |A| = 225, meaning that A has 225 elements. Suppose further that there are eleven subsets A₁, A₂, ..., A₁₁ of A such that |Aᵢ| = 45 for 1 ≤ i ≤ 11 and |Aᵢ ∩ Aⱼ| = 9 for 1 ≤ i < j ≤ 11. Prove that |A₁ ∪ A₂ ∪ ··· ∪ A₁₁| ≥ 165, and give an example for which equality holds.
