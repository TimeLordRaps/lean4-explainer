International Mathematical Olympiad 2014, Problem 4

Let P and Q be on segment BC of an acute triangle ABC such that ∠PAB = ∠BCA and ∠CAQ = ∠ABC. Let M and N be points on lines AB and AQ, respectively, such that P is the midpoint of AM and Q is the midpoint of AN. Prove that BM and CN meet on the circumcircle of triangle ABC.
