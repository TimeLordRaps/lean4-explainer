International Mathematical Olympiad 1975, Problem 6

Find all polynomials P, in two variables, with the following properties:

(i) for a positive integer n and all real t, x, y P(tx, ty) = tⁿP(x, y) (that is, P is homogeneous of degree n),

(ii) for all real a, b, c, P(b + c, a) + P(c + a, b) + P(a + b, c) = 0,

(iii) P(1, 0) = 1.
