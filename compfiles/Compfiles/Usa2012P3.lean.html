USA Mathematical Olympiad 2012, Problem 3

Determine which integers n > 1 have the property that there exists an infinite sequence a₁, a₂, a₃, ... of nonzero integers such that the equality

aₖ + 2a₂ₖ + ⋯ + naₙₖ = 0

holds for every positive integer k.
