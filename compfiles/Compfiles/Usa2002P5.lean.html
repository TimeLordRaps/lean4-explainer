USA Mathematical Olympiad 2002, Problem 5

Let a, b be integers greater than 2. Prove that there exists a positive integer k and a finite sequence n₁, n₂, ..., nₖ of positive integers such that n₁ = a, nₖ = b, and nᵢ + nᵢ₊₁ divides nᵢnᵢ₊₁ for each i (1 ≤ i < k).
