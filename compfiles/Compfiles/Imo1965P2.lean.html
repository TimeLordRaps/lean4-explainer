International Mathematical Olympiad 1965, Problem 2

Suppose that a₁₁x₁ + a₁₂x₂ + a₁₃x₃ = 0 a₂₁x₁ + a₂₂x₂ + a₂₃x₃ = 0 a₃₁x₁ + a₃₂x₂ + a₃₃x₃ = 0

where (A) a₁₁, a₂₂, a₃₃ are positive (B) the remaining aᵢⱼ are negative (C) in each row i, the sum of the coefficients aᵢⱼ is positive.

Prove that x₁ = x₂ = x₃ = 0.
