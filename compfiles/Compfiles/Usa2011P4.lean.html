USA Mathematical Olympiad 2011, Problem 4

For any integer n ≥ 2, define P(n) to be the proposition:

P(n) ≡ 2^(2^n) % (2^n - 1) is a power of 4

Either prove that P(n) is always true, or find a counterexample.
