USA Mathematical Olympiad 2000, Problem 5

Let A₁A₂A₃ be a triangle, and let ω₁ be a circle in its plane passing through A₁ and A₂. Suppose there exist circles ω₂,ω₃,⋯,ω₇ such that for k=2,3,⋯,7, circle ωₖ is externally tangent to ωₖ₋₁ and passes through Aₖ and Aₖ₊₁ (indices mod 3).

Prove that ω₇ = ω₁.
