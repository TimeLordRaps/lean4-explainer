USA Mathematical Olympiad 1993, Problem 5

A sequence xₙ of positive reals satisfies xₙ₋₁xₙ₊₁ ≤ xₙ². Let aₙ be the average of the terms x₀, x₁, ..., xₙ and bₙ be the average of the terms x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ. Show that aₙbₙ₋₁ ≥ aₙ₋₁bₙ.
