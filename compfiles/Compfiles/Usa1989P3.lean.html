USA Mathematical Olympiad 1989, Problem 3

Let P(z) = zⁿ + c₁zⁿ⁻¹ + ⋯ + cₙ be a polynomial in the complex variable z, with real coefficients cₖ. Suppose that |P(i)| < 1. Prove that there exist real numbers a and b such that P(a + bi) = 0 and (a² + b² + 1)² < 4b² + 1.
