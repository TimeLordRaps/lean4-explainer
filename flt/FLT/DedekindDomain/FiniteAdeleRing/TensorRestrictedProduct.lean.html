Tensor product distributing over a restricted product

For a flat R-module M and a restricted product of R-modules N i relative to submodules L i, we construct the natural R-linear (and M-linear) map M ⊗_R ∏'ᵢ [N i, L i] → ∏'ᵢ [M ⊗_R N i, M ⊗_R L i].
