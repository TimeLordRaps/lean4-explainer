Basics on SL₂, its centre, and the image of PSL₂ in PGL₂

This file proves the general facts about SL₂(F) and PSL₂(F) over a field F needed for Dickson's classification of finite subgroups of PGL₂(𝔽̄_p):

  • SL2.closure_elementary_eq_top: SL₂(F) is generated by the elementary matrices E12 a and E21 b;
  • SL2ToPGL_ker_eq_center and SL2_center_card: the kernel of SL₂(F) → PGL₂(F) is the centre {±1}, of order 2 when char F ≠ 2;
  • SL2_card: |SL₂(F)| = (q² - 1) * q for F a finite field with q elements;
  • PSLImageInPGL_index: for F finite of odd characteristic, the image of PSL₂(F) in PGL₂(F) has index 2, and index_two_subgroup_iso_PSL: it is the unique subgroup of index two consisting of the classes of matrices with square determinant;
  • PSL2_ZMod3_equiv_GF31: the identification PSL₂(𝔽₃) ≃* A₄ obtained by letting SL₂(𝔽₃) act on the four points of ℙ¹(𝔽₃).
