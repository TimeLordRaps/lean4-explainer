Recognition of A₅ inside PGL₂(𝔽̄_p)

This file proves Dickson.recognition_A5-style results: a finite subgroup of PGLOf (K p) = PGL₂(𝔽̄_p) of order 60 satisfying the constraints arising in the wild case of Dickson's classification is isomorphic to the alternating group A₅.

The proof is the classical Sylow-theoretic argument that a group of order 60 with no normal Sylow subgroups is simple, hence isomorphic to A₅ (via the action on the five Sylow 2-subgroups / cosets argument), combined with the dichotomy Dickson.element_dichotomy for elements of a finite subgroup of PGLOf (K p): every element either has order p or has order coprime to p.
