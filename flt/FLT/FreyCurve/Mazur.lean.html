    Source documentation

    The p-torsion in the Frey curve associated to a counterexample to FLT is irreducible.

    L4DEL explanation

    The statement is that the Galois representation on the p-torsion of the Frey curve is irreducible, a consequence of Mazur's work as used in Serre's 1987 Duke paper.

    1. The proof is the project tactic knownin1980s, which behaves like sorry; it is not a formal proof of the claim.
    2. The project's own policy (module doc of the tactic) allows it only for results the maintainers believe are provable on paper from literature known before 1990.
