Concrete Hecke operators on quaternionic automorphic forms

We define the Hecke operators Tᵥ acting on the space of weight-2 automorphic forms on a totally definite quaternion algebra. These are the concrete Hecke operators associated to a finite place v and a level subgroup U₁(S, Q), realised via the abstract double-coset construction.

  37. def TotallyDefiniteQuaternionAlgebra.HeckeAlgebra Documented declaration: HeckeAlgebra line 879 source

    Given 𝒮 : U₁Data F R p, HeckeAlgebra D 𝒮 is the Hecke algebra associated to the weight 2 R-valued automorphic forms associated to the discriminant 1 totally definite quaternion algebra D over the totally real field F, of level U₁(S, Q) where S is a finite set of nonzero primes v of 𝓞 F. To make sense of this definition we choose a rigidification, that is, a fixed 𝔸_F^∞-linear isomorphism D ⊗[F] 𝔸_F^∞ = M₂(𝔸_F^∞), enabling us to define level structures and Hecke operators Tᵥ and Uᵥ using 2x2 matrices.

    Details: 𝒮 : U₁Data F R p consist of a set of bad primes S of F with order p characters k(v) →* R, and a set of taylor wiles primes Q.

    U₁(S, Q) is the subgroup of (D ⊗[F] 𝔸_F^∞)ˣ associated, via r, to the matrices which are in GL₂(𝓞ᵥ) for all v ∉ S ∪ Q and which are of the form (* *; 0 *) mod v for all v ∈ S, and (a *; 0 d) mod v with p ∤ ord(a/d) for v ∈ Q.

    The Hecke algebra is defined to be the sub-R-algebra of the weight 2 forms of level U₁(S, Q) generated by the following two kinds of Hecke operators: first there are the operators Tᵥ associated to the matrices (ϖᵥ 0; 0 1) for v ∉ S ∪ Q (here ϖᵥ ∈ 𝔸_F^∞ is a local uniformiser supported at v). Second, there are the Hecke operators Uᵥ,ₐ for v ∈ Q and 0 ≠ αᵥ ∈ 𝓞ᵥ, associated the matries (αᵥ 0; 0 1). These slightly nonstandard Hecke operators satisfy Uᵥ,ₛ * Uᵥ,ₜ = Uᵥ,ₛₜ and in particular this Hecke algebra is commutative (Hecke operators supported at distinct primes commute because the decomposition of the double cosets into single cosets can be done purely locally).
