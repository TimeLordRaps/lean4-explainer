Finite-dimensionality of spaces of weight-2 automorphic forms

We prove that the space of weight-2 automorphic forms on a totally definite quaternion algebra over a totally real number field, of fixed level U, is a finitely generated R-module.

Main results

  • WeightTwoAutomorphicForm.finiteDimensional: the space of weight-2 forms is module-finite over the coefficient ring.
