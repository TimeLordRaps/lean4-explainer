Hurwitz integers

The ring of Hurwitz integers, the maximal order in the rational quaternions generated by 1, i, j, k together with ω := (-1 + i + j + k) / 2.

Main definitions

  • Hurwitz: the ring of Hurwitz integers, defined as a structure with an integer basis 1, ω, i, j.
  • Hurwitz.toQuaternion: the embedding into the rational quaternions.
