Adelic Hurwitz quaternions

Base changes of the Hurwitz integers to ℚ and to ZHat, providing a quaternion algebra over the rationals and its profinite completion as free rank-4 modules over the corresponding base ring.

Main definitions

  • HurwitzRat: the rational quaternions ℚ + ℚi + ℚj + ℚk.
  • HurwitzHat: the profinite Hurwitz quaternions, a free rank-4 module over ZHat.
