Finiteness theorem for the adelic quotient of a division algebra

Let D be a finite-dimensional division algebra over a number field K. Setup and intermediate results towards the classical theorem that the adelic double-coset space D^× \ (D ⊗ 𝔸_K)^× / U is finite for any compact open subgroup U.
