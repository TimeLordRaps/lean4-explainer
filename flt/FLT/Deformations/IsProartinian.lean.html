Proartinian topological rings

A topological ring is proartinian if it is linearly topologized, complete and Hausdorff, and every open ideal is the open kernel of a continuous homomorphism to a finite (Artinian) ring. We provide the basic API and show that complete Noetherian local rings with finite residue field are proartinian under their adic topology.
