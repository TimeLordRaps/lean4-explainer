Functoriality of the absolute Galois group

For a field extension K → L, the induced map between absolute Galois groups Γ L → Γ K is Field.absoluteGaloisGroup.map in Mathlib; here we record how it interacts with the chosen embedding of the algebraic closures, together with finite-index results for fixing subgroups.
