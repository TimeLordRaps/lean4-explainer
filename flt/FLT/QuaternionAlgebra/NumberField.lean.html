Definitions of various compact open subgrups of Dˣ and GL₂(𝔸_F^∞)

We define U₁(v) as a subgroup of GL₂(Fᵥ), and U₁(S) as a subgroup of GL₂(𝔸_F^∞). We introduce the concept of a rigidification r : (D ⊗[F] 𝔸_F^∞) ≅ M₂(𝔸_F^∞) in order to push U₁(S) over to a subgroup of (D ⊗[F] 𝔸_F^∞)ˣ.

    A rigidification of a quaternion algebra D over a number field F is a fixed choice of 𝔸_F^∞-algebra isomorphism D ⊗[F] 𝔸_F^∞ = M₂(𝔸_F^∞). In other words, it is a choice of splitting of D ⊗[F] Fᵥ (i.e. an isomorphism to M₂(Fᵥ)) for all finite places v together with a guarantee that the isomorphism works on the integral level at all but finitely many places. Such a rigidification exists if and only if F is unramified at all finite places.
