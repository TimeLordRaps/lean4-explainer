  9. lemma WeierstrassCurve.nodePoly_map_splits_iff_isSquare Split criterion (residue characteristic ≠ 2) line 145 source

    Source documentation

    Split criterion (residue characteristic ≠ 2). Over a field k of characteristic ≠ 2, the node polynomial splits — i.e. the two tangent directions at the node are k-rational — exactly when its discriminant -c₄ c₆ (splitPolynomial_discrim) is a square in k. This is the tool that, applied to a quadratic twist via the scaling -c₄' c₆' = (t²-4n)⁵ · (-c₄ c₆), turns a nonsplit reduction into a split one after twisting by the right square class.

  20. theorem WeierstrassCurve.isMinimal_of_valuation_c₄_eq_one Minimality criterion line 305 source

    Source documentation

    Minimality criterion. An integral Weierstrass equation over K whose c₄ is a unit of R (equivalently, valuation c₄ = 1) is already minimal: any change of variables C keeping the equation integral must have valuation C.u ≥ 1 (else valuation (C • W).c₄ = valuation C.u⁻⁴ > 1, contradicting integrality), so valuation (C • W).Δ = valuation C.u⁻¹² · valuation W.Δ ≤ valuation W.Δ. This is the tool that shows the twist W we build is minimal without minimising by hand.

    This is the unit-c₄ case of the Kraus–Laska criterion: the special case "v(c₄) < 4 or v(Δ) < 12 implies minimal" of Silverman, The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves, Remark VII.1.1, restricted to v(c₄) = 0. The hypothesis is stated at the field level — via the adic valuation of W.c₄ : K — to match mathlib's phrasing of WeierstrassCurve.HasMultiplicativeReduction, whose multiplicativeReduction field is exactly (maximalIdeal R).valuation K W.c₄ = 1.
