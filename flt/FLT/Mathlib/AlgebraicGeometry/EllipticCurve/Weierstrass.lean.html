  2. theorem WeierstrassCurve.splitPolynomial_discrim The discriminant of the node's tangent polynomial `c₄ T² + a₁ c₄ T - (54 b₆ - 3 b₂ b₄ + a₂ c₄)` (the quadratic controlling split multiplicative reduction) equals `-c₄ c₆` line 40 source

    The discriminant of the node's tangent polynomial c₄ T² + a₁ c₄ T - (54 b₆ - 3 b₂ b₄ + a₂ c₄) (the quadratic controlling split multiplicative reduction) equals -c₄ c₆. Hence the tangent directions at the node are rational over the residue field exactly when -c₄ c₆ is a square there; twisting by (t, n) multiplies -c₄ c₆ by (t² - 4n)⁵ = (t² - 4n)⁴ · (t² - 4n), i.e. by the twisting parameter up to a square (see c₄_quadraticTwistOf, c₆_quadraticTwistOf).
