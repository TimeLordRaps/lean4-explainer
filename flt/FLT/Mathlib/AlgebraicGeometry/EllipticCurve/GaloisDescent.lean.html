Galois descent for Weierstrass curve data

Proposed new Mathlib file Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurve.GaloisDescent: a change of variables (or a point) over a separable quadratic extension L/K fixed by Gal(L/K) descends to K.
