  4. instance (instance at line 73) The ring of integers of a valuative topology is linearly topologized: the open balls `integerBallIdeal` are ideals and form a basis of neighbourhoods of `0` line 73 source

    The ring of integers of a valuative topology is linearly topologized: the open balls integerBallIdeal are ideals and form a basis of neighbourhoods of 0. This is the hypothesis under which mathlib's topological power-series evaluation (PowerSeries.eval₂Hom) applies to 𝒪[K] — it can never apply to the field K itself, which has no nontrivial ideals.
