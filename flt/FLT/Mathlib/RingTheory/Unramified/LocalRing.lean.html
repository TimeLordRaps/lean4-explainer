Unramified lifts of separable residue field extensions

Material for Mathlib.RingTheory.Unramified.LocalRing, whose characterization of unramified extensions of local rings (Algebra.FormallyUnramified.iff_map_maximalIdeal_eq) this complements with an existence statement.

A finite separable extension k' of the residue field k of a discrete valuation ring R (with fraction field K) lifts to an unramified extension L/K of the same degree: writing k' = k[X]/(p̄) via a power basis (primitive element), we lift p̄ to a monic P ∈ R[X] and set L = K[X]/(P). The integral extension S = R[X]/(P) is again a discrete valuation ring, with S/πS ≅ k[X]/(p̄) = k' — so its maximal ideal is generated by a uniformizer π of R (ramification index 1) and its residue field is k'.

The ingredients — AdjoinRoot.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_irreducible_map_residue (R[X]/(P) is an unramified discrete valuation ring for monic P with irreducible reduction, in FLT.Mathlib.RingTheory.AdjoinRoot) and the separability transfer Polynomial.Monic.separable_map_algebraMap_of_separable_map_residue (in FLT.Mathlib.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.Basic) — are assembled here into

  • exists_unramified_extension_of_residueField : the lifting theorem.

The quadratic case of the lifting theorem is the local-field input to WeierstrassCurve.exists_quadraticTwist_hasSplitMultiplicativeReduction.

    **Unramified lifting, arbitrary degree.** A finite separable extension `k'` of the residue field of a discrete valuation ring `R` lifts to an unramified extension `L` of its fraction field `K`: there is a finite separable extension `L/K` with `[L : K] = [k' : k]` and a discrete valuation ring `S` with fraction field `L`, containing `R` via a local homomorphism, whose residue field is `k'`. (The residue degree being `[L : K]` forces ramification index `1`.)
