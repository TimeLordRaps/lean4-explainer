  5. theorem Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension.discrim_ne_zero Documented declaration: discrim_ne_zero line 68 source

    If θ generates a separable quadratic extension of K — that is, lies outside K — and t, n denote its trace and norm, so that θ² = tθ - n, then the discriminant t² - 4n of the minimal polynomial of θ is nonzero. (Over the nontrivial automorphism σ it equals (θ - σθ)² with σθ ≠ θ. In characteristic 2 the statement says t ≠ 0: separable quadratic extensions are Artin–Schreier extensions.)
