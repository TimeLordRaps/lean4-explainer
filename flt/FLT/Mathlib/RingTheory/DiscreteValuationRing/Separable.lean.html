    A monic polynomial over a discrete valuation ring R whose reduction to the residue field is separable stays separable over the fraction field K.

    Proof: if P.map (algebraMap R K) is not separable, it has a monic common factor g of positive degree with its derivative. Since R is integrally closed and g is a monic divisor of the monic P, Gauss's lemma descends g to a monic Q ∈ R[X], which then divides P and derivative P already in R[X] (map_dvd_map). Reducing mod 𝔪_R, the reduction of Q is a common factor of positive degree of P̄ and derivative P̄, contradicting P̄.Separable.

    A monic polynomial over a discrete valuation ring `R` whose reduction to the residue field is separable stays separable over the fraction field `K`. Proof: if `P.map (algebraMap R K)` is not separable, it has a monic common factor `g` of positive degree with its derivative. Since `R` is integrally closed and `g` is a monic divisor of the monic `P`, Gauss's lemma descends `g` to a monic `Q ∈ R[X]`, which then divides `P` and `derivative P` already in `R[X]` (`map_dvd_map`). Reducing mod `𝔪_R`, the reduction of `Q` is a common factor of positive degree of `P̄` and `derivative P̄`, contradicting `P̄.Separable`.
