R[X]/(P) as an unramified discrete valuation ring

Proposed new Mathlib file Mathlib.RingTheory.DiscreteValuationRing.AdjoinRoot: for a monic P over a discrete valuation ring R with irreducible reduction, S = R[X]/(P) is a discrete valuation ring, unramified over R, with fraction field K[X]/(P) and residue field k[X]/(P̄); separability of simple extensions; auxiliary local-ring lemmas.
