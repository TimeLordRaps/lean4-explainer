    A group G with exactly two elements — the identity 1 and a distinguished element g ≠ 1, every element being equal to one of the two — is isomorphic to Multiplicative (ZMod 2), via the isomorphism sending g to Multiplicative.ofAdd 1. The generator g and the exhaustion key are taken as explicit data (rather than a bare Nat.card G = 2) so the isomorphism is computable.
