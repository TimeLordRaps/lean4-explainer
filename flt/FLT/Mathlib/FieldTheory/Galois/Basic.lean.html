  10. def quadraticCharacter The quadratic character of `Aut(M/K)` attached to a separable quadratic subextension `K ⊆ L ⊆ M`: it sends `σ` to `1` if `σ` fixes `L` pointwise, and to `-1` otherwise line 116 source

    The quadratic character of Aut(M/K) attached to a separable quadratic subextension K ⊆ L ⊆ M: it sends σ to 1 if σ fixes L pointwise, and to -1 otherwise.

    Since L/K is normal, every K-automorphism of M maps L to L, and multiplicativity (map_mul') follows because restriction to L is a homomorphism to the order 2 group Gal(L/K). Taking M = L gives the quadratic character of Gal(L/K) itself; if M/K is normal then this character is the composite of the restriction Aut(M/K) → Gal(L/K) with the unique isomorphism Gal(L/K) ≃ {±1}, and in particular is surjective (quadraticCharacter_surjective).
