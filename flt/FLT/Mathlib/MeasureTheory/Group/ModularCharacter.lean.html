Unimodular groups

We define unimodular groups, and show that [K₂ : gK₁g⁻¹] = [K₂ : K₁] for open compacts in unimodular groups.

We also show that GLₙ(K) is unimodular for K a second countable locally compact Hausdorff topological field.
