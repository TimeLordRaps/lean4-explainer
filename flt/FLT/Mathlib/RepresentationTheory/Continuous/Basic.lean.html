The internal hom of continuous representations

This file constructs ContRepresentation.linHom ρ2 ρ3, the continuous representation of G on M2 →L[k] M3 by conjugation, g • φ = ρ3 g ∘L φ ∘L ρ2 g⁻¹, where M2 →L[k] M3 carries the topology induced from the compact-open topology on C(M2, M3).
