  2. lemma FermatLastTheorem.of_p_ge_5 It suffices to prove FLT for primes at least 5 line 38 source

    Source documentation

    If Fermat's Last Theorem is true for primes p ≥ 5, then FLT is true.

    L4DEL explanation

    Any exponent n at least 3 is divisible by 3, by 4, or by a prime at least 5, and FLT for a divisor implies FLT for the multiple.

    1. Split on the arithmetic fact that n is divisible by 3, or by 4, or by some prime p at least 5.
    2. If 3 divides n, use Euler's theorem fermatLastTheoremThree from Mathlib and monotonicity along divisibility.
    3. If 4 divides n, use Fermat's theorem fermatLastTheoremFour from Mathlib in the same way.
    4. Otherwise a prime p at least 5 divides n and the hypothesis H gives FLT for p, which passes up to n.

    Prerequisites: Nat.three_dvd_or_four_dvd_or_prime_dvd
