Vanishing filters of ideals in product rings

For an ideal p of Πᵢ R i, we define the vanishing filter vanishingFilter p of p: the filter of subsets s such that the characteristic idempotent of s lies in p. Prime ideals give rise to ultrafilters via this construction.
