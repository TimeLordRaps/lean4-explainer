The proof of Fermat's Last Theorem

This file contains the "main spine" of the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem.

The strategy of the proof is to prove the theorem via a series of reductions. In other words, we will define mathematical statements B_1=FLT, B_2, B_3, B_4, ..., up to around B_{12}, and then prove that B_2 implies B_1, B_3 implies B_2, B_4 implies B_3 etc etc, and then ultimately that B_{12} is true.

    If the p-torsion of every Frey curve must be reducible, but Mazur's theorem says it is irreducible, then no Frey package can exist.

    1. Assume B4 and suppose a Frey package P is given.
    2. B4 applied to P says the Galois representation on the p-torsion of P's Frey curve is not irreducible.
    3. FreyPackage.mazur says that same representation is irreducible, giving a contradiction, so the type of Frey packages is empty (B3).

    This is Mathlib's FermatLastTheorem, obtained by chaining the reductions B4 to B3 to B2 to B1; it is not yet a complete proof, because it rests on the unproved theorem B4_proof.

    1. The proof term is B1_proof, and B1 is defined to be exactly FermatLastTheorem.
    2. B1_proof comes from B2_proof by the lemma that FLT follows from the case of primes at least 5.
    3. B2_proof comes from B3_proof, which comes from B4_proof by the Mazur step.
    4. B4_proof is currently `sorry`, so the theorem depends on `sorryAx`. The file FermatsLastTheorem.lean records that the axioms of the final statement include sorryAx and the project axiom knownin1980s.
