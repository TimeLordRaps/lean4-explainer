Lifting hardly ramified residual representations

If ρ̄ : Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ) → GL₂(k) is an irreducible hardly ramified mod-ℓ representation, we construct (as part of the inputs to FLT) a hardly ramified ℓ-adic lift to characteristic zero.
