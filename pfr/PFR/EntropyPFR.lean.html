  2. theorem entropic_PFR_conjecture Entropic PFR (constants 11 and 6) line 54 source

    Source documentation

    entropic_PFR_conjecture: For two $G$-valued random variables $X_1^0, X_2^0$, there is some subgroup $H \leq G$ such that $d[X_1^0;U_H] + d[X_2^0;U_H] \le 11 d[X_1^0;X_2^0]$, and both $d[X_1^0; U_H]$ and $d[X_2^0; U_H]$ are at most $6 d[X_1^0;X_2^0]$.

    L4DEL explanation

    Any two random variables in an F2-vector space are within a constant multiple of their Ruzsa distance of a uniform distribution on some subgroup.

    1. Statement: for finite G of characteristic 2 and a package of two G-valued variables X01, X02 with parameter eta = 1/9, there is a subspace H and a uniform variable U on H with d[X01 ; U] + d[X02 ; U] at most 11 d[X01 ; X02], and each of d[X01 ; U] and d[X02 ; U] at most 6 d[X01 ; X02].
    2. Take a pair X1, X2 minimizing the tau functional (tau_minimizer_exists). The theorem tau_strictly_decreases then forces d[X1 ; X2] = 0 when eta = 1/9.
    3. Zero Ruzsa distance means X1 and X2 are uniform on cosets of a common subgroup (exists_isUniform_of_rdist_eq_zero), which gives H and U.
    4. The distances from the original variables to U follow from minimality of tau and the Ruzsa triangle inequality (rdist_triangle), finished by linear arithmetic.

    Prerequisites: tau_minimizer_exists, tau_strictly_decreases, rdist_triangle, exists_isUniform_of_rdist_eq_zero

    See also: entropic_PFR_conjecture_improv
