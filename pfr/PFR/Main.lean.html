    A uniform random variable on a set with |A - A| at most K|A| is within log K of itself in Ruzsa distance.

    1. Take two independent copies U and U' of the uniform variable on A.
    2. The entropy of U - U' is at most log |A - A|, because U - U' takes values in A - A.
    3. The entropy of a uniform variable on A is log |A|, so d[U ; U'] = H[U - U'] - H[U]/2 - H[U']/2 is at most log |A - A| - log |A|, which is at most log K.

    Prerequisites: rdist

    See also: rdist_triangle

  7. lemma PFR_conjecture_aux Covering lemma behind PFR line 153 source

    Auxiliary statement towards the polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa (PFR) conjecture: if A is a subset of an elementary abelian 2-group of doubling constant at most $K$, then there exists a subgroup H such that A can be covered by at most K^(13/2) |A|^(1/2) / |H|^(1/2) cosets of H, and H has the same cardinality as A up to a multiplicative factor K^11.

They have not been human-reviewed as explanations; treat them as unverified and not mathematical authority. Retained generation history: The 71 original curated annotations were produced in a Claude Sonnet 5.5 session at medium effort; Claude Opus 5.5 is recorded in that session as the advisor model, not the direct author. The bulk source-derived expansion was generated mechanically from pinned source comments by tooling developed in a GPT-5.6 Sol session at ultra reasoning; GPT-5.6 Sol did not originate the quoted source prose.

    A set of doubling K is covered by few cosets of a subgroup whose size is within a power of K of the set's size.

    1. Pick a uniform random variable UA on A and show its Ruzsa distance to itself is at most log K, using rdist_le_of_isUniform_of_card_sub_le.
    2. Apply the entropic PFR theorem with parameter 1/9 to get a subgroup H whose uniform distribution is within 11/2 log K of UA in Ruzsa distance.
    3. Compare entropies of independent copies of UA and UH to get that |A| and |H| agree up to a factor K^11.
    4. Find a point x0 where VA - VH has large probability, which says A meets the coset H + x0 in at least K^(-11/2) |A|^(1/2) |H|^(1/2) points.
    5. Cover A by translates of A' - A' (A' = A meet (H + x0)), which sits inside H, using the Ruzsa covering lemma. The number of translates is at most K^(13/2) |A|^(1/2) |H|^(-1/2).

    Prerequisites: entropic_PFR_conjecture, rdist_le_of_isUniform_of_card_sub_le

    See also: PFR_conjecture

  8. theorem PFR_conjecture Polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa conjecture (characteristic 2) line 261 source

    The polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa (PFR) conjecture: if A is a subset of an elementary abelian 2-group of doubling constant at most K, then A can be covered by at most 2 * K ^ 12 cosets of a subgroup of cardinality at most |A|.

    The ambient group need not be finite, in which case H and c are finite.

The bulk source-derived expansion was generated mechanically from pinned source comments by tooling developed in a GPT-5.6 Sol session at ultra reasoning; GPT-5.6 Sol did not originate the quoted source prose.

    A finite set in an F2-vector space with small doubling is covered by about K^12 translates of a subgroup no bigger than the set itself.

    1. Statement: if A is a nonempty finite subset of an F2-vector space G and |A + A| is at most K times |A|, there are a subspace H and a finite set c with fewer than 2 * K^12 elements such that A lies inside c + H and |H| is at most |A|.
    2. If G is infinite, the proof first replaces G by the span of A, which is finite, runs the finite case there, and pushes the answer back along the inclusion.
    3. In the finite case it applies the covering lemma PFR_conjecture_aux, which gives H and a covering set c with |c| at most K^(13/2) |A|^(1/2) |H|^(-1/2) and |H| comparable to |A| up to a factor K^11.
    4. If |H| is at most |A| that H already works. Otherwise it picks a subspace H' of H with |A|/2 < |H'| at most |A| and covers H by cosets of H' using a transversal, which multiplies the number of translates by |H|/|H'| and gives the bound below 2 * K^12.

    Prerequisites: PFR_conjecture_aux, PFR_conjecture_pos_aux'

    See also: PFR_conjecture_improv, entropic_PFR_conjecture

    Sources and attribution: Pinned Lean source (ce7ac95e620a), Pinned project documentation, Polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa proof paper, arXiv v2

    doubling constant: the number K bounding |A + A| / |A| from above; small K means A is close to a subgroup.

    elementary abelian 2-group: an abelian group in which every element has order at most 2; the same thing as a vector space over the field with two elements, here a module over ZMod 2.
