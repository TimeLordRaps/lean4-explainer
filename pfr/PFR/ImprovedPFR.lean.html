Improved PFR

An improvement to PFR that lowers the exponent from 12 to 11.

The entropy form (entropic_PFR_conjecture_improv) gives $d[X_1^0; U_H] + d[X_2^0; U_H] \le 10 d[X_1^0; X_2^0]$. Combining with the Ruzsa triangle inequality, each summand is at most $\frac{11}{2} d[X_1^0; X_2^0]$.

  18. theorem PFR_conjecture_improv PFR with exponent 11 line 953 source

    The polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa (PFR) conjecture: if $A$ is a subset of an elementary abelian 2-group of doubling constant at most $K$, then $A$ can be covered by at most $2K^{11$} cosets of a subgroup of cardinality at most $|A|$.

    The ambient group need not be finite, in which case H and c are finite.

    Annotation provenance. AI-generated and not human-reviewed. Treat this explanatory draft as unverified and not mathematical authority.

    The improved form of PFR: fewer than 2 * K^11 cosets of a subgroup of size at most |A| cover A.

    1. Statement: if A is nonempty and finite in an F2-vector space with |A + A| at most K |A|, there are a subspace H and a finite c with fewer than 2 * K^11 elements, |H| at most |A|, and A inside c + H.
    2. Reduce to finite G by passing to the span of A, as in PFR_conjecture.
    3. Use PFR_conjecture_improv_aux (covering by K^6 |A|^(1/2) |H|^(-1/2) cosets, |H| within K^10 of |A|), which is derived from entropic_PFR_conjecture_improv.
    4. Shrink H to a subspace of size between |A|/2 and |A| when |H| exceeds |A|, and cover H by its cosets.

    Prerequisites: PFR_conjecture_improv_aux, entropic_PFR_conjecture_improv

    See also: PFR_conjecture
